**Kyle Norton**: CRO at Owner.com for 2 years 7 months with 18 years 2 months of total professional experience. Previous roles include SVP Sales & Partnerships at Owner.com, Founding Member - Toronto Chapter at Pavilion, and Director of Revenue & Merchant Success, Canada at Shopify. 🚨 I’m excited to announce that I’m hiring for a world class Sales Manager! 🚨

The growth engine has been ripping the last few months so we’ve pulled forward some of our headcount growth and need to add a new Manager to the team ASAP. 😅

I’m looking for someone who is currently an award winning outbound AE manager, LOVES to coach, can solve complex problems and has transactional sales experience.

What you need to know about Sales at Owner:
👷 We sell to a blue collar, SMB buyer. Mom & pop restaurant owners want to shoot straight and need some hand holding when it comes to technology.
⏩ We manage a transactional sales cycle between 1-7 days. Great sales craft is essential to success in our environment.
🌲 We’re a growth obsessed team. I’m looking for someone who invests in themselves relentlessly and does the same with their team.
♥️ When we say we’re customer obsessed, we actually mean it. We solve problems with customer experience and customer value first. We also expect all leaders to spend significant time in front of customers.
💽 We’re a fully remote company and don’t have plans to set up offices. Confidence and excitement to work asynchronously is critical.
🏆 We hold a high bar. This is a performance culture and we expect effort and outcomes. There’s pressure in that but you also get to be part of a winning team.
💰 We pay competitively.
🤖 We’re hyper focused on enabling our teams with tools and technology. I’m looking for a systems and data oriented thinker who can maximize the impact of our team members.

While I’m really grateful for the incredible candidate volumes we receive on applications, it has quickly become overwhelming to get to the right candidates in a timely manner.

With that in mind, please only apply if:
1️⃣ You have previous AE leadership experience.
2️⃣ You have sold and built in an SMB environment with a transactional sales cycle.
3️⃣ Truly believe that you are a world class sales leader and have the track record to prove it. We’ll be asking for receipts.
4️⃣ You are located in Canada or the US.
5️⃣ You can provide references early in our process that will insist that I would be insane not to hire you. We’ll ask for references from reps to assess for servant leadership.
7️⃣You friggin LOVE sales and are energized by coaching front line managers and reps, writing scripts, testing new approaches and diving deep into data.

If this sounds like the type of environment that you want to be a part of, shoot Bret Baumbach and Natalie Sturino a message, and apply below.
