# The best AI tool your sales team built this quarter is the reason you're going to miss your number. | Kyle Norton · LinkedIn · 2026-04-30

**Kyle Norton**: CRO at Owner.com for 2 years 7 months with 18 years 1 month of total professional experience. Previous roles include SVP Sales & Partnerships at Owner.com, Founding Member - Toronto Chapter at Pavilion, and Director of Revenue & Merchant Success, Canada at Shopify. Based in San Francisco, California, United States [US].

---

The best AI tool your sales team built this quarter is the reason you're going to miss your number.

It's slick. It works. It saves four hours a week.

It's also a sales director doing rev ops work instead of being on customer calls. Which is the only thing they were actually hired to do.

Adrian Rosenkranz, CRO at Webflow, calls this the efficiency trap. His diagnostic question for any AI workflow on your team:

"Are you on more customer calls?"

If the answer is no, the tool could be a problem, no matter how impressive it looks in a demo.

The other side of this conversation is what's happening outside your building. 20% of traffic across Webflow's 300,000 customers is now AI bots. 43% of that is live queries on behalf of buyers who will never click your link. The discovery layer for B2B has fundamentally moved.

If you're a CRO, you should be doing two things this quarter:

1. Centralizing AI engineering so your reps can stop building tools and start being with customers.
2. Building a dashboard for your share of LLM citations the same way you track search rank.

Almost everyone is doing neither.
