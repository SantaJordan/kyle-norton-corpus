# 🎉 🎉 🎉 I’m super excited to announce that I’m hiring for a world class Director of Sales! 🎉 🎉 🎉 · 2024-02-13T00:00:00+00:00

🎉 🎉 🎉 I’m super excited to announce that I’m hiring for a world class Director of Sales! 🎉 🎉 🎉

We’re continuing to hire in sales to keep up with wild market demand and we need our very 1st second line sales leader ASAP (see: yesterday). 😅 This individual will consolidate the 2 AE and 3 XDR teams and grow the sales organization rapidly over the coming years.

I’m looking for someone who is third line ready because they will likely be hiring Directors of their own within the next 12 months given our trajectory and growth model. 📈

The most important thing you need to understand about this opportunity is our vision. I'm absolutely committed to building the greatest sales team in the world and I'm looking for someone who shares that aspiration and is willing to go 100% all-in with us to get there. This isn't a platitude, I really mean it and want someone who feels the same way. 🤝

What else you need to know about Sales at Owner:
👷♂️ We sell to a blue collar, SMB buyer. Mom & pop restaurant owners want to shoot straight and need some hand holding when it comes to technology.
🏃♀️ We manage a transactional sales cycle between 1-7 days. Great sales craft is essential to success in our environment.
🌲 We’re a growth obsessed team. I’m looking for someone who invests in themselves relentlessly and does the same with their team.
💙 When we say we’re customer obsessed, we actually mean it. We solve problems with customer experience and customer value first. We also expect all leaders to spend significant time in front of customers.
💻 We’re a fully remote company and don’t have plans to set up offices. Confidence and excitement to work asynchronously is critical.
🏆 We hold a high bar. This is a performance culture and we expect effort and outcomes. There’s pressure but you get to be part of a winning team.
💸 We pay competitively.
🤖 We’re hyper focused on enabling our teams with tools and technology. I’m looking for a systems and data oriented thinker who can maximize the impact of our team members.

While I’m really grateful for the incredible candidate volumes we receive on applications, it has quickly become overwhelming to get to the right candidates in a timely manner.

With that in mind, please please only apply if:
- You have previous second or third line leadership experience.
- You friggin LOVE sales and are energized by coaching front line managers and reps, writing scripts, testing new approaches and diving deep into data.
- You have sold and built in an SMB environment with a transactional sales cycle.
- Truly believe that you are a world class sales leader and have the track record to prove it.
- You are located in Canada or the US.
- You can provide references early in our process that will insist that I would be insane not to hire you.

If this sounds like the type of environment that you want to be a part of, shoot me a message, and apply below.
