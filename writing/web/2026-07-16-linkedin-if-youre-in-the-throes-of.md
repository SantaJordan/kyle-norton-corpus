# If you're in the throes of trying to figure out how to get AI working for your GTM, July 22nd is worth your time. · 2026-07-16T00:00:00+00:00

If you're in the throes of trying to figure out how to get AI working for your GTM, July 22nd is worth your time.

I'm joining Kyle Duffy from Gradient, Jordan Crawford from Blueprint GTM, Elaine Zelby from Tofu, and James Kaikis from GTMshift for the AI Fluent Revenue Leader event in downtown San Francisco.

This one is going to be different from most events I've been part of. We're all operators. We're all in these tools and building every single day, and James has asked us to get really specific, really tactical, and genuinely in the weeds. Which, for better or for worse, is my favorite way to present.

My session is going to dig into GTM AI foundations.

We're going to work through build versus buy, what your personal AI stack should actually look like, and what your team needs to be doing right now.

I'm going to share frameworks I've given my own team and some of what I've built personally. Including the mistakes, because the world has changed so fast in the last couple of quarters that some of what worked isn't working anymore.

We're not going to debate whether BDRs exist in five years. We're not going to talk about which layer will accrue all the value. We're going to talk about operating.

If you're in it and want to figure this out, come join us. Seats are limited to keep the room tight and the conversation real.

Link to register is in the comments. 👇
