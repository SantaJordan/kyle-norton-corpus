# 🎉 After a bunch more promotions, we’re back at it and hiring SDR’s and BDR’s! 🎉 · 2024-05-28T00:00:00+00:00

🎉 After a bunch more promotions, we’re back at it and hiring SDR’s and BDR’s! 🎉

(Read this entire post for tips on how to get our attention)

By the end of this month, we will have promoted 9 sales people in the last 4 months and our last 4 new AE’s were internal BDR promotions. 😲

This means we need to hire our next generation of SDR’s and BDR’s.

If you’re looking for a winning product, opportunities for growth and a team that will push you to be your best, Owner is a pretty great spot.

What you need to know about selling at Owner:
☎ We are a phone sales organization. Over 90% of Opps are booked on the phone. If you love talking to prospects instead of hiding behind a keyboard, this is your jam.
👷♀️ We sell to a blue collar, SMB buyer. Restaurant owners want to shoot straight so you need a thick skin.
🌵 We’re a growth obsessed team. We’re going to invest in you like crazy and expect you to do the same. If you don’t like reading or self-improvement, this might be a struggle.
🏗 We promote from within. Most of our XDR’s will be promoted to new roles within their first 12 months.
🏅 We hold a high bar. This is a performance culture and we expect effort and outcomes. There’s pressure in that but you also get to be part of a winning team.
🏦 We pay very competitively.
💾 We’re hyper focused on enabling our sales team with tools and technology. We’ve built a bunch of cool tech in-house to maximize the impact of the effort of our team.

If this sounds like the type of environment that you want to be a part of, please reach out to Vanessa Matthew and Claire and apply below. Bonus points if you listen to my pod with Kevin Dorsey and follow the instructions hidden in there (but replace me with the XDR managers please!).

🏆 One important tip for your outreach and this is arguably the most important! Lead with results. We only hire top performers so make sure to include some of your performance highlights in your messages. 🏆

Lastly, this may seem harsh but I want to help save applicants valuable time researching and building outreach to us. If you’re not currently a top 5-10% performer in your organization or have a history of high achievement outside of sales, it’s probably not worth reaching out. The last time I posted BDR roles, we got 800 applications in 3 days so it’s a very competitive field.
