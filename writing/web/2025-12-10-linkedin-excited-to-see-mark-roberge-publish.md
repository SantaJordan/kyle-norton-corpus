Excited to see Mark Roberge publish #TheScienceOfScaling.

Many of us involved at Stage 2 Capital have seen first-hand the impact of this framework on founders and early-stage teams. Mark's first book (The Sales Acceleration Formula) was also one of the most important books I read early in my sales leadership career.

Here is a blurb from the book that I often preach:

“Sales is not magic; it is a science like finance, engineering, and marketing. Yes, there are some creative aspects to sales, just as there are creative aspects to these peer functions. In sales, this creativity is sometimes described as the “art of sales.” However, sales is too critical to be viewed simply as an art form; it is the organization's lifeblood, and a system is necessary."

Couldn't agree more.

Order the book (link in comments). 100% of the proceeds are donated to mental health.
