I've said this to a number of up and coming revenue leaders recently but there's no better value in professional development than Pavilion. You could take Frontline Manager School, CRO School and Revenue Architecture School in your first year or two in Pavilion and walk away with wild value. Those are 3 of the best courses in revenue leadership in the world and could arguably cost $5K a piece. You get that, more courses and an incredible community for a few thousand bucks a year. So glad to hear about this success Justin!

The past 2 months have been life altering. When 2024 began, I finally decided to join Pavilion. The Go-To-Market Community had been recommended to me several times, most recently by one of my very first leaders Kyle Norton, and within minutes of becoming a member I knew I had made the right decision. For those of you who are like me, now's the time to jump off the fence. Sam Jacobs and his team have built something really special. I had originally joined with the intention of taking a couple of courses to level myself up, and I was blown away by how engaging and supportive the community was from day one. Huge shoutout to Leslie Greenwood, and the team behind crafting Pavilions member experience. The reason I'm celebrating this week, is that I just successfully completed two fantastic courses. CRO School - I can't say enough. These 10 weeks have been absolutely packed with strategy and tactics to help any sales leader significantly level up. 10/10. Thank you to Alyson Rich and the rest of the learning team for their support. The best part of this adventure was sharing it with my cohort. I was so fortunate to share an hour a week with leaders like this - Chris Thomas Colleen Martin Alana Zimmer🔬 Marshall Poindexter 💊 Hannah Goldberg Tony Rock, CFA, SPLP Steve Baumgarten Dan Plottner Nick Kelsoe Mike Petroskey Steve Feest Kimberly N. Laura Liccardi Tim Acker Michael Sheehan Brandy Emesal (Apologies I missed anyone from Cohort #6) Revenue Architecture (exam done, certificate on its way) - I only learned about this class, hosted by the team from Winning by Design, after joining The Pavilion community. That said, it came up in almost every one of my early conversations with community veterans. Over the course of 2 weeks, Beth Yehaskel changed the way we all thought about the data and Revenue as it flows through an organization. I told Beth the other day, after completing our last class, that I felt like I had a superpower now. Once again, 10 out of 10. For any sales leaders out there who are looking to take charge of their professional development, and who are looking for new ways to create impact at their current organization, i could not more strongly recommend these two courses.
