# 🏢🏢🏢 I'm hiring an Office Coordinator! 🏢🏢🏢 · 2026-01-06T00:00:00+00:00

🏢🏢🏢 I'm hiring an Office Coordinator! 🏢🏢🏢

With our beautiful new Toronto HQ now humming and a massive sales hiring push underway (20+ reps in the next 75 days), we need someone exceptional to help make this office run like a well-oiled machine.

Owner.com is on a roll right now and we're gearing up for a transformational 2026. A big part of that transformation is building a world-class in-person experience for our Toronto team.

I'm looking for a proactive, detail-obsessed Office Coordinator who takes genuine pride in creating a space where people love to work.

What you need to know about this role at Owner:
🌍 You'll be the heartbeat of our Toronto office. From ordering supplies to coordinating catering to greeting guests — you're the person who makes the magic happen behind the scenes.
🎉 You'll help plan and execute incredible events. Team lunches, offsites, team-building activities — you'll be instrumental in bringing our culture to life.
🛠️ You're tech-savvy and solutions-oriented. Wi-Fi acting up? Conference room AV issues? You're calm, resourceful, and get things sorted.
💙 When we say we're customer obsessed, that extends to our internal team too. You genuinely enjoy creating a welcoming, seamless experience for employees and visitors alike.
🏆 We hold a high bar. This is a performance culture and we expect effort and outcomes. There's pressure in that but you also get to be part of a winning team.
💵 We pay competitively. $96,000 – $127,000 CAD base plus a generous pre-IPO equity package.
🚀 Low ego, high ownership. You're willing to jump in wherever needed — nothing is beneath you and nothing falls through the cracks.

As always, I'm really grateful for the incredible candidate volumes we receive on applications but it's really tough for us to get back to the right candidates in a timely manner. With that in mind, please only apply if:
1️⃣ You have prior experience in office coordination, office management, hospitality, or similar operational roles.
2️⃣ You're genuinely warm, service-oriented, and take pride in creating an exceptional office environment.
3️⃣ You're organized, detail-obsessed, and can juggle multiple priorities without dropping balls.
4️⃣ You are located in Toronto or can relocate. This is 100% in-office.
5️⃣ You're comfortable working beyond standard 9-5 hours when events require it and can handle light physical tasks (we've got an office move coming up!).
6️⃣ You friggin LOVE operations and are energized by solving problems, building systems, and making people's work lives better.

If this sounds like the type of environment that you want to be a part of, shoot me a message and apply below.
