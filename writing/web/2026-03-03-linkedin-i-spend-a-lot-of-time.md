# I spend a lot of time on stages talking about GTM AI, sales infrastructure, enablement systems. | Kyle Norton · LinkedIn · 2026-03-03

**Kyle Norton**: CRO at Owner.com for 2 years 7 months with 18 years 1 month of total professional experience. Previous roles include SVP Sales & Partnerships at Owner.com, Founding Member - Toronto Chapter at Pavilion, and Director of Revenue & Merchant Success, Canada at Shopify. Based in San Francisco, California, United States [US].

---

I spend a lot of time on stages talking about GTM AI, sales infrastructure, enablement systems.

All real. All important. None of it is what actually makes Owner truly special.

What makes Owner.com different is people.

A 22-year-old who was making $14/hour in restaurants, riding the subway in the dark both ways. Someone gave him a shot. Two years later he's leading a team, and the thing that fires him up most is returning that fortune to the next early-career kid who doesn't know what they're capable of yet.

A recent grad who joined us five months ago and told her manager, unprompted, "I can't imagine working somewhere where you watch the clock." She opens her laptop and suddenly it's 3pm.

An entire team logging in on a Saturday because the month wasn't finished. Nobody asked them to. Everyone's Slack bubble just turned green. People who had already hit their number were in the channel hyping up those who hadn't. That's not something you train into people. That's identity.

You can replicate the systems. You can replicate the infrastructure. You can replicate the AI tools that make us more efficient than our competitors.

But you can't replicate what happens when you put hungry, humble, growth-obsessed people shoulder to shoulder and enable them to do their life's work. That's something totally different and I'm so grateful to experience that environment everyday.

We just dropped a video from our new Toronto office, which tells this story better than I can. Go watch it.

You watch is and think "hell yeah," we're hiring.
