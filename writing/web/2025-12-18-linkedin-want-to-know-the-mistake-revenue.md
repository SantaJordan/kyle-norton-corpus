# Want to know the mistake revenue leaders make with AI agents? | Kyle Norton · LinkedIn · 2025-12-18

**Kyle Norton**: CRO at Owner.com for 2 years 7 months with 18 years 1 month of total professional experience. Previous roles include SVP Sales & Partnerships at Owner.com, Founding Member - Toronto Chapter at Pavilion, and Director of Revenue & Merchant Success, Canada at Shopify. Based in San Francisco, California, United States [US].

---

Want to know the #1 mistake revenue leaders make with AI agents?

They delegate too early. Jason M. Lemkin was emphatic about this: "If you don't roll up your sleeves in the age of AI GTM, you will become obsolete."

His rule: The CRO, CMO, or VP Sales must personally deploy the first agent. No agencies. No delegating. 20-30 hours over 30 days of hands-on work.

The training process is surprisingly manual:
→ Review every single email for 1-2 hours daily
→ Correct hallucinations immediately
→ Agents remember via hive mind and improve daily

Here's the insight that changed my thinking: Deployment failures are training failures, not product failures.

Pre-Claude 4, products genuinely didn't work. Post-Claude 4, if your AI tool isn't performing, the problem is probably you.

The impact?
→ 70,000 AI-generated emails vs. 7,000 human emails before
→ 15% of SaaStr London revenue from agents
→ 20 AI agents deployed across GTM functions
→ $0 spent on agencies (he did it himself)

I wrote up the full implementation playbook from our conversation. Link in comments.

Big thanks and shoutout to the 🐐, Jason Lemkin, for joining me and sharing so much incredible insight!
