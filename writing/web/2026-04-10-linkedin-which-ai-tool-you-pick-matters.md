Which AI tool you pick matters a lot less than who owns it, how you implement it, and what you do about the 40% of your team's job description that machines can now handle.

These are the decisions that end up shaping everything, and they're not getting enough airtime right now.

▶️ Whether you build or buy your revenue tooling.
▶️ How you balance centralized control with giving your teams room to experiment.
▶️ How you assess whether someone on your team actually has the skills to own AI implementation, or whether you need to hire for it.
▶️ What a BDR, AE, or CSM role even looks like when so many of the tasks move to AI.

We're navigating these questions at Owner, and I've talked to enough revenue leaders recently to know that most orgs are going to learn this the hard way. Getting these decisions wrong is expensive and slow to fix.

Tom from Trig and I are getting into all of it on April 16th.

How we've approached these decisions at Owner, where we got it right, where we're still figuring it out, and how to think about it if you're earlier in the process. 45min of open conversation plus 15min Q&A.
