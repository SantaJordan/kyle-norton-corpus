🤖 What is a Revenue Operating System🤖
The key message from my talk was the importance of having a Revenue Operating System (ROS), which is the strategic framework that coordinates the processes, people and tools needed to drive revenue growth.

Your ROS acts as an intermediary between revenue leaders and their organization, providing a user interface and coordinating the growth and retention of revenue.

I define a ROS as the set of tools and rituals you use to:
1. Measure
2. Manage
3. Plan
4. Communicate

It’s your Iron Man suit that gives your super revenue growing powers as a revenue leader.
