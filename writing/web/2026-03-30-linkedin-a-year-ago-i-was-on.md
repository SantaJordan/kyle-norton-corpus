A year ago I was on stage at Winning by Design's Impact Summit accepting the AI Impact Award.

This May 12 in SF, I'll be back but this time as a judge.

The Impact Summit is a one-day executive gathering for senior GTM leaders focused on what actually matters right now: designing AI into your growth operating logic, governing execution with clarity, and moving from experimentation to predictable, compounding results.

The Growth Awards spotlight practitioners who've done exactly that. Not who adopted the most tools, but who architected growth systems where AI serves as operating logic, not noise. Nominations are already closed for 2026, which tells you something about the caliber of submissions this year.

Last year's inaugural summit hit 97% satisfaction and 100% would-recommend. The room was full of CROs, revenue leaders, and GTM executives who are actually doing the work and not just talking about it.

If you're a revenue leader who wants to be in a room where the conversation goes deeper than surface-level AI hype, this is it.
