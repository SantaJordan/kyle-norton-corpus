# The next frontier of sales performance may have less to do with what reps say on the call and more to do with the state they’re in before the call starts. · 2026-05-29T00:00:00+00:00

The next frontier of sales performance may have less to do with what reps say on the call and more to do with the state they’re in before the call starts.

That sounds soft until you watch an elite athlete prepare. Athletes don’t walk into competition cold. Sales leaders usually treat performance differently.

We obsess over the script, talk track, discovery framework, dashboard, comp plan, forecast call, and enablement deck.

All of that matters.

But the rep’s nervous system is still in the room.

I brought Kevin Bailey from Dreamfuel back on The Revenue Leadership Podcast for part 2 of our conversation on mental performance. Episode 1 was one of my most popular episodes of all time.

This time we went deeper on how to cascade it through a team without turning into the weird wellness boss.

My big takeaway: mental performance is sales infrastructure.
🧠 Start with the leader.
🧠 Bring in the A players.
🧠 Coach the managers.
🧠 Use the pre-call warmup.
🧠 Recognize effort.
🧠 Protect recovery.

The next unlock in your sales team may not be another field in Salesforce.
It might be the state your team is in when the moment arrives.
