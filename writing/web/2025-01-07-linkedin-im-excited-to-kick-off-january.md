I’m excited to kick off January with a big announcement… 🥁 🥁 🥁
Owner is opening an office here in Toronto later this month, which will serve as a hub for the sales team moving forward. 🚀
I've long been (and still am) a champion for remote work. For many functions and maker roles, in particular, distributed work is a tremendous advantage. You can create space for deep work, hire the best talent globally and provide tremendous work-life integration.
That said, I've had a nagging feeling that it wasn't the ideal setup for earlier career sales teams.
I spent the last few months reading all the research I could find and talking to the sharpest revenue leaders out there in search of compelling A/B tests that would give me answers.
I met a number of leaders who had high functioning remote onboarding and enablement programs and yet saw between 30-50% reductions in ramp time and 20-50% improvements in productivity. 🤯
I've been convinced that we can deliver a better experience for our team and better results for the business by bringing our team in person.
So here are the details:
The office is located at Queens Quay and Bay Street in the heart of downtown Toronto and less than a 7-minute walk from Union Station.
We plan on hiring all new SDRs & BDRs in Toronto with an in-office expectation.
Over time, reps will graduate into hybrid and remote.
Anyone hired previously as a remote employee can stay 100% remote but we're encouraging folks in the GTA to come spend time in the office, share their wisdom and build the culture.
We're moving leaders and enablement staff to Toronto to support the development of the team.
We’re doing this to:
▶️ Decrease time to ramp for our least experienced sellers
- It’s hard to learn sales and it’s even harder when you’re alone
- We’re scaling the teams aggressively in 2025 and being able to effectively ramp new talent is of paramount importance
▶️ Improve productivity
- We all remember the energy being shoulder to shoulder with our teammates.
▶️ Provide a better early career experience for our XDR team
- Two of the main pillars of our engagement approach are Community and Mastery. Both of these are much easier to provide in person
- We believe that most of the driven, learning oriented sales talent want to be in person. This is already being validated in interview conversations as we’ve shared the office news with candidates.
▶️ Attract and retain great XDR talent
- Ultimately, this will lead to a better experience for our sales team members, higher engagement and better employee retention over time.
▶️ Decrease headcount cost as we move into scale mode
- Hiring earlier career talent, tripling down on enablement and promoting from within
If you're a Toronto-based XDR, AE or Manager and miss the buzz of an electric sales floor and the learning opportunities it provides, check out our career page and reach out!
