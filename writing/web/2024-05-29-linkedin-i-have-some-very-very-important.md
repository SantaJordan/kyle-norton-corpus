# 💡 I have some very… very important advice to SDR & BDR job seekers. 💡 · 2024-05-29T00:00:00+00:00

💡 I have some very… very important advice to SDR & BDR job seekers. 💡

I can all but guarantee that your response rate and first interview rate will improve dramatically if you follow this very simple advice.

Please. I’m begging you. Please, lead with your results. 🙏

I thought my post yesterday was pretty clear about this. 😅

💬“One important tip for your outreach and this is arguably the most important! Lead with results. We only hire top performers so make sure to include some of your performance highlights in your messages.” 💬

And yet, not one message or email I received contained anything about achievement.

All I want to know in your initial outreach is if you can put up results. I’m mostly interested in sales results but if you don’t have that yet then any other form of achievement will do.

That could be Dean’s List, captaining sports teams, state chess championships. Whatever.

Sales leaders are looking for a history of excellence. Sales is a performance sport so achievement is what will get our attention.

If you don’t mention your achievements in your outreach, I’m going to assume you don’t have any and you won’t even get a recruiter pre-screen call.

My fellow sales leaders can chime in with their thoughts. I'm sure they would love the same.
