# We discovered recently that a lot of our sales hiring process was built on BS. · 2025-08-21T00:00:00+00:00

We discovered recently that a lot of our sales hiring process was built on BS.

Natalie Sturino and I ran a "hiring autopsy" with our front line managers where we pulled every single hire, mapped their interview scores against actual performance, and dissected the data like crime scene investigators.

The finding that made me question everything?
Mock demos and case studies have zero predictive value for sales success.
Zero. Zilch. Nada.

That polished presentation you're grading? That brilliant objection handling in the role play? Completely worthless for predicting who will actually hit quota.

Have I been hiring wrong for decades?? 😅

Here's what actually works: I hire insanely hard workers, with the desire and ability to learn quickly and are highly organized.

The Hiring Autopsy Process:
Every 90 days, we gather around the data like surgeons reviewing a difficult case:
→ Why did this high-scorer flame out?
→ Why did our highest performer only receive mediocre interview scores?
→ What interview signal did we miss?
→ Which manager has a 40% hit rate vs 80%?

I talk about this and much much more with 🔌 Andy Mowat on the "How I Hire" podcast.

We talk about the retro process and:
- How I've turned bias-fighting into a science
- The importance of going overboard with references
- Champion-Based vs. Consensus-Based hiring
- Why interviews are generally a terrible way to predict success

Enjoy!
