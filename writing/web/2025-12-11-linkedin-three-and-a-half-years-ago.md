# Three and a half years ago, I hoped Owner.com could become a meaningful company. I dreamt about building a 'unicorn'. | Kyle Norton · LinkedIn · 2025-12-11

**Kyle Norton**: CRO at Owner.com for 2 years 7 months with 18 years 1 month of total professional experience. Previous roles include SVP Sales & Partnerships at Owner.com, Founding Member - Toronto Chapter at Pavilion, and Director of Revenue & Merchant Success, Canada at Shopify. Based in San Francisco, California, United States [US].

---

Three and a half years ago, I hoped Owner.com could become a meaningful company. I dreamt about building a 'unicorn'.

Today, I'll consider it a disappointment if we don't build something worth tens of billions.

That shift in expectations, and what drove it, is what I unpacked with Nic Poulos and Luke on the Verticals podcast last week.

We covered a lot of ground:
→ The 4 criteria I use to evaluate generational founders
→ Why systems thinking matters more than sales acumen in high-velocity environments
→ The counterintuitive hiring insight: organizational discipline beats domain expertise
→ How we're approaching AI as a cultural transformation, not a technology project
→ Why talent density is the real compounding advantage

The hosts asked great questions and got me talking about things I haven't shared publicly before.

Worth a listen if you're building in vertical SaaS or evaluating opportunities in the space.
