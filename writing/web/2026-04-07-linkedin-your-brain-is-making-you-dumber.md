# Your brain is making you dumber at the exact moments you need it most. | Kyle Norton · LinkedIn · 2026-04-07

**Kyle Norton**: CRO at Owner.com for 2 years 7 months with 18 years 1 month of total professional experience. Previous roles include SVP Sales & Partnerships at Owner.com, Founding Member - Toronto Chapter at Pavilion, and Director of Revenue & Merchant Success, Canada at Shopify. Based in San Francisco, California, United States [US].

Your brain is making you dumber at the exact moments you need it most.

I spent years in a near-constant flight response and had no idea.

Yale research shows that even mild stress "takes the prefrontal cortex offline." Your brain shifts from strategic thinking to survival mode. And most executives live there all day.

I sat down with Kevin Bailey from Dreamfuel, who coaches executives on mental performance using applied neuroscience.

He broke down:
🧠 The performance chain: physiology drives emotions, which drive thoughts, which drive behaviors. Fix the bottom and everything shifts.
🧠 The four nervous system states (flight, freeze, fight, focus) and how to identify which one you're stuck in.
🧠 A practical warm-up protocol for meetings: 30 deep breaths + 15-second hold + visualization. Five minutes. The research on visualization alone is wild (mental piano practice produced nearly identical brain changes to physical practice).
🧠 The cognitive reframe that changed how I handle adversity: "Best thing that could have happened." Your brain automatically completes the sentence with positive reasons.

I also go deep on how cold plunging with biometric tracking (I use a Plunge Air) has become my most valuable awareness tool. Watching your heart rate drop in real time while you're uncomfortable trains a skill that transfers directly to boardrooms.
