# Here's an uncomfortable truth that actually led to the most unsubscribes I've gotten on a newsletter 😅 · 2026-01-15T00:00:00+00:00

Here's an uncomfortable truth that actually led to the most unsubscribes I've gotten on a newsletter 😅

If your RevOps function hasn't fundamentally transformed in the last 24 months, you're already losing.

Not "at risk of losing." Already losing.

I'm watching a massive divergence happening right now between companies that get this and companies that don't.

The companies pulling ahead share three things:
1. They fixed their data foundations first.
Most companies are trying to bolt AI onto a messy CRM. That's like putting a Ferrari engine in a car with square wheels.

At Owner, we made Snowflake our system of record (not CRM) and put tons of resourcing against data quality. AI is only as good as the data underneath it.

2. Their RevOps leaders think like engineers.
The RevOps leader who isn't in Claude Code, doesn't understand data architecture, and isn't shipping experiments weekly? They're going to be replaced by someone who can.

This isn't me being harsh. It's just the new baseline.

3. They understand the flywheel.
A 4% efficiency improvement doesn't sound sexy. But it compounds into faster growth → more product investment → stronger brand → better talent → more efficiency.

Our reps are 3-4x more efficient than competitors. That gap isn't closing. It's widening.

The conventional wisdom of "let's wait and see how AI plays out" is going to get companies killed.

The reckoning is already here.

I unpacked all of this in my newsletter this week, including what I think needs to change with sales engagement platforms (hint: they need to be rebuilt from the ground up).
