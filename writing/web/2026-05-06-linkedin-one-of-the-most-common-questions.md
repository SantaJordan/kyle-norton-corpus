# One of the most common questions I get is: "how do I hire for AI-first thinking?" · 2026-05-06T00:00:00+00:00

One of the most common questions I get is: "how do I hire for AI-first thinking?"

Unfortunately, if you don't know enough about these tools, the ecosystem, and AI fundamentals yourself, it's going to be really hard to figure out who does and doesn't.

You're going to get fooled pretty easily.

That said, these two questions will help:
1. "Tell me what you've built using AI for the business."
2. "Tell me about some stuff you've built for yourself. What are your favorite day-to-day productivity workflows?"

Really basic but here's why they work.

People who are AI-pilled just light up. They're like, 'Oh, I did this, and then I did these things,' and I have to cut them off because they're so excited and have so much to say.

That energy is the tell.

Someone who's actually in the tools has opinions, has favorite workflows, has a thing they built last weekend that didn't quite work. Someone who isn't will give you clean, polished, generic answers about "leveraging AI for efficiency."
