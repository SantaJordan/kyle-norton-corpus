# "I've never been strategic about my career." · 2026-02-05T00:00:00+00:00

"I've never been strategic about my career."

That's Michelle Donnelly - 10 years at Salesforce, then CRO at a pre-revenue AI chip startup that many said would fail.

That company was Groq, which just sold to Nvidia for $20 billion. 😲

Now she's running revenue at Crescendo, which hit $100M ARR in under two years.

The track record would indicate that, indeed, there was a strategy behind it.

Her approach to career moves: follow the chills. Literally. Every conversation with Groq's founder gave her goosebumps. That same tingle with Crescendo's founder. So she jumped.

But here's what makes her different: she didn't just trust her gut. She sourced insight from people living in the future:

→ A Deloitte exec watching enterprise GPU spend
→ The former AWS sales leader who knew compute demand

Most people use their network to find jobs. Michelle uses hers to find non-obvious truths about where the market is going.

5 lessons from our conversation:

1. Your network is your oracle (tap future-thinkers, not job-finders)
2. Product-market fit is binary and painful
3. Simplicity is the hardest GTM problem
4. Show, don't demo (build custom proof in 24 hours)
5. Talent density beats headcount
