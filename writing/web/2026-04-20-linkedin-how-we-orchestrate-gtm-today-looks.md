# How we orchestrate GTM today looks almost nothing like how we did it 12 months ago. · 2026-04-20T00:00:00+00:00

How we orchestrate GTM today looks almost nothing like how we did it 12 months ago.

Agents are doing work that used to require headcount. Roles I would have hired for 18 months ago don't exist on my org chart anymore. Completely new roles have emerged.

The tech is the easy part.

The hard part is rewiring how humans and agents actually work together without stepping on each other.

That's what we're getting into on April 22 at the Salesforce Startup Summit.

I'm joining Jevan Lenox (CPO at WRITER) and Amelia Lerutte (Chief AI Officer at SaaStr Ai) for a 40-minute session called "Scaling Faster with Humans and Agents: Rewiring How Teams Build, Sell, and Operate."

Amelia kicks it off with a live demo of their Agentforce setup. Then the three of us get on stage to dig into:

→ Where agents are already driving real revenue today
→ How the day-to-day actually changes when agents are in the loop
→ What breaks when you layer agents on top of an org designed for humans only
→ How to think about hiring, roles, and team structure going forward

If you're a founder or executive trying to operationalize this for real, come. If you're waiting for someone to write the playbook first, you're already behind.

Ohana Floor at Salesforce Tower, San Francisco. Wednesday, April 22. 2-6 PM PT.

Apply below.
