# 🔥🔥🔥 We're hiring a Lead, GTM Analytics & Applied AI! 🔥🔥🔥 This is one of the most important hires I'll make in 2026. · 2026-05-01T00:00:00+00:00

🔥🔥🔥 We're hiring a Lead, GTM Analytics & Applied AI! 🔥🔥🔥 This is one of the most important hires I'll make in 2026.

We're building the most data-driven GTM organization in SMB tech, and Meera Singh and I need an AI powered operator to help us go even faster.

Anyone who follows me knows how intense we are about AI in GTM at Owner. It's been transformational for us already and we're tripling down in 2026 so we're looking for a senior IC who's torn between business and technical career paths and wants to do both.

This is someone who builds AI-powered products, ships them into production, and uses them to drive real revenue impact.

What you need to know about GTM Analytics & Applied AI at Owner:
🛠️ This role isn't just about running reports. You will build the products and systems that drive GTM.
🤖 Applied AI is the job. From Claude Code to custom ML pipelines, you'll use AI to multiply output across analytics, operations, and customer-facing workflows.
🚀 We're at an inflection point. Expanding into new product verticals, scaling new acquisition channels, and standing up entirely new GTM motions. This role sits at the center of designing, measuring, and optimizing them.
🌎 This role is remote but SF and Toronto are preferred.
💼 You'll work directly with me or our CMO Wyatt Ozmore and your function's leadership team. Real executive visibility and real end-to-end ownership.
🔬 We dive deep. We expect root-cause thinking and SME-level fluency in your function, not surface-level insights.
🤝 You'll report to Meera Singh, our Director of GTM Analytics & Applied AI, who's an absolute weapon of an operator and a great human.
🏆 We hold a high bar. This is a performance culture and we expect effort and outcomes. There's pressure in that, but you also get to be part of a winning team.
💸 We pay competitively.

As always, I'm really grateful for the incredible candidate volume we receive on applications but it's tough for us to get back to the right candidates in a timely manner.
With that in mind, please only apply if:
1️⃣ You have 3–7+ years in analytics, strategy, or ops (consulting, startup ops, BizOps, or a hybrid path that's hard to label).
2️⃣ You've personally shipped data products, ML models, or AI-powered systems into production and not just delivered insights or decks.
3️⃣ You get more energy from building and shipping than from analyzing and presenting.
4️⃣ AI is your daily driver. SQL/Python skills are a plus.
5️⃣ You thrive in ambiguity and don't need a playbook to figure out what to work on next.
6️⃣ You want to build lasting systems and products, not one-off analyses.
If this sounds like the type of environment you want to be a part of, shoot Meera and me a message, and apply below.
