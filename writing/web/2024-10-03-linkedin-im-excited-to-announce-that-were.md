🚨🚨I’m excited to announce that we’re hiring externally for an Inbound Account Executive for the first time in 18 months! 🚨🚨

Growth continues to rip and we've been promoting so fast from the BDR team that we need to hire externally for our Inbound team. I try to fill these roles internally but we can't keep up. ⚡🚗⚡

What you need to know about Sales at Owner:
🌍 We’re committed to enabling our reps for success and creating the best environment possible (check out our RepVue ranking below).
👷♀️ We sell to a blue collar, SMB buyer. Mom & pop restaurant owners want to shoot straight and need some hand holding when it comes to technology.
⏩ We manage a transactional sales cycle between 1-7 days. Great sales craft is essential to success in our environment.
🌳 We’re a growth obsessed team. We’re looking for people who invest in themselves relentlessly.
💙 When we say we’re customer obsessed, we actually mean it. We solve problems with customer experience and customer value first.
💻 We’re a fully remote company and don’t have plans to set up offices. Confidence and excitement to work asynchronously is critical.
🏆 We hold a high bar. This is a performance culture and we expect effort and outcomes. There’s pressure in that but you also get to be part of a winning team.
💵 We pay competitively.
🤖 We’re hyper focused on enabling our teams with tools and technology. I’m looking for people who are excited to lean into new tech.

While I’m really grateful for the incredible candidate volumes we receive on applications, it has become overwhelming to get to the right candidates in a timely manner (apologies in advance!).

With that in mind, please only apply if:
1️⃣ You are a proven top performer. Top 10% or better is the standard for a first round interview typically.
2️⃣ You have been an AE in an SMB, transactional sales environment.
3️⃣ You thrive on change.
4️⃣ You are located in Canada, the US, Mexico or Colombia.
5️⃣ You can provide references that will insist that we would be insane not to hire you.
6️⃣ You friggin LOVE sales and want to make this your life’s work.

If this sounds like the type of environment that you want to be a part of, shoot me a message, and apply below.
