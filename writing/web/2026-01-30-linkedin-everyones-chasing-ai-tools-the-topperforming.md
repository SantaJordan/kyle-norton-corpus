# Everyone's chasing AI tools. The top-performing CROs are chasing something else: disciplined execution. · 2026-01-30T00:00:00+00:00

Everyone's chasing AI tools.

The top-performing CROs are chasing something else: disciplined execution.

📈 Jeremey Donovan just surveyed 150 CROs for Insight Partners. He advises 500 B2B SaaS companies on GTM strategy.

The data is clear:

There's no silver bullet AI use case separating winners from losers.

Top performers and average performers are adopting the same tools in the same order. The difference? Top performers were already disciplined before AI showed up.

Tight deal reviews. Rigorous pipeline inspection. Clear ICP focus.

AI amplifies what's already there. It doesn't create discipline from nothing.

Two things that stuck with me:

1. One CRO ripped out an AI tool that auto-filled MEDDIC fields because "the AEs were getting stupid." The tool worked perfectly. But it removed the thinking that made reps sharp.

2. 70% of outbound opportunities are still booked via phone. Not email. Not AI sequences. Human effort still wins.

The real unlock? Stop optimizing existing processes with AI. Rethink the processes entirely.
