# When I decided to break into SaaS, I couldn’t even get an interview for an AE role. I applied to all the big names and Canadian stars and got a total of … · 2024-01-26T00:00:00+00:00

When I decided to break into SaaS, I couldn’t even get an interview for an AE role. I applied to all the big names and Canadian stars and got a total of 0️⃣0️⃣ first rounds.

I’ll tell my story then provide a framework you can borrow if you’re on the lookout for your next thing. ✍️

Previously, I managed a team of 35 reps across multiple managers, consistently crushed my numbers and yet 🦗.

So I decided to take things into my own hands and did what I did best:
Prospect like a maniac.

I made a list of the best companies I wanted to work for, wrote a script, practiced it repeatedly and prepared notes on each DM I was calling.

Then my whole career changed with one cold call to Mark Bergen at Vision Critical. (Forever appreciative, my man! 🙏 )

Obviously, Berg gave me some typical knee jerks and early objections. The EVP of Sales of one of Canada’s biggest tech companies didn’t have time for a cold call from some random shmuck but I was prepared.

I cajoled him into a 15 minute call later that week, which turned into an hour long coffee the next week then an invite to come back to the office to meet a few leaders then a job offer a few days later to run Global BDR.

That led to my first head of sales gig then to running a revenue for a business unit Shopify then to my current role as CRO at Owner.

And I couldn't even get an AE interview. 🤷♂️

I tell this story to share to inspire people to take their destiny into their own hands and know that you can go from stuck to succeeding with a few weeks of focused effort.

My biggest piece of job hunting advice is to treat it like a 40 hour a week job and follow a process. Here’s a rough framework:

1️⃣ Go deep on research to find the right companies that are growing and have PMF.
2️⃣ Network into those companies through the reps. Ask for 15 minutes to chat because you’re interested in joining.
3️⃣ Use that intel to craft great outreach to the HM and sales leadership. Be sure to explicitly state why you’re a great fit for that company. Lead with your results.
4️⃣ Hammer your prospects with calls, emails and LI. Full cadence mode.
5️⃣ Ask the reps you talked with to put you on the HMs radar.
6️⃣ Do crazy research before every call with a HM.
7️⃣ Run a super tight search so you have multiple exciting opportunities at once and create urgency with your buyers.
8️⃣ Operate from a place of abundance and not scarcity
9️⃣ Diligence carefully. Back channel with employees. Ask questions about the financial health of the company. Get granular about understanding current sales performance.
🔟 Finally, be patient and don't take a role unless you're excited about it and are confident in the company fundamentals. ESPECIALLY if you’re hitting quota at your current company, trust your leadership and have product-market-fit. Probabilities would indicate that you won’t get all three of those things at the next place.

Hopefully this helps some of you job seekers out there and let me know what I missed!
