# What Kyle Norton said since March 2026

> 28 sources from 2026-03-24 to 2026-09-08: 11 episodes of his own show (E52 backfill, E65–E74), 9 guest appearances, and 9 Substack posts (2 paywalled, preview only). This page covers what is **new, sharpened, or reversed** compared with everything up to the first collection (2026-03-06). Each topic page holds the full evidence.

## The headline: AI went from "experiment" to "operating system"

1. **Centralized AI is now his flagship position** ([[ai-operating-model]]). He says he used to push bottom-up adoption: "I was trying to push AI adoption a lot, maybe 12 months ago" ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @11:38). Now: "We are a very centralized model" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @55:32), and "I actually don't really want the reps running agents" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @13:11). He says his Substack post arguing for centralized AI was his most popular post "by 3X" ([[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]]). He coined "AI peanut butter": put tools where reps already work instead of making every rep a power user ([[src-substack-2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young]]).
2. **AI fluency now depends on the role.** "My views on this have changed": fluency is mandatory for senior and leverage roles, not for frontline managers ([[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @08:06). If he were starting again, he would stop pushing managers to learn AI ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @26:26).
3. **A named maturity model** ([[ai-sophistication-ladder]]): Levels 0–4, introduced at SaaStr in May 2026. He credits Brendan Short, and says Owner is not yet at Level 4 ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @04:05). He later reuses it with "harness" vocabulary (Level 3 = "AI as infrastructure") ([[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @42:00; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @28:02). The paywalled post names Level 4 the "Recursively Self-Improving Revenue System" ([[src-substack-2026-05-26-dont-buy-your-gtm-brain]], preview only).
4. **Buy infrastructure, build intelligence** ([[build-vs-buy-software]]): "buying your infrastructure and building your intelligence" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @14:12), and "renting your GTM brain" is the failure mode ([[src-substack-2026-05-26-dont-buy-your-gtm-brain]], preview only). In practice: "not going to go vibe code Salesforce", but Owner did build its own LMS and contest system ([[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @31:58, @38:16).
5. **Context and harness** ([[context-harness-and-memory]]): Owner's "Gardener" maintains a master business-context file that every agent reads ([[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @49:12). He also talks about a "company meta harness" ([[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @05:19). He predicts GTM will reach a "dark factory" state, where product development is today, within 18 months ([[src-E71-the-most-efficient-marketing-org-in-the-world-35-people-350m]] @55:41).
6. **Kyle's personal AI stack** is new material ([[personal-ai-stack]]): OpenClaw, a "Kai" digital chief of staff, an agent card with a $500 limit ([[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @63:08–79:37), a Clay table that preps podcast guests ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @19:03), and an LLM wiki of "atomic ideas" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @40:40).

> **Correction (2026-10-05, after full backfill of 149 sources):** several shifts below began **before** March 2026. Owner was already *"sending 1,800 cold outbound emails every single day"* in Dec 2025 ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @31:30) and *"piloting an AI SDR now"* in Aug 2025 ([[src-E41-jessica-chiew-of-canva-on-building-ai-native-gtm-org]]). AI was *"centralized. I'm, it's in my biz ops and rev ops team."* by May 2025 ([[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @07:18). He was *"leaning into Salesforce more than ever which I am personally surprised by"* in Dec 2025. He decided to go in person *"midway through 2024"* ([[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]]). What is genuinely new since March 2026 is that he **talks about these openly, names them, and builds frameworks around them**: the sophistication ladder, the harness, PROFIT, NASCAR, and *buy infrastructure, build intelligence*. Treat the *Before 2026-03* column as what he *said publicly* in the earlier sources ingested first, not as when Owner changed.

## Figures that don't reconcile (don't repeat them uncritically)

- **ARR per rep at "20X":** $2M on a $150K salary is ~13x, while $3M is 20x. Either the May figure was understated or productivity rose ~50% in three months.
- **Pre-call research lift:** 85% more opportunities (May) vs 86% more decision-maker connects (Aug). Probably the same experiment described with different metrics.
- **"$60M ARR"** (aired 2026-02-20) vs **"almost at 100"** (March 2026). In Dec 2025 Kyle gave the 2026 goal as "go from $65M to $135M" in the *core business*, so the bases may differ. Unreconciled; see [[owner-com]].
- **Cold outreach:** "I don't personally want to just let AI write my cold outreach" (Apr) vs AI-generated cold email with no rep sending (Aug). Either the policy changed in about four months, or the April quote refers to his *personal* outreach.
