# Owner's AI Sales Stack

> As of 2026-09-08, Owner's stack is bought infrastructure: Salesforce (the CRM and workflow hub, which he is now bullish on), Salesloft (dialer), Momentum (call data, replacing Gong) and vendor AI (Avara sims, OneMind avatars, DataLane data, Guru, Crescendo support). Kyle calls Snowflake, not Salesforce, the system of record. On top of that sits intelligence built in-house (ML scores, eConnect, AI pre-call research, centrally run AI email), all deployed into surfaces reps already use ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @10:24–12:26, @18:44–19:44; [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @14:12–20:20; [[src-E45-the-inside-scoop-on-gtm-engineering-with-clays-everett-berr]] @44:54).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- Start with data foundations: a third-party market map, then first-party call data via Momentum. Only then layer on use cases [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @02:05–03:06; [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @04:12–05:16. He has said this since at least Dec 2024 ([[src-momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy]] @04:07–07:11).
- "No new surfaces": AI output is injected into Salesforce and the Salesloft dialer screen, not into a new app [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @12:40; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @18:44–19:44. The rule dates to Aug 2025 ([[src-E41-jessica-chiew-of-canva-on-building-ai-native-gtm-org]] @34:36).
- Data layering: Snowflake is the system of record and scoring is built there; outputs are pushed into Salesforce, which he now sees as the governed "home for agents" [[src-E45-the-inside-scoop-on-gtm-engineering-with-clays-everett-berr]] @43:52–44:54; [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-01-15); [[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @35:38–36:40.
- The tech is "not that much more sophisticated" than other companies'. The edge is change management [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @03:06–04:06; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @12:26.
- Buy infrastructure (dialer, CRM, avatars, sims, call recording) and build intelligence (AI pre-call research, scoring) [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @14:12–20:20.
- Vendor test: does it let him own the data and write his own prompts? That is why Gong was cut for Momentum [[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @63:38; [[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @29:51–30:55.
- Unsolved: a shared context layer or knowledge graph across sales and marketing [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @14:27–16:29. Also unsolved: governed "data harness" access for analysis [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @62:05–65:09.

- **Pavlov vs. Trig/Trigg:** possibly the same post-sales tool under transcription variants. Unverified.
- **WorkRamp vs. own LMS:** WorkRamp named as a channel (published Apr 2026, likely recorded earlier) vs. built its own LMS (May 2026). Possibly replaced. Not stated.
