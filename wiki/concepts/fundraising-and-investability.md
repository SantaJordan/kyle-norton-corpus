# Fundraising & Investability

> As of 2026-05-13, Kyle's position is "know the game you're playing": venture-backed companies must play for a power-law, "generational" outcome and keep strategy congruent across fundraising, product, GTM and resourcing. The "know the game" frame is older than the wiki first showed: Owner was playing "a more traditional venture-backed game" by Oct 2024 ([[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @03:13–04:17), and he paired growth with efficiency from the start (2023: burn multiple under one, 4.5:1 LTV:CAC, [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @04:06). Since 2026 he adds that AI is reshaping what's investable: foundation models eat point solutions, and boards want P&L impact from AI, not activity ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @08:29–@11:39; [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @16:15–@17:16; [[src-substack-2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young]]).

## Current position (as of 2026-05-13)
- **Strategic congruence.** Venture money means spend fast and build a platform; raising venture and playing efficiency, or bootstrapping while spending like a venture company, are "both dead ends" ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @23:05–@24:13). Congruence spans fundraising, product and GTM ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @10:35) and resourcing ([[src-accountaim-revops-ai-advantage]] @10:24–@13:30).
- **Power law, personally.** "a modest PE outcome doesn't do anything for me as a CRO really because you're going to get squashed under a pref stack" ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @09:32). Owner plays for a "grand slam," not a PE exit ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @47:39–@48:42).
- **Growth plus efficiency, not either/or.** The Series B came from insiders excited by growth "with pretty rare unit economics" ([[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @14:25–15:25); "there's a danger in being overfunded" ([[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @13:23).
- **Raise from strength.** Owner raises with "almost 24 months of cash left" and has "been preempted most times"; avoid the "okay growth, meaningful burn" middle zone ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @38:43–@41:30).
- **Founder evaluation:** force of nature, super-computer learner, founder-market fit, enough humility "not to blow it all up" ([[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @28:14–@32:23; [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @61:13–@66:35; [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @13:31–@15:39).
- **The venture bar has moved up.** Triple-triple-double-double "no longer reliably gets venture funding"; the 2020–21 "magical middle ground" is gone (E58 discussion with Lemkin, [[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @70:06–81:15). Venture is "a flight to quality" ([[src-all-about-ai-and-gtm-at-clay-round-two-with-everett-berry]] @45:48).
- **AI reshapes investability.** Foundation models eat categories (Claude Design vs. Figma, managed agents "killed a bunch of startups") ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @16:15–@17:16); point solutions are now risky ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @36:26–@39:42).
- **Owner's profile:** "more triple, triple, double, double plus than... 10X," with durability plus an AI story ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @37:33).
- **Benchmarks need context.** Revenue per employee isn't like-for-like for Owner because "close to half of my team is in LATAM and then another 40% is in Canada" ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @29:44).

## How his view evolved
- **2018-09-25 (League, at the time):** Series A "$33 million Canadian, so about $25 million US"; "We saw some early signs of success, and we tried to hire too many reps too quickly" ([[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @04:11, @16:39). The over-funding lesson he tells in 2024 was already forming.
- **2020-08-17 (Shopify):** Founders should stay in sales longer than comfortable: "No one's going to believe anybody in your company more than the founder."; "I'm of the opinion that winter is coming." ([[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @28:21, @30:33).
- **2022-11-01 (SVP Sales, Owner):** Prioritize systems, people and playbooks before hitting the gas, fundamentals that let you raise "in any economic environment"; joined via GTM Fund's investment and said he wouldn't do Series A again ([[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @17:27–19:32, @04:09–05:11).
- **2024-02-05:** League raised $25M at Series A before SaaS revenue; over-funding drove a premature push upmarket ("every deal was brute force"). As an investor, what matters is a truly elite founder ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @02:02–@09:11, @27:47–@28:50).
- **2024-02-07:** VC still rewards growth over efficiency: "growth is still the thing that's going to get you funded" ([[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @36:28–@37:28).
- **2024-06-04:** Series B came from insiders after a board meeting, no deck; "there's a danger in being overfunded"; "So we grew over two and a half X last year."; "We are trying to win. We're not building for an acquisition." ([[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @13:23–16:31, @26:58).

- **2024-10-16:** As a Stage 2 LP: "Just pick the founders that you think are gonna win that market Even if the product might might not be quite as good" at seed/Series A ([[src-E04-mark-roberge-on-redefining-sales-leadership-in-the-ai-era]] @48:17).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Growth vs. efficiency (largely resolved).** The older sources show efficiency was always in his story: "pretty rare unit economics" at the Series B and a sub-1 burn multiple in 2023 ([[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @14:25; [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @04:06), and fundamentals that let you raise "in any economic environment" (2022). The 2026 "bloat era is over" line is a sharpening, not a reversal. Original note: 2024: "growth is still the thing that's going to get you funded." 2025: venture means "spend fast"; Owner deliberately skews CAC by over-investing in ops. 2026: "the bloat era is over," boards want P&L impact. His position moved from growth gets funded toward growth with demonstrable efficiency, without an explicit acknowledgment of the shift. Partly the guest's frame (Cassie Young) that he adopted.
- **Series C timing.** "beginning of last year" (said Apr 2026, i.e. early 2025) vs. the AccountAim host saying Owner "just raised big series C" in Oct 2025. The episode may have been recorded earlier or the host may be loose.
- **ARR timeline anomaly.** "over 60" (Union Square, published 2026-04-20) vs. "almost at 100" (2026-03-24). Treat "over 60" as an earlier recording, not a decline.
- **Founder-evaluation consistency.** The four traits are stable from Oct 2024 to Dec 2025 (as VC/LP), but he hasn't revisited them in the AI-era framing.
- **Overfunding vs. "go big or go home."** He warns "there's a danger in being overfunded" (2024) and blames League's big Series A for brute-force growth, yet says Owner's game is to "raise a bunch of money" (2025). His reconciliation: raise from strength with discipline, not a lot of money early.
- **Owner ARR base, Dec 2025.** Kyle's "$65M" core-business base vs. the STA interviewer's "about to cross $100M" and Lemkin's "past $100M" in May 2026. Possibly core vs. total/run-rate; unreconciled.
- **Kyle's investor titles.** "LP in GTM Fund and Stage 2 Capital" (2024–2025) vs. "partner in GTM Fund and Stage 2 Capital" ([[src-caliber-outbound-objection-navigation]] @00:00, 2026-06-04). Possibly a role change or loose phrasing.
