# Operating Rhythm

> As of 2026-09-08, Kyle runs Owner on a named, documented cadence he has been refining since his 2015 daily "fire drill" (monthly MBR and churn deep dive, bi-weekly Tiger Teams, weekly written MAPLE updates, no update meetings and no pipeline reviews, "chop wood, carry water" manager mornings) and is now pairing it with subtraction (Chaos Monkey calendar wipes, a wish for a "delete some stuff" process every 90 days) and AI (a "CRO daily", AI-drafted weekly updates) ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @37:16–@39:19; [[src-E72-i-collapsed-my-gtm-org-chart-this-is-why]] @56:31; [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @62:05–@65:09; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @24:52).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- **Monthly core.** A revenue-org MBR, a monthly churn deep dive, a product cadence, and bi-weekly **Tiger Teams** for big rocks, which had "spider webbed out" and were being consolidated ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @37:16–@38:18). The funnel is deconstructed monthly ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @03:07).
- **Manager mornings are sacred.** "Chop wood, carry water": no internal meetings, Slack or spreadsheets in the first three hours; only operating, coaching and craft on the front line ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @24:52; [[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @85:49).
- **Prune the calendar.** **Chaos Monkey** (from Shopify): every leadership-team member deleted every meeting at the start of the year and rebuilt; "If you forget about that meeting, it wasn't that important." Needed "every 12 months" ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @38:18–@39:19). He wants the same for process, roles and tools "every 90 days" ([[src-E72-i-collapsed-my-gtm-org-chart-this-is-why]] @54:25–@56:31).
- **Meeting rules (written into his operating methodology).** No update meetings: "If you are speaking for 80% of the time in the meeting, that should be in a loom video and a document." Recurring meetings get "a six week expiry"; async and document-first by default ([[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @21:04–@24:16; [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @38:51–@42:56).
- **MAPLE is still the weekly spine** (as of Oct 2025): every leader writes Metrics, Advanced, Planned, Learnings, Emergencies; he reads them Sunday nights and pulls SLT agenda topics from them ([[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @23:14–@24:16; [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @55:10–@56:13).
- **No pipeline reviews, no forecast meeting.** In high-velocity SMB the forecast is programmatic from input metrics ([[src-coffee-scaling-smarter]] @18:11–@19:12; [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @39:19–@40:22).
- **Rigor vs. bureaucracy.** "It feels rigorous to have another meeting," but it becomes "a massive drag" ([[src-E72-i-collapsed-my-gtm-org-chart-this-is-why]] @67:01).
- **One change at a time, finish the swing.** One behavior per month, concentrated training, coaching and inspection; don't "hurl it into a Slack channel" ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @05:07–@06:09).
- **Month-to-month rhythm as culture.** "we get 12 Super Bowls a year"; first day of each month the team goes to Othership ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @11:17–@12:21).
- **AI in the rhythm.** His weekly update is an AI workflow ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @23:14); Kai drafts leaders' weekly updates ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @60:48–@66:05); he "built a CRO daily" in a data-harness tool ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @62:05–@65:09); experiments are brainstormed monthly with a queue of "the next six things" ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @06:12–@07:18).

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09-28 (Vision Critical, earliest):** A daily 8:30 "fire drill" stand-up (shout-outs, yesterday's numbers, a learning), two-week skill themes and weekly training ([[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @51:16–@54:21).
- **2018-09-25 (League):** Process must serve reps: "If you're designing process for the sake of process, for the sake of oversight, you're doing it wrong" ([[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @28:10–@30:19).
- **2023-12-15 (written):** "Operate" is one of six frontline-manager pillars (sell, coach, hire, operate, build, inspire) and "most likely overlooked"; his example is a Friday-afternoon weekly report and forecast check ([[src-web-2023-12-15-amplemarket-blog-sales-management-tips-expert-insights-with]]).
- **2024-02-05:** Hyper-transparency: a monthly exec business assessment published to the whole sales team plus an AMA ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @45:18).
- **2024-02-07:** Owner documents operating rhythms from IC to leadership, including a monthly "math of sales"; dry-runs board meetings; rebuilding the leadership system on Dalio's *Principles* ([[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @11:30–@13:35, @28:09–@30:15, @36:28).
- **2024-02-23:** Defines operating rhythm as "the set of standards that we use to help us know we can hit targets today and in the future"; rhythms cascade annual → quarterly → monthly → weekly → daily; the monthly **Revenue Review & Game Plan** (numbers, commentary, shout-outs, red/yellow/green strategic review, next month's targets, change log) goes to the whole company with an AMA ([[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @01:04, @08:22, @28:58–@36:12).
- **2024-04-18:** Three operating tools: MBR, tight performance management (stack-ranking), documentation; async MBR template ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @29:43–@33:49).
- **2024-05-15:** Separate weekly coaching (30–45 min) from the 15–20 min 1:1/pipe review ([[src-techto-sales-coaching-adversity]] @04:10–@05:11).
- **2024-08-01:** Same three "Pareto habits" (async-prepped MBRs in Notion, stack-rank performance management, documentation) at a second venue ([[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @16:20–@19:22).
- **2024-09-24 (post):** Introduces the **Revenue Operating System** from his SaaStr Annual talk: "the set of tools and rituals" to Measure, Manage, Plan, Communicate, "your Iron Man suit" ([[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (post 2024-09-24)). The talk itself was posted to YouTube in Jan 2025: MAPLE "Sunday nights at eight", a monthly game plan graded A–F, minimum effective dosage ("every single rhythm that you introduce... has a cost"), stoplight change ([[src-saastr-ai-revops-playbook]] @07:07–@30:42; [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]] (post 2025-01-27)).
- **2024-10-08:** "Operate" (rhythms, reporting, "math of sales") is one of six manager jobs; coaching is kept out of 1:1s ([[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @06:23–@10:35, @22:06–@23:06).
- **2024-10-30:** **Revenue OS** (name credited to "Brandon Gailey" as transcribed; spelled Brandon Ghaeli in his 2025-01-27 post): measurement cadence → lightweight monthly planning → team rhythms. **MAPLE** weekly update (Metrics, Advanced, Planned, Learnings, Emergencies), reviewed Sunday nights and used to drive 1:1s; "can't miss metrics" on the SLT agenda; name your rituals ([[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @23:42–@29:52).
- **2024-11-06 (own show):** Owner is "a transactional business So we do monthly business reviews instead of quarterly"; reps stack-ranked monthly ([[src-E07-building-a-learning-and-revenue-machine-with-stevie-case-cr]] @46:02, @63:58).
- **2024-11-19 / 2025-01-31:** Weekly "biggest needle mover" doc; each rep's monthly "secret sauce" skill focus ([[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @41:38–@42:42; [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @27:15).
- **2025-01-01 (own show):** "we don't run a forecast meeting at all"; a bottoms-up model paces daily ([[src-E14-a-systematic-approach-to-gtm-infrastructure-and-talent-with]] @77:33–@78:35).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **More rituals vs. fewer meetings.** He keeps adding named rituals (MAPLE, can't-miss metrics, Tiger Teams, report cards, Othership days, experiment pods) while praising calendar wipes and deletion. He admits the cycle: after Chaos Monkey "meetings crept back and they were pruning again" ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @38:18–@39:19).
- **Chaos Monkey every 12 months vs. delete every 90 days.** Different cadences for different targets (meetings vs. process, roles, tools); the 90-day one is an aspiration borrowed from Elon, not something he says Owner does.
- **~~MAPLE has gone quiet~~ (resolved).** MAPLE was restated in Jan 2025 ([[src-saastr-ai-revops-playbook]] @16:17), Aug 2025 ([[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @55:10) and Oct 2025 ([[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @23:14), and it predates Owner (built at Shopify). Open: whether the 2026 AI-drafted weekly updates still use the MAPLE format.
- **"Revenue OS" name (largely resolved).** Credited to "Brandon Gailey" in the Oct 2024 transcript; his own post spells it Brandon Ghaeli ([[src-linkedin-2025-h1]] (post 2025-01-27)). The ROS was first presented at SaaStr Annual in Sept 2024 ([[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (post 2024-09-24)).
- **Chaos Monkey dating.** Described in Nov 2025 as a start-of-year wipe; the new sources place it in early 2025 ("about to nuke all of our meetings", Mar 2025; "eight weeks" zero-basing at the start of 2025). Whether it was repeated at the start of 2026 is unstated.
- **Daily cadence then and now.** In 2015 and in 2024 he described daily rhythms (fire drill; managers' midday dashboard checks); since 2025 he stresses fewer meetings and async. The daily layer seems to have moved into dashboards and "chop wood, carry water" rather than meetings.
- **"No pipeline reviews" vs. weekly deal review advocates.** Guests like Mark Kosoglow call the weekly deal review "the most important meeting" ([[src-E09-building-repeatable-revenue-engines-at-outreach-and-catalyst]] @49:31–@50:35); Kyle holds that SMB velocity makes it unnecessary.
