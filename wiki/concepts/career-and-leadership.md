# Career & Leadership

> As of 2026-09-08, Kyle's career advice is "downstream of company selection": take a smaller title at a tier-one hyper-grower, diligence the CEO, and requalify for your job every quarter. His leadership creed is servant leadership ("the obligation of leadership"), a line he has held since at least 2015 ([[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @50:14). New in 2026: the leader who doesn't personally build with AI gets left behind, and his job "is to automate your job and then do a new job" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @36:26–@43:52; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @43:39–@49:46; [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @42:42).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- **Company quality beats title.** "The company that will give you the best title is probably the worst company for you to join" ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @49:23). In 2026's "flight to quality," take the first- or second-line seat at a tier-one hyper-grower; diligence customers, board members and especially former employees ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @36:26–@43:52).
- **Diligence the CEO.** Everything is "downstream of company selection" ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @70:41–@71:44); "You only need to learn that less, less than once" ([[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @63:05).
- **Company quality, not title, is a view he has held since at least 2024.** "Opt for quality of company over basically everything else" ([[src-E08-sam-blonds-blueprint-for-building-exceptional-leaders-tea]] @30:06, 2024-11-13); "you're way better off being the number two person at a number one company versus being the number one person at like a number seven company" ([[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @07:20). The 2026 "flight to quality" framing goes back to Nov 2024 (see evolution).
- **Requalify constantly.** "I have to requalify for my job every single quarter" ([[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @06:16).
- **Servant leadership is non-negotiable.** "you work for the people that work for you" ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @29:48); "the obligation of leadership"; if you don't care about people, "Go be an enterprise rep" ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @43:39–@49:46).
- **Work ethic, post-ZIRP.** The pre-2022 era was "make-believe"; "you got to be strapped the hell in right now" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @51:17–@52:18).
- **Build with AI yourself.** Learning comes from "building with it, getting stuck" ([[src-substack-2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young]]); build a compounding personal AI stack and "lead from the front" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @36:37–@42:42). Early-career people should "Become the person whose expertise makes AI useful" ([[src-substack-2026-03-31-ai-can-do-everything-a-salesperson]]).
- **Systems engineer over pure people manager.** "I think the era of the pure people manager is is ending" ([[src-the-new-rules-of-executive-recruiting-in-the-ai-era-asad-zaman-ceo]] @08:11, 2025-12-10); "the people-person manager is sort of going away" ([[src-signal-learn-build-scale]] @08:08).
- **Self-regulation as a leadership bar.** He can't trust someone with others' careers if they "can't control their own emotions and inner state" ([[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @84:47).
- **Treat finance as a partner.** Treating finance as an obstacle "is a career limiter" ([[src-substack-2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young]]).

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09-28 (Vision Critical):** Earliest source. Boiler-room origin ("making 150 calls every single day"); broke into tech by cold-calling the EVP of sales; leader's job is service: "You're not there for you. You're there for your people"; "servant leadership is the best way to say it." ([[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @03:02, @26:37–30:45, @46:09, @50:14).
- **2018-09-25 (League):** First-time VP Sales; "If you're designing process for the sake of process, for the sake of oversight, you're doing it wrong"; servant leadership again ([[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @28:10–30:19).
- **2020-08-17 (Shopify):** Picks jobs on "people, product, problems"; servant leadership "probably the most important principle I have"; "you can't lead people until you can lead yourself"; "my fault first" ([[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @04:04, @34:45–@52:37).
- **2022-11-01 (SVP Sales, Owner):** Learning to disagree well via an exec coach ("I was preaching and prosecuting and I was not operating as a scientist"); "your rate of growth is really the only thing that matters when it comes to being successful over time."
