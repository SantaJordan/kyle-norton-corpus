# Buyer AI, AEO and GEO

> As of 2026-04-30, Kyle says buyers now research and fact-check vendors through LLMs. CROs therefore have to "shape, or be shaped": track share of LLM citations, put someone in charge of AEO, and design the website for both humans and agents ([[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]]; [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-04-30)).

**Evidence base.** The prescriptive view (AEO, LLM citations) appears only in Mar–Apr 2026: one essay, one episode plus its companion post, and one LinkedIn promo. The fact base there is mostly a guest's (Webflow) data. The newly ingested sources show an earlier, thinner thread from 2025: Kyle as an AI-armed buyer himself and the claim that brand matters more in an AI world. Those are precursors, not an AEO program.

## Current position (as of 2026-04-30)
- Buyers arrive pre-researched. Half of B2B software buyers now start in an AI chatbot, so the human seller's value moves to perspective and trust [[src-substack-2026-03-31-ai-can-do-everything-a-salesperson]].
- Pipeline is "changing shape in a pretty significant way, whether or not people are paying attention" [[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @02:05.
- CRO to-do list: a dashboard for "your share of LLM citations", "someone whose job is AEO", and a website treated as "two simultaneous experiences" (buyer and agent) [[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]]. The LinkedIn promo puts the dashboard first among his CRO prescriptions [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-04-30).
- AEO is "a content strategy. A messaging discipline. A competitive intelligence function", not just schema markup. Every objection, alternative and pricing question needs "a clear, sourceable answer" [[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]].
- Discovery has moved. "most revenue leaders haven't updated a single dashboard to reflect it" [[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]].

## How his view evolved
- **2025-01-08 (precursor).** Citing Brian Halligan, he says "uniqueness and quality" will matter most, so "brand may be re-emergent in an AI world" [[src-E15-the-blueprint-for-sales-and-marketing-alignment-with-kyle-la]] @54:23.
- **2025-04-30 (precursor).** "breaking through the noise is like 80% of the game." He expects the next wave to be network and dark-social recommendations, and says he trusts the "10 people that I think really know go to market" over a generic G2 reviewer [[src-E31-the-rise-of-the-10x-sdr-with-florin-tatulea-of-common-room]] @49:48–50:50.
- **2025-06-18 (first buyer-side AI statement; re-dated from 2026-03-31).** As a buyer he ran ChatGPT deep research to build a full market map (competitors, pricing) before a purchase. A well-armed buyer is "a nightmare situation" for an unprepared AE [[src-E37-jason-wolf-on-uncovering-hidden-growth-opportunities-in-go-t]] @49:58–50:58.
- **2026-02-04.** Buyer leverage and low trust: "I know exactly who's paying what for what", and "the level of trust in AI is very low because there's so much vaporware" [[src-cro-life-from-a-20b-exit-to-a-hyper-growth-ai-org-michelle-donnell]] @38:42, @46:52–47:54.
- **2026-03-31 (new).** First explicit framing. He relays Daniel Pink's "buyer beware" → "seller beware" information-parity point and cites a 2025 G2 survey: buyers "already asked Claude or ChatGPT about your product, your competitors, and your pricing" [[src-substack-2026-03-31-ai-can-do-everything-a-salesperson]].
- **2026-04-29 (new).** Hosts Webflow CRO Adrian Rosenkranz. He opens with Webflow's "125% jump in LLM bot crawls", then asks whether companies need a separate agent-facing "big markdown file of all of the FAQs" (the guest says no: one site, two audiences) [[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @02:05, @08:28.
- **2026-04-30 (new).** The companion essay turns it into a CRO prescription (dashboard, owner, two-audience site, AEO as content and competitive intel). It also calls this "the single biggest shift in B2B discovery since SEO" [[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]]. The same-day LinkedIn post repeats the dashboard line [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-04-30).
- **After 2026-04-30:** no further statements found through 2026-09-08.

## Numbers he's cited
- Third-party (2025 G2 survey): half of B2B software buyers start in an AI chatbot, up 71% in four months (2026-03-31) [[src-substack-2026-03-31-ai-can-do-everything-a-salesperson]].
- Webflow (guest data Kyle repeats): "125% jump in LLM bot crawls across their 300,000-customer base" [[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @02:05. AI-bot share of traffic went from <1% to nearly 20%, and 43% of that is live agents. A ChatGPT citation-format change caused a 30–40% referral drop. Referred visitors convert 6x better (2026-04-29/30) [[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @03:07–07:24; [[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]]. Repeated in writing: 20% AI-bot traffic, 43% live queries [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-04-30).
- No Owner.com AEO data has been cited.

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Kyle's own data:** every number is a third party's (G2) or a guest's (Webflow). He has said nothing about whether Owner tracks LLM citations or has an AEO owner.
- **Fit with Owner's buyer:** elsewhere he stresses that Owner's customers aren't on LinkedIn ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @15:54–18:02) and that "90, 95% of meetings get booked on a phone call" ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @05:14). His own buyer-side example (deep research before a purchase, Jun 2025) is a software buyer like himself, not a restaurant owner. It is unclear how much LLM-led research applies to Owner's own buyers as opposed to B2B software buyers in general.
- **Precursor vs. program:** the 2025 remarks are about being an AI-armed buyer and about brand; the 2026 remarks prescribe an AEO function. The jump from observation to prescription happened in one month (Mar–Apr 2026) and was driven by a guest.
- **Terminology:** his essay defines AEO as "agent experience optimization", where the industry usually says answer engine optimization. "GEO" never appears in his words.
- **Thin and quiet since:** this is one essay plus one episode-and-companion and one promo, with nothing after 2026-04-30. Treat it as an emerging view, not an operating program.
