# AI Operating Model: Centralized vs. Decentralized

> As of 2026-09-08, Kyle's doctrine is centralized AI deployment. A small, technical applied-AI team (sitting in Data/BizOps) builds production tools and pushes them into surfaces reps already use. Reps don't run agents. AI fluency is mandatory for senior and leverage roles but not for frontline managers. He calls this a reversal of his ~2025 push for broad AI adoption ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @11:38; [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @13:11; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @28:02–@42:37).

**Ingest note (2026-10-05).** The backfill shows that centralized frontline AI was Owner's practice and Kyle's stated position from at least **May 2025**, not a 2026 discovery. "No new surfaces" dates to Aug 2025. What he reversed in 2026 was the parallel push to make *managers* build with AI. His central model for ICs has stayed the same.

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
Unchanged by the backfill.
- **Centralize.** A central team of "super experts" brings tools to production. "I actually don't really want the reps running agents per se. I want to run that all centrally". Outputs land in Salesforce or SalesLoft ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @13:11–@14:12, @26:26–@27:27). In writing: "A lot of teams think the answer is giving every rep a Claude skill and letting them generate their own lead lists. It’s not." ([[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]])
- **Quality gap.** What the applied-AI lead builds is "an order of magnitude" better than a savvy rep's or AE's side project ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @12:40; [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @09:08–10:08; [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @12:11). In writing, he says it is "20x better" ([[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]).
- **"AI peanut butter"** is his name for the decentralized failure mode: 50 initiatives with no owners. He prefers "a centralized model with decentralized idea flow" ([[src-substack-2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young]]). Rep-built tools risk "AI performance theater" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @10:08–@11:09) and the "efficiency trap" ([[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]]).
- **No new surfaces.** "We did not ask every rep to become an AI power user. We built tools and pushed them into the surfaces reps already use" ([[src-substack-2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young]]). Example: AI pre-call research injected into the SalesLoft dialer screen ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @18:44–@19:44). He has said this since Aug 2025 (see evolution).
- **Where it sits.** Applied AI started as Kyle's first post-Series C headcount and now reports to the VP of Data in BizOps ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @08:20–@09:28; [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @26:26–@27:27). In May 2026 he was hiring a Lead, GTM Analytics & Applied AI reporting to a Director of GTM Analytics & Applied AI ([[src-linkedin-2026]], post 2026-05-01). "You don't necessarily need an AI czar". The function could live in engineering/applied AI or in RevOps ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @36:52). To fund it, move "two of those BDR headcount" into one GTME ([[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]).
- **Change management is the moat.** Pilot pods put the applied-AI lead (YG) beside 2–4 top reps ("pair prospecting"). The tools are "not that complicated" ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @06:09–@11:23; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @12:26–@15:34).
- **Fluency bar differs by role.** Senior people and leverage roles (RevOps) must be "AI-pilled." Frontline managers need less because AI is built centrally ([[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @08:06–@10:08). "What’s probably more controversial: I don’t think your AEs need to be super AI-savvy." ([[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]). A CRO without systems thinking can compensate: "pay up a little bit more for your RevOps slash applied AI person" ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @40:26–@42:37).
- **Caveat.** If you sell tokens (e.g., at an AI lab), decentralized makes more sense ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @08:07–@09:07).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **When did centralization start? Largely resolved.** E68 says "from November 22 and beyond" (@36:52). That fits the core: lead scoring in 2022 and the first production use case in Dec 2022 ([[src-signal-learn-build-scale]] @05:05). Frontline AI was described as "top down" in Mar 2025 (E25) and "centralized" in BizOps/RevOps in May 2025 (SaaStr) and Nov 2025 (E55). The "push AI adoption... 12 months ago" was a **parallel** push for managers and leaders to build personal fluency (Karpathy videos, Topline Mar 2025). It was not a decentralized model for building rep tools. What he reversed in 2026 was that parallel push for managers.
- **Applied-AI hire timing: resolved.** He was about to post the role in Apr 2025 (E30) and was still hiring in May–Jun 2025 (SaaStr, Collin Cadmus). The role was in seat by Aug 2025 (E41), and Kyle described the hire in Oct 2025 (E48, E49). The Signal (~Nov–Dec 2025) says "a couple months ago," followed by a director of applied AI. That matches AccountAim's "only just hired" (Oct 2025). The first-role-after-the-Series-C framing in Captivate (@13:12–@14:13) refers to budget, not fill date, and E74 says the headcount "ultimately went to the data and BizOps team" (@29:26–@31:29).
- **Per-role fluency bar: older than his "my views on this have changed" implies.** In May 2025 he already exempted ICs and required AI-native leaders and managers (SaaStr @06:13–@09:20; E33 @30:01–31:01). The Apr 2026 change lowers the bar only for frontline managers, so the "change" is narrower than the phrase suggests.
- **Pure central vs. "both."** SaaStr (May 26) is absolute: reps shouldn't run agents. E68 (May 7) and the Substack (May 13) want "both central and decentral" or "decentralized idea flow." E65 wants more ideas from the bottom. In Mar 2025 he said "I would kill to be a rep right now" because he'd be "building clay tables" (E25 @43:36). The stance is centralized building with distributed ideation, and he does not state it consistently.
- **Leader fluency.** He says leaders "probably" don't need Claude Code in a terminal and a GitHub repo ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @25:25–@26:26). He also says AI strategy "falls on the leader" ([[src-substack-2026-03-31-ai-can-do-everything-a-salesperson]]; [[src-ivey-future-of-sales-irreplaceable]] @06:11), that you can't outsource fluency to "an AI guy" (Polaris, Oct 2025), and that leaders not in the tools are "cooked" (Signal).
