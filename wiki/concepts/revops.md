# RevOps

> As of 2026-09-08, Kyle still argues that RevOps should be funded far earlier and heavier than is normal, as a servant function sitting beside reps. That view goes back to at least early 2024, and the "invest early" message is the most consistent thing he has said on any topic. Since late 2025 he has added that RevOps leaders must work like engineers (in Claude Code, on a Snowflake system of record). Since spring 2026 he says RevOps is turning into a *product* team ("We're all product thinkers") whose boundaries with GTM engineering, applied AI, data and BizOps are now blurry ([[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @11:48; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @18:44–@19:44; [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-01-15)).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- **Invest early, even at the expense of quota-carrying heads.** Enablement was "twice or three times bigger" than peers', and headcount was diverted from reps into RevOps and enablement ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @47:39–@48:42). Not having that infrastructure is "one of the biggest own goals that revenue leaders make" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @18:43). The fullest version is on his own show: "invest in this team way earlier than you think you need", because "what's expensive is having a hundred frontline go to market reps that are 2% 5% 20% less efficient than they should be" ([[src-E46-the-revops-mind-behind-ownercoms-billion-dollar-valuation]] @68:20–@69:22).
- **RevOps is becoming a product org.** "It really feels like the roadmap for this is a product org", with embedded PMs, builders and a back-end team for evals ([[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @34:33–@35:36). New RevOps "looks much more like product management" ([[src-substack-2026-05-17-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook]]). RevOps as product means "jobs to be done... building a PRD... build your MVP" ([[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @11:48). This builds on Owner's move to agile RevOps with product owners per function, which began in early 2025 ([[src-E19-architecting-systems-for-hypergrowth-with-nate-follen-of-ram]] @09:19–@10:27).
- **RevOps leaders must be builders.** "The RevOps leader who isn't in Claude Code, doesn't understand data architecture, and isn't shipping experiments weekly? They're going to be replaced by someone who can." ([[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-01-15)). Owner's VP RevOps was "building into our um sandbox Salesforce from the cloud code terminal" ([[src-usage-based-comp-ai-and-the-end-of-the-sdr-tyler-will-vp-of-revops]] @58:56).
- **Data foundation first; Snowflake is the system of record.** "our system of record is Snowflake not Salesforce" ([[src-E45-the-inside-scoop-on-gtm-engineering-with-clays-everett-berr]] @43:52–@44:54); "At Owner, we made Snowflake our system of record (not CRM) and put tons of resourcing against data quality." ([[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-01-15)).
- **RevOps and GTM engineering / applied AI are distinct, but the edges are blurring.** In March he called them "quite distinct functions": RevOps owns rep tools, applied AI builds intelligence ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @22:47–@24:57). By May the five functions "overlap so much now" and "who owns that today is, is a big question" ([[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @55:47–@56:48).
- **Embedded, servant, in the room.** RevOps has always been centralized with specialists embedded in each team ([[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @32:28–@33:31). RevOps must sit beside reps and hear "why what you've built sucks" without taking offense ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @12:24). Design for trust: "if you assume no we need guard rails and and and validation fields... people will adopt that mindset if that's the way you treat them" ([[src-E46-the-revops-mind-behind-ownercoms-billion-dollar-valuation]] @22:23).
- **The 80/20 is "boring stuff":** lead quality, a mapped and enriched market, scored accounts, contacts, and transcripts auto-filling Salesforce ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @20:46–@21:46). "AI makes bad infrastructure louder" ([[src-substack-2026-05-17-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook]]).
- **Keep the CRM vanilla.** Mostly out-of-the-box Salesforce until ~$10M ARR; custom objects and Apex create cost and break integrations ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @41:30–@43:32). The scar is from Shopify: "so much Apex code that nothing worked" ([[src-E09-building-repeatable-revenue-engines-at-outreach-and-catalyst]] @36:57–@37:57). "we're not going to go vibe code Salesforce" ([[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @31:58).
- **Friction removal is a RevOps job.** "No new surfaces": AI pre-call research is injected on the SalesLoft dialer screen; he cites *The Human Element* and nudge theory, and credits a head of RevOps who has worked with him ~10 years ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @18:44–@19:44). The same idea was already in 2025: "make our golden path the lowest friction path" ([[src-E46-the-revops-mind-behind-ownercoms-billion-dollar-valuation]] @28:28).

## How his view evolved
- **2020-08-17:** As Head of Revenue for Shopify Retail, his remit already included RevOps alongside sales, enablement, solutions engineering and implementations ([[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]], header).
- **Pre-Owner (looking back, told 2024-02-23):** At Vision Critical he taught himself Salesforce and built BDR reporting from scratch with RevOps, including a "Lead Audit Sheet" ([[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @12:29–@16:37). As a head of BDR about 10 years before 2025, he ran a "monster pivot table" lead audit whose results "are going to horrify you" ([[src-E20-building-data-driven-sales-processes-in-the-ai-era-with-jord]] @55:04–@56:10).
- **2023-12-15 / 12-17:** His Owner scope covers sales, revenue operations, enablement and launch ([[src-web-2023-12-15-amplemarket-blog-sales-management-tips-expert-insights-with]]). Salesforce is his can't-lose app, with a hedge: "Maybe the shift to the data warehouse as the source of truth (Snowflake for us) and AI change my opinion here though." ([[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]]). This is the first sign of the Snowflake-as-system-of-record position.
- **2024-02-05:** At Shopify, great PMF covered up weak GTM ops; Shopify "wasn't sophisticated" in GTM data/RevOps ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @21:34–@23:41).
- **2024-02-07:** "invest in RevOps earlier than you think you need." Point it at prospect data first; trade AEs out of the plan if needed; strong RevOps makes every rep "10, 15, 30% better" ([[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @33:20–@34:20).
- **2024-04-18:** Hire RevOps and enablement early, even fractionally on contract (10, then 15, then 20 hours/week) ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @26:33–@27:34).
- **2024-08-01:** Steve was hired at "less than 10 million ARR, maybe 5 million"; directors of enablement and RevOps for "an eight-person sales team" look crazy but are cheap relative to the plan ([[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @62:28–@63:29). He told Steve to spend 60% of his time on CS systems once CS came under him ([[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @57:22–@61:28). Middle path: a contractor at ~5 hours a week ([[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @15:19–@16:20).
- **2024-09-24:** Introduces the **Revenue Operating System**, "your Iron Man suit" ([[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (post 2024-09-24)); the full talk is in [[src-saastr-ai-revops-playbook]] (published 2025-01-29). See [[operating-rhythm]].
- **2024-10-06:** RevOps probably doesn't need a C-suite seat: "Force multiplier is, I think, a more accurate description for RevOps." His RevOps leader "literally operates as an extension of myself" ([[src-topline-e78-executive-fear-boards]] @35:29–@36:31). RevOps supplies high-propensity accounts and clean data, the sales-acceleration stack, business process as "organizational lubricant", and reporting, working with BizOps/data, which owns the single source of truth ([[src-topline-e78-executive-fear-boards]] @40:38–@43:43).
- **2024-10-23:** "I think you're going to see a new golden age of RevOps." ([[src-E05-mark-niemiec-on-steering-business-transformation]] @17:54).
- **2024-11-19:** Gives his first ratio: 3 full-time + 2 contractors (+ consultants) supporting "like a hundred people" in GTM, "one to 20" ([[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @19:55–@20:56). Under-investment explains why "50% of your reps don't work out" ([[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @20:56).
- **2024-12-03:** RevOps and enablement fail when bureaucratic ("checking a box"); hire leaders who serve the rep ([[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @17:46–@19:54).
- **2024-12-18:** Competitors under-invest in enablement and RevOps, so a 40-rep team with one enablement person has "no consistency in execution" ([[src-E12-bravados-ceo-sahil-mansuri-on-the-future-of-sales-reps-and]] @56:07–@57:13).
- **2025-01-19:** Post-2015 CROs skew to systems operators; non-systems CROs succeed with a long-time RevOps/BizOps partner ([[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] @12:28–@14:34).
- **2025-02-05:** Staging: RevOps/enablement on contract by ~$3M ARR, full-time "by four or five"; ~4 FTE in RevOps and 3 in enablement at ~$30M ARR ([[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @43:30–@44:35). Owner is moving RevOps to agile, with "one product owner for each of those functional areas" (sales, launch, CS); "My head of RevOps was a former sales leader" ([[src-E19-architecting-systems-for-hypergrowth-with-nate-follen-of-ram]] @09:19–@12:29).
- **2025-02-12:** CROs can't do the data work themselves: "somebody has to be full-time dedicated to this project" ([[src-E20-building-data-driven-sales-processes-in-the-ai-era-with-jord]] @51:55–@54:03).
- **2025-03-05:** Plan for Momentum → Salesforce → Snowflake as a "single source of truth where I will be able to just slap a language model on top of it" ([[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @29:51–@30:55).

- **2025-05-28:** AI centralized in RevOps/BizOps: "most of the automation and AI is centralized. I'm, it's in my biz ops and rev ops team." ([[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @07:18). This is the earliest dated statement of the centralized model (see [[ai-operating-model]]).

RevOps + enablement leaders ≈ "$400,000 in salary"; contractors at ~5 hours a week (2024-08-01, [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @15:19–@16:20).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **The RevOps ratios — partly resolved.** Nov 2024: 3 FTE + 2 contractors for "like a hundred people" ([[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @19:55; Kyle first said "supporting 50", then corrected to "like a hundred" because it's GTM ops, not just sales ops; raw transcript). Sep–Oct 2025: 7 people at $50M ARR ([[src-E46-the-revops-mind-behind-ownercoms-billion-dollar-valuation]] @69:22), and "7 RevOps people for 30 sales reps" said on audio and repeated in writing ([[src-accountaim-revops-ai-advantage]] @12:28; [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]] (post 2025-10-21)). The headcount of seven is now confirmed three ways. The "30 sales reps" denominator is a deliberate public framing (written, not a mis-transcription), yet it still conflicts with other counts: ~60 in sales in Feb 2025 ([[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @14:43), and a sales team of "100" in Dec 2025 ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @25:22–@26:25). The likeliest reading is that "30" counts ramped AEs only. By Aug–Sep 2026 the sales org is "close to 150 people in the sales function" ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @05:14); no current RevOps headcount has been given.
- **Steve's tenure drifts but is consistent.** It goes from "eight years" (Oct–Nov 2024) to "well over nine years" (Apr 2025) to "almost a decade" (Sep 2025). That fits a League → Shopify → Owner history. "two companies ago" ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @29:22) also fits.
- **Snowflake vs. Salesforce as the hub.** He has said Snowflake, not Salesforce, is the system of record (Sep 2025, Jan 2026). In Dec 2025 he also said Owner is "leaning into Salesforce more than ever which I am personally surprised by" ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @63:00; see [[ai-sales-stack]]). And in 2026 he says "we're not going to go vibe code Salesforce". So the data lives in Snowflake while Salesforce is the workflow and agent layer. He hasn't stated that split outright.
- **"Distinct" vs. "overlap so much."** Within two months (Mar → May 2026) he went from RevOps and GTME as "quite distinct" to the functions overlapping with unclear ownership. Not a full reversal, but his org model is unsettled.
- **Who builds AI: RevOps or engineers?** In May 2025 AI was centralized "in my biz ops and rev ops team" ([[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @07:18). He wanted the applied-AI role in RevOps originally ([[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @13:12–@17:16), and his VP RevOps builds in Claude Code (Dec 2025). Yet in May 2026 he prefers engineering/data hires over converted RevOps ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @28:28).
- **C-suite seat.** In Oct 2024 RevOps "probably" doesn't need a C-suite seat ([[src-topline-e78-executive-fear-boards]] @36:31). In Oct 2025 heads of RevOps and BizOps sit in the SLT ([[src-accountaim-revops-ai-advantage]] @21:43–@26:53). SLT is not the C-suite, so this is a drift in emphasis, not a contradiction.
- **Conditionality.** Early RevOps "applies only with strong PMF and funding in SMB/mid-market" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @17:43–@19:45) and "skew your CAC and LTVCAC pretty badly" ([[src-accountaim-revops-ai-advantage]] @10:24–@13:30). Already in Feb 2025 it was "a chicken-and-egg spend" to commit once you have evidence of GTM fit ([[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @42:22–@47:46), and in May 2025 only if you're "playing for a big outcome" ([[src-coffee-scaling-smarter]]). His blanket "earlier than you think" is softer than it sounds.
