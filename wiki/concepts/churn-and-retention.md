# Churn and Retention

> As of Aug 2026, Kyle's position is that churn starts upstream in sales and onboarding ("a lot of churn is our fault"), so the CRO owns customer quality. He is not convinced that CS should report to the CRO by default ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @13:48, [[src-E72-i-collapsed-my-gtm-org-chart-this-is-why]] @27:07).

## Current position (as of 2026-08-06)
- "Everybody owns retention, really." CS technically owns the metric, but sales- and onboarding-process churn is "my churn" [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @15:43–17:47.
- Fix churn as far upstream as possible: ICP thresholds, expectation-setting, and configuration quality at launch [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @05:09–11:30, [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @11:39–14:54.
- Owner sells month-to-month and makes cancelling easy. Earning retention without lock-in is a long-term trust moat: "I don't believe in doing business that way" [[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @14:45–15:46.
- Elite CROs trade new ARR for retention when they need to [[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @25:45–27:47. "if you close a deal and it churns in 12 months", that's worse than never having the revenue [[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @18:28.
- Churn is now partly an AI/data problem. A CS/support BizOps IC became a de facto applied-AI director building churn-analysis agents and a churn-prediction model [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @13:12–17:16.
- He questions making CRO-owned CS the default, since revenue leaders may neglect CS because new business is "easier to move" [[src-E72-i-collapsed-my-gtm-org-chart-this-is-why]] @27:07.
- The comp plan is his main churn lever: AEs and sales leaders are paid on *launched* customers, not closed-won, since July 2024 [[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @13:33–14:38, [[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @03:12. Paying on retention itself is "probably a step too far" [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @64:40–66:49.
- Churn is reviewed in the operating rhythm. A monthly 90-minute churn review goes "churn by churn", and data science diagnoses deals that looked good on paper [[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @02:06–03:12.
- (Unchanged in substance since 2022–2024; the new sources mostly push the history back and add the comp and rhythm mechanics.)

## How his view evolved
- **2020-08 (Shopify):** Early-stage companies should over-invest in service and CS, because satisfied customers are the snowball [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @23:06–24:11.
- **2022-11 (earliest Owner source, ~5 months in):** Since June 2022 Owner "reduced our early churn rate by like 85%" by being patient and niching down. He argues the 50/50 head-of-sales plan breeds misaligned customers and churn: "Show me the incentives and I'll show you the outcome." [[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @17:27–18:28, @24:42–26:44.
- **2024-02:** Earn trust with month-to-month contracts, data export, and help reverting. Owner dropped a money-back guarantee because it "reduced customer commitment" [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @35:58–39:03, [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-skeptical-icp]] @01:02 (clip of the same episode).
- **2024-03 (Latka keynote; published 2024-08):** When he joined there was "upwards of like a 30% churn" in the first 90 days after close. He fixed early churn before scaling and presented a declining growth number at his first board meeting. "I would strongly advocate that sales leaders should own onboarding." [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @02:03–06:08, @15:19.
- **2024-04:** Early churn was "the hardest thing" of the past year. Fix: say no, plus an ML "expected GMV" score. He's considering tying comp to customer outcomes [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @02:01, @14:17–15:18, @40:00–41:05.
- **2024-06:** Month-to-month as a trust stance: "We actually help the customer churn." [[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @16:31.
- **2024-07:** AE comp moves from closed-won to customer launch. He describes the change in Dec 2024 ("We changed our comp plan recently") and later dates it to July [[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @29:06, [[src-coffee-scaling-smarter]] @17:06–18:11.
- **2024-08:** Full-stack revenue: bringing onboarding and then CS under him improved outcomes "within weeks", and he had his RevOps lead spend 60% of his time on CS systems [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @57:22–61:28.
- **2024-10:** Churn is one of three areas a C-suite revenue leader must master. Triangulate gross and net retention, because NRR can hide logo bleed [[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @17:26–23:42. SaaS compounds only at ~90–95% retention, so revenue leaders must own customer quality and handoffs [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @12:33–14:39.
- **2024-12:** Revenue leaders should own onboarding. He cut ~40% of deals to reach "world-class" churn [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @36:41–38:50. (Re-aired Dec 2025 with a new cold open: "If you can, you want your revenue leader to own onboarding." [[src-gtmshift-lessons-in-scaling-2025]] @00:00; same interview, counted once.) His Dec 2024 purview covers new-customer onboarding but **not** CS or support, and Crescendo AI has replaced the "entire tier one BPO" [[src-momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy]] @03:07–04:07, @16:19.
- **2025-01 to 2025-03 (own show):** He has owned onboarding, CS and support on and off for about six years: "As a VP of sales, you never really appreciate the downstream impact until you're the one cleaning it up." [[src-E18-confessions-from-a-first-time-cro-with-catie-ivey-of-walnut]] @20:28–21:29. In month-to-month SMB, "equally as important is the quality of the setup" in the first 90 days [[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @26:34–28:46. Sales and CS "need to live with one another", but few leaders will "actually give up revenue" to make it work [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @63:33.
- **2025-04:** On paying at launch: "Everything got better" [[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @13:33–14:38.
- **2025-05:** About 30% of signups churned in the first 60–90 days, which he calls "wasted calories". In an AI era, word of mouth matters "more than ever" [[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @10:19–13:25.
- **2025-05 (also):** AI in support: Crescendo plus Guru contain "almost 50% of our tier one support tickets", expected to reach 80% [[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @32:59. Ramp was extended because "the deal doesn't matter unless it launches". He helps leaving customers move cleanly (DNS records) rather than hold domains hostage [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @40:22–41:22, @68:12–69:16.
- **2025-06:** Owner is "the single highest rated software in all of restaurant technology", and treating churning customers well is a long-term advantage [[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @16:47.
- **2025-07:** His "blind spot": churn was the only non-world-class metric in 2024, so he went after "the atomic unit of churn". Configuration quality beat time-to-value [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @04:08–11:30.
- **2025-07 (also):** Recalling his first 60 days, he spent them on deal quality and accepted two down months: "it was silly to just spend a bunch of money and scale on this rickety foundation" [[src-pocus-scaling-3m-to-21m]] @12:36–14:45. Do LTV:CAC by channel with retention baked in [[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @55:13–56:14.
- **2025-09:** "a lot of churn is our fault." He took over onboarding in year one [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @13:48.
- **2025-10:** Owner has repeatedly shrunk its ICP or "added friction to the buying journey" for first-90-day retention, and runs a monthly 90-minute churn review [[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @02:06–03:12. His own target is company growth, which "means it's got to be efficient and low churn high retention" [[src-work-sprawl-and-the-future-of-marketing-with-global-vp-marketing-cli]] @40:48–41:49.

- **2025-12:** Scope change: CS is listed as *formerly* his [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @12:27.
- **2026-02:** A churn root-causing method: crowdsource hypotheses, turn each into data, use a data-science partner and an opportunity solution tree [[src-a-product-informed-approach-for-gtm-usha-iyer-chief-customer-growt]] @25:28–30:31.
- **2026-04 (new):** Churn work is moving to applied AI: churn-analysis agents and a churn-prediction model built by a BizOps IC [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @13:12–17:16.

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **"Everybody owns retention" vs. who owns CS (partly resolved).** The new sources show a consistent belief with a moving org chart. He has owned onboarding, CS and support "on and off for about six years" [[src-E18-confessions-from-a-first-time-cro-with-catie-ivey-of-walnut]] @20:28, argued for a "full stack model" including CS in Aug 2024 [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @57:22–61:28, and said sales and CS "need to live with one another" in Mar 2025 [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @63:33. Yet his Dec 2024 purview excluded CS and support [[src-momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy]] @03:07–04:07, CS is listed as *formerly* his in Dec 2025 [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @12:27, and an outlet-written Jul 2026 bio lists CS and support as his again [[src-web-2026-07-29-n2growth-ownercoms-kyle-norton-on-succeeding-in]]. In Aug 2026 he questions CRO-owned CS as "the default" [[src-E72-i-collapsed-my-gtm-org-chart-this-is-why]] @27:07. The constant is onboarding: he has always wanted the revenue leader to own it. Whether CS sits under him in 2026 is unconfirmed in his own words.
- **Churn as the only weak metric vs. "world-class" earlier.** In Dec 2024 he described cutting deals to make churn "world-class" [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @36:41. In Jul 2025 he said churn was *still* the one non-world-class metric throughout 2024 [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @04:08. That's consistent with work still in progress, but it means the 2022 cleanup alone didn't fix it, even though he reported cutting *early* churn "by like 85%" by Nov 2022 [[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @17:27–18:28. The likely reading: early (first-90-day) churn fell fast, and overall churn stayed the weak metric.
- **Time-to-value orthodoxy.** He reports that slowing onboarding to improve configuration raised retention [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @10:24–11:30. Yet Owner's GTM is built for speed (a "six-day sales cycle" [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @06:15). How the handoff resolves that tension isn't described after 2025.
- **Pay on launch, but not on retention.** He moved AE and sales-leader pay to launch to fight churn [[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @13:33–14:38, while calling pay on retention "probably a step too far" [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @64:40–66:49. In Apr 2024 he had been "considering" tying comp to customer outcomes [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @40:00–41:05. Launch is where he settled; retention-based pay never shipped.
- **No current churn figure.** Since Jul 2025 he hasn't given any churn or retention metric. The 2026 sources mention churn only through tooling (agents, prediction model) and culture.
