# AI in GTM (hub)

> As of 2026-09-08, Kyle's position is that AI in GTM is a transformation and change-management problem, not a tooling or use-case problem. Start with first- and third-party data, point early AI at pipeline, deploy centrally into existing surfaces, keep deterministic structure with human evals, and climb toward a governed harness (L3). Owner's edge is "our ability to change behavior," because the tools themselves are "not that complicated" ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @12:26–@35:11; [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @00:00–@04:06).

This is the hub page. Details live in [[ai-operating-model]], [[ai-sophistication-ladder]], [[context-harness-and-memory]] and [[personal-ai-stack]].

**Ingest note (2026-10-05).** The backfill of 93 older sources shows that most of this doctrine (data first, narrow and deterministic AI, centralized deployment, no new surfaces, Jevons, AI cold email at scale) was already in place in 2025. Several items the page previously dated to 2026 have been re-dated below.

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
Unchanged in substance by the backfill. What changed is how long he has held it.
- **Data first, always.** Third-party data means a scored market map with contacts and mobile numbers. First-party data means call transcripts parsed into Salesforce fields via Momentum. "just start with the data" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @20:20–@22:21; [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @04:12; [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @02:05–@03:06). He has held this since at least Dec 2024 ([[src-momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy]] @04:07–07:11).
- **Pipeline first.** "80% of your AI efforts in your early innings should be pipeline focused" ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @35:50). Examples: AI pre-call research with "the three things," ML scoring and pickup prediction ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @07:10–@09:15; [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @02:01–@05:11).
- **Change management is the differentiator.** Change one thing at a time, "finish the swing," and run pilot pods with top reps. Rep apathy "is the most important signal" that a tool isn't good enough ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @03:06–@11:23; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @12:26–@19:44). In late 2025 he said adoption deserves at least as much time as the build ([[src-signal-learn-build-scale]] @22:27–23:28).
- **Centralize and add no new surfaces.** See [[ai-operating-model]] ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @08:07–@14:12).
- **Deterministic scaffolding plus human evals.** Chaining generative steps causes "lossiness" and "AI slop" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @30:29–@35:35; [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @36:00–@43:22).
- **Buy infrastructure, build intelligence** ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @14:12; [[src-substack-2026-05-26-dont-buy-your-gtm-brain]], preview only). See [[build-vs-buy-software]].
- **The goal is capacity creation, not cuts (Jevons).** Owner ships "four times the number of PRs per engineer" and is pulling BDR headcount forward ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @12:04–@14:12). "The better goal is capacity creation" ([[src-substack-2026-05-17-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook]]).
- **AI multiplies what is there.** "AI amplifies what's already there. It doesn't create discipline from nothing." ([[src-linkedin-2026]], post 2026-01-30). "AI is a multiplier. Multiplying zero still gives you zero." ([[src-linkedin-2026]], post 2026-03-31)
- **Leaders own it.** AI strategy "falls on the leader" and can't be outsourced to consultants ([[src-substack-2026-03-31-ai-can-do-everything-a-salesperson]]; [[src-ivey-future-of-sales-irreplaceable]] @06:11). "Lead from the front" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @42:42).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **AI outbound: an earlier turn than it looked.** In Nov 2024 it was "we don't do any AI outbound." From Dec 2024 to Jan 2025 he was "not yet a believer" in replacing BDRs, called AI-SDR a "party trick," and called 95% of AI-SDR use "pretty crap." Owner was "piloting an AI SDR" in Aug 2025 (E41 @33:35–34:36), building cold outbound email infrastructure in Oct 2025 (E49 @24:28), and sending 1,800 AI cold emails a day by Dec 2025 (E58 @31:30). **Corrected:** the turn happened in H2 2025, not Aug 2026. The Aug 2026 "sales reps don't send emails for us" restates an established practice. What remains in tension is his Apr 2026 line, "I don't personally want to just let AI write my cold outreach" ([[src-ivey-future-of-sales-irreplaceable]] @10:23). One reading is that this is personal writing versus a centrally engineered, field-filled system. The calls-first motion also stays ("90% of your meetings will be from calls", [[src-linkedin-2026]], post 2026-05-27). Owner also uses OneMind, an AI avatar that sells full deals ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @60:48).
- **ARR per AE: partly resolved.** "Over 2 million" (May) and "$3 million" (Aug) are both called "20X" of $150K. The June 2026 written interview gives 20x = $3M, which supports $3M. The "2 million" may echo the top BDR's annualized "two million in ARR in a single year" (Attio). That is unconfirmed.
- **AI PCR lift metric.** "85% more calls and 85% more opportunities" (Jan 2026 pilot), "85% more ops" (May), and "86% more decision maker connects" with the full opp lift explicitly not retained (Aug) are different metrics for similar numbers. Prefer the August framing for the at-scale result.
- **BDR productivity series: consistent (lint correction 2026-10-05).** About $10K closed-won MRR per BDR in a month (E68, May 7) = ~$120K of new ARR that month, which matches "closer to $120k" (Attio, May), "over $100,000 in ARR per month" (SaaStr, May 26) and ≈ $1.2M ARR a year (Aug). The Jan 2026 "70K" is an earlier point on the same curve.
- **Use-case count.** About six (spring 2025), "seven plus" and "seven" (Oct–Dec 2025), nine (Nov–Dec 2025: Signal and E58), then eight (Union Square, published Apr 2026). The count does not rise steadily. Union Square may have been recorded earlier, and the counts may differ in what qualifies as "high impact."
- **Headcount.** "We get more done" and Jevons (held since May 2025) sit alongside "fire your worst reps and give those ops to your best ones" ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @21:33–@26:46) and funding applied AI by cutting bottom performers ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @27:23–@29:27). In writing he says "Roles I would have hired for 18 months ago don't exist on my org chart anymore" (2026-04-20) and reallocates "two of those BDR headcount" into a GTME (Attio). Capacity creation and selective cuts coexist.
- **Craft vs. systems.** In Jan–Feb 2025 he predicted AI would make sales craft more important ([[src-E18-confessions-from-a-first-time-cro-with-catie-ivey-of-walnut]] @54:19–55:20; E19 @71:04). By Mar 2026 he said being systems-oriented matters far more than sales craft (paraphrase; [[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @41:44–42:45). The first claim is about reps and the second about leaders, but the emphasis has shifted.
