# Outbound at Scale

> As of Sep 2026, Kyle runs a phone-first, centrally orchestrated BDR machine at an $8–12K ACV: the math says it works, AI does the data, research and email around the call, and humans make the calls ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @09:24, [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @17:29). His phone-first belief goes back to his 2008 boiler-room start and his 2015 Vision Critical BDR team ([[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @33:54–37:59). The AI cold-email engine is older than the 2026 sources implied: it was sending 1,800 emails a day by Dec 2025 ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @31:30).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- Outbound at a small ACV is a "high probability practice", not a best practice. Owner's own math (win rates above 50%, 2–3 rep-hours per deal) works back to about two meetings per BDR per day and ~200 dials [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @00:00, @09:24–10:24.
- Outbound isn't dead, but Owner does it differently. Average outbound BDRs close "over $100,000 in ARR per month" [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @03:01; in writing, the average went from "$72k per rep per month" to "closer to $120k" [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]. About $75K USD take-home brings in about $1.2M closed-won ARR per year [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @01:01.
- Data comes first. That means a mapped, scored, enriched market with mobile numbers, because restaurant data "is not in ZoomInfo". BDRs never build their own lists [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @04:12–07:22, [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @29:29. "No training, hiring profile, call volume or slick techniques can overcome giving your team bad data." [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]
- AI is centralized under one specialist owner, not handed to reps: giving every rep a Claude skill "is not" the answer [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]].
- Calls are routed by AI (value, conversion and pickup likelihood), and AI pre-call research ("three fields") sits on top of the SalesLoft dialer screen so reps don't open tabs [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @10:24–12:26, @24:52.
- Reps no longer send email. Cold email is "AI generated" and sent centrally, and after a single-threaded owner was put on replies it grew to almost 5% of the qualified lead funnel [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @17:29–18:30. (The engine itself dates to late 2025: see evolution.)
- Brush-offs on a cold call are reflexes, not objections. Owner's NASCAR framework breaks the pattern instead of arguing [[src-caliber-outbound-objection-navigation]] @01:02–03:08.
- Specialization beats full-cycle selling: "There is no way that any of my AEs could cold call the way my BDRs call now." [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @14:50. Owner AEs source no more than 10–15% of their own deals [[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @17:23–18:24.

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09 (Vision Critical):** Origin in a boiler room making "150 calls every single day". At Vision Critical he picked SalesLoft and DiscoverOrg for direct dials ("I'm still a believer in the phone"), and argued there's no universal cadence: "you don't know unless you test" [[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @02:02–04:04, @33:54–43:05.
- **2018-09 (League):** Specialization: League AEs sourced only ~20–25% of their pipeline, because "seasoned account executives don't want to pick up the phone because they're out of practice and they don't want to get embarrassed." He ran trigger- and hypothesis-based outreach plus video prospecting [[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @18:49–27:07.
- **2022 (Owner start):** BDR call-to-decision-maker connect rate was "3%" in late 2022 [[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] @24:01–25:07. Owner's outbound team was built in Q4 2022 [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @23:55.
- **2024-02:** Owner is "fully digital". Data on the highest-fit restaurants is worth more than in-person conversion gains [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @40:05–41:07. Fund BDRs before AEs, and point RevOps at prospect data first [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @33:20–35:26. He recalls missing an early number by not bench-building BDRs a quarter ahead, and building Vision Critical's "Lead Audit Sheet" [[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @03:10–06:16, @12:29–16:37.
- **2024-03/04:** "Don't bother hiring more than one or two BDRs until you figure out this data piece" [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @19:24. Data accuracy went from 40% to 85% (80% in the Latka version) and BDR efficiency roughly doubled [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @18:24, [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @10:15. If BDRs need to do anything with their leads, "means your model's broken" @12:16. In writing: "Very few companies are so good at marketing that they can survive on inbound." (post 2024-04-18) [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]].
- **2024-05:** "We are a phone sales organization. Over 90% of Opps are booked on the phone." (post 2024-05-28) [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]].
- **2024-08:** Channel bets for 25→100: a food-distributor partnership could be a quarter of 2025 net-new; the inbox is "torched with gen AI", so warm and near-bound intros matter more [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @18:20–27:36.
- **2024-10/11:** On his show, early LinkedIn and the inbox have been "thoroughly thoroughly competed away" [[src-E09-building-repeatable-revenue-engines-at-outreach-and-catalyst]] @16:16–18:19. Owner built outbound from zero (first BDR on 2022-11-01) to about 26 BDRs at a 2:1 BDR:AE ratio, justified "from first principles". He states plainly: "we don't do any AI outbound" [[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @03:07, @07:16, @37:32.
- **2024-12:** BDRs do no lead research. An internal tool auto-loads leads so they can hit 80–100 calls a day [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @31:34–33:40. "I'm not yet a believer" in replacing the BDR team with AI; BDRs produce "probably three times the close one ARR that they did 12 or 18 months ago" [[src-momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy]] @09:12, @11:14. "There's no way you can spam your entire market sustainably through $50 million ARR." [[src-E12-bravados-ceo-sahil-mansuri-on-the-future-of-sales-reps-and]] @64:32. He cites "the list is the message" [[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @19:21.
- **2025-01:** Building outbound only once inbound wanes is "nine months" too late [[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] @26:58. About 95% of AI-SDR use is "pretty crap" [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @01:02. "I generically am not a big believer in AI SDR writ large." [[src-E15-the-blueprint-for-sales-and-marketing-alignment-with-kyle-la]] @42:09.
- **2025-02:** "I'm a big outbound believer". Owner's perfect email is built on a "hypothesis of need" from public data; generic us-centric spam worked 2015–2023 but no longer [[src-E20-building-data-driven-sales-processes-in-the-ai-era-with-jord]] @06:18–07:19, @19:14–26:32.
- **2025-03:** Mobile enrichment took connect rate from 3% to "like 13%", per-BDR output "basically 4X better" [[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] @24:01–25:07. "our outbound team looks like it's got incredible efficiency, but more than half of the ops that they book were former inbound leads." [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @38:22. His outbound guide: up to 50% of theoretical BDR capacity is lost to ramp, low productivity and turnover (post 2025-03-26) [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]]. AI email has "bot breath" [[src-E25-transitioning-from-product-led-to-sales-led-growth-with-andr]] @49:55–50:59.
- **2025-04:** Owner outbound has "been 90% cold call driven"; Owner is experimenting with cold or signal-based email. He DQs vendors who send AI-SDR email he can "smell" [[src-E31-the-rise-of-the-10x-sdr-with-florin-tatulea-of-common-room]] @24:46, @43:31–44:35.
- **2025-05:** BDRs do zero lead research; ~100 calls → ~16 conversations → ~2 meetings a day; "Why would you pay a 250k rep money to make cold calls"; AI-SDR advantage "will get competed away" (Red Queen's race) [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @20:45–27:00, @62:02–63:03. "I'm not into the full cycle rep because I believe much more in specialization." [[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @22:42.
- **2025-06:** "The phone is better than ever." Email moves to the background and is auto-sent. Owner started a project with Jordan Crawford "to do cold email at scale", sent from reps' inboxes. BDRs are being tilted "back towards cold" [[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @05:15–11:32.
- **2025-08:** Owner is "piloting an AI SDR now" [[src-E41-jessica-chiew-of-canva-on-building-ai-native-gtm-org]] @33:35–34:36. Channel mix swung inbound → outbound → "predominantly inbound again"; outbound "floors" keep BDRs on high-value targets [[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @19:24–20:25, @51:07–53:11.
- **2025-10:** The GTM AI lead is building "our cold outbound email infrastructure and internal automations"; Kyle has "earmarked" custom-building the rep stack because SalesLoft and Outreach are built for email. A parallel dialer made reps make "three times as many dials, booking less ops" [[src-all-about-ai-and-gtm-at-clay-round-two-with-everett-berry]] @24:28–26:32. [[person-yg]] He announces 20+ SDR/BDR hires before Jan 5 (post 2025-10-08) [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]].
- **2025-11:** With a new CMO's lead flow "flying", BDR capacity pivots from cold outbound to the new demand; "we're hiring like 25 BDRs in 90 days". If demand doesn't show, "I'm going to take a much bigger swing at at cold outbound" [[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @25:28–28:32.
- **2025-12 (re-dated):** The AI cold-email engine is live: the GTM AI lead "built the infrastructure in like three weeks", and Owner was "sending 1,800 cold outbound emails every single day" [[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @31:30–32:30. Pushing dials makes "decision maker connect rate go down because people are just doing ghost dials" [[src-usage-based-comp-ai-and-the-end-of-the-sdr-tyler-will-vp-of-revops]] @13:12–14:13. Door-knocking is "a little bit of a relic of the past" [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @40:57. In writing, he plans "more redundancy in outbound" [[src-web-2025-12-04-sta-topline-asad-zaman-q-a-qa-kyle-norton-on-scaling-yourself]].
- **2026-01:** A four-XDR AI pre-call-research pilot "made 85% more calls and produced 85% more opportunities" in about two weeks of work; tasks are split into "prime number" units, one agent each [[src-the-claude-code-era-mastering-autonomous-gtm-agents-jordan-crawford]] @39:53–42:57. Jevons paradox: if AI makes each BDR twice as effective, keep the reps and get "2x the funnel"; BDRs generate ~70K closed-won a month at a ">10x" cost-to-close ratio [[src-gtm-strategy-5-insights-from-500-b2b-saas-orgs-jeremey-donovan-evp]] @27:32–28:35, @37:46.
- **2026-02:** The same pilot told again: "we're able to improve the per rep output by 85% of our BDR team", with hourly iteration loops in Toronto [[src-a-product-informed-approach-for-gtm-usha-iyer-chief-customer-growt]] @51:56–53:59. Owner uses AI to target restaurants in one neighborhood with what their competitors are doing ("that competitive FOMO") [[src-cro-life-from-a-20b-exit-to-a-hyper-growth-ai-org-michelle-donnell]] @23:23–24:24. In writing: connect rate "went from 3% to 16%", "BDR productivity tripled then doubled again" (post 2026-02-25) [[src-linkedin-2026]].
- **2026-03-04:** AEs prospecting is "potentially overrated"; a BDR costs a third to half of an AE, so AE cold-calling economics "fall apart" [[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @17:23–18:24, @55:56.
- **2026-03-25 (boundary):** BDRs make 150–200 calls a day and reach 20–30 decision makers, "probably 5X" where they started. eConnect pickup scoring gave a 2.3x lift. Email is centralized [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @03:02–06:12.
- **2026-04 (new):** "Now the economics of a BDR are completely different" [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @09:31. He says he doesn't want an LLM free-writing cold email, only filling fields inside a structure [[src-ivey-future-of-sales-irreplaceable]] @10:23.
- **2026-05 (new):** AI-driven BDR productivity is "pulling forward headcount" by about four months [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @13:09–14:12. "when people say outbound is dead, obviously we disagree with that, but the way we do it is really different" [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @03:01. In writing: "start with the data"; top BDR "$174,000 in closed won ARR" in a month, cold; Owner is "swinging the pendulum back" toward outbound after "90% inbound-driven" periods; fund a GTM engineer with two BDR headcount [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]. Toronto hiring posts still promise "90% of your meetings will be from calls" (post 2026-05-27) [[src-linkedin-2026]].
- **2026-06 (new):** "we've built it on the backs of the phone" [[src-caliber-outbound-objection-navigation]] @00:00.
