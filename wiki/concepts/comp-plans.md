# Comp Plans

> As of 2026-05-27, Kyle's comp view has four planks. (1) Revenue executives should carry little variable pay: he has argued against 50/50 head-of-sales plans since at least November 2022, and by 2026 calls them "insane". (2) Pay is "shifted right": BDRs are paid on closed-won and AEs on customer launch, a change Owner made in July 2024. (3) Quotas should be hittable, and they only go up, but reps share the efficiency gains so they can "smoke their number". (4) Commissions stay, because removing them drops talent density ([[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @24:42; [[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @13:33; [[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @03:12; [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @32:48–@33:48, @68:09–@70:14; [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @27:23).

## Current position (as of 2026-05-27)
*Extended after the 2026-10 backfill, not reversed. The pay-on-launch plank, previously a "black box", is now documented.*
- **Low-variable executive comp, tied to company growth.** "I know a lot of CROs that are on 50-50 plans. I think it's insane." He has "very little variable in my plan today," a lesson from Shopify ("just cash and equity") ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @68:09–@70:14). In Nov 2024 he gave his Owner split as "80 base 20 variable" ([[src-E10-mark-bergen-on-aligning-sales-product-partners-and-mission]] @21:40). By Oct 2025 that variable was "not on the sales number, it is on overall company growth" ([[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @07:28).
- **Why it matters:** a less-levered plan let him propose cutting ~40% of deals that wouldn't produce world-class churn ([[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @35:40–@36:41).
- **Pay shifted right.** "Sales reps, their quotas and their comps all on launched customers, not close one." BDRs are paid only on closed-won, and the launch team is paid on launch and steered by a "growth potential score" ([[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @03:12, @06:26). AE pay is set upfront on a predicted deal value (the eGPV model) rather than waiting for real volume, because "sales reps don't want to wait to get paid" ([[src-how-lightspeed-commerce-built-a-1b-sales-engine-jd-saint-martin-p]] @52:59–@55:00; [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @28:02).
- **Hittable quotas.** "we generally try to set a number that we feel like people can really hit and the top reps can go smoke" ([[src-the-secret-to-high-trust-high-performance-gtm-teams-with-cro-of-houzz]] @60:05). Average OTE attainment was ~138–148% ([[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @22:27; [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @37:42–@40:57).
- **Quotas only go up.** "It's a law of startup physics" ([[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @29:55). He said the same thing in Dec 2025 ([[src-usage-based-comp-ai-and-the-end-of-the-sdr-tyler-will-vp-of-revops]] @41:36).
- **Share the AI dividend.** Owner captures some efficiency in quota increases but shares much of it so reps "smoke their number" ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @32:48–@33:48).
- **Keep commissions.** Only the fastest-growing AI companies can skip them; everyone else will "see talent density drop" ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @27:23).
- **Comp take-off goes to leverage roles.** No "$250,000 BDR": a BDR who could generate that much becomes a GTME or growth marketer; AI-native VPs/CROs are where comp is rising ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @54:25–@56:38).
- **Spiffs must buy new behavior.** A spiff "has to drive some warp" ([[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @22:50). Contests use "wrinkles" (mid-contest rule changes, triple points on the last day) from *Hooked* ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @55:35; [[src-the-secret-to-high-trust-high-performance-gtm-teams-with-cro-of-houzz]] @36:36–@37:36).

## How his view evolved
- **2020 (Shopify, recalled 2024-11-27):** probably Kyle: Shopify's sales team had no variable, then had variable added, then had it pulled for COVID, with "No discernible change in, in active behavior" ([[src-E10-mark-bergen-on-aligning-sales-product-partners-and-mission]] @23:44). See also 2024-02-05 below.
- **2022-11-01 (earliest Owner-era source):** He was already arguing against the 50/50 head-of-sales plan, inspired by Shopify's "100-year company" incentives (base plus stock, no variable for senior revenue leaders). "how can you necessarily think about the business holistically and the long-term if 50% of your comp is tied up in very short-term incentives?" Founders should tie revenue leaders to company growth numbers ([[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @21:37–@26:44).
- **2024-02-05:** In COVID, Shopify paused variable comp and guaranteed full OTE ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @24:42–@26:46).
- **2024-02-23:** Told Owner reps that comp "has not changed since 2021 because that market was silly and we were overpaying" ([[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @26:55).
- **2024-04-18:** Paid above market early for top talent, then ratcheted comp back as playbooks matured. Considering tying rep comp to customer outcomes (EGMV, activation) ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @40:00–@41:05). Paid ~$30K above the SMB market ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @24:30).
- **2024-07 (the event):** Owner moved AE commission from closed-won to *launched* revenue "in July". Pay moved out "between four and 14 days", and deal quality and early churn improved: "Everything got better" ([[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @13:33–@14:38, told 2025-04-16; "last July" in [[src-coffee-scaling-smarter]] @17:06). The rollout was a "red light" change: written doc, video, training, team meetings and 1:1s ([[src-saastr-ai-revops-playbook]] @27:36–@30:42; [[src-getaccept-change-management]] @18:36–@20:38).
- **2024-10-09:** Comp visibility and simplicity: leaders should help reps "see the math"; "Simplicity is a part of the comp philosophy from sales acceleration formula" ([[src-E03-dini-mehta-on-mastering-multi-product-strategy-building-hi]] @34:18–@35:23).
- **2024-11-27:** First disclosed his own split, "80 base 20 variable": "you don't want me on a 50 50 plan. I cannot resist my own urges to make money." Founders tie a first head of sales to a number when "you're going to give them the wrong quota" ([[src-E10-mark-bergen-on-aligning-sales-product-partners-and-mission]] @21:40).
- **2024-12-03/04:** Owner's comp plan is "very much tied to customer outcomes", and 50/50 executive plans are "pretty silly" ([[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @34:40–@36:41). A day later he named the mechanism: "We changed our comp plan recently to make the sales rep 100% only paid when the customer goes live." ([[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @29:06)
- **2024-12-18:** "Why are you putting a VP of sales on a 50-50 plan? They need to think six to 18 months in the future." ([[src-E12-bravados-ceo-sahil-mansuri-on-the-future-of-sales-reps-and]] @50:49)
- **2025-03-12:** Pay-on-launch extended up the chain: the VP and director of sales are "paid not on close one business, but on launched MRR". Paying on retention is "probably a step too far" ([[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @64:40–@66:49).
- **2025-04-16:** Fullest comp philosophy. A plan "has to be fair for the customer, the rep, and the business". "clawbacks suck". Spiffs must "drive some warp". Bottoms-up quota math is "one of the most important activities" ([[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @15:39, @22:50, @54:32, @63:47).
- **2025-05-21:** Spiff design rests on behavioral psychology (the overjustification effect) ([[src-E33-what-it-takes-to-be-a-revenue-leader-in-the-ai-era-with-mike]] @56:02–@59:10).
- **2025-05-30:** BDRs are paid only on closed-won. AE comp includes an ML estimate of payments volume (eGPV) ([[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @28:02–@29:04).
- **2025-10-10 / 10-23 / 10-30:** His own comp is on "overall company growth" ([[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @07:28). He prefers hittable quotas: "If you have an environment where 40 or 50% of your reps aren't hitting quota, then people don't care about quota. It stops meaning anything." ([[src-the-secret-to-high-trust-high-performance-gtm-teams-with-cro-of-houzz]] @62:06). AEs are paid upfront on predicted deal value ([[src-how-lightspeed-commerce-built-a-1b-sales-engine-jd-saint-martin-p]] @52:59–@55:00).
- **2025-11-12:** For new products: "give somebody a number they can go beat" and grow quotas from there ([[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @14:14–@15:16).
- **2025-12-03:** "we give CRO's these 5050 comp plans and wonder why they're optimizing for the quarter." ([[src-becoming-a-cfo-whisperer-revenue-architect-allison-metcalfe-cro]] @51:59). - **2025-12-31:** "quotas go up because that's how startups work and there's it's a you know a law of startup physics." This is the first appearance of the line later repeated in May 2026 ([[src-usage-based-comp-ai-and-the-end-of-the-sdr-tyler-will-vp-of-revops]] @41:36).
- **2026-03-04:** 3–4x per-rep productivity lets Owner set fair quotas and "pay top of market": OTE attainment ~138%, ~80% of reps hit target ([[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @22:27–@23:28).
- **2026-03-24:** No "$250,000 BDR"; comp rises for high-leverage, AI-native roles ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @54:25–@56:38).
- **2026-04-05 (new):** Commissions stay except at hyper-growth AI labs ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @27:23).
- **2026-04-14 (new):** The rep profile moved from President's Club reps "paid top of market" to ~80% new grads ([[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @18:18–@20:20). This changes Owner's pay strategy, though he gives no new pay figures.
- **2026-05-07 (new):** "Quotas only go up," with efficiency shared with reps; 50/50 CRO plans are "insane" ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @32:48, @69:12).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Resolved: the customer-outcome comp is pay-on-launch.** The earlier page called Owner's Dec 2024 "tied to customer outcomes" plan a black box. The backfill shows it was the July 2024 shift from closed-won to launched revenue, later combined with an eGPV deal-value estimate ([[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @13:33; [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @28:02). Retention-based pay is still "a step too far".
- **Pay on launch vs. paid upfront.** He says AEs are paid only at go-live ([[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @29:06) and also that they are paid "up front" on predicted value ([[src-how-lightspeed-commerce-built-a-1b-sales-engine-jd-saint-martin-p]] @52:59). These reconcile if "upfront" means at launch on a model estimate, without waiting for realized payments volume. He hasn't stated that explicitly.
- **"Overpaying" vs. "top of market".** In Feb 2024 he froze comp because 2021 pay was "silly". He also says Owner paid above market (Apr 2024), "at the top of the SMB market" ([[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @37:42), and can "pay top of market" (Mar 2026). His reconciliation is take-home: OTE attainment of 138–148% makes realized pay top of market even when nominal OTE underwhelms candidates ([[src-the-secret-to-high-trust-high-performance-gtm-teams-with-cro-of-houzz]] @60:05).
- **"Top of market" vs. new grads.** By 2026 ~80% of hires are new grads ([[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @20:20). He hasn't said how pay bands changed.
- **"Very little variable" vs. 80/20.** In Nov 2024 his split was 80/20. In May 2026 he called it "very little variable", and since Oct 2025 the variable is on company growth, not bookings. That is consistent unless the 20% has shrunk; he gives no new split.
- **"Quotas only go up" vs. hittable quotas and sharing the gains.** He holds all three. The split between quota increases and rep upside isn't quantified.
- **20X arithmetic.** See [[metrics-and-forecasting]]: the May 2026 $2M figure doesn't match "20X"; the Aug 2026 $3M figure does.
- **Exec comp and the board.** Low-variable CRO comp assumes a board that trusts the CRO. He hasn't said how boards should hold a low-variable CRO accountable (see [[cro-role-and-board]]).
