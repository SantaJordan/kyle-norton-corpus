# Sales Hiring

> Kyle's current position (2026-09-08): most of Owner's sales hiring is now new university grads, put through school in person in Toronto. He says "70%" of hires since the start of 2025 in June 2026 and "80 percent" in April and September 2026. He selects for DNA (hard work, learning speed, organization, a history of excellence) because interview sales craft has "very little correlation" with performance ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @21:48–23:50; [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]]; [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]] (post 2025-08-21)).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- **New grads, trained from scratch.** "I would say 80 percent of our hiring now is like fresh out of college." He hires from top schools and trains them up, while AEs who sold blue-collar/SMB do best among experienced hires [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @21:48–23:50. Recruiting pulls "mostly from... the top business schools in Canada, GTA really specifically" [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @19:19. The written version gives a lower figure: "Since the beginning of 2025, I would say 70% of our new hires have been brand new grads from university", which "required a massive investment in enablement and training" [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]].
- **Potential over tenure.** He prefers "the craft, the hustle, the smarts, the learning motor and the effort". Ten-year reps "haven't worked out as frequently" [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @23:23–25:25. He'd rather have product conviction than "clean MEDDICC vocabulary" [[src-substack-2026-05-17-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook]]. The 2025 hiring autopsy is the evidence base: "Mock demos and case studies have zero predictive value for sales success." [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]] (post 2025-08-21).
- **Hire for fit, and be blunt about it.** Leadership candidates get a "don't work here" video. Owner is "a performance culture", not work-life balance [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @18:02–21:12; [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @48:25. "Transparency is actually the kindest thing you can do" (preview only) [[src-substack-2026-04-09-sales-hiring-has-a-charisma-problem]].
- **References are paramount for leaders.** He does mutual references: director-plus candidates get a list of every direct report from the past 10+ years [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @10:18. Talk to multiple former direct reports, because "You can't obfuscate with your direct reports" [[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @42:44.
- **Self-regulation and AI use are now explicit criteria.** He weighs self-regulation "heavily in hiring and promotion decisions" [[src-substack-2026-04-03-how-to-optimize-exec-performance]]; [[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @84:47. Senior and leverage roles must be "AI-pilled"; frontline managers need less [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @08:06–13:12. His two AI-first interview questions are what you've built with AI for the business and for yourself: "That energy is the tell." [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-05-06). He wonders why new hires don't use AI more [[src-ivey-future-of-sales-irreplaceable]] @04:05.
- **Brand is the recruiting engine.** "Kyle writes a LinkedIn post and then 1,600 people apply". Owner has never used outsourced or outbound recruiting [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @15:54–18:02.
- **The BDR seat is a launch pad.** "the number one objective of a BDR is to become not a BDR" [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @69:16. Internal promotion is now "a little more than 50-50" [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @22:23. Owner plans to "promote 20-30 BDRs to AE roles in 2026" and "just lit up an additional 30 sales headcount on top our original 2026 plan." [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-05-27).

## How his view evolved
- **2018-09-25 (League). The first lesson is pace:** "We saw some early signs of success, and we tried to hire too many reps too quickly". The early team came through his network [[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @09:19–10:20, @16:39.
- **2020-08-17 (Shopify):** Early-stage reps should be generalist "trailblazers", not purely money-motivated. At League he tried to scare candidates off at the end of every interview, because those who opted out "were gonna crumble anyways" [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @44:12–48:28.
- **2022-07-05:** Owner's first hiring post on record is for "two remote Account Executives", pitched on top-of-market comp [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (post 2022-07-05).
- **2022-11-01:** Looking back on League: "We would have gotten to the answer much faster had we shown a little patience and hired more slowly." [[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @16:26–21:37.
- **2023-12-15:** He builds the scorecard before posting a role, treats sourcing like outbound (LinkedIn content as an inbound candidate funnel) and asks the same questions in the same order across three interviews [[src-web-2023-12-15-amplemarket-blog-sales-management-tips-expert-insights-with]].
- **2024-02-05:** He hires remote "people with a very high sense of drive" and tells candidates it's "not a real work-life balance culture" [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @42:09, @48:25.
- **2024-02-07:** Organization and discipline can be taught; growth mindset, resilience and drive can't. Early on you hire "Renaissance reps" [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @14:35–16:38, @28:09–30:15.
- **2024-02-23:** During a hiring push he spends "20 hours of interviews like a week" [[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @25:51.
- **2024-04-18:** Recruit SMB reps from startups one stage ahead that sell to similar buyers; big-company hires rarely work. Pay about $30K above market for top-5% President's Club reps. He spends up to about 15 hours a week recruiting [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @24:30, @50:14–56:30.
- **2024-05-28 / 05-29:** Promote-from-within is already the engine: "promoted 9 sales people in the last 4 months", and the last four new AEs were BDR promotions. To applicants: "Sales is a performance sport so achievement is what will get our attention." [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (posts 2024-05-28, 2024-05-29).
- **2024-06-04 / 08-01:** His pre-interview video says the work is intense: "We're not a work-life balance culture. Like we're here to win." His SMB scorecard is "60 to 70%" DNA [[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @25:57–30:00; [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @46:04, @54:18.
- **2024-09-25:** Stale experience disqualifies: "if you worked at toast 10 years ago, I don't think I'd hire that person at owner." [[src-E01-saastr-founder-jason-lemkin-on-why-revenue-leaders-must-run]] @09:29.
- **2024-10-08:** The biggest hiring mistake is having no system. He uses a DNA / craft / knowledge scorecard with questions mapped to each stage, through a finalist meeting with him [[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @34:42–38:58.
- **2024-10-30:** He opens his own show's first recorded episode on hiring, the topic "I get asked about the most of anything". Candidates get "a 20, 25 minute video of me talking about the role, our culture, the tradeoffs", and offers come with mutual references, about "twenty five names" [[src-E06-untold-strategies-of-high-impact-sales-recruitment-with-asad]] @01:05–02:05, @26:55–30:00.
- **2024-10-31:** AI "won't change who he hires". He "already hires many new grads with upside", and AI cuts ramp [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @21:54–22:58.
- **2024-11-19:** He hires BDRs on DNA (mindset, habits, intelligence, character) over craft [[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @46:51–47:53.
- **2024-11-26:** Hiring posts still say "fully remote" with no office plans, for the January 2025 cohort [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (post 2024-11-26).
- **2024-12-03:** Domain experience isn't required for a VP Sales at Owner; curious learners pick it up [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @10:22–12:30.
- **2025-01-01:** "I think we've only had one AE mishire out of maybe 16." [[src-E14-a-systematic-approach-to-gtm-infrastructure-and-talent-with]] @10:23.
- **2025-01-07. The cost profile changes.** He announces the Toronto office. One of five reasons is lower headcount cost through earlier-career hires, "tripling down on enablement and promoting from within" [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]] (post 2025-01-07). On his show he said it plainly: "if we can hire an earlier career rep, groom them up from scratch, then we can change the cost profile" [[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @53:24.
- **2025-01-19:** A head-of-revenue hire is "two bets": a stage-appropriate leader and a super-learner [[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] @08:22–10:26.
- **2025-01-31:** SDRs and BDRs are hired only in Toronto, five days in office. Management capacity is "usually the place that things fall apart" [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @32:37, @39:54.
- **2025-02-05:** At Owner he first paid top of market for proven performers and now hires earlier-career people and promotes from within [[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @49:50–51:57.
- **2025-05-21 / 05-28. AI fluency becomes a gate for leaders:** "I don't care how good this person is, if they're not in the tools and what I consider AI native, like we can't hire them." Manager candidates with no AI answer "are disqualified" [[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @06:13; [[src-E33-what-it-takes-to-be-a-revenue-leader-in-the-ai-era-with-mike]] @30:01–31:01.
- **2025-05-30:** Recruiting is the CRO's job, not TA's. A mishire costs about nine months. Senior reps underperform 2–4-year reps because Owner is "highly opinionated". A GTA university tour is the new funnel [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @06:16–11:25, @52:44–53:49, @57:54–59:57.
- **2025-08-07. The new-grad shift is already named:** Owner "this year made a big shift to hiring uh mostly new grads into SDR", and "the results have been bonkers" [[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @61:19.
- **2025-08-20 / 08-21. The hiring autopsy:** rep hiring "is not at all vibes and feel. That is programmatic". New grads are ramping faster than experienced SDRs. He runs champion-based (not consensus) hiring [[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @05:12, @20:52, @28:11–30:18. Mock demos and case studies showed zero predictive value, so he now hires "insanely hard workers" [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]] (post 2025-08-21).
- **2025-09-16:** Early hires were all President's Club, one degree from Kyle. Interviews are partly "theater". He plans "100 people" in GTM for 2025 [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @03:15–04:19, @15:56, @25:32.
- **2025-10-08:** "20+ SDRs/BDRs between now and January 5th" for 2026, after about 10 BDR→AE promotions in 2025 [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]] (post 2025-10-08).
- **2025-11-12 / 12-17. Jevons in hiring:** "we're hiring like 25 BDRs in 90 days" [[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @25:28. Bookings per AE dollar are "3x any team I've ever managed before", yet "I'm hiring like 25 people in sales over like a 75day period" [[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @25:22–26:25.
- **2025-12-03:** He poached President's Club reps from adjacent, stage-ahead VSaaS companies ("~11 people from one Toronto company"). "sales craft is actually a lot less important than we thought" [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @38:51, @43:00. On his show the same day: "We are a champion-based hiring environment, not a consensus based hiring environment." [[src-becoming-a-cfo-whisperer-revenue-architect-allison-metcalfe-cro]] @17:17–18:18.
- **2025-12-10:** "I've personally found it difficult to hire senior leaders." [[src-the-new-rules-of-executive-recruiting-in-the-ai-era-asad-zaman-ceo]] @02:03–03:04.
- **2026-01-09:** Open house at the "brand new Toronto Sales Hub" for early-career SDRs/BDRs, with a path from XDR to AE to leadership [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-01-09).
- **2026-01-28:** He plans to regress interview scorecards against how people score once in role [[src-gtm-strategy-5-insights-from-500-b2b-saas-orgs-jeremey-donovan-evp]] @80:40–82:41.
- **2026-03-24 (new):** He goes public with mutual references. Shopify's logo didn't produce great sales talent ("five pound weights") [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @10:18, @34:18.
- **2026-03-31 (new):** "So why do I keep hiring so many salespeople?" [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-03-31).
- **2026-04-14 (new). Stated as a change:** the profile moved from President's Club reps "paid top of market" and hired remote to about 80% new grads in person [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @18:18–20:20. The shift itself dates to 2025 (see 2025-01-07 and 2025-08-07 above). He also said "my views on this have changed" about how much AI fluency each level needs [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @08:06.
- **2026-04-20 (new):** Hiring every net-new BDR and inside rep in Toronto is "One of our big bets this year". He plans "25 XDRs in the next 90 days" and likes hiring marathon runners [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @28:57, @48:42, @51:47–54:51.
- **2026-05-06 (new):** Two questions to hire for AI-first thinking. If you don't know the tools, "You're going to get fooled pretty easily." [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-05-06).
- **2026-05-13 (new):** About 100 sales hires planned for 2026. He built a Claude-based hiring analysis and a bar-raiser copilot (see [[hiring-scorecards]]) [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @41:20, @46:30–49:34.

- **2026-06-15 (new):** About 70% of hires since the start of 2025 are new grads. The cost is that experienced AEs became harder to hire, so Owner mostly promotes from within [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]].

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **The "flip" happened in 2025, not 2026 (largely resolved).** In 2026 he frames it as a reversal: "Every rep hired was President's Club at their previous company", paid top of market, remote [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @18:18–22:23. The new sources date the change. Hiring posts were still "fully remote" through late 2024 [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (post 2024-11-26). The Jan 2025 Toronto announcement named earlier-career hiring as a cost lever [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]] (post 2025-01-07). By Aug 2025 Owner had "made a big shift to hiring uh mostly new grads into SDR" [[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @61:19. President's Club poaching continued for AEs as late as Dec 2025 [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @37:42–40:57. The best reading is new grads for the XDR entry funnel from 2025 and promoted BDRs filling AE seats, with experienced hires shrinking.
- **"70%" vs "80%".** The written Morning Brew interview says 70% of hires since the start of 2025 [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]]. The podcasts on either side say about 80%, both hedged ("I don't know"; "I would say") [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @20:20; [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @22:50. The two may be measuring different windows.
- **Case studies: in the process, then "zero predictive value".** In 2024 the case study was a standard stage ([[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @34:42–38:58; [[src-E06-untold-strategies-of-high-impact-sales-recruitment-with-asad]] @26:55), and he hands founders "my case study that I've written" [[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]] @22:48. The Aug 2025 autopsy found mock demos and case studies predict nothing [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]] (post 2025-08-21). He hasn't said whether case studies remain for leadership roles.
- **"Hire slowly" vs. 25 hires in 75–90 days.** His founding lesson from League is to hire more slowly [[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @17:27. In 2025–26 he hires in 20–25-person bursts [[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @25:22; [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @48:42. His implied reconciliation is that the League mistake was hiring before demand and systems existed, and Owner now has both. He also plans capacity so demand is never short [[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @25:28.
- **AI efficiency vs. headcount growth.** He claims 3x bookings per AE dollar while adding about 25 reps in 75 days, and concedes "it's not like for like" [[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @25:22–26:25. He asks the question himself in 2026: "So why do I keep hiring so many salespeople?" [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-03-31).
- **How much AI fluency, and for whom?** In May 2025 he would not hire a leader who isn't "AI native" [[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @06:13, and manager candidates without an AI answer were disqualified [[src-E33-what-it-takes-to-be-a-revenue-leader-in-the-ai-era-with-mike]] @30:01. In 2026 he says frontline managers need less and "my views on this have changed" [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @08:06–13:12. The bar for senior and leverage roles stayed. The bar for frontline managers softened.
- **Pay top of market vs. change the cost profile.** In 2024 he paid about $30K over market for proven reps [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @24:30. By 2025 cost was an explicit reason to hire earlier-career reps [[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @53:24. He gives a trade-off: experienced AEs got harder to hire under five-day in-office, so Owner promotes from within [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]].
- **"Big-company hires rarely work" vs. Shopify.** The 2024 view that recruits should come from startups one stage ahead [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @50:14 matches the 2026 remark about Shopify's "five pound weights" [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @34:18. That second remark is about his own former employer.
- **Where is the team?** He says the sales team is mostly in Toronto [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @06:09 and every net-new BDR/inside rep is hired there [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @28:57. Yet "close to half of my team is in LATAM and then another 40% is in Canada" [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @29:44, and an earlier LATAM sales-talent attempt "was really tough" [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @11:38. Owner's 2024 inbound-AE post listed Canada, US, Mexico and Colombia [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (post 2024-10-03). Which "team" the LATAM figure covers isn't clear.
- **The Toronto bet is dated inconsistently.** Union Square (published 2026-04-20, probably recorded earlier) calls Toronto hiring a bet "this year" [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @28:57. The office was announced 2025-01-07 and the first five-day Toronto hiring post ran in April 2025 [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]] (posts 2025-01-07, 2025-04-03).
