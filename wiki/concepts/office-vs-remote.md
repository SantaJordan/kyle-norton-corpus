# Office vs. Remote

> Kyle's current position (2026-06-15, the latest explicit statement): every net-new sales hire at Owner since early 2025 is five days a week in person in Toronto, a decision he says he made "midway through 2024" and after which "Basically every single metric got better". Office policy is set from first principles by role, tenure and segment, and he calls remote-vs-office "such a silly false binary". Senior reps and engineering stay remote or flexible, and the company still runs on remote, written, async norms ([[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]]; [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @19:19; [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @09:16–11:20; [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @48:39–53:56).

## Current position (as of 2026-06-15)
- **Entry-level reps are in person, five days a week.** SDRs, BDRs and new associate AEs are in the Toronto office five days a week, because the job is "so input driven and it's so hard to do that job if you're sitting at home in your tiny little apartment" [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @50:47. In 2026: "we went from remote to hiring fully in person, we're five days a week in Toronto" [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @19:19.
- **A graduation ladder.** 5 days (SDR/BDR, associate AE for the first 3–6 months) → 4 days once hitting quota → 3 days as an outbound AE after 18–24 months → fully flexible in the top segment [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @50:47–51:49.
- **Not a moral question.** Decide by role from first principles. "People make it this silly, moralistic decision" [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @52:51. It's about congruence: remote without strong documentation or international hiring gives up connection "without getting all of the outcomes" [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @48:39.
- **The office is where AI gets built with reps.** The applied AI lead (YG, New York) flies to Toronto to sit beside reps in "experiment pods" for "pair prospecting" [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @07:18–08:20; [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @06:09–11:23. The AI lead "in the office" is part of the pilot method [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @15:34.
- **The office is designed on purpose.** Owner "just moved offices for the third time in 15 months or something", into "a purpose-built space that has a bunch of these habits designed into the environment" [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @38:03; [[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @62:21.
- **Run it like a remote company, even with offices.** "You can't really work as an in-off company and and support remote people well", so remote norms are the default and set "a higher floor" for communication and enablement [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @22:33–23:35. Owner is "hybrid but run like a remote company", with offices in San Francisco and Toronto [[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @21:04–22:09.
- **2026 hiring is Toronto, 100% in-office.** The new Toronto Sales Hub (185 Spadina) opened for a January 2026 push of "20+ reps in the next 75 days" (post 2026-01-06; post 2026-01-09). A May 2026 post lists 17 reps starting June 2, Toronto, 100% in-office (post 2026-05-27) [[src-linkedin-2026]]. "we don’t do field sales. No in-person meetings." (post 2026-02-25) [[src-linkedin-2026]].
- **Remote still has a place.** Engineering is fully remote, and senior AEs and top segments are remote or flexible. "It is still way easier to hire people remote" [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @51:49.

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09-28. Pre-remote baseline:** at Vision Critical he ran an office-based inside-sales team in Toronto with a daily 8:30 "fire drill" [[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] (no remote discussion).
- **2020-08-17. Forced remote at Shopify went well:** "when you're forced to be remote like does your did your team implode like my team kicked it into high gear" [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @55:45. Looking back he says he was "not excited about it" after a career in bullpens, studied Matt Mullenweg and wrote a "digital by design" working guide [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @30:41–31:43, and was "horrible at managing remote" at first [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @14:17–15:18.
- **2022-07-05 → 2024-12-05. Remote in every hiring post:** the first post hires "two remote Account Executives" (post 2022-07-05); from 2024 posts describe a "fully remote company and don’t have plans to set up offices" (post 2024-02-13), a line repeated through the 2024-12-05 post, even though that post already lists BDR/SDR roles in Toronto [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]].
- **2024-02-05. Committed remote:** "we're fully remote... We're very committed to a remote environment". As others return to office, remote "will be a differentiator for us into the future". The fix is to hire for internal drive, and "culture is actually less important than values and employee experience"; all Gallup Q12 items except "best friend at work" can be delivered remotely [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @42:09–45:18.
- **2024-02-07:** Documentation matters most "in remote or hybrid teams" where people learn less by osmosis [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @07:17–08:22.
- **"Midway through 2024." The private decision:** written in 2026, "midway through 2024, I decided that I wanted to get back to building in-person teams." [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]]. This re-dates the turn: the public "remote" messaging below lagged the decision by six months or more.
- **2024-08-01. Still publicly remote-first:** no employee offices, ~60% of his team LatAm-based; "most companies do it really poorly. They just transpose what they did in an office and they try to do that online"; only one or two Gallup Q12 items depend on proximity [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @08:10–13:16.
- **2024-12-04. "100 remote... the entire company's history":** speaking from Owner's new San Francisco office, Owner is "staying remote in our like in our foundation" while "layering on enough in-person connectivity to go faster" [[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @64:39–65:39. Two weeks later, as a dad of young kids, a daily commute "would be hard" [[src-E12-bravados-ceo-sahil-mansuri-on-the-future-of-sales-reps-and]] @70:56 (2024-12-18).
- **2025-01-01:** Owner is "a fully distributed team and it's working great." [[src-E14-a-systematic-approach-to-gtm-infrastructure-and-talent-with]] @36:10.
- **2025-01-07. Public announcement of the Toronto office:** all new SDRs/BDRs hired in Toronto in office, reps later "graduate into hybrid and remote", existing remote hires may stay remote. "I've long been (and still am) a champion for remote work." "It’s hard to learn sales and it’s even harder when you’re alone" (post 2025-01-07) [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]].
- **2025-01-29 → 2025-02-13. Remote operating norms stay:** managing is remote and async by default [[src-saastr-ai-revops-playbook]] @15:16–18:25; "we still run the business remote by default because we believe in this written culture." [[src-getaccept-change-management]] @30:50.
- **2025-01-31. The rationale on record (decision already made mid-2024):** "We're still remote first technically", but SDRs and BDRs "are basically only hired in Toronto with the five day in office expectation now". Peers' side-by-side pilots "pretty heavily" favored in person: 20–40% faster ramp, +30% to 2x productivity. He'd have run the experiment earlier. KD was "a big influence". He found early return-to-office advocates unconvincing: their office being better "is like not that consequential because you were doing remote poorly" [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @34:44–44:07.
- **2025-02-05. The Toronto hub as a "bold bet":** all new SDR/BDRs five days in office, existing remote reps stay remote, graduation to hybrid/remote with gates TBD by data [[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @58:13–62:22; "you have to make bold bets even when things are going well or else at some point growth slows" @67:39.
- **2025-03-05. Cost was part of the why:** "if we can hire an earlier career rep, groom them up from scratch, then we can change the cost profile." [[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @53:24.

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **A clear reversal, and the public story lagged the decision.** In Feb 2024 he was "very committed to a remote environment" [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @42:09. By 2026 he says he decided "midway through 2024" to go back to in-person teams [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]]. Yet in public he kept saying remote: StartWell in Aug 2024 (remote-first, no offices) [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @08:10–13:16; hiring posts with "fully remote company and don’t have plans to set up offices" through 2024-12-05, a post that already lists Toronto BDR/SDR roles [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]]; "100 remote... the entire company's history" and "staying remote in our like in our foundation" in Dec 2024 [[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @64:39; "fully distributed... working great" on 2025-01-01 [[src-E14-a-systematic-approach-to-gtm-infrastructure-and-talent-with]] @36:10; and, announcing the office on 2025-01-07, "still am" a remote champion [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]]. Plausible reading: remote stayed the company default while in-person was a targeted bet for new early-career sales hires, but the "midway through 2024" date is a 2026 recollection and the gap is not explained in any source. He frames the shift as evidence from pilots, not a change in values [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @36:50–37:51.
- **"Graduate into hybrid and remote" vs. "100% in-office".** The Jan 2025 announcement and Bravado describe graduating out of the office [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]]; [[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @61:21–62:22, and the Sept 2025 ladder keeps it [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @50:47. But hiring posts from Oct 2025 say "100% in-office" with no graduation line [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]]; [[src-linkedin-2026]]. Unclear whether the ladder still applies to new hires.
- **Why he did it: performance vs. cost.** Most accounts cite ramp and productivity.
