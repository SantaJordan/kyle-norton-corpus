# GTM Engineering (Applied AI)

> As of 2026-09-08, Kyle runs GTM engineering as a centralized **applied AI** team (housed in Data/BizOps) that builds intelligence on top of first- and third-party data foundations and ships it into existing rep surfaces. He now says the typical GTM engineer ("the clay table guy") only gets a company to L2. Reaching L3, a governed GTM harness, takes real engineering and architecture pedigree ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @28:02–39:24). The centralized model is not new: AI was "centralized" in BizOps/RevOps by May 2025 ([[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @07:18–08:19).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- Data first. "Nothing else will really work until you have good first-party and third-party data" [[src-ivey-future-of-sales-irreplaceable]] @05:07. Start with a third-party market map, then first-party call capture [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @02:05–03:06. "start with the data" in writing [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]].
- Centralize. An applied AI team brings work to production, and reps shouldn't run agents. Expert output is "five, 10X better" than a rep's [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @08:07–14:12; "20x better" in writing [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]. "You don't necessarily need an AI czar" [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @36:52.
- Funding: move two BDR headcount into one GTM engineer, or pay a consultant [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]].
- People stack: a well-paid team of "super experts" sitting in the data function. He prefers engineering/data backgrounds over converted RevOps [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @26:26–28:28. "a GTM engineer is mostly like a list builder person". L3 needs architects [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @36:14–39:24. A senior IC "Lead, GTM Analytics & Applied AI" was posted in May 2026: "Applied AI is the job." [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-05-01).
- Method: experiment pods. YG builds a prototype, sits with 2–4 top reps for about four days, iterates 3–5 times a day ("pair prospecting"), and only stat-sig winners become enablement tickets [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @06:09–11:23; [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @07:18–08:20. The first public version of this pod was the four-XDR pilot of Jan–Feb 2026 [[src-a-product-informed-approach-for-gtm-usha-iyer-chief-customer-growt]] @51:56–53:59.
- The function is converging with RevOps into something like a product org ("We're all product thinkers"). Who owns it is still open [[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @11:48; [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @55:47–56:48.
- The next build target is a "company meta harness" or context layer that agents share [[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @05:19; [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @14:27–16:29.

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Distinct or converging?** "RevOps and GTME… quite distinct" (2026-03-24) vs. "overlap so much now… who owns that today is, is a big question" (2026-05-13) vs. RevOps as "a product org" (2026-04-29). - **GTME status downgrade.** In Apr 2025 the GTME was "a new Venn diagram" of Clay skills plus sales acumen. On 2026-03-25 applied AI = GTME ("tomato, tomato"). On 2026-09-08 the typical GTME is "the clay table guy", good for L2 only. Either he now separates applied AI (architects) from GTME (list builders), or he has cooled on the title.
