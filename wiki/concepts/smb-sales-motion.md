# SMB Sales Motion

> As of Sep 2026, Kyle runs micro-SMB sales as "all systems all the time". It's a fully digital, phone-booked, one-to-six-day motion at an $8–12K ACV, staffed mostly by new grads in a Toronto office, with rules worked out from Owner's own math rather than enterprise best practice ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @05:14–10:24, [[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] @31:05). The shape was set early: a "transactional sales cycle between 1-7 days" and a phone-led, blue-collar buyer were already in his Feb 2024 hiring posts ([[src-linkedin-2022-2024]]). The restaurant-specific twist, that owners "don't email", dates to at least Jun 2024 ([[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @36:08).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- Deal shape: "8 to 12K" ACV, a "six-day sales cycle", and discovery, demo and close often on one call. Win rates are above 50% [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @06:15–10:24.
- Every meeting is booked by phone ("90, 95%"). Restaurant owners book and then don't show, so reps call, text, and walk them onto Google Meet on their phones [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @05:14, @19:44–21:48. This is the deskless-buyer pattern he described in 2024: "restaurant owners don't email" [[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @36:08–38:08.
- Digital, not field. Owner has no territories, uses pure round-robin, and does no site visits. "strategic congruence" beats the "no man's land" between models [[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @18:53–23:05. In writing: "we don't do field sales. No in-person meetings." "That's not laziness. It's congruency." (post 2026-02-25) [[src-linkedin-2026]]. Door-knocking is "a little bit of a relic of the past" [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @40:57.
- Transactional SMB is emotional: "excitement is everything", and end-of-call skills (pricing, value stack, asking for the business) drive wins rather than discovery [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @04:08–07:13.
- Selling to skeptical, blue-collar owners calls for radical transparency, a lower-stakes contract (month-to-month), and an explicit trade-off pitch [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @16:45, [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @35:58–39:03.
- Unit economics: ACV is just one input. "if your ACV is 10k and you're closing at 80%, that's way better than if you were at 12k closing at 40%" [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @10:32–11:38.
- The gate to scale in SMB is demand, not talent: "the really hard puzzle to solve is how do you generate the demand economically" [[src-E14-a-systematic-approach-to-gtm-infrastructure-and-talent-with]] @34:06–36:10. AEs run about four booked and three held demos a day: "That's about where things start to crack" [[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @24:29–25:29.
- Fully automate where you can ("If you can, you should"), but Owner's agentic bottom-of-market experience has had "mediocre success so far" [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @09:28–10:32.

## How his view evolved
- **2018-09 (League):** League assumed an SMB high-velocity motion, then had to go "way upmarket"; he would start with "a little less process up front" [[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @30:19–32:29.
- **2020-08 (Shopify):** In a downturn, cut aspirational segments, including running SMB and enterprise at once, before you are forced to [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @30:33–34:45.
- **2022-07/11 (Owner start):** Hiring "two remote Account Executives" for SMB at a sub-50-person company (post 2022-07-05) [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]]. Owner (Series A) is targeting "$10 million in more revenue" from "a couple thousand customers", after narrowing to a core segment [[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @34:55–35:55.
- **2023-12 (written):** An AI call-scoring tool scored calls against Owner's frameworks and "helped us improve win rates from 38% to 53% in a 10 week period in Q2 of this year" [[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]].
- **2024-02:** Owner is fully digital and (then) fully remote, selling to a blue-collar buyer with a "transactional sales cycle between 1-7 days" (post 2024-02-13) [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]]. Month-to-month contracts, data export, and the "choose Bentobox" trade-off. Reps close ~3x competitors' revenue per rep [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @35:58–44:15. In transactional SMB you deliver a number every month, and the business is too transactional for deal dry-runs [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @03:10–04:10, @11:30–13:35. Rhythms cascade down to daily in transactional SMB; "The time horizons are just different" from enterprise [[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @08:22, @20:44.
- **2024-03/04:** "Less reps is better than more reps." Sam Blond's calendar test: without four or five customer meetings a day, "you don't need more reps" [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @12:16–14:18. Recruit reps from stage-ahead startups selling to similar buyers. Customers are "smoking cigarettes on our demos and swearing" [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @50:14–52:21.
- **2024-05:** The sales cycle is "like a week". Expect half of quota in month one and full quota in month two [[src-techto-sales-coaching-adversity]] @16:28–17:29.
- **2024-06/08:** "restaurant owners don't email", so pre- and post-sale run on phone, text and WhatsApp ("deskless buyer") [[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @36:08–38:08. Paid ads don't pencil for mom-and-pop takeout, so restaurants win via search and word of mouth; Owner is remote-first with no offices and ~60% of his team (support, CS, onboarding) in LatAm [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @08:10–09:10, @68:35–72:39.
- **2024-10:** SMB data and fast cycles allow "a very systems engineering mindset" [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]] @08:21. SMB is "an operator's business" [[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @02:11–03:13.
- **2024-11:** Split inbound SDRs (speed and show-up) from outbound BDRs. Use pods and round-robin. Dedicated BDRs beat full-cycle AEs [[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @08:16–15:45.
- **2024-12:** "High probability practices": e.g., don't run bottoms-up weighted commit forecasts in SMB [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @08:14–09:18. ## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **The 38% → 52% win-rate story is probably from 2023 (correction).** In Apr 2026 he tells a 38% → 52% win-rate jump after an end-of-call rollout [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @07:13. In Dec 2023 he wrote that an AI call-scoring tool took win rates "from 38% to 53% in a 10 week period in Q2" 2023 [[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]].
