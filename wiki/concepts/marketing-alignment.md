# Marketing Alignment

> As of Aug 2026, marketing at Owner no longer reports to Kyle (the CMO reports to the CEO, as of Apr 2026). He still wants a revenue-focused marketing partner, one positioning, and a context layer shared across sales and marketing. He credits Adam Guild's early organic-brand bet as a moat ([[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @33:35–34:36, [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @14:27–16:29, [[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @12:19–15:26).

## Current position (as of 2026-08-05)
- Org design depends on the people: "It's more about the people in the seats than it is the org chart". Kyle owned growth/demand gen (never brand) until a new CMO, Wyatt Ozmore, arrived around Sep 2025, and now the CMO reports to the CEO [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @32:33–35:36.
- He values a CMO who is "very like revenue focused" and thinks about closed-won rather than lead counts [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @34:36. Exceptional hires like the CMO visibly bend the curve [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @55:36–57:45.
- Sales and marketing need one shared context and knowledge layer. That's the unsolved problem Owner is investing in [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @14:27–16:29. GTM intelligence and skills should be shared by everything market-facing, from marketing to support [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @31:36.
- Organic brand built where customers live (YouTube/Instagram/Facebook, not LinkedIn) is a compounding moat competitors can't quickly copy [[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @12:19–15:26.
- The revenue leader in vertical SMB SaaS must think like a growth marketer and love the data [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @23:02–25:06.
- Give sales and marketing "one number" and don't fight over attribution: "I'm very anti attribution in general." [[src-E15-the-blueprint-for-sales-and-marketing-alignment-with-kyle-la]] @15:26, [[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @23:42. Never let marketing celebrate pipeline while sales misses bookings [[src-E16-jd-millers-cro-playbook-for-pe-backed-companies]] @47:32–48:34.
- Sales capacity must follow marketing's demand: being short of capacity for the leads marketing produces is "a cardinal sin of a sales leader" [[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @27:31.
- AI buyer discovery is a marketing–revenue job. Dashboard LLM citation share, assign an AEO owner, design the site for two audiences [[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]].

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **He wants a growth-marketer CRO but gave up marketing.** He still plans BDR capacity around the CMO's demand [[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @25:28–27:31. He argues the vertical-SMB revenue leader should think like a growth marketer [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @23:02–25:06 in the same episode where growth marketing moves to a new CMO (@12:27). By Apr 2026 the CMO reports to the CEO [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @33:35. He frames this as being about people, not the org chart, and he hasn't said whether he pushed for or against the change.
- **Organic brand moat vs. paid inbound.** He credits organic brand as the moat [[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @12:19–15:26, but the majority of demand he describes in 2026 is direct-response Google/Facebook inbound [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @20:46. How much pipeline organic brand actually produces is never quantified.
- **Brand is personal or company?** He calls his own LinkedIn brand a compounding asset used for recruiting, and says he doesn't monetize it for Owner because customers aren't on LinkedIn [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @15:54–18:02. He also says brand makes "everything easier", including fundraising and partners [[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @34:55–37:59. Both can be true; the boundary between his personal brand and Owner's isn't drawn.
- **Brand spend figure is soft.** The ~$1M at ~$10M ARR is hedged by Kyle himself. In May 2025 he said "millions of dollars" [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @32:09–34:13. Don't cite either as exact.
- **Anti-attribution vs. channel economics.** He calls attribution "a rat's nest" [[src-E15-the-blueprint-for-sales-and-marketing-alignment-with-kyle-la]] @16:28 and is "very anti attribution in general" [[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @23:42, yet he tracks cost per opp, cost per closed-won and LTV by channel [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @37:19–38:22, [[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @33:44–35:46. His line seems to be: measure channel economics for spend decisions, but don't let credit fights drive comp or org behavior.
- **When did marketing leave his scope? (re-dated).** Brand never reported to him (Dec 2024 purview "not brand" [[src-momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy]] @03:07–04:07). He owned growth/demand gen through at least May 2025 [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @32:09–34:13. The new CMO was "60 days in" by 2025-11-12 [[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @25:28–27:31, so growth moved around Sep 2025, earlier than the Dec 2025 episode that first recorded it.
- **Centralized AI vs. marketing's own applied AI.** Growth marketing has its own applied-AI leader [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @08:20–09:28, while he argues GTM intelligence should be central and shared [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @31:36. The unsolved shared context layer [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @14:27 may be what that split costs.
