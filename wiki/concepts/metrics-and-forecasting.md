# Metrics & Forecasting

> As of 2026-08-26, Kyle runs revenue as a "manufacturing line" (pipeline × conversion = output). He forecasts SMB algorithmically from input metrics rather than from rep commits; he has said since at least January 2025 that he runs no forecast meeting or pipeline reviews. He has added a new worry: AI analysis over ungoverned data gives "super confidently wrong" answers, so he is building a governed "data harness" and a "CRO daily" ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @58:01–@65:09; [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @25:45; [[src-E14-a-systematic-approach-to-gtm-infrastructure-and-talent-with]] @77:33).

## Current position (as of 2026-08-26)
- **Growth math is pipeline × conversion, not reps × quota.** Repeated from 2024 to 2026 ([[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @14:36; [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @25:06; [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @25:45). Reps × quota is "an incorrect narrative" ([[src-coffee-scaling-smarter]] @04:17); "The reps don't produce revenue" ([[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @18:22).
- **SMB forecasting should be algorithmic, with no pipeline reviews.** He prefers stage-weighted forecasting from lead volume, opps booked, carryover pipeline and historical percentages over bottoms-up commits, "at least in SMB" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @12:26). "because we're high velocity, I don't do any pipeline reviews as controversial as that may sound." ([[src-coffee-scaling-smarter]] @18:11).
- **Instrument by segment, channel and cohort.** A monthly MBR grid of every segment × every stage, red/yellow/green ([[src-E16-jd-millers-cro-playbook-for-pe-backed-companies]] @67:13–@68:15); "measurement by segment and cohort or motion is a massive one" ([[src-E09-building-repeatable-revenue-engines-at-outreach-and-catalyst]] @27:41).
- **Data discipline over raw AI.** Control for mix shift (e.g., 70% → 60% inbound) and channel; throwing everything into Claude gives "super confidently wrong" answers ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @58:01–@60:04). An MCP-wired Claude "lies to you" ([[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @22:10).
- **The "data harness" is an emerging category.** He "built a CRO daily in there" (a tool transcribed "Vasco"/"Basco"); ungoverned access risks "people going off with confident wrong opinions" ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @62:05–@65:09).
- **Unit economics and enterprise value over vanity metrics.** ACV is one input: "10k and you're closing at 80%" beats "12k closing at 40%" ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @10:32–@11:38). He proposes payback, LTV/CAC and cost of sales as the roadmap ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @61:50). "AI activity is table stakes. P&L impact is the story." ([[src-substack-2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young]]). His own target is company growth, not the sales number: "how fast is this company growing and how much enterprise value are we creating" ([[src-work-sprawl-and-the-future-of-marketing-with-global-vp-marketing-cli]] @40:48–@41:49).
- **Headline efficiency metric:** closed-won ARR to rep comp. "like a 20X multiple" ($150K reps bringing $3M) ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @01:01); closed-won ARR ÷ BDR comp "well over 10 X" ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @09:31).
- **Track record claim:** "We have still never missed a quarter," though Q1 2026 was "the deepest we had to dig" ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @42:11–@43:15).

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09-28:** At Vision Critical he ran a daily 8:30 "fire drill" covering yesterday's numbers, and brought the A/B-testing rigor of his MMA gym's marketing back into sales ([[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @12:14–@18:25, @51:16–@54:21).
- **2020-08-17:** Crisis playbook at Shopify: calm down first, then gather data through daily dashboards ([[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @14:40–@17:52).
- **2022-11-01:** "your rate of growth is really the only thing that matters when it comes to being successful over time." Founders should tie revenue leaders to company growth, not short-term bookings ([[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @24:42–@26:44, @46:14). Early Owner results: early churn down ~85%, average deal size up ~50% ([[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @17:27–@18:28).
- **2022 (told 2024-09-25):** In his first Owner board meetings, results got worse while he cleaned up bad deals. He showed leading indicators (deal quality, size, pipeline velocity), and Lemkin said they were interesting "but it's not revenue" ([[src-E01-saastr-founder-jason-lemkin-on-why-revenue-leaders-must-run]]). "In my first board meeting, I was basically presenting a declining growth number with the promise of, no, no, no, don't worry." ([[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @05:07).
- **2023-12-15 / 12-17:** "Operate" (weekly report and forecast check) is the most overlooked manager pillar ([[src-web-2023-12-15-amplemarket-blog-sales-management-tips-expert-insights-with]]). AI call scoring (Replayz) took win rate "from 38% to 53% in a 10 week period in Q2 of this year" (i.e., Q2 2023) ([[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]]).
- **2024-02-07:** Teams miss ARR targets mostly because of "bad target setting"; reps do a monthly "math of sales" working back from goal to daily inputs ([[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @28:09–@30:15, @36:28).
- **2024-02-23:** Operating rhythm is "the set of standards that we use to help us know we can hit targets today and in the future." Owner's monthly math confirms reps, marketing pipeline and win rates add up; "if you're not doing that monthly review, you'll just keep getting surprised by stuff." ([[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @01:04, @16:37–@17:37, @39:15).
- **2024-04-18:** Async MBR within four days of month close; "Do not read the deck in MBR"; revenue leaders need P&L fluency ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @29:43–@37:54).
- **2024-08-01:** 2023 results: "two and a half X, burn multiple under one, four and a half to one LTV CAC"; contact quality "only works because the data is good" ([[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @04:06, @11:16).
- **2024-10-30:** Three C-suite metric competencies: churn (including sales-driven churn), unit economics by channel, P&L fluency. Triangulate GRR and NRR because NRR can hide logo bleed. "Can't miss metrics" on the SLT agenda ([[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @17:26–@24:42).
- **2024-11-20:** Owner heat-mapped every segment × funnel stage P0–P4; "the importance of instrumenting every piece of your revenue chain" ([[src-E09-building-repeatable-revenue-engines-at-outreach-and-catalyst]] @27:41, @53:46).
- **2024-12-03:** "high probability practices": in SMB, don't run bottoms-up weighted commit forecasts ([[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @08:14–@09:18).
- **2024-12-25:** Before taking a job, vet the CFO's model against the trailing three months ([[src-E13-scott-leese-on-bridging-the-gap-between-founders-and-vps]] @44:57). He sees the annual plan as the main breaking point between founders and VPs ([[src-E13-scott-leese-on-bridging-the-gap-between-founders-and-vps]] @56:20).
- **2025-01-01:** "we don't run a forecast meeting at all." A bottoms-up model called the month within 2.5% while he was ~30% to number mid-month; he paces daily opportunity targets ("42 ops every single day") ([[src-E14-a-systematic-approach-to-gtm-infrastructure-and-talent-with]] @77:33–@78:35).
- **2025-01-08:** Attribution is "a rat's nest" ([[src-E15-the-blueprint-for-sales-and-marketing-alignment-with-kyle-la]] @16:28). By May 2025: "I'm very anti attribution in general." ([[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @23:42).
- **2025-01-15:** "Don't even spend time trying to problem solve until you can have your baseline of confidence in the inputs." ([[src-E16-jd-millers-cro-playbook-for-pe-backed-companies]] @67:13–@68:15).
- **2025-01-19:** Enterprise is different: scripted forecast calls, surgical systems ([[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] @31:05–@35:19).
- **2025-01-29:** The Revenue Operating System: laddered control towers, rep scoreboards, MBR, target setting; measure inputs you can manage, not only outputs; growth is "the pipeline that you generate, the ability for you to convert that pipeline into revenue" ([[src-saastr-ai-revops-playbook]] @09:09–@15:16, @32:45).
- **2025-01-31:** Annual planning triangulates top-down target vs. a bottoms-up "manufacturing line" model ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @18:59–@23:08).
- **2025-02-05:** Sets his own "Kyle number" ~20% above finance's ([[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @69:50–@70:50).
- **2025-02-19:** Before joining Owner he spent an hour with the COO going through the growth model segment by segment; refusal to share it is "a pretty big red flag" ([[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]] @45:58).
- **2025-03-05 / 03-12:** "more and more, these businesses are manufacturing lines" ([[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @17:00). Channel attribution is hard because "more than half of the ops that they book were former inbound leads"; he tracks cost per opp, cost per closed-won, and LTV by channel ([[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @37:19–@38:22).
- **2025-04-09 / 04-16:** With 400–500 demos a week Owner can model; it delays the SDR call 5–10 minutes on some channels because instant calls lowered win rates ([[src-E28-how-cresta-built-a-category-leader-with-cro-alex-cramer]] @46:51–@47:54). No pipeline or deal reviews in SMB; forecast is programmatic from dashboards; CROs must know the ARR model "cell by cell" ([[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @30:04–@32:09, @39:19–@40:22).

- **2025-08-07:** "the bottleneck 90% of the time is opportunity volume"; slice win rate, velocity and ASP by channel, carry it through to 3-year LTV; the MBR spends about "six of 90 minutes" on what's working ([[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @18:22, @33:44–@35:46, @42:57–@44:00, @55:13).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Win rate 38% → 52%: resolved as one old event.** The 2026 Union Square story ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @07:13) matches the Q2 2023 Replayz result Kyle wrote up in Dec 2023 ("38% to 53% in a 10 week period"; [[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]]). The host's 2026 intro also says 53%. Treat it as a 2023 data point, not evidence of 2026 AI gains.
- **$2M vs. $3M ARR per rep at the same "20X".** May 2026: $150K OTE → "over 2 million" and "20X" (arithmetic ≈ 13X). Aug 2026: $150K → "$3 million", "like a 20X multiple" (arithmetic matches). Either the metric rose ~50% in ten weeks or the May figure was misstated ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @02:01; [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @01:01).
- **Forecast accuracy band widens.** Within 2.5% (Jan 2025) vs. "within 5% to 10%" (May 2025). The two describe different moments (mid-month call vs. before the month starts), so they are compatible, but the tighter number is the one-off.
- **OTE attainment: 148% → 138%.** 148% "last quarter" (Oct 2025) and over ~6 months (Dec 2025); ~138% in Mar 2026. Either Q1 2026 (his hardest quarter) pulled it down or the windows differ.
- **"Never missed" vs. missed months.** Late 2024 he described missing a month ([[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @10:26; [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @35:40). The 2026 "never missed a quarter" claim is about quarters, so the two are compatible, but the framing has shifted from monthly to quarterly.
- **No forecast meeting vs. MBR rigor.** He runs no forecast meeting or pipeline reviews (2025), yet runs a heavy monthly MBR and daily dashboards. The rule is about rep-commit roll-ups in high-velocity SMB, not about inspection in general; he says enterprise needs scripted forecast calls ([[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] @31:05–@35:19).
- **Spreadsheet skepticism vs. spreadsheet mastery.** "That's how the spreadsheet works. That's not how a business works" ([[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @34:20) sits alongside "you need to be awesome in the spreadsheet" for rep hiring ([[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @05:12) and knowing the model "cell by cell" ([[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @30:04). His target is naive capacity models, not modeling itself.
- **Revenue per employee.** He discounts the metric for Owner because of LATAM/Canada headcount ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @29:44), while citing per-rep efficiency multiples instead (20X, 10X, "3x" bookings per dollar per AE). He hasn't said which single efficiency metric he would put in front of a board.
- **Data-driven vs. "dataism."** He calls himself "a data-driven CRO" ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @03:07) but warns against "dataism" and says executives must "scale down" to the work ([[src-substack-2026-05-17-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook]]); "a dashboard is an abstraction" (2025-11-26); see also "gut data judgment" ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @21:33–@26:46).
