# Sales Coaching and Enablement

> As of Sep 2026, Kyle's view is that Owner's real edge is "our ability to change behavior". That means oversized enablement, one change at a time run to ground, learning science (4i, spaced repetition), and AI sims for practice. AI tooling is built centrally so managers can stay on "hiring, coaching, managing to the number" ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @12:26, [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @27:29). The coaching principles themselves are old and stable: one focus, question-based, isolation drills, separate from 1:1s, fully stated by Oct 2024 ([[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @18:53–24:09) and with roots in his 2015 Vision Critical rituals ([[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @51:16–54:21).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- Change literacy is the moat. Owner's tools are "not that complicated". What friends who copy them lack is adoption [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @12:26–13:29, [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @03:06–04:06.
- Change one thing at a time and "finish the swing": concentrate training, coaching and inspection on one behavior per month [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @05:07–06:09, [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @08:15.
- Adoption is a learning-science problem. One-and-done training fails, so the fix is 4i, spaced repetition, Bloom's taxonomy, social learning, and manager reinforcement in a weekly coaching meeting kept separate from 1:1s [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @10:21–24:48.
- "At the end of the day, our entire job is behavior change." The alternative is "paying tippy top market for tenured reps" [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @24:48–25:52. Hiring mostly new grads since 2025 "required a massive investment in enablement and training", including a PhD learning-experience designer [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]].
- Practice happens on AI sims. New SDRs do 15–30 hours of simming on 50+ tuned sims before they touch the phones [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @58:45–59:46.
- Frontline managers do coaching, not AI building. Their first three hours are "chop wood, carry water" [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @24:52. "frontline sales management is one of the most critical jobs in a revenue org" [[src-substack-2026-05-29-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]].
- Remove friction before adding fuel (*The Human Element*) [[src-substack-2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing]].

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09 (Vision Critical):** A daily 8:30 "fire drill" (shout-outs, numbers, a learning), two-week skill themes ("prospecting with posture"), weekly training, and one-on-one coaching as "a really big differentiator". Tools need a training and rollout plan with enablement [[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @33:54–37:59, @51:16–54:21.
- **2020-08 (Shopify):** In the COVID shock, sales rewrote cadences and tone and "coached constantly" [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @09:23–13:35.
- **2023-12:** Written: six pillars of frontline management (sell, coach, hire, operate, build, inspire), with coaching of "at least an hour with each team member" [[src-web-2023-12-15-amplemarket-blog-sales-management-tips-expert-insights-with]]. AI call scoring gave frontline managers "coaching scale" and preceded the 38%→53% win-rate jump in Q2 2023 [[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]].
- **2024-02:** Score rep calls against the script (Replays, Gong). Teach the "why" (reactance, negativity bias). Scripting frees reps' attention for listening [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @08:22–23:57. He checks managers' calendars for coaching sessions separate from 1:1s and how many calls they reviewed [[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @21:45–24:51.
- **2024-04:** Bring enablement in early, even on contract [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @26:33–28:38, [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @15:19–16:20 (a talk recorded March 2024).
- **2024-05:** Most managers avoid coaching because they're bad at it. Flip enablement from 90/10 rep/manager to 60/40 manager/rep. Keep coaching separate from 1:1s, coach through questions, one skill at a time, with isolation drills [[src-techto-sales-coaching-adversity]] @01:02–16:28.
- **2024-10:** The full coaching doctrine: five principles (focus, questions-based, deep practice, caring, reinforcement against the forgetting curve). "So I don't allow my managers to coach in their one-on-ones", never review a call front to back, and the goal is to "become a talent factory" [[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @18:53–34:42. The AI roleplay simulator is built on Owner's calls and methodology ("sort of scary accurate"), and a four-days-to-phones ramp is not "the right thing from an outcomes perspective" [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]] @43:20–45:23, [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @18:51–22:58.
- **2024-11:** Under-investment in RevOps/enablement explains why "50% of your reps don't work out" [[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @20:56. "Biggest needle mover" is a one-lever weekly focus @41:38–42:42.
- **2024-12:** Enablement must serve reps, not tick boxes. Late enablement leaves you "nine months behind" at ~15 AEs [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @15:41–19:54. A competitor with 40 reps and one enablement person has "no consistency in execution"; Owner starts 2025 with 23 AEs and four enablement staff [[src-E12-bravados-ceo-sahil-mansuri-on-the-future-of-sales-reps-and]] @56:07–58:15. One new SDR "did 200 sims before the first time they hit the phones" [[src-momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy]] @17:20.
- **2025-01:** The six-pillar framework is becoming a leadership academy with 7–15 lessons per pillar; "I hate the team lead role" [[src-E14-a-systematic-approach-to-gtm-infrastructure-and-talent-with]] @58:57–64:08. A monthly report card with a rep-chosen "secret sauce" skill [[src-E16-jd-millers-cro-playbook-for-pe-backed-companies]] @71:18. "Everybody should be investing in enablement way earlier than they do." [[src-E18-confessions-from-a-first-time-cro-with-catie-ivey-of-walnut]] @31:51. The SIM is "a better mock call partner than the managers" [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @11:39. Toronto's in-office SDR hub means "tripling down on enablement and promoting from within" [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]] (post 2025-01-07).
- **2025-02:** **Traffic-light change management** (re-dated: first described here, not 2026). A summer 2024 enablement survey showed reps couldn't keep up with change, so red/yellow changes must go through enablement: "you can't push something to reps unless it goes through enablement" [[src-getaccept-change-management]] @04:12–20:38. PIPs are renamed "coaching plans" that managers "fight like hell" to help reps beat [[src-E22-designing-a-gtm-strategy-for-multi-product-companies-with-je]] @75:05–77:12. A Revenue Leadership Academy is being built [[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @45:37.
- **2025-03:** Reps with the most Avara sim time were the best starters in three new segments (he flags selection bias). A manager with seven or eight reps can't give four hours of practice a week. He wants an "AI Iron Man suit" to grade managers against his seven-pillar framework [[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] @22:00–24:01, @56:06–59:09.
- **2025-04:** A "management intelligence layer": know whether each rep is on or off each taught behavior, weighted by win-rate correlation [[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]] @18:19–20:23.
- **2025-05:** "great enablement looks way more like sports training than it looks like uh HR learning and development"; enablement is "criminally underinvested in". Every candidate hears "even if you're good at your job, you're going to get coached." [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @05:07, @24:34–26:36. On 20VC: five enablement staff for 26 AEs and 30+ BDRs, an in-person onboarding week with sims, and phones moved from day 5 to "day 11 or 12" [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @15:34–19:44, @40:22. ROI was thin at six reps but "night and day" at 22 AEs [[src-coffee-scaling-smarter]] @03:16–04:17.
- **2025-08:** In-person SDR ramp "literally 3x" remote on the same program. Owner "significantly underenable[s]" managers, so coaching mastery was redeployed train-the-trainer, directors first [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @63:21–66:24, @72:32.
- **2025-09:** Managers as coaches, with no meetings in the first three hours [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @55:01–58:09.
- **2025-10:** Sims work on discrete chunks, not "sim a demo" [[src-accountaim-revops-ai-advantage]] @06:15–07:17. An automatic "get on track plan" after "two months in a row below 75%" [[src-how-lightspeed-commerce-built-a-1b-sales-engine-jd-saint-martin-p]] @33:37–34:38.
- **2025-11:** "I'm a huge advocate of AI roleplay and AI coaching." New-BDR roleplay went from about two hours with a manager to about 20 hours on "40 to 50 Sims" [[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @59:05–61:08.
- **2025-12:** VP candidates must "coach me as if I'm the rep", on one thing in depth; many failed [[src-the-new-rules-of-executive-recruiting-in-the-ai-era-asad-zaman-ceo]] @53:10–55:15.
- **2026-03-25 (boundary), reversal:** He says he pushed managers too hard on AI (Karpathy videos as "required watching by Monday"): "I've changed my stance there." Managers should stay on hiring, coaching and managing to the number [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @27:29. - **2026-04 (new):** 4i, the forgetting curve, the traffic-light model, enablement "twice or three times bigger" than peers' [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @11:26–24:48, @47:39–48:42. ## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Sims vs. managers.** In 2025 the sim was "a better mock call partner than the managers" [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @11:39, and he explained why: a manager with seven or eight reps can't give four hours of practice a week [[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] @23:00. In 2026 frontline management is "one of the most critical jobs" [[src-substack-2026-05-29-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]]. He also flipped from pushing managers into AI (from at least Mar 2025) to keeping them out of it [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @27:29. The manager's role has narrowed to coaching and inspection, while practice reps have moved to software.
- **Manager-focused vs. centralized (partly resolved).** His 2024 call to shift enablement toward managers [[src-techto-sales-coaching-adversity]] @04:10 did carry through: the Revenue Leadership Academy for leaders started mid-2024 [[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @19:58, and in Aug 2025 he admitted Owner still "significantly underenable[s]" managers and added train-the-trainer [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @72:32. The 60/40 figure itself hasn't been revisited.
- **Enablement vs. hiring.** He frames heavy enablement as the alternative to "paying tippy top market for tenured reps" [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @25:52. Yet early Owner did exactly that: about $30K above the SMB market for President's Club reps [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @24:30. The timing is now clear: the shift came with the in-person, early-career Toronto model from January 2025 [[src-linkedin-2025-h1]] (post 2025-01-07), [[src-web-2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for]]. The new-grad share is given as ~70% (Jun 2026) and ~80% [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @22:50 (Sep 2026); unclear whether that's growth or loose recall.
- **AI scoring every call vs. never listening to a whole call.** He credits AI call scoring with "coaching scale" since 2023 [[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]] but forbids managers from reviewing whole calls [[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @30:29. These fit: the machine reviews the whole call, the manager coaches one isolated skill.
