# Sales Methodology (Owner's named systems)

> As of Sep 2026, Owner runs a homegrown, rigid-but-evolving system: the +PROFIT methodology (built "from scratch" out of top reps' calls), the PCRIOS cold-call structure, NASCAR for brush-offs, and 4i for drilling skills. Kyle defends all of it as "high probability practice", not best practice ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @33:40, [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @00:00). The roots are much older than Owner: the PCRIOS cold call descends from the "checkpoint selling" (credibility, relevance, intrigue) he taught at Vision Critical in 2015 ([[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @06:06–07:08).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- **Build your own.** Off-the-shelf options are either B2C-cringe (Belfort/Miner) or enterprise frameworks like Command of the Message that don't fit high-velocity deals. Owner "had to create our own from scratch" from top reps' clips, and "win rates like climb overnight" [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @33:40–36:42. He has "always aired towards like assembling my own methodology" [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @54:08.
- **+PROFIT ("Plusprofit").** The "plus" is really a lowercase t, for table setting (how you open). It blends ACE/SPICE (Winning by Design), gap selling, Challenger and *Made to Stick* [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @13:28–16:43. O = "offer evidence" [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @36:03. I = "impact math", renamed from "napkin math" so the acronym works [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @37:42. The other letters aren't spelled out in any source.
- **PCRIOS cold-call structure:** pattern interrupt, credibility, relevance, intrigue, offer of value, steps. The pattern interrupt is a "local name drop", and the "I noticed" line creates intrigue [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @03:02–05:11.
- **NASCAR** for cold-call brush-offs: Notice, Acknowledge, Sidestep, Continue, Ask a question, Real talk. It rests on Kahneman's System 1 vs. System 2 [[src-caliber-outbound-objection-navigation]] @01:02–03:08.
- **4i** for skill adoption: introduce, isolate, integrate, inspect, adapted from Matt Thornton's 3i in martial arts [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @20:36.
- **Shared language is enforced.** It's woven into onboarding, deal assessment and Salesforce field names, and Kyle corrects anyone who says "napkin math" [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @37:42–38:43. New leaders must "create within the system" [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @16:43.
- **"Flexibility within a framework."** Script the basics so reps can offload cognitive load, but over-rigid scripts kill autonomy [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @57:13–59:15, [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @17:39–18:43.
- **"High probability practices"** replace "best practices": reason from your own math [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @00:00, @08:23–09:24.

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09 (Vision Critical):** **Checkpoint selling** for the cold call: gravitas, then credibility (a name-drop), relevance, intrigue, then stop and ask questions. Test messaging constantly: "it changes business by business and you don't know unless you test." This is the direct ancestor of PCRIOS [[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @05:04–09:11, @41:01. He had also done Challenger and light Force Management work there [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @54:08.
- **2018-09 (League):** Challenger-style "evangelist" selling of an unwanted change. Adding steps to the sales process lengthened it but raised win rate. Reading list: *Challenger*, *Challenger Customer*, *Sales Acceleration Formula*, *Thinking, Fast and Slow* [[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @06:15–08:19, @17:46, @33:31–34:33.
- **2020-08 (Shopify):** In a downturn, return to fundamentals: Challenger's teach, tailor, take control [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @30:33–34:45.
- **2023-12:** AI call scoring (Replayz.io) graded calls against Owner's own frameworks. He wrote that it "helped us improve win rates from 38% to 53% in a 10 week period in Q2" 2023 [[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]]. **Re-dating:** this is the same event as the 2026 "38% to 52%" story (see Tensions).
- **2024-02:** The **golden script**: everything written down word for word, taught top-down (the script) and bottom-up (the psychology: reactance, negativity bias). His closing framework is question-based. There's also a monthly "math of sales" sit-down [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @07:17–08:22, @20:50–23:57, @28:09–30:15.
- **2024-04:** Scripting is "the most powerful thing you could do". Leaders from inbound backgrounds rarely learn outbound late [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @46:11–48:12.
- **2024-05:** Coaching mechanics that later fed 4i: isolation drills, progressive resistance, one skill per session [[src-techto-sales-coaching-adversity]] @12:25–16:28.
- **2024-10:** The cold-call principles are already PCRIOS minus the S: pattern interrupt, credibility, relevance, intrigue, offer of value. New reps write their own scripts from them (Bloom's taxonomy) because "the process of creation bakes in" [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]] @32:51–35:01. **Re-dating:** the structure was public 17 months before the 2026 PCRIOS naming.
- **2024-11:** He coins (earliest source) "high probability practices": "There is no best practice" [[src-E07-building-a-learning-and-revenue-machine-with-stevie-case-cr]] @14:46. Golden script (learn the core before "you color outside the lines"), plus the local name drop surfaced in Salesloft [[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @37:32–39:35, @43:45.
- **2024-12:** "High probability practices" restated [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @08:14–09:18. Momentum fields audit methodology behaviors reps would never log (close attempts, his own "setup and tie down" closing method, SPICED notes) [[src-momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy]] @17:20–21:25.
- **2025-01:** His "radically transparent sales methodology" (taught "seven or eight years") with "staggered discovery": give value first, not 15 minutes of interrogation [[src-E18-confessions-from-a-first-time-cro-with-catie-ivey-of-walnut]] @58:22–62:24.
- **2025-02/03:** "there's no such thing as a best practice. There's high probability practices" [[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]] @32:16. "I'm on a mission" to replace the phrase, and reps should give "an offer of value", not set a next step [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @21:48–22:53.
- **2025-04:** The "sell-between" pre-call email (an "I noticed that" observation plus a hypothesis of need) audited manually on about 20 calls, with the plan to have Momentum score it at scale [[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]] @06:05–07:06.
- **2025-05:** "Flexibility within a framework"; shared deal language is "the oil that really like lubricates an organization" [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @57:13–59:15. On 20VC: "Scripting the basics enables you to offload that cognitive work", and GTM is "systems engineering" reasoned from first principles (Opportunity Solution Tree), not by analogy [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @03:08–05:12, @17:39.
- **2025-06/07:** "You can't really gatekeep a demo anymore" (likely Kyle): "I've changed my mind on so much sales stuff" [[src-E38-how-adam-alfano-is-leading-salesforce-into-its-ai-era]] @44:47–45:48. Act as a "buying Sherpa" to avoid reactance [[src-pocus-scaling-3m-to-21m]] @26:18–27:24.
- **2025-10:** Momentum measures word-for-word script adherence [[src-accountaim-revops-ai-advantage]] @08:18–09:22. His boiler-room scripting roots are "such an advantage", though "people poo poo it now" [[src-the-secret-to-high-trust-high-performance-gtm-teams-with-cro-of-houzz]] @04:05–05:07.
- **2025-11:** The point-of-view positioning doc and pratfall effect become part of the system [[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @46:36–47:41.
- **2025-12:** The traffic-light change-management model, with "high probability practices" restated as "know the game you're playing" [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @31:25–34:32. *The Sales Acceleration Formula* was "one of the most important books I read early in my sales leadership career" [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]] (post 2025-12-10).
- **2026-02:** Frameworks are collected "like trading cards"; pilot with 3–4 people before deploying to 50 [[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-02-17).
- **2026-03 (boundary):** He names the methodology publicly (Plusprofit). "Language is very powerful and it's very underrated." [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @13:28. PCRIOS is named [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @03:02.
- **2026-04 (new):** 4i is named and credited. AI call scoring found 10 of 12 win-correlated skills are end-of-call ("very much not discovery"). The script is "almost identical today" about two years after rollout [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @05:10–09:17, @20:36.
- **2026-06 (new):** NASCAR, packaged as a Caliber course [[src-caliber-outbound-objection-navigation]] @02:06.
- **2026-08 (new):** O = offer evidence. The methodology is "firmly ingrained", with triggers that flag reps who go off script [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @36:03–39:07. The origin story (built from the reps outward; impact math) [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @33:40–38:43.
- **2026-09 (new):** "I try not to say best practice. I try to say high probability practice" [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @00:00.

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Rigid script vs. letting reps cook (mostly reconciled).** He insists on "a pretty rigid cold call structure" [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @03:02 and word-for-word adherence scoring [[src-accountaim-revops-ai-advantage]] @08:18. He also wants to "let my very best reps cook and like, you know, bring their own flavor" [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @38:07. His stated reconciliation has been consistent since 2024: learn the core before "you color outside the lines" [[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @43:45, and "Flexibility within a framework", because overly rigid scripts have "very like negative motivational consequences" [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @57:13. In 2024 he even had reps write their own scripts from the principles [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]] @32:51–35:01.
- **Two win-rate stories (largely resolved: one event, told differently).** His Dec 2023 write-up dates the 38%→53% jump to 10 weeks in Q2 2023 and credits AI call scoring [[src-web-2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro]]. The 2026 Union Square version (38%→52%, 10 weeks, "a full two years ago") matches that timing and credits a one-skill-at-a-time rollout [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @07:13–09:17. Both are probably the same change: AI scoring found the skills, then the rollout drilled them. The 52% vs 53% gap is recall. E73's "climb overnight" after the homegrown methodology [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @36:42 is still undated and may or may not be the same event.
- **"Not discovery" vs. hypothesis-of-need selling.** The call-scoring data says wins come from end-of-call skills, not discovery [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @05:10. Yet the methodology includes gap selling [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @13:28, and AI pre-call research exists to sharpen the "hypothesis of need" [[src-ivey-future-of-sales-irreplaceable]] @11:23. Older sources suggest how this fits: discovery was "staggered" (value first, then questions) [[src-E18-confessions-from-a-first-time-cro-with-catie-ivey-of-walnut]] @59:22, the hypothesis of need moved into the pre-call "sell-between" email [[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]] @06:05, and "You can't really gatekeep a demo anymore" [[src-E38-how-adam-alfano-is-leading-salesforce-into-its-ai-era]] @45:48. Discovery was compressed and front-loaded, not dropped.
- **Incomplete acronym.** Only t (table setting), I (impact math) and O (offer evidence) are defined in the sources. P, R, F and the final T are never stated.
- **Anti-"best practice" while selling a framework.** He rejects imported best practices, yet packages NASCAR as a universal course ("the same principles of psychology" across industries) [[src-caliber-outbound-objection-navigation]] @04:11. He half-acknowledged this in 2025: "I jokingly call them high probability practices" [[src-E35-building-a-multi-product-platform-with-seismics-cro-hayden]] @55:21 (probable Kyle).
- **Imported frameworks then vs. now.** Early in his career he leaned on Challenger and Force Management [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @54:08, [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @30:33–34:45. By 2026 off-the-shelf methodologies "didn't fit" [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @33:40, though Challenger still feeds +PROFIT [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @13:28. It's an evolution from adopting to assembling, not a reversal.
