# AI SDRs and Agents

> As of 2026-09-08, Kyle runs AI on the outbound engine centrally (reps no longer send cold email; Owner buys OneMind avatars) and calls AI SDRs "good and important tools", but he still says an AI SDR should not be the first AI project and that chained generative agents without human checkpoints produce slop ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @16:39–17:41, [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @17:29). The AI cold-email engine itself is not new: it was sending 1,800 emails a day by Dec 2025 ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @31:30–32:30).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- AI SDRs are legitimate tools, and Owner uses OneMind for some of this work. Leading with an AI SDR on the homepage as your first AI project, though, makes reps fear for their jobs. His team "isn't fearful of AI" because AI has only made them more successful [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @16:39–17:41. The "wrong first project" framing dates to Oct 2025: "Most companies are implementing AI backwards." [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]] (post 2025-10-21).
- Owner's reps no longer send cold email. It is "all just blasted out through our cold AI generated email tooling". Cold email went from ~0.5% to ~5% of the qualified-lead funnel once a manager became the single-threaded owner [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @17:29–18:30. The engine was built by the GTM AI lead in about three weeks in 2025 [[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @31:30.
- Agents run centrally, not at the rep level: "I actually don't really want the reps running agents per se" [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @13:11. This has been his line since at least May 2025 ([[src-saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin]] @07:18–08:19) and Nov 2025 ([[src-gtm-ai-advice-from-a-salesforce-siebel-veteran-peter-grant-cro-y]] @57:01). Humans still make the calls. AI does the scoring, pre-call research and routing around them [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @10:24–12:26, @24:52–26:59.
- He prefers deterministic scaffolding with a few generative steps. "Lossiness" compounds across chained generative steps, so put a human checkpoint in before too many are stacked [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @30:29–33:33; first aired Sep 2025 [[src-E45-the-inside-scoop-on-gtm-engineering-with-clays-everett-berr]] @16:17–18:20. "You don't need an agent at every single step of the way" [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @12:43.
- Break work into atomic tasks, one agent each: "what is the prime number" of the job [[src-the-claude-code-era-mastering-autonomous-gtm-agents-jordan-crawford]] @39:53–40:54.
- AI avatars (OneMind) are being repurposed to "sell a full deal" to a customer segment that reps can't serve economically [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @57:43–60:48. Fully automating SMB sales is right "If you can". Owner's agentic bottom-of-market experience has had "mediocre success so far" [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @09:28–10:32.
- AI makes reps more valuable, not fewer (Jevons paradox): "I'm pulling forward headcount" [[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @38:41–39:43; on the SDR going away, "we haven't seen that at all yet" [[src-gtm-strategy-5-insights-from-500-b2b-saas-orgs-jeremey-donovan-evp]] @59:13.
- Agent sprawl is the new risk: RevOps, growth marketing and sales leaders all build agents that collide and send customers duplicate emails [[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @31:29–33:38.
- On vendor agents: the agent "is not the smart part". Vendors sell a bundle of model + harness + memory, and that bundle is what you should evaluate (preview only) [[src-substack-2026-05-26-dont-buy-your-gtm-brain]].

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Clear reversal on AI outbound, dated earlier than the page first had it.** "we don't do any AI outbound" (2024-11-19) became an AI cold-email engine sending 1,800 emails a day by Dec 2025. The steps are on record: auto-sent cadences and the Jordan Crawford project (June 2025), "piloting an AI SDR" (Aug 2025), YG building the infrastructure (Oct 2025), "AI SDR" as a production use case and 1,800/day (Dec 2025). The Aug 2026 "reps don't send emails" line is a public restatement, not the moment of change.
- **Four months apart:** "I don't personally want to just let AI write my cold outreach" (2026-04-23) vs. emails "blasted out" by AI tooling (2026-08-05). They can be reconciled if the tooling only fills fields inside a fixed template, which is the structure he described at Ivey. He has not said so explicitly.
- **Sender identity unclear.** In June 2025 AI cold email would "come from the inboxes of the sales rep". By Dec 2025 the engine needed outside help on "domain infrastructure", and in Aug 2026 reps "don't send emails". He has not said whether the mail still goes out under reps' names.
- **OneMind one day apart:** "transformational" for selling full deals (2026-03-24) vs. the agentic bottom-of-market experience having "mediocre success so far" (2026-03-25). These may be different experiences, or different stages of the same one.
- **Lemkin endorsement vs. his own skepticism.** The softening started in Dec 2025 ("changed my thinking"), not in 2026. He praises Lemkin tuning AI BDRs while still calling AI-SDR-first a trust mistake (Oct 2025 and Sep 2026). His actual point is narrower: do the cold-start work by hand before automating.
- **The rep's role.** "I don't actually think the, the, the role of a rep is going to change that much." ([[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @31:34, Dec 2024) sits awkwardly with "Roles I would have hired for 18 months ago don't exist on my org chart anymore" (Apr 2026), "the death of the administrator" ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @06:21) and the unbundling of jobs into agent tasks. His consistent line across 2025–2026 is Jevons: more valuable reps and more hiring, not fewer.
