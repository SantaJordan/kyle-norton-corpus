# Recognition & Motivation

> Kyle's current position (2026-09-01): recognize effort and personal bests, not just outcomes ("Recognize what you want repeated"), so the team isn't "emotionally hostage to variance". Motivation is "manufactured" through rituals, and before adding "fuel" (comp, contests, PIP threats) you should remove friction ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @40:07–43:15; [[src-substack-2026-05-29-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]]; [[src-substack-2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing]]).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-01)
- **The base layer is intrinsic, and it is old.** His engagement model is CRAMPS (community, recognition, autonomy, mastery, purpose, steps), an extension of Daniel Pink, plus promoting from within [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @68:26–70:28; [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @54:52–56:53. The aim is to "stoke the fire within somebody as opposed to like lighting a fire behind them" [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @65:24. The 2026 effort-and-friction framing below builds on this; it does not replace it.
- **Recognize inputs and personal bests.** Quota shout-outs spotlight what went into the result. Town halls include a slide for everyone with a personal-best month: "recognize everybody who set a personal best" [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @40:07–42:11. "If the only thing you recognize is the final number, your culture becomes emotionally hostage to variance" [[src-substack-2026-05-29-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]].
- **The norm polices itself.** A new manager who wrote an outcomes-only shout-out would be corrected by peers [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @42:11.
- **Effort recognition is why Owner survived slumps.** He prepared the team for a slump. Q1 2026 was "the deepest we had to dig", and "We have still never missed a quarter" [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @42:11–43:15.
- **Rituals manufacture motivation.** "12 Super Bowls a year" in a month-to-month business, with a first-of-month Othership (sauna and cold plunge) outing and group Dreamfuel visualizations [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @11:17–12:21. The "Kyle number" is a stretch target beyond the financial plan [[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @54:07–55:08.
- **Contests are deliberately designed.** SPIFFs and contests use "wrinkles" (mid-contest rule changes, triple points on the last day) from *Hooked* [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @55:35. Owner built its own recognition and contest system because it is "not that central" [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @38:16.
- **Friction before fuel.** Leaders pile on "fuel" (comp, incentives, PIP threats) but rarely remove friction [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @52:57–55:00. "Most revenue leaders I know, myself included, reach for fuel first" [[src-substack-2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing]].
- **Money, promotion and AI as motivators.** Quotas "only go up", but Owner shares much of the AI efficiency so reps "smoke their number" [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @32:48–33:48. "the number one objective of a BDR is to become not a BDR" [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @69:16. "my team isn't fearful of AI because AI has only done one thing, make them more successful and more money" [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @17:41.

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09-28. Earliest view (Vision Critical):** a leader's mood matters. Positivity, encouragement and recognition beat "high pressure, maximum accountability" management, and "people are people": not everyone is money-hungry [[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @45:09–48:12.
- **2020-08-17. Servant leadership (Shopify):** coin-operated, disposable-rep management is "a legacy of a past time". Engagement comes from trusting your boss, believing in the mission, learning, career path and values [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @34:45–37:54. On retention: "I want to win you over every month" [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @50:33–55:45.
- **2024-02-05:** Engagement over "fun": Gallup Q12, development roadmaps for every rep, lots of internal promotions, hyper-transparency, and a team eNPS of about 87 [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @44:15–45:18.
- **2024-02-07:** A monthly "math of sales" sit-down in which each rep works back from their goal to daily inputs. A "Stoic squad" health and fitness channel [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @28:09–31:17.
- **2024-02-23:** Engagement comes from Gallup Q12-type drivers (mission, trust, transparency, ability to succeed, learning, career path). The key system is coaching, and promoting from within is "80% of the battle" [[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @21:45–24:51.
- **2024-08-01:** Only one or two Q12 items depend on proximity. His favorite part of the job is telling people they're promoted [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @11:14–13:16, @50:13–53:16.
- **2024-10-08:** Elevate the senior IC track: top sellers are recognized, well paid and included in product strategy. At a prior company he gave a top rep a director title [[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @16:46–18:53.
- **2024-10-09 (own show):** The constant top driver of engagement is "continuous ongoing recognition and not like a recognition program" (*The Power of Moments*, Gallup Q12), and "it doesn't need to be just about the money." [[src-E03-dini-mehta-on-mastering-multi-product-strategy-building-hi]] @29:14–30:14.
- **2024-10-31:** "growth is the only thing that matters. You are optimizing for getting better as quickly as you can" [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @38:35.
- **2024-11-19:** "the number one objective for any BDR is to stop being a BDR as fast as possible" [[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @12:33.
- **2024-11-27 (own show; unlabelled line, most likely Kyle):** at Shopify, pulling reps off variable comp during COVID made "No discernible change in, in active behavior". "Find the intrinsic motivators" for junior reps [[src-E10-mark-bergen-on-aligning-sales-product-partners-and-mission]] @23:44.

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Effort vs. outcome.** He champions effort and personal-best recognition [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @40:07, yet the culture runs on outcome-centric frames: "can't miss mentality" [[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @31:46, the "Kyle number" stretch target [[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @54:07, and "Quotas only go up" [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @29:55. He doesn't address how the two coexist.
- **Fuel vs. friction.** In Sept 2026 he says remove friction "before you add fuel" [[src-substack-2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing]]. But in May 2026 he described building a contest system and contest "wrinkles" [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @38:16; [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @55:35. He acknowledges the habit ("myself included"). The friction-first theme itself goes back to 2025 [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @28:20–29:22.
- **"Never missed."** "We have still never missed a quarter" [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @43:15; [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @69:12. That fits monthly misses: "every month, but one for the last 20 months" [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @35:40, and the first monthly miss "a couple of months ago" in Oct 2024 [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @10:26–11:30. The claim is quarterly, not monthly.
- **Sharing AI gains.** "Quotas only go up", but Owner shares much of the efficiency so reps "smoke their number" [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @32:48–33:48. He hasn't said how much is shared.
- **Recovery.** He calls the guest's "serotonin day" "a nightmare to me" while endorsing recovery as part of the operating model [[src-substack-2026-05-29-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]]. See [[executive-performance-and-mindset]].

- **Intrinsic vs. extrinsic (new tension from older evidence).** Since 2015 he has said not everyone is money-hungry [[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @45:09, and in 2025 he steered managers off carrots and sticks toward internal locus of control [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @64:21; [[src-E33-what-it-takes-to-be-a-revenue-leader-in-the-ai-era-with-mike]] @56:02. Yet he runs spiffs with wrinkles, a stretch "Kyle number", and pitches AI as making reps "more money". He reconciles the two partly through spiff design (avoid the overjustification effect, reward incremental "warp"), but he has never squared them explicitly.
- **Effort vs. outcome, partly resolved.** The older evidence shows how he keeps outcome targets motivating: set quotas most reps can hit, because if 40–50% miss "people don't care about quota" [[src-the-secret-to-high-trust-high-performance-gtm-teams-with-cro-of-houzz]] @60:05–62:06. The tension that remains is with "Quotas only go up".
- **Dreamfuel-era ideas predate Dreamfuel.** Contest "wrinkles" and anticipatory dopamine were already in his Oct 2025 vocabulary [[src-the-secret-to-high-trust-high-performance-gtm-teams-with-cro-of-houzz]] @28:29–37:36. The 2026 Kevin Bailey episodes codify existing practice more than they change it.
