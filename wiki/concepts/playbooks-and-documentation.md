# Playbooks and Documentation

> As of Sep 2026, documentation for Kyle has grown from writing everything down (scripts, operating rhythms) into living context: a shared GTM harness and business-context files (the Gardener) that both humans and agents read. Shared language remains non-negotiable ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @28:02–30:06, [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @38:43).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- Document and standardize so everyone improves one system: "innovating our system" rather than everyone innovating their own [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @16:38, [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @16:43.
- Shared language is reinforcement. The methodology is woven into onboarding, deal assessment and Salesforce field names [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @37:42–38:43. "Language is very powerful and it's very underrated." [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @13:28.
- Roll changes out by size. Green-light changes go out by Slack. Red-light changes need many channels (video, Guru, WorkRamp, town hall). "The problem is people try to roll out red light changes with a slack message." [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @18:35–19:36.
- Docs are becoming context for AI. The VP of RevOps's "Gardener" maintains business context from Slack, Notion and calls, and GTM context is consolidating in GitHub [[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @49:12–50:15. The L3 "GTM harness" shares ICP, playbook and stage definitions with every agent [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @28:02–30:06.
- The context layer is still unsolved, because model outputs get "locked in Slack" [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @14:27–16:29.
- Count how many people exist "to keep the wiki honest" [[src-substack-2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing]].
- Prescriptive basics, freedom above them: script the fundamentals so reps can think about higher-order tailoring ("Scripting the basics enables you to offload that cognitive work." [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @17:39), but keep "flexibility within a framework" [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @57:13–59:15. He says "high probability practices", never "best practices" [[src-E07-building-a-learning-and-revenue-machine-with-stevie-case-cr]] @14:46.
- Good documentation is now an AI prerequisite: "the winners of AI are people with like structured businesses and good data and process and documentation" [[src-how-lightspeed-commerce-built-a-1b-sales-engine-jd-saint-martin-p]] @50:56.
- (Unchanged in direction; the new sources mainly extend the history back to 2015 and fill in 2024–2025.)

## How his view evolved
- **2015-09 (Vision Critical):** Learn from experts and don't reinvent the wheel; even young reps given good systems still try to. On process: "it changes business by business and you don't know unless you test." [[src-hunter-craft-radio-vision-critical]] @23:33–26:37, @41:01.
- **2018-09 (League):** "If you're designing process for the sake of process, for the sake of oversight, you're doing it wrong." In hindsight he'd have had "a little less process up front" [[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @28:10, @31:24. Looking back in 2025, League had little repeatability: "I was personally involved in all of the biggest deals." [[src-E28-how-cresta-built-a-category-leader-with-cro-alex-cramer]] @39:35.
- **2020 (Shopify; told 2025-08):** When Shopify went permanently remote he wrote a "digital by design" working guide (Slack vs phone vs Notion, response times) [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @30:41–31:43.
- **2024-02 (also):** "the secret sauce to operating rhythms is what's the system that you can give to your frontline leaders so that they can execute almost as well as you can execute". Monthly plans are built from a Notion "leadership command center" [[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @14:31, @28:58–36:12.
- **2024-02:** Document everything, especially in remote teams: the demo philosophy, component training, and a word-for-word script. Docs must be measured and audited. Use AI to draft: record a session, export the transcript, have ChatGPT write the guide (it gets "70% of the way there") [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @07:17–10:26.
- **2024-03 (Latka keynote, same deck as the Apr 2024 SaaStr talk):** Documentation is one of three Pareto habits, done with ChatGPT at "like a two to four X higher rate" [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @16:20–19:22.
- **2024-04:** Documentation is one of three operating tools, alongside the MBR and performance management. The AI drafting hack makes content 2–4x faster [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @29:43, @36:52.
- **2024-10 (also):** "you need structure, you need artifacts and systems that can help people be successful so that they're not reliant on a person." Put v1 on paper even if it's bad [[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @04:17–06:23. On his own show, reps write their own scripts from his cold-call principles, because "the process of creation bakes in" [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]] @32:51–35:01.
- **2024-10:** The **Revenue OS**: a measurement cadence, then a light planning rhythm, then team operating rhythms. Notion templates are shared on Substack, and rituals get names (MAPLE, "can't miss metrics"). You can't add headcount confidently without systems [[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @08:08–09:10, @24:42–29:52.
- **2024-11:** "I am on a on a uh mission to document everything a manager does" [[src-E07-building-a-learning-and-revenue-machine-with-stevie-case-cr]] @39:45. - **2024-12:** He is writing a GTM engine doc "all the way from mission to playbooks", and Owner is "a hundred percent no slides" [[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @19:21, @33:12. ## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Static docs vs. living context.** In 2024 the emphasis was human-authored, audited docs and scripts. In 2026 he favors self-maintaining context files and treats a big documentation team as a warning sign [[src-substack-2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing]]. He hasn't said whether Owner's own enablement docs (Guru, WorkRamp, the LXD playbook) are moving to the Gardener or GitHub.
- **AI drafting: speed-up or slop?** In 2024 ChatGPT got docs "70% of the way there" [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @10:26. In 2026 the output has "bot breath" unless "run to ground" [[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @24:27–25:28. That's consistent if the last 30% is where the work is. He also says "all the value is in the last 20%" [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @19:31.
- **Is the context layer solved?** In Apr 2026 the Gardener is presented as working [[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @49:12. By Aug 2026 the shared context layer is "unsolved" [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @14:27–16:29. In Sep 2026 Owner is "actively building" L3 and is "not masters" of it [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @28:02–30:06. Read the Gardener as one part of the context layer, not the whole of it.

- **Document everything vs. too much documentation (resolved).** In Nov 2024 he was on a "mission to document everything a manager does" [[src-E07-building-a-learning-and-revenue-machine-with-stevie-case-cr]] @39:45. By Feb 2025 reps said there was "too much documentation" [[src-getaccept-change-management]] @14:29. His fix wasn't writing less but making it findable: role knowledge moved to Guru, answerable from Slack, with fewer surfaces [[src-E43-geraldine-maccarthy-cro-of-oyster-hr-demystifies-distribut]] @56:13–57:14. The 2026 Gardener and context files continue that line.
