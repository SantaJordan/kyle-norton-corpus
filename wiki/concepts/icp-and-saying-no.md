# ICP and Saying No

> As of Aug 2026, Kyle's view is that the right ARR beats more ARR: narrow the ICP, let bad-fit buyers disqualify themselves, and treat misfit pipeline as "fake" ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @35:02). The new sources push the start of this view back to 2018 (League loss interviews) and 2020 (Shopify: cut aspirational segments), and show it in action at Owner from his first months in 2022 ([[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @34:55). It hasn't reversed since.

## Current position (as of 2026-08-05)
- Buyers without fit should disqualify themselves early. Pipeline that sticks around without fit "was fake" [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @35:02.
- Saying no is the CRO's job because "your job is enterprise value". That justifies closing fewer deals in the short term [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @13:32–14:40.
- ICP should be tightened with data. Owner did it repeatedly: in his first 30–60 days (2022), by raising the EGMB score floor (1,500 → 2,000 → 2,500, already by Mar 2024), and again in 2024 by raising the GPV floor where E-Win/E-Churn models showed churn break-points. It's "easier to enforce on outbound" [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @06:10–08:19, [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @06:08–07:12.
- Outbound lists are tiered and floored, not just targeted. Tier C "is who you will never close" [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]. Outbound "floors" are raised via the lead rotator toward "the highest expected value actions" [[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @51:07–53:11.
- Off-ramp bad fits on the call by stating the tradeoff ("less choice and flexibility" for more money) [[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @48:15–53:29, and disqualify honestly with the math, naming the better-fit tool [[src-pocus-scaling-3m-to-21m]] @24:12–26:18.
- Do the ICP work yourself ("eat your vegetables") before you layer AI on top [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @36:00–39:11. ICP is part of the shared context in a GTM harness [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @28:02–30:06.
- Comp design makes saying no possible. A less-levered plan let him propose cutting almost 40% of deals [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @35:40–36:41.

## How his view evolved
- **2018-09 (League):** About 30 late-stage loss interviews run by non-salespeople fed strategy changes and a move upmarket. League had assumed an SMB high-velocity motion, then had to go "way upmarket" [[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @13:29–15:34, @30:19–32:29.
- **2020-08 (Shopify):** Early-stage companies should narrow focus on the buyers who see their value and sell to change agents who share their worldview. "winter is coming": cut aspirational segments and geographies before you are forced to [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @23:06–27:20, @30:33–34:45 (same DMZ chat as [[src-dmz-chat-shopify-2020]]).
- **2022-11 (earliest Owner evidence):** About five months in, he describes narrowing TAM by roughly 90%, from about 300,000 US independent restaurants to a core of "about 10,000. Yeah, I call it 30,000". The CEO had spotted a lower-churn, higher-performing segment, while League had taken any deal that could "fog the glass". He credits patience with cutting early churn ~85% and lifting deal size ~50% [[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @17:27–18:28, @33:54–37:00. [[person-adam-guild]]
- **2024-02:** At League, over-funding pushed the company upmarket too early: "every deal was brute force" [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @06:07–07:07. Point-of-view positioning gets "the wrong people out of your funnel fast" @16:26.
- **2024-03/04 (Latka talk, SaaStr talk):** "Say no" is pillar one: pause the growth plan until churn is fixed, and nail one niche [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @05:03, @11:12–16:19. In the shorter Latka version he says they turned away 40% of deals that would have closed "30 days before I got there", raised the EGMB floor three times, and lifted the share of deals in the healthy cohort from "40, maybe 50%" to about 80%. Outbound always gets a higher quality threshold than inbound [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @05:07–09:14.
- **2024-06:** The ICP is one to "six, seven locations": "just restaurants, just take-out and delivery centric concepts, just the U.S.", with no plans to go upmarket [[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @17:37–20:46.
- **2024-10:** "half or more of churn is probably customer quality" [[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @10:13. He slowed growth intentionally right after joining and tightened again later, which led to his first missed month after 18 straight [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @09:21–11:30. On his own show, point-of-view positioning "makes it very clear who should opt in and opt out" [[src-E05-mark-niemiec-on-steering-business-transformation]] @51:02–52:04.
- **2024-12:** He cut almost 40% of closed deals on churn-quality grounds, which the less-levered comp plan made possible [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @36:41. He cites Jordan Crawford: "the list is the message" [[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @19:21. [[person-jordan-crawford]]
- **2025-02:** "There's like 500,000 independent restaurants and we narrowed that down to our top 30,000", using ML models: "we did hardcore picking, but certainly easier in vertical SaaS than horizontal" [[src-E20-building-data-driven-sales-processes-in-the-ai-era-with-jord]] @54:03–55:04.
- **2025-03:** Few leaders who own both sales and CS will "actually give up revenue, if it means setting better expectations, selling into a tighter ICP, being better about handoff mechanisms and spending money post-sales" [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @63:33.
- **2025-05:** He reframes saying no as enterprise value (the VP Sales → CRO shift), with ~30% early churn as the trigger [[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @10:19–16:28. Narrow ICP early: "anybody who can fog a mirror" customers churn and make you uninvestable [[src-coffee-scaling-smarter]] @24:39–25:40. ICP is restaurants doing roughly 500K–5M, and wrong-fit customers opting out shortens cycles because "time to lose" is the drag [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @35:15–38:19, @43:23.
- **2025-06:** Companies pick growth vectors badly through "reasoning by analogy" (copying competitors going upmarket) [[src-E37-jason-wolf-on-uncovering-hidden-growth-opportunities-in-go-t]] @09:12.
- **2025-07:** He describes the two tightenings (2022 and 2024), now driven by ML models [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @06:10–08:19. "You really have to get way more dialed in on the ICP than you are today." [[src-pocus-scaling-3m-to-21m]] @16:54.
- **2025-08:** Outbound "floors" set and raised via the lead rotator; larger targets book and win less but at higher ASP, so find "the top of that hill" [[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @51:07–53:11.
- **2025-09:** "like 30% of the customers that we could have closed previously, we were saying no to now" [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @12:46–13:48.
- **2025-10:** Owner has repeatedly shrunk its ICP or "added friction to the buying journey" to improve first-90-day retention [[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @02:06.
- **2025-11:** In crowded markets, take a sharp point of view for one segment "even though it means alienating potentially other parts of the market" [[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @20:45–21:49.
- **2025-12:** In a written Q&A, part of his plan for a bigger target is stretching to mid-market customers [[src-web-2025-12-04-sta-topline-asad-zaman-q-a-qa-kyle-norton-on-scaling-yourself]].
- **2026-02:** He contributes a "disqualification cascade" to a guide on selling to restaurants: a "5,000 account territory" is really "1,200 workable accounts" (post 2026-02-25) [[src-linkedin-2026]].
- **2026-05 (new):** Lead tiering in writing: "25% might be A, 50% B, 25% C", and tier C "is who you will never close" [[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]].
- **2026-08 (new):** Same doctrine, new vocabulary: buyers self-disqualify, misfit pipeline is "fake", and Owner doesn't "really do the signals thing" [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @03:06, @35:02.
- **2026-09 (new):** ICP becomes a harness asset, part of the shared context every agent points back to [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @28:02–30:06.
- No reversals across eight years. The idea has moved from a lesson (League, Shopify) to a day-one cleanup (2022) to scoring infrastructure (EGMB/GPV floors, outbound floors, lead tiers) and now harness context.

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Which months dipped.** Bookings fell in months 1 and 2 [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @12:16, [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @05:07, [[src-pocus-scaling-3m-to-21m]] @12:36–14:45 ("two down months"), [[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @06:10–07:13, [[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @13:25–14:25. In another telling, growth "slowed down in my second month and my third month" [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @10:26. The differences are small, but the stories don't match.
- **How many tightenings? (partly resolved).** Most venues tell a single day-one story. But by Mar 2024 he had already raised the EGMB floor three times [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @07:12, and in Oct 2025 he says Owner "repeatedly" shrank its ICP [[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @02:06. So the two-tightening account (Pavilion) understates it. Tightening has been continuous, by score floor.
- **TAM base: 300K or 500K?** He gave ~300,000 restaurants in 2022 and 2024 [[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @34:55, [[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @09:14, and 500,000 in 2025 [[src-E20-building-data-driven-sales-processes-in-the-ai-era-with-jord]] @54:03. The core moved from ~10K–30K (2022) to "top 30,000" (2025). Different universes (US independents vs. all) may explain it. He doesn't say.
- **Narrow ICP vs. expansion.** He praises narrowness, and in 2024 had "no plans to go upmarket" [[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @17:37–20:46. Yet by Dec 2025 he plans on stretching to mid-market customers [[src-web-2025-12-04-sta-topline-asad-zaman-q-a-qa-kyle-norton-on-scaling-yourself]], Owner is repurposing OneMind avatars to sell a full deal to "a new customer base" [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @57:43–60:48, and he is looking at LATAM reps for the bottom of the market [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @11:38. He hasn't said how those new segments are qualified.
- **No-signals stance.** Owner doesn't "really do the signals thing" [[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @03:06. Yet in Feb 2025 he pushed back on Jordan Crawford's claim that signal platforms are a trap [[src-E20-building-data-driven-sales-processes-in-the-ai-era-with-jord]] @43:28, and he extracts best-rep "triggers" and digital-footprint signals to build ICP scores [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @06:18–07:22. The difference seems to be static fit scoring vs. intent/timing signals, but he never states it.
