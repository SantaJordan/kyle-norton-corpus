# Capacity Planning

> As of 2026-09-08, Kyle treats capacity planning as always-on and reasoned from the sales system's own math. Growth is "pipeline times conversion rate", not "AEs times quota", a line he has used since at least December 2024. "80% of the time you have too much AE capacity," so he pushes ops per AE until reps "break", and he removes friction before adding heads. AI-driven productivity has led him to *pull headcount forward*, not cut it. He has done this since late 2025, not only from May 2026 ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @21:33–@27:50; [[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @25:22; [[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @38:41; [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @13:09–@14:12).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
*Unchanged in substance after the 2026-10 backfill. The Jevons / pull-forward plank is re-dated earlier.*
- **Always-on, not annual.** Trade conversion rates against cost; "fire your worst reps and give those ops to your best ones". Great CROs live at two altitudes, the spreadsheet and the rep's day ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @21:33–@26:46). He has used the "two levels of abstraction" line since 2024 ([[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @17:20). Owner replans "almost every quarter" ([[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @23:23).
- **Pipeline × conversion, not reps × quota.** "it is not AES times quota it's pipeline times conversion rate" ([[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @20:26); "the bottleneck 90% of the time is opportunity volume" ([[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @18:22).
- **Find the break point.** He overloaded one transactional team from 60 to 80 ops/month and suggests testing +20% to +40% ops per AE ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @24:39–@27:50). The AE target is four booked and three held demos a day: "That's about where things start to crack." ([[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @24:29–@25:29)
- **First-principles math.** With >50% win rates and 2–3 rep-hours per deal, work back to ~two meetings booked per BDR per day and ~200 dials ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @09:24–@10:24).
- **Never under-capacity on demand.** "I am not going to get caught in a position where I don't have enough capacity to meet to meet the AQL demand. That's like a cardinal sin of a sales leader." ([[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @27:31)
- **Jevons paradox, not headcount cuts.** AI made reps more productive, and Owner hires more. In Dec 2025 he was "hiring like 25 people in sales over like a 75day period" ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @25:22). In Mar 2026 he moved June headcount into March ([[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @38:41–@39:43). In May 2026 he said "I'm pulling forward headcount now," about four months ahead of plan, because "our CAC payback pencils out really nicely" ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @13:09–@14:12), and he added 30 sales heads on top of the 2026 plan ([[src-linkedin-2026]], post 2026-05-27). AI's goal is "capacity creation," not headcount reduction ([[src-substack-2026-05-17-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook]]).
- **Fix the machine before adding bodies.** He agrees leaders "throw bodies into a system" when improving the system would do more ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @47:51); "Headcount is easier to add once the machine is working better" ([[src-substack-2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing]]).
- **Ramp is a capacity lever.** Cutting ramp changes "the economics of a business... pretty materially" ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @38:43–@39:45).
- **Management capacity is the usual breaking point** ("usually the place that things fall apart"); a ramped manager runs ~10 reps ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @31:31–@33:42).

## How his view evolved
- **2018-09-25 (League, earliest):** The origin lesson is over-hiring. "We saw some early signs of success, and we tried to hire too many reps too quickly", so funnel generation and CS couldn't keep up: "slow is smooth, and smooth is fast" ([[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] @16:39–@17:46).
- **2022-11-01:** He repeats the League lesson at Owner: the spreadsheet pressured him to put reps in seats before PMF and ICP were clear. "We would have gotten to the answer much faster had we shown a little patience and hired more slowly." ([[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @16:26–@21:37)
- **2024-02-07:** Don't over-hire AEs because "AEs times... the ARR per AE" is how the spreadsheet works, not how a business works; fund BDRs first. Recruit second-line leaders 60–120 days ahead ([[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @03:10–@04:10, @34:20–@35:26).
- **2024-02-23:** Bench building. Early in his career he missed a number because he realized too late that he needed more BDRs next quarter; hiring, notice and ramp left him "six weeks or eight weeks too late". He now plans a year out ([[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @03:10–@08:22).
- **2024-04-18:** "80% of the time" companies have "a pipeline problem and not a rep capacity problem"; calendar test (3–4 customer meetings/day); 2 BDRs per AE ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @22:27–@23:29).
- **2024-08-01:** "Less reps is better than more reps." Sam Blond's calendar test: reps need four or five customer meetings a day before you add more ([[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @14:18). Find GTM fit before adding headcount ([[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @03:03).
- **2024-08-16:** Headcount pulled forward on LinkedIn because "The growth engine has been ripping" ([[src-linkedin-2022-2024]], post 2024-08-16). This is the earliest pull-forward.
- **2024-10-30:** Two dated views. Plans that land in January with an eight-week hiring cycle and a three-month ramp mean "you're already cooked" ([[src-E06-untold-strategies-of-high-impact-sales-recruitment-with-asad]] @09:15–@10:17). Stage and gate investments; add heads confidently only once systems and a track record exist ([[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @05:06–@09:10).
- **2024-11-19:** BDR:AE ratio is a math question: "You want as arguably as many BDRs per rep as possible" as long as BDRs produce ([[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @07:16).
- **2024-12-03:** Growth is "pipeline generated times a conversion rate," not reps × quota ([[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @14:36).
- **2025-01-08:** Bring the CMO into capacity-model and envelope conversations; finance sees sales and marketing as one CAC line ([[src-E15-the-blueprint-for-sales-and-marketing-alignment-with-kyle-la]] @13:25–@14:25).
- **2025-01-19:** Real executives plan in years; outbound takes ~9 months to build ([[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] @25:54–@26:58).
- **2025-01-29:** The reps × quota growth model is wrong. Revenue comes from pipeline and conversion, with rep count a non-linear function of them ([[src-saastr-ai-revops-playbook]] @31:44–@32:45).
- **2025-01-31:** Hiring pace is bounded by ramp lag, CAC payback and, above all, management capacity ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @31:31–@33:42).
- **2025-02-13:** As a first-time VP of Sales he over-hired against an unchallenged model: "we ended up having to lay off like 20% of the team. I won't make that mistake again." ([[src-getaccept-change-management]] @07:22)
- **2025-03-05:** Sales headcount scaling "from 50 people in sales to 90 or plus this year", partly with cheaper earlier-career reps in the new office ([[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @53:24).
- **2025-03-26:** "up to 50% of your theoretical capacity is lost to extended ramp time, low productivity, and turnover." ([[src-linkedin-2025-h1]], post 2025-03-26)
- **2025-04-09 / 04-16:** A ramped AE could take 60–80 opps a month ([[src-E28-how-cresta-built-a-category-leader-with-cro-alex-cramer]] @46:51–@48:56). Bottoms-up quota math is "one of the most important activities" ([[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @54:32–@55:33).
- **2025-05-30:** Jevons paradox first appears: AI likely increases demand for reps rather than causing mass unemployment ([[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @71:20–@73:26).
- **2025-08-07:** "the bottleneck 90% of the time is opportunity volume"; "The reps don't produce revenue" ([[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @18:22).
- **2025-08-20:** "Hiring reps is not at all vibes and feel. That is programmatic"; fund senior hires out of growth ([[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @05:12, @09:23–@10:24).
- **2025-10-10:** In 2026 planning he offered GTM headcount back to engineering. A solo VP can hire and ramp "maybe like six reps" before systems are needed ([[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @08:28–@09:29, @13:43).
- **2025-11-12:** "the death of linear capacity planning". Handing a reps × quota plan to a new VP is "one of the the least kind things we do to firsttime VPs of sales". He was hiring "like 25 BDRs in 90 days" ahead of the new CMO's demand ([[src-sales-capacity-planning-that-doesnt-suck-jenny-dingus-svp-global-sa]] @01:01, @11:11, @25:28–@28:32).
- **2025-12-17:** Jevons in practice: ~3x bookings per dollar per AE, yet ~25 sales hires in ~75 days ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @25:22–@26:25).
- **2026-03-04:** "My answer isn't like, okay, great. I'm going to like hire less sales reps now and just like grow at the same rate but cheaper." He was pulling June headcount into March, and it was the first time Owner lacked AE capacity ([[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @38:41–@39:43).
- **2026-03-24:** Going from 10 to 40 reps in 18 months needs infrastructure; Owner "grew faster in 2025 than we grew in 2024" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @19:45).
- **2026-05-01 (new):** Headcount trade: "Take two of those BDR headcount and put it into one GTME, or pay a consultant." ([[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]])
- **2026-05-07 (new):** Pulling headcount forward ~4 months because of AI-driven BDR economics ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @13:09–@14:12). This continues the Dec 2025–Mar 2026 pattern; it is not a new stance.
- **2026-05-27 (new):** "we just lit up an additional 30 sales headcount on top our original 2026 plan." ([[src-linkedin-2026]], post 2026-05-27)
- **2026-08-05 (new):** Always-on capacity planning; "80% of the time you have too much AE capacity" ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @25:45).
- **2026-08-26 / 09-01 (new):** Friction before fuel; system before bodies ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @47:51–@55:00; [[src-substack-2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing]]).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **Re-dated: "pulling headcount forward" isn't a May 2026 development.** The earlier page treated it as new. It appears on LinkedIn in Aug 2024, as the Jevons thesis in May 2025, as 25 hires in 75 days in Dec 2025, and as June-into-March in Mar 2026. The May 2026 version is the latest restatement.
- **"Slow is smooth" vs. hiring ~100 a year.** His foundational lesson (League 2018, Owner 2022, the ~20% layoff he recounted in 2025) is against over-hiring. Yet Owner hires aggressively and pulls headcount forward. His implicit reconciliation is sequence: PMF, then GTM fit, then data and systems, then heads ([[src-top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro]] @03:03). The CAC-payback threshold that triggers hiring is never stated.
- **Don't throw bodies vs. hiring ~100 a year.** He argues against headcount-led growth ([[src-E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps]] @47:51) while running one of the faster hiring plans he describes ([[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @41:20; [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @14:12).
- **Offering headcount to engineering vs. adding 30 heads.** In Oct 2025 he offered GTM headcount to engineering for 2026 ([[src-polaris-lodestar-s1e1]] @08:28). By May 2026 he had added 30 heads over that plan. Demand outran the plan; he gives no reconciliation beyond "momentum".
- **Anti-spreadsheet vs. pro-spreadsheet.** 2024: reps × ARR-per-rep is "not how a business works". 2025: rep hiring is "programmatic" and needs spreadsheet skill. These reconcile (bad model vs. good model), but the rhetoric swings by venue.
- **"Pipeline problem" vs. "too much AE capacity" vs. the first AE-capacity shortfall.** Both "80% of the time" lines describe the same imbalance from opposite ends. Mar 2026 was explicitly the exception, when AE capacity ran short ([[src-my-team-drives-4x-revenue-per-ae-vs-competitors-aviv-canaani-cro]] @38:41).
- **Calendar test drift.** 3–4 customer meetings/day (Apr 2024), 4–5 (Aug 2024, citing Sam Blond), 4 booked / 3 held (Mar 2026). The numbers are close; the venue differs.
- **"100 people" in which year and which org?** Plans for 2025 rose from "40 or 50" (Feb), to "90 or plus" (Mar), back to "like 50" (May), to "100 people and go to market" (Sep). May 2026 says "like a hundred people in sales". The figures mix scope (sales vs. GTM) and dates and can't be reconciled from the sources.
- **Fewer, better reps vs. scale.** 2024's "fewer, better, higher-paid reps" ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @23:29–@24:30) sits awkwardly beside a sales org approaching ~150 ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @05:14) staffed ~80% by new grads.
