# Prioritization: the 4 P's → 5P Framework

> As of 2026-05-26, Kyle prioritizes with a "5P framework", adapted from Annie Duke (*Thinking in Bets*, *How to Decide*): map the **possibilities**, estimate **payoff** × **probability** (expected value), then weigh against **perspiration** (effort, including *whose* effort). He calls it "a mental model," not a spreadsheet ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @22:21–@24:23; [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @29:59–@37:13). The P-framework goes back to his SaaStr Annual revenue-operating-system talk (Sept 2024, "potentials"), and the "5P" label was already in use in a talk recorded around Nov–Dec 2025 ([[src-saastr-ai-revops-playbook]] @22:29; [[src-signal-learn-build-scale]] @16:20–@18:23).

## Current position (as of 2026-05-26)
- **The steps:** (1) map possibilities, i.e. the problems and ways to solve them (opportunity solution tree or Kaizen 5 Whys for root cause); (2) payoff if it works; (3) probability it works, so payoff × probability = expected value; (4) perspiration, meaning build effort *and* adoption effort ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @22:21–@24:23; [[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @29:59–@34:05).
- **Formula form:** (payoff × probability) ÷ perspiration over the mapped possibilities, producing a "priority score" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @22:21–@24:23).
- **"Whose perspiration?"** Effort that falls on another team matters, because "if they don't prioritize this project, then you're out of luck." Above ~$25–50M ARR the cross-team roadmap-alignment "tax is very high" ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @32:02–@37:13).
- **It's a mental model.** "you don't actually have to write out all these numbers" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @24:23, raw transcript); force-rank and work on "the top one or two" ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @29:59–@37:13).
- **Applied to AI bets:** it decides which AI use cases to start with after data foundations ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @20:20–@24:23; [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @33:40–@34:45).
- **Few, big bets.** "you really just need to make like two to five high leverage bets a year and they have to work" ([[src-the-secret-to-high-trust-high-performance-gtm-teams-with-cro-of-houzz]] @63:08). Annual plans are framed as "three north stars and two growth horizons" ([[src-web-2025-12-04-sta-topline-asad-zaman-q-a-qa-kyle-norton-on-scaling-yourself]]).
- **Self-aware about over-committing.** He calls himself "an ideas person" and admits Owner takes on too many things ([[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]] @39:32; [[src-cro-life-from-a-20b-exit-to-a-hyper-growth-ai-org-michelle-donnell]] @42:47).
- **Projects that move no P&L metric go "in the toy box, not the operating plan"** ([[src-substack-2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young]]).

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **The name changed across venues, and the count didn't always match.** Sept 2024 (SaaStr Annual, published Jan 2025): **potentials**, payoffs, perspiration, probability, unnamed. Jan 2025: "4 P's" / "four P's" with **potentials**. Dec 2025: "4-P" with **possibilities**. ~Nov–Dec 2025 (Signal): "5P methodology" with four Ps listed. Apr 5, 2026: "5P" but only four Ps listed. Apr 20, 2026: "5P" with **priorities** as the added fifth. May 2026 SaaStr: "5P" listing possibilities, payoff, probability, perspiration. The fifth P (priorities) is explicit only on Union Square; elsewhere "5P" is a label on a four-step method. Cite the version of the venue you are quoting.
- **"Potentials" → "possibilities."** A quiet rename between Jan and Dec 2025; the meaning (the option set) is unchanged.
- **Additive vs. divisive perspiration.** Early versions weigh perspiration alongside the others ("really big payoff bets with high probability, low perspiration"); the SaaStr version divides EV by perspiration. Same intent, different math.
- **"4-P" and "5P" overlapped.** The Signal talk (recorded ~Nov–Dec 2025) already said "5P", while the Dec 3, 2025 Verticals episode still said "4-P". The label was not a clean 2026 rebrand.
- **Framework vs. practice.** He preaches "two to five" bets and force-ranking "the top one or two", yet says Owner is "unfocused" and he hasn't "perfected" backlogging ideas (Mar 2025, Feb 2026). The gap is self-reported and not resolved.
- **Attribution.** Always "adapted"/"modified" from Annie Duke; Duke's books don't use a P-acronym, so the P-naming is Kyle's packaging.
