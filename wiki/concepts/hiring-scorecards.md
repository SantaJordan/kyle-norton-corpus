# Hiring Scorecards

> Kyle's current position (2026-09-08): interviews are mostly noise, so structure them. Use discrete scorecard criteria weighted to DNA (drive, coachability, organization, history of excellence) to box in the charisma "halo effect", and retro scores against in-role results. Owner's own data, repeated in at least seven venues, shows the sales-craft case study barely predicts performance. The framework is old (a coachability/history-of-success scorecard at Shopify in 2020; *Who*-style structured interviews by 2023); the 2026 additions are a redo the case after feedback coachability test, an AI-fluency screen, and a Claude-built analysis that scores candidates against in-role data ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @21:48–23:50; [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @32:13; [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @46:30–49:34).

## Current position (as of 2026-09-08)
- **Structure beats vibes.** Use the same questions in the same order, independent criteria (including each company value), and divided-and-conquered interviewers. "Get away from hiring on vibes" [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @35:36–39:41; [[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @16:45–18:47.
- **Box in charisma.** Discrete criteria counter the halo/horns effect (Kahneman) [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @26:26–27:27; [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @29:05–30:05. "Sales hiring has a charisma problem" (preview only) [[src-substack-2026-04-09-sales-hiring-has-a-charisma-problem]].
- **DNA, then craft, then knowledge.** DNA means resilience, history of excellence, coachability, curiosity, preparedness and organization. The best reps are "sicko workers and they're operators", which he calls "the most common trait" [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @29:05–31:10. His test after a mis-hire: did you have "conscientiousness or organizational skills" on the scorecard? [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @38:39.
- **The case study only tells you one thing.** It isn't "broken" [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @32:13. It has been "deprioritized in importance" in the loop [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @28:30. Interview sales craft has "very little correlation" with performance [[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @21:48–23:50.
- **Redo the case for coachability (2026 framing).** Candidates redo the case study after feedback, which turns it into a coachability signal [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @32:13–33:14.
- **Retro everything.** Correlate stage scores and each manager's scoring accuracy with in-role results at 3–6 months. Go back to the interview notes after mis-hires [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @35:36–39:41.
- **The bar raiser is Kyle, and it's DNA/values only.** The loop is recruiter screen → hiring manager → mock call/case → a 30-minute bar raiser with Kyle for all market-facing roles [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @28:30; [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @46:30–49:34. Decisions are champion-based, not consensus [[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @20:52.
- **AI fluency is now a scored criterion.** Since May 2025, manager candidates with no answer on AI "are disqualified" [[src-E33-what-it-takes-to-be-a-revenue-leader-in-the-ai-era-with-mike]] @30:01–31:01. In 2026 he asks what a candidate has built with AI for the business and for themselves: "That energy is the tell." (post 2026-05-06) [[src-linkedin-2026]].
- **References as a scorecard input.** The last two direct managers as references, where hesitation is "a massive red flag" [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @13:27–14:32.
- **Claude-built hiring analysis (2026, after attempts since 2025).** He pulled Ashby scorecards, MetaView recordings and in-role performance, and had Claude find common traits and "who's good at identifying talent". The goal is a copilot that scores each candidate's likelihood of success [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @46:30–49:34; [[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @55:34–56:37.

## How his view evolved
- **2020-08-17 (Shopify era, earliest):** A later-stage scorecard: good listener, coachable, a history of success in any domain (tuba, soccer captain, debate), and a growth mindset that "never changes" [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @48:28–50:33. At League he tried to scare candidates off at the end of every interview [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @44:12–48:28.
- **2023-12-15 (written):** "For hiring, I use the 'source scorecard select secure' framework from the book 'Who.'" Scorecard before posting; three interviews with the same questions in the same order [[src-web-2023-12-15-amplemarket-blog-sales-management-tips-expert-insights-with]]. *Structured interviewing predates every podcast statement below.*
- **2024-02-07:** Organization and daily discipline can be taught; growth mindset, resilience and drive cannot [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @28:09–30:15. *Note: by 2025–26 he treats "organized" as a DNA trait to select for, not something to teach.*
- **2024-04-18:** Scorecard criteria include preparation and conscientiousness. He uses a real strategic-project case study instead of a "first 90 days" plan, and a pre-watch video. Over-prepared candidates have become "a little bit of a flag" [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @54:25–57:31.
- **2024-08-01:** "60 to 70% of our hiring framework is DNA" for SMB early-career hiring, including a stress-is-enhancing mindset (Alia Crum) and a history of success in anything [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @54:18–56:21. A sales-manager job post: "We'll ask for references from reps to assess for servant leadership." (post 2024-08-16) [[src-linkedin-2022-2024]].
- **2024-10-08:** The named three-part scorecard: DNA (mindset, habits, intelligence, character), craft, specialized knowledge, with questions mapped across recruiter screen, hiring manager, case study and a finalist meeting. Structure counters halo/horns bias [[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @34:42–41:04.
- **2024-10-30:** Kyle wants to go in unbiased: "I don't want you to tell me about a candidate before I meet them because I want to be unbiased when I go in." Recruiters calibrate on resumes against "a fairly detailed written scorecard" (a practice from Shopify) [[src-E06-untold-strategies-of-high-impact-sales-recruitment-with-asad]] @21:46, @39:12–40:13.
- **2024-11-13:** Every ATS interview questionnaire ends with "Heck yeah or no", and the same bar applies to internal promotions; "Maybe" means go external [[src-E08-sam-blonds-blueprint-for-building-exceptional-leaders-tea]] @22:46.
- **2024-12-03:** 50–65% of the scorecard is hard-to-teach traits, with identical questions in identical order. A mock call with feedback and then a re-run tests coachability, even for VP Sales candidates. The framework comes from Mark Roberge (2015) plus Kahneman. He is exploring AI to check question adherence [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @20:59–26:13.
- **2024-12-25:** Scorecards counter the "halo effect": "people just gravitate to communicators" [[src-E13-scott-leese-on-bridging-the-gap-between-founders-and-vps]] @19:21–20:24.
- **2025-01-22. Values enter the scorecard:** "We've just recently weaved this in, in a more systematic way where every hiring scorecard has our values assessment." [[src-E17-from-1m-to-1b-arr-ron-gabriskos-databricks-story]] @44:17. *This dates values-in-scorecards ~10 months before the Nov 2025 statement below.*
- **2025-02-05:** For a first sales leader, the *Who* sequence: jobs to be done → competencies → values/DNA → validated history of excellence. Dig into "the how" of past numbers [[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @29:37–31:40, @53:01–57:13.
- **2025-03-09:** Values sit in the monthly report card, the promotion scorecard (1–5) and his final-stage hiring scorecard [[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] @51:02–54:05.
- **2025-05-21:** AI mandate for manager hires: "need you to ask about AI and if somebody has no answer to it, then they are disqualified." [[src-E33-what-it-takes-to-be-a-revenue-leader-in-the-ai-era-with-mike]] @31:01.
- **2025-05-30:** DNA/craft/knowledge, used "for five to eight years", weighted by role: "I'm like 70% DNA and mindset." Insist on the last two direct managers as references [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @11:25–14:32.
- **2025-08-07. The retro finding first surfaces (own show):** every hire from the last two years, scored out of 16, vs in-seat results. A 13 became a top producer, a 15 was mediocre, and the top scorer was one of the fastest exits ("they were just lying"). "I want to get more scientific about hiring." [[src-E42-cutting-the-fat-and-building-sales-machines-that-scale-with]] @69:27–73:31. *Re-dated: this is two weeks before the Frontlines episode previously treated as first.*
- **2025-08-20:** Five values scored independently on a four-point scale, a controlled process with the same case-study inputs and the same feedback to everyone, and quarterly hiring retros vs. attainment. Case-study sales craft was "not that indicative" of success, so they simplified the case study. One interviewer kept rating future top performers as 3s. A Metaview regression is planned [[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @16:45–27:10, @19:49.
- **2025-08-21 (written, strongest form):** "Mock demos and case studies have zero predictive value for sales success." Every 90 days the team reviews misses, including which manager has a "40% hit rate vs 80%" (post 2025-08-21) [[src-linkedin-2025-h2]].

- **2025-11-05:** Company values are "in all of our interview scorecards" and in performance reviews. "If you can't hire against your values... your values probably don't mean anything" [[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @57:05–59:13.

## Tensions, contradictions & open questions
- **The strength of the finding keeps changing.** First told on his own show as anecdotes (a 13 vs a 15, Aug 7 2025) → "not that indicative" (Aug 20 2025) → "zero predictive value" (LinkedIn, Aug 21 2025) → "no correlation... inversely correlated" (Sep 2025) → "not very highly correlated" (Mar 2026) → "basically nonexistent" (Apr 2026, preview) → "very little correlation" (Sep 2026). He has given no correlation coefficient or sample size, so it's unclear whether it is zero, weakly positive or negative.
- **Scrap it or keep it?** In Sept 2025 he was "tempted to scrap the case study" [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @21:12–24:28. In 2026 he keeps it, deprioritized and re-purposed as a coachability test [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @32:13; [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @28:30.
- **The "redo" twist isn't new.** It's presented in 2026 as new ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @32:13), but he described "a mock call with feedback then a re-run" to test coachability in Dec 2024 [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @20:59–23:05. Better read as a revival or re-emphasis.
- **Teachable or innate?** In 2024 organization was "teachable" [[src-accord-documentation-dry-runs]] @28:09–30:15. By 2026 it is the top DNA trait to select for [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @31:10. That's an unacknowledged shift.
- **Preparation as a criterion vs. a red flag.** In the same 2024 Q&A he scored for preparation and said over-preparation was "a little bit of a flag" [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @56:30–57:31.
- **The DNA weight creeps up.** 60–70% (Aug 2024) → 50–65% (Dec 2024) → 70–80% for SMB (May 2025) [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @54:18; [[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @20:59; [[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @11:25. Probably role-dependent ranges, not a fixed policy.
- **Old roots, new framing.** Coachability, history of success in any domain and growth mindset were already his scorecard in 2020 [[src-shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020]] @48:28–50:33. The 2025–26 retro mostly re-validated what he already believed rather than overturning it; the real change is the demoted case study.
- **"Champion-based" vs Kyle as final bar raiser.** He says the hiring manager makes the call and he doesn't "really veto people" [[src-becoming-a-cfo-whisperer-revenue-architect-allison-metcalfe-cro]] @17:17, yet he sits as the last stage for every IC hire [[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] @53:04.
- **Validation horizon.** He says the AI copilot needs years of in-role data before its scores can be trusted [[src-E69-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook-driving-a-1b-trajectory]] @49:34. Agreeing with five bar raisers shows consistency with his own judgment, not that it predicts performance.
