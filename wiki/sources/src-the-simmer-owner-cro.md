# The Simmer: Kyle Norton, Chief Revenue Officer, Owner.com

**Show / host:** The Simmer (restaurant-tech podcast) / Kristen Hawley (Expedite newsletter) and Brandon Barton (CEO, Bite) — transcript spells her "Kristen Holley" · **Published:** 2024-06-04 · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/the-simmer-owner-cro/transcript.md` · **Audio:** https://anchor.fm/s/f3bf9a70/podcast/play/87556021/

Note: Kyle joins @09:16; @00:00–09:16 is the hosts' restaurant-tech news segment (Amazon/Grubhub, NY reservation-resale bill) and is not Kyle.

## TL;DR
- Pitched to a restaurant-industry audience: Owner is "Shopify plus HubSpot for mom and pop restaurants" — an **opinionated product** trading customization for results (orders up "50, 75, 100 percent in the first six months"; site conversion 9–11% vs 1–3% industry).
- The Series B wasn't pitched: insiders (Redpoint, SaaStr Fund, Jack Altman) asked to put more in after a board meeting; Owner "grew over two and a half X last year" with rare unit economics; Kyle worries overfunding erodes discipline.
- Growth comes from results, month-to-month contracts, helping customers churn cleanly, radical transparency, and ICP discipline (1–7 locations, US, takeout/delivery). Culture: "We are trying to win… not building for an acquisition" — stated in a pre-interview video.

## What Kyle argued
- Owner = website, online ordering, email/text marketing, loyalty, branded app, "all done for the restaurant owner"; less customization than Wix-style rivals in exchange for better results @10:16–11:21. [[point-of-view-positioning]] [[owner-com]]
- It's not just "get people off of DoorDash": third-party apps are "great billboards"; most discovery is Google/listings; average ticket lift 10–15% @11:21–12:22. [[smb-sales-motion]]
- Series B came from insiders after a board meeting — no pitch deck; they debated because "there's a danger in being overfunded" (valuation catch-up, reduced discipline); wrote the deal memo afterward for the market and recruits @13:23–15:25. [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- Investors were excited by growth "with pretty rare unit economics" and capital efficiency @14:25–15:25. [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- Growth "really comes down to results": month-to-month, "We actually help the customer churn" (cached old site, set them back up); an algorithm predicts the GPV Owner will produce; word of mouth incl. Reddit @15:25–17:37. [[churn-and-retention]]
- Radically transparent selling: "I lead with the trade off" — less customization, but tests across thousands of customers @17:37. [[point-of-view-positioning]]
- ICP: single location to "six, seven locations" sweet spot; customization desire rises with location count; 20+ location customers come in but aren't targeted; "just restaurants, just take-out and delivery centric concepts, just the U.S."; scoring algorithm for fit; no plans to go upmarket — Toast proves a generational business can stay focused @17:37–20:46. - New eyeballs come from search, not social; customers move from average to top-three search rank in months; background AI; a conversion scoring model on photos, story, page structure, clicks-to-basket @21:51–23:54. [[marketing-alignment]]
