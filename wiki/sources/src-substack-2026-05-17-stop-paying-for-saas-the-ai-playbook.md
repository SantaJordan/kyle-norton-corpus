# Stop Paying For SaaS: The AI Playbook Driving A $1B Trajectory | Ghazi Masood, CRO @ Replit

**Type:** episode companion (guest: Ghazi Masood, CRO, Replit; ex-Auth0, Retool) · **Published:** 2026-05-17 · **Paywalled:** no · **URL:** https://www.therevenueleadershippodcast.com/p/stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook · **Raw:** `raw/substack/2026-05-17-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook.md`

**Attribution note.** Replit facts, quotes and hiring practices are Ghazi's. Kyle's own: AI goal = capacity creation not headcount cuts, the build/buy heuristic as he'd apply it at Owner, RevOps-as-product-team, "scale down"/compression, and a Revstar plug with Owner.com outcome claims.

## TL;DR
- Despite the title, the lesson is "don't automate the org chart. Redesign the work": Replit automates heavily yet is hiring 200+.
- Build the workflow layer, buy the plumbing (Replit keeps Salesforce as system of record).
- RevOps is becoming a product team; the future GTM leader must "scale down" and stay close to the work.

## Kyle's argument
- AI's purpose is "capacity creation," not headcount reduction; "What roles can we automate?" leads to "lazy answers." See [[ai-in-gtm]], [[talent-density]].
- In new categories reps become the customer's "AI advocate" (guest: Ghazi Masood); AI can't "create conviction in a buyer."
- Build vs. buy heuristic Kyle takes from Ghazi: buy if downtime stops revenue or governance/security/financial accuracy matter; build if the workflow creates unique leverage or is coordination/visibility. Kyle: "I don’t want to vibe code Salesforce." See [[build-vs-buy-software]], [[ai-sales-stack]].
- Replit isn't building its own CRM; it builds a revenue copilot, CPQ, forecasting, health views on top of Salesforce (guest: Ghazi Masood).
- "AI makes bad infrastructure louder" (Kyle): messy CRM/territories get amplified by agents. See [[revops]].
- RevOps is breaking first in hypergrowth (guest: Ghazi Masood); Kyle: new RevOps "looks much more like product management" and may become one of the highest-leverage GTM roles. See [[revops]], [[org-design]].
- Hire non-traditional sellers with product passion and AI fluency (Marine, school teacher) (guest: Ghazi Masood); Kyle: "Give me that over clean MEDDICC vocabulary with no product conviction." See [[sales-hiring]].
- Ghazi's 15-minute screens, role-specific panel, 80% hiring bar with enablement closing the gap (guest); Kyle: you can't hire 200 people with processes "built for perfect certainty." See [[sales-hiring]], [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]].
- Executives must "scale down": AI removes the prep tax so CROs can stay close to customers, candidates and workflows; warns against "dataism." See [[cro-role-and-board]].
- Revstar plug (Kyle): five decisions for an AI-native GTM org; claims Owner "quadrupled revenue per AE" and "doubled" decision-maker connect rates. See [[owner-com]].

## Key quotes
- "That’s the real AI-native GTM lesson: don’t automate the org chart. Redesign the work."
- "The better goal is capacity creation."
- "The big behemoth question is, hey, are we building our own CRM? No, we’re not." (guest: Ghazi Masood)
- "I don’t want to vibe code Salesforce. I do want to build the operating layer around Salesforce that makes our team faster, sharper, and more consistent."
- "The unsexy truth is that AI makes bad infrastructure louder."
- "Give me that over clean MEDDICC vocabulary with no product conviction."
- "I’m not a chair CRO that’s looking at spreadsheets and doing orders. I’m actually doing the work." (guest: Ghazi Masood)
- "The old model was delegation through layers. The new model is compression."

## Numbers & facts
- Owner.com (Kyle, Revstar plug): "quadrupled revenue per AE," "doubled the rates at which we connect with decision makers." Revstar Toronto, June 2 (2026); Jordan Crawford listed as another speaker.
- (guest: Ghazi Masood) Replit: ~$2M → >$150M revenue in less than a year; hiring 200+ over next 12 months; moving HubSpot → Salesforce; 15-minute screens; ~80% hiring bar.
- Third-party, cited by Kyle: McKinsey — ~75% of gen-AI value in customer ops, marketing & sales, software eng, R&D; sales productivity +3–5% of global sales spend. BCG — only 30% of managers and 28% of frontline employees trained on how AI changes their jobs.

## Frameworks & named ideas
- **Redesign the work, don't automate the org chart** (Kyle).
- **Build the workflow layer, buy the plumbing** + four-line build/buy heuristic (Ghazi → Kyle).
- **RevOps as a product team** (Kyle).
- **Scale down / compression** of executive leverage; **dataism** risk (Kyle, citing HBR).
- **Five decisions for an AI-native GTM org**: who owns AI, build vs. buy, where to start, org design, where humans stay in the loop (Kyle's Revstar talk).
