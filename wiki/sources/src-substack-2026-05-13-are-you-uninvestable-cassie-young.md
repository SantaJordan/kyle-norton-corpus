# Are You Uninvestable? Cassie Young, GP @ Primary Venture Partners

**Type:** episode companion (guest: Cassie Young, General Partner, Primary Venture Partners; ex-CRO Sailthru, Marigold) · **Published:** 2026-05-13 · **Paywalled:** no · **URL:** https://www.therevenueleadershippodcast.com/p/are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young · **Raw:** `raw/substack/2026-05-13-are-you-uninvestable-cassie-young.md`

**Attribution note.** PRIME, Core Four, "take your CFO to lunch," and the Claude Code bar for executives are Cassie's. Kyle's own: "AI peanut butter," how Owner pushed AI into existing rep surfaces, learning GTM AI from product/eng, and building-to-learn.

## TL;DR
- AI erases the slack that 80% SaaS gross margins used to hide; boards want P&L impact, not AI activity.
- Use PRIME to score AI/GTM initiatives; centralize to avoid "AI peanut butter"; partner with the CFO from the start.
- The next CRO is GM-minded and AI-fluent: part operator, systems builder, financial thinker.

## Kyle's argument
- "Most CROs were trained for a world that no longer exists"; AI ends tolerance for bad pricing and bloated process. See [[cro-role-and-board]].
- Next-gen CROs win "by understanding the financial architecture of the business and using AI to change it." See [[cro-role-and-board]], [[fundraising-and-investability]].
- "Boards are done rewarding effort"; connect AI work directly to P&L metrics (guest: Cassie Young; Kyle: "AI activity is table stakes. P&L impact is the story."). See [[metrics-and-forecasting]].
- PRIME framework (guest: Cassie Young); Kyle: projects that don't move one of the five belong "in the toy box, not the operating plan."
- Kyle coins "AI peanut butter" for 50 initiatives with no owners; prefers a centralized model with decentralized idea flow. See [[gtm-engineering]], [[ai-in-gtm]].
- Owner's approach (Kyle): didn't ask every rep to become an AI power user; built tools into surfaces reps already use, lowering "the change management tax." See [[ai-sales-stack]].
- AI pricing: inference/compute costs make mispricing dangerous; ARR with different margins can make ARR-based comp incent the wrong behavior (guest: Cassie Young; Kyle extends to metrics like ARR per headcount dollar). See [[comp-plans]], [[metrics-and-forecasting]].
- "Take your CFO to lunch" (guest: Cassie Young); Kyle: treating finance as an obstacle "is a career limiter." See [[career-and-leadership]].
- Core Four executive scorecard (guest: Cassie Young); Kyle: "I learn more about GTM AI from product and engineering conversations than from most sales conversations right now."
- AI fluency = executive fluency (guest bar: Claude Code, MCPs, agents); Kyle: his most valuable AI learning came from "building with it, getting stuck." See [[ai-in-gtm]].
