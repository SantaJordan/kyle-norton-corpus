# Amplemarket: Kyle Norton's 6-step framework for sales reps transitioning to management

**Outlet:** Amplemarket blog (interviewer/author: Arjun Krisna) · **Published:** 2023-12-15 (page notes an edit on 2026-04-28) · **Type:** written interview · **URL:** https://www.amplemarket.com/blog/kyle-nortons-6-step-framework-guiding-reps-in-their-transition-to-effective-managers · **Raw:** `raw/web/2023-12-15-amplemarket-blog-sales-management-tips-expert-insights-with.md`

*Truncated: the raw text is capped at 6,000 characters and stops mid-sentence in the hiring answer. The AI section the intro promises is not captured.*

## TL;DR
- Kyle's six pillars of frontline sales management: **Sell, Coach, Hire, Operate, Build, Inspire**.
- "Operate" is the most overlooked pillar because it is unglamorous; his own example is a Friday-afternoon weekly report and forecast check.
- Hiring uses *Who*'s "source scorecard select secure"; three structured interviews with the same questions in the same order.
- Career recap and an early Owner data point: joined at ~$3M ARR, ~$15M eighteen months later.

## What Kyle said
- Career: cold calling out of university → managing teams → "gold BDR" (likely a mis-transcription of *global* BDR) at Vision Critical → head of sales at League ("from zero to twenty-five million in three years") → three years at [[shopify]] running the Canadian market → [[owner-com]]. See [[career-and-leadership]].
- Owner described as "a combination of Shopify and HubSpot for small restaurants"; his scope then: sales, revenue operations, enablement, and the launch function. See [[revops]].
- Frontline-manager framework with six pillars; coaching means at least an hour per team member (comparative call listens, mock calls). See [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]].
- "Operate" (weekly rhythms, forecasts, 1:1 and reporting cadences) is the most overlooked pillar; he cites Kevin Dorsey's operator course. See [[operating-rhythm]].
- Hiring: scorecard before posting a role; sourcing works like outbound, with LinkedIn content building an inbound candidate funnel; three interviews, same questions, same order, to reduce bias and improve candidate experience. See [[sales-hiring]], [[hiring-scorecards]].
- Inspire: autonomy, mastery, and purpose; balance "micro-management and micro-coaching". See [[culture-and-high-standards]].

## Key quotes
- "In summary, effective sales leadership means being able to sell, hire, coach, build, operate, and inspire."
- "The 'operate' pillar is most likely overlooked because it is not very fun and is quite operational"
- "For hiring, I use the 'source scorecard select secure' framework from the book 'Who.'"
- "we have three interviews in our process, each asking the same questions in the same order."

## Numbers & facts
- Owner ARR at join: "around three million"; "Eighteen months later, we've reached about fifteen million" (as of the Dec 2023 interview).
- League: "zero to twenty-five million in three years".
- Shopify Canada: "a two hundred and fifty million dollar business unit".
- Coaching: "at least an hour with each team member".
