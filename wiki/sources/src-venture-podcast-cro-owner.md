# The Venture Podcast: Kyle Norton — CRO of Owner.com, Investor, and Sales Leader

**Show / host:** The Venture Podcast — two recent Queen's University grads (James and Bryce) · **Published:** 2024-10-31 (YouTube upload date) · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/venture-podcast-cro-owner/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NkkilunQhkQ

## TL;DR
- Career origin story (MMA, Smith School of Business, boiler-room first sales job) plus why he left Shopify for Owner.com (~$2M ARR at join, 10x since).
- Core business thesis: revenue leaders must think beyond new bookings — SaaS compounds only with retention — so he deliberately slowed growth early to tighten qualification.
- Practical playbook: Owner's custom restaurant data infrastructure as a moat, internal AI (Momentum, an AI roleplay simulator), his four-part founder evaluation, and early-stage advice (narrow ICP, go-to-network, "point of view positioning").

## What Kyle argued
- Early-career: sell a product with traction if you can, but "really optimize for the leader" — someone invested in people's growth with a track record of promoting from within @05:12. [[career-and-leadership]]
- Shortly after joining he tightened sales process/qualification, intentionally slowing growth in months two and three to fix low-quality deals and churn; confidence (not fear) let him explain it to the board @09:21–10:26. [[icp-and-saying-no]] [[churn-and-retention]] [[cro-role-and-board]]
- Founders under-respect VPs of Sales because they are myopic about new bookings; SaaS compounds only with ~90–95% annual retention, so leaders must own customer quality, handoffs, and SE continuity into onboarding @12:33–14:39. [[churn-and-retention]] [[cro-role-and-board]]
- Making hard short-term trade-offs separates "real revenue leaders" from "amateurs" who churn through VP Sales roles @14:39. [[cro-role-and-board]]
- Blue-collar vertical SaaS buyers (restaurant owners working 70–80 hr weeks) need "radically transparent selling" — straight, to the point, clear expectations @16:45. [[smb-sales-motion]]
- Owner built its own data infrastructure (purchased database → Snowflake → custom web scrapers → deal-size and propensity ML models → mobile-number lookup) because restaurant owners aren't in ZoomInfo/LinkedIn; it is now a moat @17:47–18:51. [[gtm-engineering]] [[outbound-at-scale]]
- Owner doesn't use AI to "carpet bomb your market"; it uses Momentum to fill Salesforce fields from transcripts and an AI simulator for unlimited roleplay reps built on Owner's calls and Kyle's methodology @18:51–20:52. [[ai-sales-stack]] [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]
- AI won't change who he hires (already hires many new grads with upside) but reduces ramp and raises per-AE productivity; he ships something every month to make reps better and is building a Sales Leadership Academy for frontline managers @21:54–22:58. [[sales-hiring]] [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]
- Short term, "reps with AI will replace the reps without AI"; long term AI avatars mostly replace self-serve/PLG journeys at the bottom of the market @24:02–26:13. [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]]
- Founder evaluation = four things: force of nature, super-computer learner, founder-market fit, and enough intellectual humility "not to blow it all up" @28:14–32:23. [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- Reps: the best are the fastest learners; "growth is the only thing that matters"; work on one component skill at a time (his "coaching mastery framework") @38:35–40:43. [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]
- Founder-led sales: start with a narrow, real ICP; resist "I just need ARR"; go-to-network concentric circles; messaging via April Dunford / Andy Raskin; his own "point of view positioning" @45:59–52:20. [[icp-and-saying-no]] [[marketing-alignment]]

## Frameworks & named ideas
- **Radically transparent selling** (for blue-collar vertical SaaS).
- **Restaurant data stack**: database → Snowflake → scrapers → deal-size + propensity models → mobile phone enrichment.
- **Four founder traits**: force of nature, super-computer learner, founder-market fit, intellectual humility.
- **Coaching mastery framework** (Substack): focus on one component skill / "reverse salient" at a time.
- **Concentric circles / go-to-network** (credits Scott Leese; Martin Roth).
- **Point of view positioning**: "if you believe X, then we're the only solution…" (Shopify global narrative example).
- **Optimize for the leader** when choosing early-career roles; vet via 3–5 phone back-channels.
