# Grow & Tell: The 2 steps to selling a skeptical ICP - Kyle Norton, Owner

**Show / host:** Grow & Tell (Dock) / Alex Kracov (transcript spells "Krakow") · **Published:** 2024-02-10 (YouTube upload date; metadata had none) · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/grow-and-tell-selling-skeptical-icp/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_LmCV_EajbA

> **Note:** a ~6-minute clip cut from the full episode [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] (that episode's @35:58–41:07). Timestamps below are the clip's own. Cite the full episode for the same content when possible; this page exists so the clip is not double-counted as independent evidence.

## TL;DR
- Restaurant owners are rightly skeptical; Owner earns trust two ways: low-stakes contracting (month-to-month, one-click data export, help reverting) and radically transparent positioning.
- The trade-off pitch: less choice and customization in exchange for more money and less work; if you care about design, "choose Bentobox."
- Owner is fully digital (no feet on the street), betting data infrastructure beats in-person conversion; reps close ~3x competitors' per-rep revenue.

## What Kyle argued
- Skepticism is earned: owners "have gotten sold a bad bill of goods a lot" @00:00. [[smb-sales-motion]]
- No long-term contracts: month-to-month, full data export, help reverting to the old website — lowering the stakes signals confidence @00:00–01:02. [[churn-and-retention]] [[smb-sales-motion]]
- Owner once had a money-back guarantee on top; dropped it because "we don't need it and it reduced customer commitment" @01:02.
- "Radically transparent sales style": reps say what's good and not good about Owner @01:02. [[smb-sales-motion]]
- Frame the trade-off explicitly (templates, no custom fonts) so the right buyers opt in fast and the wrong ones disqualify themselves @02:04–03:06. [[icp-and-saying-no]]
- Fully digital beats door-knocking because data on the highest-fit, highest-revenue restaurants nationwide is "way more valuable than the potential conversion rate improvements" of face-to-face @04:07. [[outbound-at-scale]] [[gtm-engineering]]
- Per-rep productivity comes from relentlessly removing low-value work, bad data, and inefficiency @05:08. [[revops]]
