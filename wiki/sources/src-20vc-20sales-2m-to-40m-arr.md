# 20VC (20Sales): From $2M ARR to $40M ARR: The Playbook

**Show / host:** 20VC — *20 Sales* monthly series / Harry Stebbings (recorded in person) · **Published:** 2025-05-30 · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr/transcript.md` · **Audio:** https://traffic.libsyn.com/secure/thetwentyminutevc/Kyle_Norton__Owner.com.mp3

Transcript note: Whisper inserted bogus speaker labels ("Matt Stauffer", "Jameel Jameel") from @66:11 onward; they are artifacts — the "Matt Stauffer" lines are Kyle, the "Jameel Jameel" lines are Harry. Harry mis-hears Kyle's name as "Carl" @03:08. Ad reads run @00:00–03:08 and @75:30–end.

## TL;DR
- Owner's SMB machine is "instrumentation" and first-principles reasoning, not best practice: centrally managed, ~95% mobile-enriched leads lift call-to-decision-maker from ~3% to 13–16%, so a 2:1 BDR:AE ratio and ~3 held demos/AE/day pencil out at an "eight, nine K" ACV.
- Talent is the CRO's job, not TA's: content-driven sourcing, a DNA/craft/knowledge scorecard (70–80% DNA for SMB), hard reference checks, fast exits on uncoachable traits, heavy enablement (5 people for 26 AEs / 30+ BDRs), in-office new SDRs/BDRs breaking ramp records.
- Biggest regret: not hiring a head of applied AI sooner; "radically transparent selling" (lead with weaknesses) shortens cycles to under a week; Owner optimizes for the long term (month-to-month, easy exits) and aims to be "a generational public company".
