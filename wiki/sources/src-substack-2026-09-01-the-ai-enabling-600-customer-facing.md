# The AI Enabling 600 Customer-Facing Reps | Lauren Hughes, VP Revenue Effectiveness @ Justworks

**Type:** episode companion (guest: Lauren Hughes, VP Revenue Effectiveness, Justworks) · **Published:** 2026-09-01 · **Paywalled:** no · **URL:** https://www.therevenueleadershippodcast.com/p/the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing · **Raw:** `raw/substack/2026-09-01-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing.md`

**Attribution note.** Nearly all numbers and stories are Lauren's (Justworks). Kyle's own contributions are diagnostics and recommendations ("I'd pick one role…", "sit with one SDR for 30 minutes"), the fuel-vs-friction framing via *The Human Element*, and an on-air correction. No Owner.com data.

## TL;DR
- Lauren cut enablement 16 → 6 (from an earlier 32), shifted content ownership to product/PMM, and served it via Slack/Spekit; ramp went 18 → 8 months.
- Measure onboarding with gates and sensors (quizzes, AI roleplay, camera-on); release reps when data says ready; flag strugglers in days.
- Kyle's frame: remove friction before adding fuel; a 30-minute SDR ride-along is the operating-system diagnosis.

## Kyle's argument
- Headcount is not the growth lever; a "tighter machine" is (Kyle framing Lauren's results). See [[org-design]], [[capacity-planning]].
- Diagnostic: "Count how many people exist to keep the wiki honest"; if it looks like a team, you've built content ops inside enablement (Kyle, from Lauren's 32-person story). See [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]], [[playbooks-and-documentation]].
- Content ownership moved to product/PMM/customer education, stale pages alert, AI suggests refresh; no LMS (guest: Lauren Hughes). See [[playbooks-and-documentation]], [[ai-sales-stack]].
- Release reps at week 3 if data says ready; diagnose the 2 of 12 who aren't (guest: Lauren Hughes). Kyle: "I'd pick one role. Write the week-3 and week-4 gates in actual skills." See [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]].
- 30-minute SDR ride-along across LinkedIn/Salesforce/6sense ended in a rejection (guest: Lauren Hughes). Kyle: "skip the tooling RFP," time a single contact, "That list is your backlog." See [[outbound-at-scale]], [[revops]].
- Unadopted tools you already pay for are often the first unlock (dialer POC in 4 days) (guest: Lauren Hughes; Kyle concurs). See [[ai-sales-stack]].
- SDR results via focus: pipeline ARR added to comp next to logo count, Claude unlocked for list building (guest: Lauren Hughes). See [[comp-plans]].
- Early signals predict failure ("first couple days"); Kyle: put three sensors on week one and make the miss a scheduled conversation. See [[sales-hiring]].
- Best AEs/managers come from the SDR org; hiring and onboarding are one system (guest: Lauren Hughes). See [[sales-hiring]].
- Fuel vs. friction (Nordgren & Schonthal): Kyle admits leaders, "myself included," reach for fuel first; remove a step before running a contest, training or PIP.

## Key quotes
- "Yeah, we needed 32 people to keep Confluence updated." (guest: Lauren Hughes)
- "Count how many people exist to keep the wiki honest."
- "We're finding out by week 3 that they're like ready to hit the phones, then awesome. Like, why are we holding them in training till week 4?" (guest: Lauren Hughes)
- "If you want the same diagnosis, I'd skip the tooling RFP. Sit with one SDR for 30 minutes and time a single contact."
- "We can tell if somebody's not going to make it just based on how they show up the first couple days." (guest: Lauren Hughes)

- "Headcount is easier to add once the machine is working better."

## Numbers & facts
- (guest: Lauren Hughes / Justworks) Enablement 32 → 16 (before her) → 6; now back to "10 or 12" with open roles. Supports 600 customer-facing people across 20 roles. Ramp 18 → 8 months, path under 6; 300 revenue hires through the program last year. AE bookings +28% last year, +44% over two years. SDR team: 2x pipeline opps (logo count) and 2x pipeline ARR in Q1 (Justworks Q1 starts in June), down 4 heads to AE promotions. 50% of Gong calls unrecorded; dialer POC within 4 days; took SDR team ~4.5 months ago; SDR comp/JD not refreshed in 3 years. Promotions: 6 SDRs → AE, 2 → partnership acquisition, 1 manager, 1 into revenue effectiveness. Tangelo schedules first 3–5 weeks.
- Third-party, cited by Kyle: McKinsey GI 2012 — ~20% of week searching for info, searchable records cut by up to 35%; CSO Insights 2018 — seats empty ~4 months + 9 months to full productivity; Bridge Group 2025 (351 cos) — SDR ramp 3.0 months; Salesforce State of Sales (7,775 sellers) — 28% of week selling, avg 10 tools, 66% overwhelmed; Bauer/Erdogan — 985 new hires; Johnson & Goldstein 2003 organ donation — opt-in 4–28% vs. opt-out 86–100%.
- Kyle admits he "was sloppy with the numbers" on the organ-donation example on air.
