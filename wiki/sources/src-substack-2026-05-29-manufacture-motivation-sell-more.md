# Manufacture Motivation, Sell More | Kevin Bailey, CEO @ Dreamfuel (pt. 2)

**Type:** episode companion (guest: Kevin Bailey, CEO, Dreamfuel; part 2, follows [[src-substack-2026-04-03-how-to-optimize-exec-performance]]) · **Published:** 2026-05-29 · **Paywalled:** no · **URL:** https://www.therevenueleadershippodcast.com/p/manufacture-motivation-sell-more · **Raw:** `raw/substack/2026-05-29-manufacture-motivation-sell-more.md`

**Attribution note.** Pre-call warmup, effort recognition and serotonin days are Bailey's. Kyle's own: "mental performance is sales infrastructure," the deployment path, the frontline-manager emphasis, and his reaction to the serotonin day.

## TL;DR
- Treat team mental performance as sales infrastructure: opt-in, leader-led, tied to moments that matter.
- Cascade it: leader embodies → A players validate → frontline managers operationalize → team adopts what works; wedge = a 4-part pre-call warmup.
- Recognize effort, not only outcomes, to "pulse dopamine"; executives need recovery ("serotonin days") because judgment quality depends on it.

## Kyle's argument
- Performance isn't only in Salesforce; "the rep’s nervous system is still in the room." See [[executive-performance-and-mindset]].
- Leaders can't mandate personal routines, so make it "opt-in, leader-led" (Kyle's synthesis). See [[culture-and-high-standards]].
- Mental performance must be embodied by the leader first, not delegated to Enablement (guest: Kevin Bailey; Kyle: "don’t roll this out. Live it first."). See [[career-and-leadership]].
- Start with A players (already hunting for an edge) and frontline managers (guest: Kevin Bailey). Kyle: frontline sales management "is one of the most critical jobs in a revenue org." See [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]].
- 4-part pre-call warmup: breathwork, music, journal the call as if it happened, rehearse the hard behaviors (guest: Kevin Bailey). Kyle's wedge: start with one big call, then inspect the difference.
- Recognize effort to pulse dopamine when the scoreboard isn't cooperating (guest: Kevin Bailey). Kyle: outcome-only recognition makes culture "emotionally hostage to variance"; "Recognize what you want repeated." See [[culture-and-high-standards]].
- Serotonin day every 2 weeks (guest: Kevin Bailey); Kyle: "that sounds like a nightmare to me," but recovery is "part of the operating model."
- "The sales team is not a spreadsheet": CRM/forecast/methodology sit on top of a human performance system (Kyle).

## Key quotes
- "But the rep’s nervous system is still in the room."
- "My takeaway: don’t roll this out. Live it first. Then invite people in."
- "I believe frontline sales management is one of the most critical jobs in a revenue org."
- "Don’t just visualize the win. Use the juice from the win to reinforce all the elite behaviors that you need to do to win." (guest: Kevin Bailey)
- "If the only thing you recognize is the final number, your culture becomes emotionally hostage to variance."
- "You know I’m sick because that sounds like a nightmare to me."
- "Mental performance is not a side quest. It is sales infrastructure."

## Numbers & facts
- Pre-call warmup: 30 deep belly breaths, ~1 second between, hold on the 30th (guest: Kevin Bailey).
- Serotonin day: 1 every 2 weeks (guest: Kevin Bailey).
- Third-party, cited by Kyle: Gallup — only about 1 in 3 U.S. workers received praise/recognition in the last week; 2024 *Scientific Reports* — 40-minute nature walk improved executive-attention markers vs. urban walk.
- No Owner.com numbers.

## Frameworks & named ideas

- **4-part pre-call warmup** (Bailey).
- **Recognition as dopamine management / recognize effort** (Bailey).
- **Serotonin day** / "no-striving day" (Bailey; Kyle's alternative names).
