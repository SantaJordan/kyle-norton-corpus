# SaaStr (Jason Lemkin): Owner.com Did an AI Rebuild to Accelerate Past $100M ARR

## TL;DR
- Lemkin: Owner has "rocketed past $100M ARR, growing at triple digits", and more than 83% of new customers now start inside an AI product.
- The rebuild replaced a 100% sales-led inbound path (demo → salesperson → onboarding specialist) with **Grader**, a free AI website build that finishes in five minutes.
- The opinionated product is what produces comparable outcome data across restaurants. See [[point-of-view-positioning]].

## What the article says (attributed to Lemkin / Guild)
- Acquisition path rebuilt, not features; free product delivers the whole outcome (site, upscaled photos, generated video, SEO/CRO audit).
- "Inverted the engagement metric": a login to fix what the software did counts as a software failure.
- Owner's revenue is a cut of payment volume, so it is insulated from per-seat pricing problems.
- Lead-qualification agent estimates a prospect's gross payment volume "to within about $250". Relevant to Kyle's lead-scoring work; see [[ai-in-gtm]].
- Internal agent "Owen" absorbs ~90% of builder coordination; finance moved its primary artifact from Excel into Claude; Adam shipped five products personally in two months.
- Outcome data (not a public corpus) is the moat; configuration flexibility destroys comparability.

## Key quotes (Lemkin)
- "They’ve rocketed past $100M ARR, growing at triple digits and faster than the year before"
- "Owner was 100% sales-led inbound: book a demo, talk to a salesperson, then an onboarding specialist."
- "Every login to fix what the software did is counted as a failure of the software."

## Numbers & facts (Lemkin-stated)

- GPV estimate "within about $250".
