# Attio GTM Atlas: Start with the data

**Outlet:** Attio GTM Atlas (Outbound section) · **Published:** May 2026 (day not given; valid_from uses the 1st as a month marker) · **Type:** Kyle-authored · **URL:** https://atlas.attio.com/start-with-the-data · **Raw:** `raw/web/2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas.md`

*Truncated: the raw text is capped at 6,000 characters and stops mid-sentence in the argument that AEs need not be super AI-savvy. Several paragraphs appear twice in the raw text (a scrape artifact); they are counted once here. The `Artifact 04.0x` images are not captured.*

## TL;DR
- Outbound has one point of failure: **the data**. "No training, hiring profile, call volume or slick techniques can overcome giving your team bad data."
- AI's job is to **remove wasted motion**: "AI PCR" (pre-call research) lets BDRs make 150–250 calls a day while hyper-prepared.
- New BDR numbers: top outbound BDR **$174,000 closed-won ARR in one month**, cold; average went from **$72k** to **closer to $120k** per BDR per month.
- **Centralize** AI under one specialist owner rather than giving every rep a Claude skill; fund a GTM engineer with two BDR headcount.

## What Kyle said
- Outbound ebbs and flows; Owner has had periods "90% inbound-driven" and is now "swinging the pendulum back" to diversify channel mix and target a certain customer type. See [[outbound-at-scale]].
- At an $8–10k ACV, cold outbound only works by obsessing over data quality and stripping wasted motion; BDRs who spend 70% of the day researching book "maybe" a meeting every two days. See [[smb-sales-motion]].
- Lead tiering illustration: 25% A, 50% B, 25% C; tier C "is who you will never close" — high dial targets on bad lists waste time. See [[icp-and-saying-no]].
- AI can deliver only "Glengarry leads"; gains came partly from training and scripts, "but a lot of it came from giving them only the leads worth calling". See [[ai-in-gtm]].
- "Our applied AI lead" (see [[person-yg]]) watched BDRs work and collapsed everything between calls into one box, AI PCR.
- Build it in-house, use [[clay]], hire a GTM engineer, or pay a consultant; then enrich the ICP and build a deterministic or ML score for which leads to call. See [[gtm-engineering]].
- Centralization: one specialist owner takes in good ideas, builds them to production quality, deploys one version to every rep; giving every rep a Claude skill is "not" the answer except when very small. Expert output is "20x better". See [[ai-operating-model]].
- Hiring math: a GTME costs twice a BDR; move two BDR headcount into one GTME. See [[gtm-engineering]].
- Opens a "more controversial" point that AEs don't need to be super AI-savvy (truncated).
