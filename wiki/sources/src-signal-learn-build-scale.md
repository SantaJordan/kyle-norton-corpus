# The Signal (AI x GTM Summit): Learn, Build, Scale: A Revenue Leader's AI Transformation Roadmap

**Show / host:** The Signal club's AI x GTM Summit / host Brendan (surname not given) · **Published:** 2026-02-11 (upload date; **recorded earlier, likely Nov–Dec 2025**: Kyle mentions Gemini 3 having just come out, "expanding that team next year", and Lemkin's SaaStr keynote "in May... earlier this year" [2025]) · **Kyle's role:** speaker (~20-minute deck + short Q&A) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/signal-learn-build-scale/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aama2OEZ9Tg

## TL;DR
- A "great bifurcation" is coming between AI-leveraged GTM orgs and the rest; AI-native CROs need first-principles LLM understanding, daily hands-on use, systems-engineering thinking beyond their function, and change-management skill—the "people-person manager" is fading.
- Roadmap: data foundations (third-party + first-party + documentation) → capability building (~70% from you and your team, then hire applied-AI talent) → use cases picked with his 5P expected-value method.
- Adoption beats cleverness: boring foundations first, make reps' jobs easier, pilot small with evals, integrate into existing surfaces (Salesforce) instead of adding new ones, and spend as much time on change management as on the build.

## What Kyle argued
- People still aren't treating AI as "a primary core existential threat and opportunity"; leaders not in the tools daily are "cooked... in 2026 certainly" (building on Lemkin's SaaStr line) @02:01–03:02. [[ai-in-gtm]] [[cro-role-and-board]]
- Owner's per-rep productivity (3–4x competitors) lets it shift spend from sales to EPD, growth marketing and brand—a compounding advantage he attributes largely to early AI adoption @04:02–05:05. [[ai-in-gtm]]
- "AI native" ≠ asking ChatGPT for answers: understand pre/post-training, feedback loops, hallucination; use tools daily; per Lemkin, personally implement one tool end to end @05:05–07:07. [[cro-role-and-board]] [[personal-ai-stack]]
- He was hands-on with RevOps/data on early implementations and has vibe-coded apps, "none of which really worked that well in a live production environment"—still worth it for fluency and taste @07:07. [[personal-ai-stack]]
- The people-manager CRO archetype is "sort of going away" (slower in enterprise and markets without an AI-native competitor); the new leader is a systems engineer, cross-functional, analytical, a tinkerer and a change manager @08:08–10:12. [[cro-role-and-board]]
- Phase 1, data foundations: third-party market data (proprietary enrichment is an advantage), first-party customer-journey data, plus structure and documentation because "AI loves writing and docs" @11:14–12:17. [[ai-in-gtm]] [[playbooks-and-documentation]]
- Phase 2, capabilities: ~70% of literacy must come from you and your team; then hire expertise (GTM AI lead, then a director of applied AI for growth—inspired by Vanta CRO Stevie Case, [[src-E07-building-a-learning-and-revenue-machine-with-stevie-case-cr]]); be wary—"90% of the consultants" don't know what they're talking about @13:17–14:18. [[ai-operating-model]] [[gtm-engineering]]
- Phase 3, use cases: stop picking AI projects by who called you; tie them to business priorities using the 5P method (possibilities, payoff, probability → expected value, then perspiration) @14:18–18:23. [[prioritization-5p]]
- Easy, high-probability wins: CRM auto-enrichment after calls (Momentum), support, inbound AI SDR @18:23–19:24. [[ai-sales-stack]] [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]]
- Adoption: boring foundations before "avatar-based sales or AI solutions engineers"; focus first on things that make reps' jobs easier, or they'll feel replaced @19:24–21:27. [[ai-in-gtm]] [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]
- Force vendors into pilots and rigorous references ("so much vaporware"); have evals/testing infrastructure @21:27. [[build-vs-buy-software]]
- Integrate, don't add surfaces—AI runs behind the scenes and lands in Salesforce @21:27–22:27. [[ai-operating-model]]
- Culture transformation and adoption deserve at least as much time as the technical build; a Slack-announcement launch explains why nobody uses it @22:27–23:28. [[culture-and-high-standards]]
- Personally spends "10 plus hours" a week learning/tinkering; ChatGPT for memory/projects, Claude for writing, testing Gemini 3; keeps a group chat of AI peers @24:32–27:38. [[personal-ai-stack]]
