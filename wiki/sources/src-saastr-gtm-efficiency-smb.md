# SaaStr: How to Build GTM Efficiency in SMB Sales - Kyle Norton, CRO @ Owner.com

**Show / host:** SaaStr (live SaaStr workshop; Q&A with Jason Lemkin and a SaaStr moderator) · **Published:** 2024-04-18 (YouTube upload date) · **Kyle's role:** guest (presenter) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lbjESpLLo8A

**Same talk as [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-owner]].** This upload (~60 min) is the full live workshop: it adds a warm-up interview (@00:00–@04:03) and ~11 minutes of audience Q&A on post-sales, SMB recruiting, and interviewing (@48:12–@59:34) that the edited 2024-04-30 upload cuts. The presentation itself is word-for-word the same; timestamps differ between the two pages.

## TL;DR
- Owner went from ~$3M ARR (June 2022) to $21M ARR in 22 months at a sub-1 burn multiple and ~4.5:1 LTV:CAC by first saying no — pausing growth, cutting ~40% of prospects, and fixing early churn with an ML "expected GMV" score.
- Scale smart: invest early in prospect data (40%→85% accuracy roughly doubled BDR efficiency; connect rate 3%→14–16%), fund pipeline before AEs (2 BDRs per AE), hire fewer/better-paid reps, give sales single-threaded ownership of post-sales, and bring in RevOps/enablement early (even on contract).
- Operate with rigor: async-prepped MBRs, stack-rank performance management, relentless documentation, and P&L fluency for revenue leaders; Q&A adds his recruiting playbook (~15 hrs/week, pre-watch video, real strategic-project case studies).

## What Kyle argued
- Hardest thing in the past year was early churn, balancing deal quality against the sales number (@02:01). See [[churn-and-retention]].
- Say no: pause the growth plan until churn is fixed; trying to scale too early "made us scale slower" (@11:12–@12:16). See [[icp-and-saying-no]].
- Do less — fewer segments, customer types and reps — and nail one niche before hitting the gas (@16:19).
- Invest "way earlier than you think" in prospect data; BDRs shouldn't research leads; don't hire more than one or two BDRs until data is fixed (@16:19–@20:27). See [[gtm-engineering]], [[outbound-at-scale]].
- Most companies have a pipeline problem, not a rep-capacity problem; use the calendar test (3–4 customer meetings/day) (@22:27–@23:29). See [[capacity-planning]].
- Fewer, better, higher-paid reps beat more average ones (@23:29–@24:30). See [[talent-density]].
- Single-threaded ownership: sales leader owns post-sales, which kills finger-pointing — but AEs should not do onboarding themselves (@24:30–@26:33, @48:12–@49:13). See [[org-design]].
- Hire/invest in RevOps and enablement earlier, via fractional contract if needed (@26:33–@28:38). See [[revops]].
- Three operating tools: MBR, tight performance management, documentation (@29:43). See [[metrics-and-forecasting]], [[culture-and-high-standards]], [[playbooks-and-documentation]].
- Revenue leaders need P&L fluency (@37:54).
- Comp: paid above-market early for top talent in an ambiguous startup, ratcheted comp back as structure/playbooks matured; considering tying comp to customer outcome (EGMV, activation) (@40:00–@41:05). See [[comp-plans]].
- Scripting ("golden script") is "the most powerful thing you could do"; leaders from mostly-inbound backgrounds are unlikely to learn outbound late (@46:11–@48:12).
- Recruit SMB reps from startups one stage ahead selling to similar buyers; big-company hires rarely work; screen for preparation and conscientiousness (@50:14–@56:30). See [[sales-hiring]], [[hiring-scorecards]].

## Numbers & facts he cited
- 16 years in sales, 15 in leadership, 10 in tech; second early-stage startup; previously ran "a quarter billion dollar go-to-market unit at Shopify" (@04:03).
- Joined June 2022 at ~$3M ARR, shortly after Owner's Series A; Owner is Series B (@00:00, @04:03, @07:06).
- ARR: $3M→$6M in H2 2022; $6M→$16M in 2023; "just broken through 21 million in ARR in 22 months"; strings of 10% MoM growth (@07:06).
- Burn multiple of one or sub-one (@08:06). Grew 2.5x in 2023 at ~4.5:1 LTV:CAC; growth rate better in Q4 2023 than Q1 (@09:07).
- Inherited 1 manager + 4 reps; exited 2 of 4 AEs within ~45 days (@08:06). AE team 6→10 in 2023; BDRs 1–2 → 10–12 → 20+ (@09:07).
- Said no to ~40% of prospects; closed-won fell in his first and second months (@12:16).
- Prior enterprise BDR role: 40% of BDR time wasted on bad data (@18:24).
- Data accuracy 40%→85%; each BDR now >$60K closed-won ARR per month (@18:24). Call-to-DM-connect 3% → 14–16% (@20:27).
- First startup: laid off ~25% of over-hired team, burned ~6 months (@21:27).
- Paid ~$30K above SMB market; hired top-5% President's Club performers (@24:30).
- Single-threaded ownership adopted October 2023 (@25:32).
- RevOps/enablement leaders started on contract at 10, then 15, then 20 hours/week; Steve (RevOps) has worked with him ~8 years, third stint (@26:33–@27:34).
- MBR within four days of month close; RevOps data ready two days before; 90-minute pre-read block (@30:43–@31:45).
- AI documentation hack: 2–4x content speed (@36:52).
- 2024: basically doubled BDR team while modestly adding AEs; BDRs generate 6–12 closed-won deals cold per month; aggregate monthly bookings more than doubled with AE team roughly constant (@42:05–@44:09).
- At League (first tech role, global BDR): ~5x per-BDR output (@45:10).
- Recruiting: up to ~15 hours/week; for the VP Sales hire met 22 first-round candidates, ran six case studies (~1.5 hrs each + 1 hr prep); ~3 hrs/week writing LinkedIn content, all for candidate attraction (@53:24–@54:25).

## Frameworks & named ideas
- **Three pillars:** say no / scale smart / operational excellence (@05:03).
- **Expected GMV (EGMV) score** — ML on review quality, web traffic, third-party marketplace volume (@14:17–@15:18).
- **"Garden of Eden"** enrichment flow — incomplete leads get punted back for re-enrichment (@17:24).
- **Calendar test** (credited to Sam Blond) (@22:27–@23:29).
- **Single-threaded ownership** of pre- and post-sale (@24:30).
- **Async MBR** — template, RevOps populates, managers answer, comments, discuss only top topics (@29:43–@33:49).
- **Golden script** (@46:11).
- **Pre-watch video** for candidates; scorecard criteria preparation + conscientiousness (@56:30).
- **Strategic-project case study** instead of a "first 90 days" plan (@54:25).
