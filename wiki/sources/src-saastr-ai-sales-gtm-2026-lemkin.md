# SaaStr AI: AI, Sales + GTM in 2026: This Changes Everything (with Jason Lemkin)

**Show / host:** SaaStr AI (live on-stage session at SaaStr Annual, "day three"; Jason Lemkin presents slides and interviews Kyle) · **Published:** 2025-05-28 · **Kyle's role:** guest (co-presenter) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/saastr-ai-sales-gtm-2026-lemkin/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9oLxWRF9Uys

Note: recorded the same week Owner announced its $1B-valuation raise ("Tuesday", per Lemkin @01:03). Lemkin speaks for more than half the session; his predictions (AI better than 80% of reps by April 2026, commissions halving, mediocre BDRs out of work, CROs managing 50/50 human-AI teams, "June 30" AI deadline) are **his**, not Kyle's. No speaker labels; lines below are attributed to Kyle only when he speaks of Owner in the first person. Whisper renders SaaStr as "Sastr" and Amanda Kahlow as "Amanda Colo".

## TL;DR
- Kyle has "mandated" AI as a mission-critical priority, leads from the front (Clay workflows, ChatGPT projects, vibe-coding internal apps in Windsurf off the Lever API) and now screens every leadership hire for being "AI native" — but **not** ICs, because Owner's AI is centralized in RevOps/BizOps (hiring a GTM AI lead).
- Results so far: reps "three and four times more efficient" than competitors; Momentum automation has raised revenue-generating activity time 25–30%; Crescendo + Guru contain almost 50% of tier-1 support tickets; AI augments reps while a OneMind "superhuman" anchors a semi-self-serve journey.
- Lessons: orchestrate the whole customer journey before inserting AI (the "management intelligence layer"), pick vendors by founder quality, demand pilots, and commit to weekly tuning — "None of it's magic." The CRO who can't think in systems "will not survive in this era."
