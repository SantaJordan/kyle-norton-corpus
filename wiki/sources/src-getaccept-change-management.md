# GetAccept (On the Record #14): Change management made simple

**Show / host:** On the Record (GetAccept) / Morgan Ingram · **Published:** 2025-02-13 · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/getaccept-change-management/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qrd0pfxow0c

Note: the "July survey" and "July" comp change @09:25–10:26, @19:38 put recording in the second half of 2024. Whisper renders GetAccept as "GetXcept".

## TL;DR
- Kyle's "number one challenge": change management as Owner scaled (~10x revenue in two-plus years, team from ~3 to 100+); a summer enablement survey showed reps couldn't keep up with constant changes.
- Fixes: move role knowledge from Notion into Guru (searchable, Slack-answerable), stop using Slack for important updates, and a **traffic-light (green/yellow/red) change methodology** wired into project templates so red/yellow changes must go through enablement.
- Bigger lesson: write down an **operating methodology** of principles (slow is smooth; customer → business → team → reps → us; async by default; minimum effective dosage; do the work, not talk about it) — he'd pull all of this forward to the 20–100 stage next time.

- Keep a pulse via skip-levels with frontline managers, listening to Slack channels, and surveys every six months; the survey reprioritized the roadmap @08:23–09:25. [[operating-rhythm]]
- Surveys only work with trust: share the raw findings deck (even pointed feedback) with the whole team and loop back on what you changed @10:26–11:26. [[culture-and-high-standards]]
- Documentation can become too much: everything was in Notion but it was overwhelming and unsearchable; they moved role-specific content into Guru, searchable from Slack with auto-answers, rather than buying another AI point solution @13:28–18:36. [[playbooks-and-documentation]] [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]

- Traffic-light change management: red-light changes (e.g., July comp overhaul) require a Notion page, explainer video, enablement-led deployment, team-meeting agendas and 1:1 discussion; project templates auto-create enablement tasks @18:36–20:38, @24:41. - No pushback because managers are steeped in servant leadership and were "in the line of fire" for confused reps @21:38–22:39. [[culture-and-high-standards]]
- Environmental design / nudge theory (James Clear): a project = 2+ people, 2+ weeks, 2+ tasks and needs a brief; SLT agenda template auto-pulls key projects @22:39–25:44. [[operating-rhythm]] [[playbooks-and-documentation]]
- In hindsight he'd pull this forward to the 20–100 stage; he is now writing Owner's "operating methodology" of principles @26:44–29:49. [[playbooks-and-documentation]]
