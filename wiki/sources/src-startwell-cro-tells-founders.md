# StartWell: The CRO Who Tells Founders Exactly What They Don't Want to Hear About Sales

**Show / host:** StartWell (Toronto coworking/innovation hub podcast) / host not named in transcript · **Published:** 2024-08-01 · **Kyle's role:** guest (in studio, Toronto) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/startwell-cro-tells-founders/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6U2KVbpk28

Note: the opening @00:00–02:03 is a cold-open teaser that repeats @44:04–48:09. The long Canadian-media/government digression @28:36–43:00 is mostly the host; Kyle's contributions there are brief.

## TL;DR
- Owner origin story (Adam Guild's Minecraft → mom's dog-grooming shop → restaurants; $1M ARR to zero in COVID, rebuilt online ordering in five weeks); Kyle joined June 2022 at ~$3M, now "three to 25 ARR in the last two years", ~130 people, aiming "25 to 100 now in the next 24 months".
- Mid-2024 Owner was **remote-first with no employee offices**, ~60% of Kyle's team LatAm-based; he defended remote via Gallup Q12 (only 1–2 items need proximity) — a position later revised for SDRs/BDRs (see 2025 sources).
- Culture and hiring: "we're not a work-life balance culture… we're here to win" stated up front in a pre-interview video; 60–70% DNA scorecard; full-stack revenue ownership (sales + onboarding + CS) with RevOps/enablement hired early lowered churn.

## What Kyle argued
- Adam Guild's founder–market fit / "earned secret": rescuing his mother's dog-grooming business led to restaurant digital marketing; pivot to online ordering in five weeks when COVID zeroed usage-based revenue @03:04–07:10. [[person-adam-guild]] [[owner-com]]
- Remote-first lets Owner hire the best (ex-Tobi design partner, ex-Instagram engineer), but most companies "transpose what they did in an office" instead of redesigning from first principles; don't force remote socials @09:10–11:14. [[office-vs-remote]]
- Retention is a function of engagement; of Gallup's Q12 only one or two items (e.g., best friend at work) depend on proximity; Owner brings a customer to the engineering team weekly and features customers at all-hands @11:14–13:16. [[office-vs-remote]] [[recognition-and-motivation]]
- At Shopify in 2020 he was "horrible at managing remote" and spent a week reading (Matt Mullenweg's *Distributed*, GitLab's handbook) @14:17–15:18, @56:21. [[office-vs-remote]] [[shopify]]
- A great revenue leader operates "at two levels of abstraction simultaneously": fine-grained mechanics of the machine plus quarters/years-out bets @17:20–18:20. [[cro-role-and-board]] [[capacity-planning]]
- Placing channel bets for 25→100: a national food-distributor partnership (thousands of reps "knocking on doors, delivering crates of food") could be "a quarter or more of our net new revenue in 2025"; outbound still works via data/infrastructure (his SaaStr talk) @18:20–20:25. [[capacity-planning]] [[outbound-at-scale]]
- Paid channels are arbitrage that gets competed away; Owner inbound ~80–90% Meta/Google but "That can't be the only thing we do"; at League he cut performance marketing to zero for ABM (CEO emails, handwritten notes, dinner series) @21:26–23:29. [[marketing-alignment]]
- Inbox "torched with gen AI"; companies must shift to near-bound/warm intros — and treat difficulty as opportunity ("the obstacle is the way", anti-fragile) @24:30–27:36. [[outbound-at-scale]] [[executive-performance-and-mindset]]
- Canada has fewer founders "willing to take the risk" to go all the way; reinvention needed around $50M ARR @32:40–33:43. [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- Gen Z layoff trauma → "if I could get fired, then I'm not gonna try"; his advice: trying hard doesn't guarantee success but "not trying hard will guarantee that you're not going to be successful" @44:04–46:04. [[career-and-leadership]]
- Hyper-transparent hiring video: great product and people, but "We're not a work-life balance culture"; four-day-week companies can't build multi-billion outcomes — "I'll bet on the five and a half day a week company" @46:04–49:12. [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[sales-hiring]]
- Meaningful work = service (Haidt's *Happiness Hypothesis*); leadership values: servant leadership #1, growth #2, transparency/authenticity #3; favorite part of job is telling people they're promoted @50:13–53:16. [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[recognition-and-motivation]]
- SMB early-career hiring: "60 to 70% of our hiring framework is DNA"; stress-is-enhancing mindset (Alia Crum); history of success in anything @54:18–56:21. [[hiring-scorecards]]
- Full-stack revenue: bringing onboarding then CS under him improved outcomes "within weeks"; CEOs who nest CS under sales but only talk bookings break it; told Steve (RevOps) to spend 60% of time on CS systems; invest in systems over people @57:22–61:28. [[churn-and-retention]] [[org-design]] [[revops]] [[person-steve-dinner]]
- Hire senior RevOps/enablement early (Steve at "less than 10 million ARR, maybe 5 million"): directors for "an eight-person sales team" look crazy but are cheap relative to the 12-month plan @62:28–63:29. [[revops]] [[capacity-planning]]
- Opinionated product + radically transparent selling: less customization, more performance; switching customers "pop like 50, 100%" in direct delivery volume; only right-fit customers opt in → highest-rated restaurant tech on G2 @64:30–68:35. [[point-of-view-positioning]] [[icp-and-saying-no]]
- Paid ads don't pencil for mom-and-pop takeout ($45 ticket, $10 margin); restaurants win via search + word of mouth — Owner is "all in on SEO", hired a top SEO expert, and welcomes algorithm chaos @68:35–72:39. [[smb-sales-motion]] [[marketing-alignment]]
