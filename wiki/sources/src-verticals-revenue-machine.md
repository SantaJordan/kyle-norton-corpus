# Verticals: How to Build a Revenue Machine with Kyle Norton, CRO (Ep 7)

**Show / host:** Verticals: A Weekly Biz Show on Vertical AI — hosts Nick and Luke (Kyle is the "Vertical Titan" guest after the news segment, from @09:19) · **Published:** 2025-12-03 (YouTube upload date) · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/verticals-revenue-machine/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V-EdpNEhYgs

Attribution note: the first ~9 minutes are host news (Palantir/Weave GTM research, BrainCo, Doctronic) — not Kyle. The "industry engineer" role and 100K ACV / 12–18 month cycles are a host's own company, not Owner.

## TL;DR
- Owner.com went from ~$2M ARR / ~30 people to ~$50M "in just over three years," still growing "almost two and a half X year over year"; Kyle's edge is treating revenue as a "revenue factory" instrumented end to end and fixed via a monthly business review.
- In vertical/VSMB SaaS the revenue leader must be a data-loving systems thinker; for reps, drive, learning, and organization beat sales craft (from a hiring retro); hire for deal-size relevance, not industry.
- "Strategic congruence": product, ACV, GTM, leader, comp, and culture must fit — "to run my playbook, you've got to run all of the playbook." AI success is about transformation, change management, and resourcing, not use cases.

## Frameworks & named ideas
- **Founder framework** (4 traits) and the "post-Adam" bar for checks/advisory.
- **Revenue factory / manufacturing line**: volume × conversion = output; micro-stage instrumentation.
- **MBR loop**: data foundations → problem identification → highest payoff/probability → rally resources → project execution.
- **4 Ps** (possibilities, probability, payoff, perspiration), modified from Annie Duke.
- **Traffic light change management** (green/yellow/red), auto-generated enablement projects in Notion; nod to *Atomic Habits*.
- **High probability practices** ("know the game you're playing").
- **Hiring DNA**: drive, learning, organization > sales craft; deal-size relevance > domain.
- **Strategic congruence** (also discussed on his GTM Now episode).
- **Solve the money problem** / painkiller wedge; Shopify:Lightspeed :: Owner:Toast.
- **Talent density** as the compounding critical mass.
