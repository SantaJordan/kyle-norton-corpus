# How To Optimize Exec Performance | Kevin Bailey, CEO @ Dreamfuel

**Type:** episode companion (guest: Kevin Bailey, co-founder & CEO, Dreamfuel; part 1 of 2, see [[src-substack-2026-05-29-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]]) · **Published:** 2026-04-03 · **Paywalled:** no · **URL:** https://www.therevenueleadershippodcast.com/p/how-to-optimize-exec-performance · **Raw:** `raw/substack/2026-04-03-how-to-optimize-exec-performance.md`

**Attribution note.** The frameworks (performance chain, four states, warm-up/hold/cool-down, ACT) are Kevin Bailey's. Kyle's own content is unusually heavy here: first-person confessions, his cold-plunge practice, amor fati, his starter list, and a hiring/promotion criterion. Research citations are Kyle's write-up additions.

## TL;DR
- Kyle admits he spent years in a near-constant flight response without knowing it; physiology drives emotions, cognition, behavior and results.
- Bailey's model: four nervous-system states (flight, freeze, fight, focus), aim for equanimity, and treat every meeting like a game with warm-up, hold-form and cool-down protocols.
- Kyle's personal prescription: daily cold plunge with heart-rate feedback + 10 min meditation for 30 days; he weighs self-regulation in hiring and promotion.

## Kyle's argument
- Self-disclosure: he was "in a near-constant flight response and had no idea"; his estimate of how often he lives in flight is "closer to 110% of the time." See [[executive-performance-and-mindset]].
- Performance chain: physiology → emotions → feelings → cognition → behaviors → results; fix the bottom (guest: Kevin Bailey). Kyle: "You can’t think your way out of a bad physiological state."
- Four states (flight, freeze, fight, focus) on heart-rate × emotional-tonality axes; goal is equanimity (guest: Kevin Bailey).
- Meetings are the executive performance arena; warm up (30 breaths + 15s hold, visualization), hold form (relax stomach, "best thing that could have happened," physiological sigh), cool down (anger journaling) (guest: Kevin Bailey).
- Kyle's experience: a Dreamfuel breathwork + creative visualization session carried him "through a grind period"; he sent his team through it too. See [[culture-and-high-standards]].
- Kyle's reframe is amor fati (keeps a Ryan Holiday coin on his desk); he self-identifies as "a suppressor," and Bailey pushed back on suppression.
- Kyle's strongest personal claim: cold plunging (Plunge Air, most mornings) is "the most underrated awareness tool available to executives"; the heart-rate feedback loop is what makes it training. He can bring HR "into the low 60s" during cold exposure.
- ACT framework: Awareness, Choose, Transition (guest: Kevin Bailey); Kyle recommends it as the starting point.
- Drop one dopamine waster for 30 days (guest: Kevin Bailey); Kyle adds: curate inputs, unfollow negative/sarcastic accounts, "Your brain is an LLM" (half-joking).
- Leadership/hiring: self-regulation is something Kyle "weigh[s] heavily in hiring and promotion decisions." See [[sales-hiring]], [[career-and-leadership]].
- Elite standard is consistency ("the tightest bound of performance"), not grinding hardest.

## Numbers & facts
- Kevin Bailey (guest): three-time Inc 500 founder; first company to $12M ARR and 100 employees by age 27; master's in applied neuroscience, King's College London; coached 1,000+ leaders incl. at Owner.com; Dreamfuel teaches 52 transition techniques.
- Warm-up protocol: 30 deep breaths, hold 15 seconds on the 30th exhale; Kyle's Dreamfuel session was 20 minutes of somatic breathwork.
- Kyle: plunges most mornings; brings heart rate to "the low 60s" during cold exposure; first plunges spike HR to 100+.

## Frameworks & named ideas

- **Four states** (Bailey): Flight, Freeze, Fight, Focus; target = equanimity.
- **Warm up / Hold form / Cool down** meeting protocol (Bailey).
- **ACT: Awareness, Choose, Transition** (Bailey).
