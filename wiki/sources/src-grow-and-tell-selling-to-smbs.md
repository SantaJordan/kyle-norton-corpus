# Grow & Tell: Leading Revenue at Shopify & Owner - Kyle Norton on selling to SMBs

**Show / host:** Grow & Tell (Dock) / Alex Kracov (transcript spells "Krakow"/"Krakoff") · **Published:** 2024-02-05 (YouTube upload date; metadata had none) · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VUxK-W94JFM

## TL;DR
- Career arc: VP of sales at League (Series A healthtech; over-funding forced a premature push upmarket, never true PMF) → Shopify POS/retail (5 → 70+ reps in 18 months, COVID pivot) → Owner, joined out of FOMO about founder Adam Guild.
- Signature idea: **point-of-view positioning** — admit what you're not, sell a belief about the future, let buyers opt in or out fast. At Owner: month-to-month contracts + an explicit "less choice, more money" trade-off.
- Owner is fully digital and (then) fully remote; Kyle argues values and employee experience (Gallup Q12) beat "culture," and sustains pace through health discipline and "requalify for your job" learning.

## What Kyle argued
- League raised $25M at Series A on the back of a third-time founder before SaaS revenue; he joined for the learning @02:02. [[career-and-leadership]]
- Raising too much drove League to chase enterprise before it had references — "every deal was brute force" — and forced short-term decisions; he shares this lesson with founders he advises @06:07–09:11. [[fundraising-and-investability]] [[icp-and-saying-no]]
- Startup people should consider big companies for different skills, pattern recognition and mental models @10:12–11:14. [[career-and-leadership]]
- Shopify POS won against Square/Lightspeed not on feature parity but on ecosystem: unified operations, one customer record, omnichannel marketing, and being future-proof @14:24–15:25. [[smb-sales-motion]]
- **Point-of-view positioning**: concede weaknesses (e.g., Lightspeed's reporting is better) and sell the belief; it builds trust and gets "the wrong people out of your funnel fast" @15:25–16:26. [[icp-and-saying-no]]
- Shopify's late-2020 regionalized "every region sells all products" reorg was "basically a train wreck"; he wrote an eight-page doc arguing against it @18:28–20:33. [[org-design]]
- Great PMF covers up weak go-to-market ops; Shopify wasn't sophisticated in GTM data/RevOps @21:34–23:41. [[revops]]
- In COVID, leadership guaranteed reps' full OTE and made decisions "from the viewpoint of the customer," changing qualification weekly @24:42–26:46. [[comp-plans]] [[churn-and-retention]]
- Investing philosophy: market and product can change; what determines success is a truly elite founder @27:47–28:50. [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- Owner earns trust with skeptical restaurants via month-to-month contracts, data export, and a radically transparent trade-off pitch ("choose Bentobox" if design matters most) @35:58–39:03. [[smb-sales-motion]] [[churn-and-retention]]
- Fully digital sales beats feet-on-the-street because data on the highest-fit restaurants outweighs in-person conversion gains @40:05–41:07. [[outbound-at-scale]]
- Remote works if you hire for internal drive; "culture is actually less important than values and employee experience"; Gallup Q12 items (except "best friend at work") can be delivered remotely @42:09–44:15. [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[org-design]]
- Be transparent with candidates: Owner is "a performance culture," "not a real work-life balance culture" @48:25. [[sales-hiring]] [[culture-and-high-standards]]
- Keep pace through discipline (exercise, sleep, diet) and keep up with growth through daily learning; cites Tobi's "100% better" rule @46:21–50:30. [[executive-performance-and-mindset]]

## Numbers & facts he cited
- League: Series A of $25M with no SaaS revenue yet; founder had sold two prior companies "for hundreds of millions of dollars" @02:02; League ~30-person company at the time @04:04; company target went up 3x the next year @07:07.
- Host-stated: at League Kyle grew revenue to $20M+ and the team to ~40 reps over three years; joined League 2016, Shopify 2019 @01:00.
- Shopify POS: ~$70M revenue business when he joined @11:14; team grew "from like five people to 70 and change in 18 months" @12:17; mix shifted from ~80% expansion to ~50/50 new vs. existing during COVID @13:22; Plus sales org "probably four to six hundred people" @18:28; team under four months of tenure when COVID hit @24:42.
- Owner: customers often double direct delivery business "in like 90 days" @34:57; third-party apps charge ~30% commission @33:57; reps close "probably 3x the revenue on a per rep basis" vs. competitors @41:07; sales-team employee NPS "like 87" @45:18.
- Joined Owner when his son was three months old @29:53; Adam Guild read *Made to Stick* within 18 hours of Kyle mentioning it @30:55.

## Frameworks & named ideas
- **Point-of-view positioning** (League, Shopify, Owner) @15:25–17:28.
- **Market / product / team** — team (elite founder) is the variable that matters @27:47–28:50.
- **Gallup Q12** as the remote-engagement playbook @44:15.
- **Hyper-transparency**: monthly exec business assessment published to the whole sales team/company + AMA @45:18.
- **Requalify for your job** (Tobi Lütke) @49:29; *Atomic Habits* as "answer key" @49:29–50:30.

## Stories / anecdotes
