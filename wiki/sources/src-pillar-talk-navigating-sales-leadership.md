# Pillar Talk (Rick Smolen): Navigating Sales Leadership with Kyle Norton

**Show / host:** Pillar Talk / Rick Smolen · **Published:** 2024-02-23 · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership/transcript.md` · **Audio:** https://www.buzzsprout.com/2301388/episodes/14555449-navigating-sales-leadership-with-kyle-norton.mp3

Host frame: six pillars of sales leadership (hiring, operating rhythm, business planning, mastering the craft, communication, ownership); Kyle picked **operating rhythm** as his superpower. Host's closing summary @41:17–end is not Kyle.

## TL;DR
- Operating rhythm = "the set of standards that we use to help us know we can hit targets today and in the future" — a cascade from annual channel bets (Owner "from 16 to 40 this year", then "40 to 80") down to managers' midday dashboard checks.
- Origin lessons: missing a number early as a second-line leader because he didn't bench-build BDR headcount a quarter ahead; building Vision Critical's BDR reporting from scratch (the "Lead Audit Sheet") and an early-morning dashboard routine.
- Signature tactic: a monthly **Revenue Review & Game Plan** (numbers, commentary, shout-outs, red/yellow/green strategic-focus review, next month's quotas, "what you need to know", "ruminations") shared with the whole company plus an AMA — transparency starts at the top.

## What Kyle argued
- Operating rhythm determines much of a sales leader's success and is the hardest shift for new frontline managers, who must see around the corner @01:04–03:10. [[operating-rhythm]]
- Painful early lesson: he had pipeline for the current quarter but realized too late he needed ~25% more pipeline and more BDRs next quarter; hiring + notice + ramp meant he was "six weeks or eight weeks too late" and missed @03:10–06:16. [[capacity-planning]]
- Now he thinks a year+ out ("already thinking about the 2025 number today") and about channel bets — inbound, outbound, partner, self-serve (a 6–9-month build) — because 40→80 can't be done with "the same things" @05:13–08:22. [[capacity-planning]] [[metrics-and-forecasting]]
- Rhythms cascade: annual (exec) → quarterly (headcount, pipeline, interlock) → monthly/weekly → daily in transactional SMB @08:22. [[operating-rhythm]] [[smb-sales-motion]]
- Thrown into management after ~a year as a rep (great financial crisis, promote-from-within); real education was running an enterprise BDR team at Vision Critical — studied *Sales Acceleration Formula*, *Predictable Revenue*, *From Impossible to Inevitable* @09:25–11:29. [[career-and-leadership]] [[person-jason-lemkin]]
- Vision Critical: BDRs reported to AEs (no BDR managers); he consolidated, taught himself Salesforce, built reporting from scratch with RevOps (Navin), created the Lead Audit Sheet (lead relevance, freshness, rep execution, conversions) and an hour of early-morning dashboard review to nudge managers @12:29–16:37. [[revops]] [[outbound-at-scale]]
- "the secret sauce to operating rhythms is what's the system that you can give to your frontline leaders so that they can execute almost as well as you" @14:31. [[playbooks-and-documentation]]
- Owner monthly math: confirm reps, marketing pipeline, win rates add up (most SMB deals created and closed in-month); reps pick their own target and work back the "math of sales" to daily leads and dials — agency plus accountability @16:37–17:37. [[metrics-and-forecasting]] [[operating-rhythm]]
- Managers check core dashboards midday and nudge (e.g., Monday after the Super Bowl); a big spreadsheet of daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly rhythms is pushed into calendars as reminders @17:37–19:43. [[operating-rhythm]]
- Systems are the same across SMB and enterprise; "The time horizons are just different" @20:44. [[operating-rhythm]]
- Engagement comes from Gallup Q12-type drivers — mission, trust, transparency, ability to succeed, learning and career path (hedonic adaptation); the key system is coaching: he checks managers' calendars for dedicated coaching sessions separate from 1:1s and checks in Gong how many calls managers reviewed; promote from within ("80% of the battle") @21:45–24:51. [[recognition-and-motivation]] [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]
- Skip-levels and Slack with reps; "feedback is a gift" culture; asks new managers' reps for feedback on them @24:51–25:51. [[culture-and-high-standards]]
- Transparency is top-down: his monthly review goes to execs, the board and the whole org, followed by an AMA; told reps comp "has not changed since 2021 because that market was silly and we were overpaying"; Adam shared his own critical feedback at an all-hands @25:51–27:56. [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[comp-plans]] [[person-adam-guild]]
- Revenue Review & Game Plan structure, built from a Notion "leadership command center" button; written the night the month closes, shipped within a day; big changes get a change log + video explaining the data and why @28:58–36:12. [[operating-rhythm]] [[playbooks-and-documentation]]
- Advice: block the time weeks ahead, build a template (spend 50–100% more time once to make it 50% easier the next 50 times), and send it to everybody @38:13–40:16. [[operating-rhythm]]
