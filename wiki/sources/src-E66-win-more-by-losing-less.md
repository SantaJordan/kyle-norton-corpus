# E66: Win More (by Losing Less)
**Show:** The Revenue Leadership Podcast · **Guest:** Kevin Bailey, co-founder & CEO @ Dreamfuel ([[person-kevin-bailey]]) · **Published:** 2026-04-01 · **Kyle's role:** host · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/own-show/E66-win-more-by-losing-less/transcript.md`

## TL;DR
- Bailey's thesis: elite = consistency ("raise that floor", "a tight bound of performance"). His performance chain runs physiology → emotions → feelings → cognition → behaviors → results, so you fix physiology first. There are four nervous-system states (flight, freeze, fight, focus) on heart-rate × emotional-tonality axes, and the target is equanimity.
- Treat meetings as the executive performance arena, with a protocol to warm up (30 breaths + hold, then creative or rehearsal visualization), hold form (relax stomach, "best thing that could have happened", physiological sigh) and cool down (anger journaling). To get started, use ACT (Awareness, Choose, Transition).
- Kyle shares a lot of himself: a daily cold plunge with heart-rate feedback, a Dreamfuel breathwork/visualization session that left his hands vibrating for hours, the "Kyle number" team visualization, amor fati, "I am a suppressor", and a hiring/promotion rule based on self-regulation.

## Guest's core ideas
- Elite performance = consistency. "Raise that floor." "Just a tight bound of performance." @06:07–07:08 ([[executive-performance-and-mindset]])
- Performance chain: physiology → emotions → feelings → cognition → behaviors → results, running in both directions. Cortisol plus adrenaline puts you in flight "doesn't matter if it was the best day of your life". @08:10–11:15
- About 95% of thoughts are automatic, and roughly 70% of those are negative (NATs). "You gotta win the battle with yourself before you win the battle outside." @25:36–26:38
- Four states: flight (high HR, negative), freeze (low HR, negative, including procrastination), fight (high HR, positive, dopamine/noradrenaline, "use mindfully"), focus (low HR, positive, serotonin). Target equanimity: fight for rallying a team, focus for deep work. @15:22–34:48
- Awareness comes first. Somatic breathwork and cold plunges build body awareness because "your body always tells the story". Bailey's biggest life investment is his meditation practice. @19:28–29:41
- A cold plunge is a warm-up that does three things: resilience, dopamine/energy, and somatic awareness. @35:50–37:50
- Meeting protocol. Warm up with mental reset breathing (30 deep breaths, exhale, hold 15–20s), then creative visualization (picture the prospect giving a case study) or rehearsal visualization (see yourself handle what could go wrong). Hold form: relax the stomach, say "best thing that could have happened", use the physiological sigh. Cool down: vent into a burnable journal instead of suppressing. @39:52–69:27
- The Alex session explained: somatic breathwork (200–300 breaths) then narrated visualization "to install a memory of the future", which builds resilience. @49:01–53:06
- ACT: Awareness, Choose, Transition. Start with focal (breath) meditation and progress to open monitoring. Dreamfuel teaches 52 transition techniques. @70:29–74:32
- Non-duality is "the academic word for enlightenment" and is the topic of Bailey's master's dissertation. The brain "compares things, chooses a preference, and then creates momentum to get the preference". @74:32–81:42
- Drop one dopamine waster (social media, gambling, drinking); his family drops screens for Lent. "Learn how to recover well." @88:53–89:54
- Scaling to a team starts with the leader: "You gotta model the behavior you wanna see." @83:44 (part 2: [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]])
