# E73: The AI Enabling 600 Customer-Facing Reps
**Show:** The Revenue Leadership Podcast · **Guest:** Lauren Hughes, VP Revenue Effectiveness @ Justworks · **Published:** 2026-08-26 · **Kyle's role:** host · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/own-show/E73-the-ai-enabling-600-customer-facing-reps/transcript.md`

## TL;DR
- Hughes runs RevOps and enablement under one "revenue effectiveness" roof for 600+ customer-facing staff in 20 roles. She cut enablement from 16 to 6, removed traditional roles (instructional designer, LMS admin, onboarding coordinator, field enablement), and reinvested in RevOps and technical builders. Personalized enablement now runs on a central Claude skills library with feedback loops. Ramp fell from 18 to 8 months, and AE bookings per head rose 28%.
- Kyle shares the most Owner detail he gives in these five episodes: Owner built its own sales methodology from scratch (renamed "napkin math" to "impact math" so the acronym spells PROFIT), "win rates like climb overnight," shared language is enforced obsessively, and AI output has "bot breath" unless you iterate.
- Systems before headcount: remove friction rather than add "fuel" (Kyle cites Loran Nordgren's *The Human Element*). Hughes doubled SDR productivity in one quarter with a dialer, signals, a new engagement platform, a comp refresh and a promotion path. Both warn about "confidently wrong" AI analysis and the need for a data harness.

## Guest's core ideas
- Diagnose before acting: sit down 1:1 with everyone, do ride-alongs across SDR, AE, CS and support, and lead with data. In the first 60 days she found there was no revenue planning and analysis function. @02:02–04:05 ([[revops]])
- Enablement shrank from 32 to 16 before she joined. All 16 were working on the same thing, and reps were trained on country launches months before they needed them. She cut to 6 and rebuilt with AI content creators and builders (now 10–12). @04:05–07:09 ([[org-design]], [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]])
- Roles retired: onboarding coordinator (Workday-triggered AI scheduling and knowledge checks via Tangelo), LMS admin (no LMS; Spekit "Spacket" in Salesforce, a Slack bot, a clean help center/Confluence), instructional designer (bite-sized learning), field enablement (doesn't scale to 20 roles). @07:09–17:20 ([[sales-coaching-and-enablement]], [[build-vs-buy-software]])
- AI enablement planning system: personas built from Granola-recorded ride-alongs. The first ChatGPT attempts were "slop," then they rebuilt in Claude. A central library of Claude skills produces project plans, content, Synthesia videos and AI role plays, with "revenue readiness professionals" as human-in-the-loop. Results feed back into the skills. @17:20–31:38 ([[ai-in-gtm]], [[playbooks-and-documentation]])
- Measure training efficacy: conversation-intelligence mentions, then pipeline, bookings, closed-lost and attach-rate velocity. Enablement must be "a systems thinker." @27:32–29:34 ([[metrics-and-forecasting]])
- Ramp levers: kept a few weeks of in-classroom onboarding, then role-specific paths. Measurement lets SDRs graduate early. Hiring profile changed (SDR-promoted AEs ramp best, as do adjacent-industry hires, e.g., rental car sales). Intelligent document processing for health-insurance census quotes, prototyped in RevOps and later built into the product. @39:45–46:50 ([[sales-hiring]], [[build-vs-buy-software]])
- SDR rebuild: one SDR spent 30 minutes researching a contact who replied "not interested." Fixes: signals, a dialer POC in four days, a new engagement platform, Gong recording (only 50% of calls had been recorded), a comp refresh adding a pipeline-ARR component, a higher comp band for technical profiles, and AE promotions. @47:51–53:59, @66:10–69:16 ([[outbound-at-scale]], [[comp-plans]])
- RKO promises to the field: time back, personalized capability, and more opportunity ("no excuse" not to deliver more pipeline). @56:00
- The CRO's 120-page weekly deck is being replaced by an AI "CRO skill" that alerts on deviations, built around ~50 KPIs. Outside departments may not publish GTM analytics without her review. @60:04–64:09 ([[metrics-and-forecasting]])
