# Momentum (AI Advantage webinar): Planning Your 2025 AI Strategy

**Show / host:** Momentum's "AI Advantage" webinar series (first episode) / host Jonathan Carford ("Coach"), with Momentum co-founder Ashley · **Published:** 2024-12-03 · **Kyle's role:** featured guest (short deck + Q&A; vendor-hosted—Owner is a Momentum customer) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/momentum-planning-2025-ai-strategy/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RznL6-oauPE

Note: @22:26–28:30 is mostly Ashley presenting Momentum's own internal AI use; not Kyle's views (except his "do it in ChatGPT instead" aside @24:28).

## TL;DR
- Three principles for a 2025 AI strategy: data quality first (garbage in, garbage out—Momentum was an early buy for that reason), keep AI applications narrow to limit hallucination, and augment reps rather than replace them.
- AI today is "world's best intern": good at pattern-matching, enrichment, drafting; not trusted to blast unreviewed content—he is "not yet a believer" in replacing BDRs with AI and fears scorching inboxes the way LinkedIn InMail was scorched.
- Owner's live uses: Momentum prompts writing structured Salesforce fields from calls (close attempts, fee waivers, under-construction flags, SPICED notes), lead scoring/routing (EGMV × E-win → I-route), Crescendo replacing tier-1 BPO support, Guru, Avara sims, custom GPTs, an AI website grader.

## What Kyle argued
- First principle: AI outcomes depend on CRM data quality; fix it with clean prospect data plus tooling that turns unstructured call/email data into discrete fields @04:07–07:11. [[ai-in-gtm]] [[ai-sales-stack]] [[momentum]]
- Second: specificity—tools that promise to pick customers, write and send everything have "way too much surface area"; apply AI where it can pattern-match a known "good" @07:11–08:11. [[ai-in-gtm]]
- Third (flagged as speculation): the best AI will "enhance and augment the rep as opposed to replacing it"; not yet a believer in replacing his BDR team with AI @08:11–09:12. [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]]
- "RGAs over everything": strip low-value rote work so reps spend time on revenue-generating activities @09:12–11:14. [[outbound-at-scale]]
- Internal routing tool: EGMV (deal size) × E-win (win probability) → I-route score that drops leads straight into BDR cadences—no list building @10:13. [[gtm-engineering]] [[outbound-at-scale]]
- AI is the "world's best intern"—drafts, but humans keep discernment; carpet-bombing bland AI messaging risks burning the channel, as happened to the LinkedIn inbox @11:14–13:17. [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]]
- His own writing: ChatGPT drafts, he does "a pretty fulsome edit" for voice @14:18. [[personal-ai-stack]]
- Momentum fields let him measure things reps would never log (close attempts, setup-and-tie-down usage, fee waivers, partner mentions) and drive workflows (approval flags for restaurants not yet open) @17:20–19:21. [[sales-methodology]] [[metrics-and-forecasting]]
- The CRO superpower: create a field for the specific behavior you're trying to change, then watch a daily report @20:24–21:25. [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]] [[revops]]
- Build intuition by exposure: "every time you go to Google something, just do it in ChatGPT instead" @24:28. [[personal-ai-stack]]
