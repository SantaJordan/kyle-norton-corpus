# Top Founders (Nathan Latka): $5m in ARR? Your CRO Should Be Focused on These 3 Things

**Show / host:** Top Founders with Nathan Latka (recording of Kyle's ~20-minute keynote at Latka's live event, "recorded March 28th and 29th" 2024 per Latka's intro) · **Published:** 2024-08-01 · **Kyle's role:** speaker (solo talk) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/top-founders-latka-5m-arr-cro/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZM59pcrW0I4

**Same deck as [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] / [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-owner]]** (focus → scale smart → operational excellence), delivered as a compressed 20-minute version at a different event roughly three weeks earlier. Not a re-upload: wording and a few numbers differ (e.g. contact quality 40%→**80%** here vs. 85% at SaaStr). Read the SaaStr pages for the long version and Q&A.

## TL;DR
- Owner (Series B, "about 20 million ARR") got efficient by fixing early churn first: turned away ~40% of closable deals, built an "estimated GMB" score and kept raising the floor.
- Before scaling headcount, "nail your prospect data": scraped/enriched restaurant DB + PDL mobile numbers took contact quality 40%→80% and decision-maker connect 3%→16%; each BDR now sources >$60K closed-won ACV a month.
- Scale smart (less is more, calendar test, sales owns onboarding, early RevOps/enablement—even on contract), then run three minimum-effective habits: async MBRs, stack-rank performance management, documentation (ChatGPT as co-author).

## What Kyle argued
- Audience: companies with product-market fit and repeatable deals that "rhyme"; you can't slam the gas on "friends of the founder deals" @01:00. [[smb-sales-motion]]
- Three phases: find product-market fit, find go-to-market fit, then GTM fit "that really pays off" before adding headcount @03:03. [[capacity-planning]]
- Fix early churn before scaling; the hard reset was "mostly on the sales team"; presented a declining growth number at his first board meeting @04:06–06:08. [[churn-and-retention]] [[icp-and-saying-no]] [[cro-role-and-board]]
- Data-driven ICP: with BizOps, built the EGMB (estimated GMB) score; raising the floor three times made the model more efficient while sacrificing "almost nothing in terms of growth" @06:08–07:12. [[icp-and-saying-no]]
- The one slide to take away: nail prospect data before scaling spend; outbound always gets a higher quality threshold than inbound @08:13–09:14. [[outbound-at-scale]] [[gtm-engineering]]
- Don't let BDRs do lead research—"if they say they need to do anything with them, means your model's broken" @12:16. [[outbound-at-scale]]
- Less is more: at his first startup they scaled off one "unicorn rep" and fired 25–30% of hires; do fewer segments, reps and markets @12:16–13:16. [[talent-density]]
- Sam Blond's calendar test: unless reps have four or five customer meetings a day, "you don't need more reps" @14:18. [[capacity-planning]]
- Single-threaded ownership: merging onboarding into sales made things "multiple months better in the course of weeks"; sales leaders should own onboarding @14:18–15:19. [[org-design]] [[churn-and-retention]]
- Invest in RevOps/enablement earlier than feels comfortable; middle path is a contractor doing ~5 hours a week @15:19–16:20. [[revops]]
- Three Pareto habits: async-prepped MBRs in Notion, stack-rank performance management, documentation @16:20–19:22. [[operating-rhythm]] [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[playbooks-and-documentation]]
- Revenue leaders need P&L fluency; both his sales managers are in Pavilion's revenue architecture school @20:23–21:24. [[cro-role-and-board]]

## Numbers & facts he cited
- Owner: Series B, "about 20 million ARR"; at Owner "just about two years"; "We started at three. We're just passing 20 right now" @00:00–02:03. Shopify: ran "a quarter billion dollar go-to-market unit" @00:00.
- Joined June 2022; "upwards of like a 30% churn" in the first 90 days post-close; inherited four reps, fired half, moved the manager to a rep @02:03–03:03.
- 2023: pilot team of six, then 10 AEs plus BDRs @03:03. "last year we grew two and a half X, burn multiple under one, four and a half to one LTV CAC" @04:06.
- "We basically turned away 40% of closed ones that we would have closed 30 days before I got there"; first two months each worse than the previous @05:07.
- Share of deals in the healthy EGMB cohort: "40, maybe 50%" before → "basically like 80%" now @06:08. EGMB floor raised 1,500 → 2,000 → 2,500 @07:12.
- Market ~300,000 mom-and-pop restaurants; scraped 50,000 highest-probability fits into Snowflake; built an internal ML score @09:14.
- Typical BDR team works "between 40 and 60%" irrelevant leads (his analysis "three companies ago") @10:15.
- Contact quality 40% → 80%; People Data Labs for mobile numbers @10:15–11:16. Decision-maker contact per 100 calls: 3 → 12% → 16%; a BDR talks to 16 DMs/day and books 2–3 meetings/day; "every BDR on my team kicks out over $60,000 in closed one ACV" per month @11:16.
- First startup: fired "25, 30%" of reps hired after one star rep @13:16.
- RevOps + enablement leaders ≈ "$400,000 in salary" over 4–5 reps; contractors at ~5 hours a week @15:19–16:20.
- Documentation with ChatGPT at "like a two to four X higher rate" @19:22.

## Frameworks & named ideas
