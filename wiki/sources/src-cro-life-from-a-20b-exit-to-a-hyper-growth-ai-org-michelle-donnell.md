# E62: CRO Life: from a $20B Exit, to a Hyper-Growth AI Org (Michelle Donnelly, CRO @ Crescendo)
**Show:** The Revenue Leadership Podcast · **Guest:** Michelle Donnelly, CRO @ Crescendo (ex-CRO @ Groq, transcribed "Grock"; about 10 years @ Salesforce) · **Published:** 2026-02-04 (RSS; metadata has no date) · **Kyle's role:** host · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/own-show/cro-life-from-a-20b-exit-to-a-hyper-growth-ai-org-michelle-donnell/transcript.md`

## TL;DR
- Kyle framed the episode around what has not changed in the AI era. Donnelly's answers were pain-first discovery, executive multi-threading, and talent. What did change is noise, intensity and speed.
- Crescendo (AI-native contact center, $0 to $100M ARR in under two years) differentiates through simple messaging (the fifth pitch deck in six months), "show me" custom-built agents instead of demos, outcome-based pricing, and "become a customer of your customer".
- Career and leadership: pick moves through your smartest network, not résumé strategy. Run on three priorities a quarter. Use AI to cut ramp from five months to three rather than cutting headcount.

## Guest's core ideas
- Career moves came from asking "10 of the smartest people I know". A Deloitte contact pointed her to Nvidia and the AI wave. She is "very strategic in how I grow a business" but "never been strategic about my career". @05:04–14:13 ([[career-and-leadership]])
- Groq: no product-market fit at first, with a new go-to-market motion tried every month. Fit arrived with Llama and ChatGPT, through side-by-side speed demos ("like a 100 demos a day") and a Dubai inference-center deal ("a Ramco digital bought it for 1.5 billion"). She left when the motion pivoted to developer and inside sales. Her hardest lesson: "Project Market fit". @16:15–19:19, @49:55
- The AI market is "so noisy". Crescendo differentiates on the AI-to-human handoff. CX leaders lose budget to CIOs, so Crescendo must "go up" to the C-suite. @20:21–23:23 ([[point-of-view-positioning]])
- Messaging: "If you can't understand who we are in 30 seconds, we have hashtag failed." The team tests it live through BDRs and customers. They're on the fifth deck in six months. "clarity is kindness". @25:24–30:30 ([[point-of-view-positioning]], [[marketing-alignment]])
- Scale first: "nine out of 10 companies don't hire enough capacity". She didn't cut 20% of headcount for AI. Instead she uses AI to shorten ramp from 5 months to 3. She merged the install-base and new-logo teams. @30:30–32:33 ([[capacity-planning]], [[org-design]])
- "Show me" instead of demos: build a custom AI agent from a prospect's public knowledge base within 24 hours. Send prospects to try live customer experiences. Bring operations people, not sellers. Price on outcomes ("If you're not happy, we're not going to charge you"). @26:26, @33:35–39:42 ([[sales-methodology]])
- Three priorities a quarter that everyone can repeat, reinforced through a biweekly "CRO connect". She runs 13–14 direct reports on purpose to stay close to the work. Gong is used for forecasting and win/loss. "Harmony" is an AI sales launchpad. @39:42–46:52 ([[prioritization-5p]], [[operating-rhythm]], [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]])
- Hire "gritty" people. All of her BDRs are athletes (crew, swimming). "Make changes quickly." @47:54–49:55 ([[sales-hiring]])
