# GTMnow: GTM 5: Slow is Smooth; Smooth is Fast

**Show / host:** GTMnow (GTM Fund's Go-To-Market podcast) / Scott Barker · **Published:** 2022-11-01 · **Kyle's role:** guest (then SVP of Sales at Owner, ~5 months in; also a GTM Fund LP) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth/transcript.md` · **URL:** https://thegtmnewsletter.substack.com/p/gtm-5-slow-is-smooth-smooth-is-fast-d19

Note: the opening @00:00–01:00 is a cold-open teaser that repeats @14:25–15:26. Not a duplicate of [[src-sales-leadership-podcast-15-league]] despite the shared "slow is smooth" title phrase — different show, year and content.

## TL;DR
- Earliest Owner-era interview in the wiki: what Kyle does differently as a second-time early-stage sales head vs League — prioritize health, don't scale reps before you've figured out PMF/ICP ("slow is smooth and smooth is fast"), and niche down.
- Early Owner results he credits to patience: early churn down ~85%, average deal size up ~50%; TAM deliberately narrowed from ~300K US independent restaurants to a ~10K (call it 30K) core segment.
- Sales-leader comp: inspired by Shopify (no variable for senior revenue leaders, Ian Black's "don't chase this year"), he argues against the 50/50 head-of-sales plan; personal lesson = "learning to disagree well" (socialize 1:1, seek advice, common objectives, ration urgency).

## What Kyle argued
- E-commerce still "very much in the early innings" (~25–30% retail penetration, ~15% YoY trend); startups should avoid building what platforms consider core (e.g., "Shopify is checkout") @07:14–13:24. [[shopify]]
- #1 change vs League: prioritize physical and mental health — he was "really burnt out" at League; brought in mental-health practitioners (Jeff Riseley, Sales Mental Health Alliance) for teams @14:25–16:26. [[executive-performance-and-mindset]]
- At League the spreadsheet pressured him to put reps in seats before figuring out PMF, positioning and ICP; hiring/training distracted from figuring it out, ending in layoffs @16:26–21:37. [[capacity-planning]] [[sales-hiring]]
- At Owner: prioritize systems, people and playbooks before hitting the gas — fundamentals that let you raise "in any economic environment" @17:27–19:32. [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- The pressure is mutually reinforcing (CEO, board, his own comp); "not being honest with yourself" about one or two good reps out of ten — someone must be "the sober second thought" @19:32–21:37. [[cro-role-and-board]]
- Shopify's "100-year company" value is an operating system: senior revenue leaders get base + stock, no variable; Ian Black told him not to chase an inherited number at the cost of foundations — so he invested in onboarding/launch @21:37–24:42. [[comp-plans]] [[shopify]]
- Disdain for the 50/50 head-of-sales comp plan: short-term incentives produce misaligned customers, poor sales–CS relations and churn; founders should tie revenue leaders to company growth numbers @24:42–26:44. [[comp-plans]] [[churn-and-retention]]
- Learning to disagree well: competitive sales/boxing/wrestling wiring made his disagreements win-lose; exec coach taught him to socialize big ideas 1:1, seek advice (Adam Grant's *Think Again*: don't preach/prosecute), return to shared objectives, and reserve "urgent" for the truly urgent @27:46–33:54. [[career-and-leadership]] [[executive-performance-and-mindset]]
- Focus and niching down: the CEO spotted a lower-churn, higher-performing segment; narrowing TAM ~90% sharpened messaging and productivity. League took any deal that could "fog the glass" @33:54–37:00. [[icp-and-saying-no]] [[person-adam-guild]] [[prioritization-5p]]
- Entering a new market (listener Q): a senior revenue leader must own the push; with little traction pair a proven home-market rep with a local hire to isolate variables; with PMF send a manager + 1–2 top reps + local hires as a pod; seed new markets with top talent @38:02–44:09. [[org-design]] [[sales-hiring]]
- Career learning: "growth is the only thing that matters" — your rate of learning beats your starting point @45:11–47:14. [[career-and-leadership]]

## Numbers & facts he cited
- Since he started at Owner (June 2022): "reduced our early churn rate by like 85%", "increased our average deal size by like 50%" @17:27–18:28.
- TAM: "call it 300,000 independent restaurants in the US" → "a TAM of about 10,000. Yeah, I call it 30,000, but 10 of them that we're really, really interested in" @34:55.
- Owner (Series A) targeting "$10 million in more revenue" which "That's gonna come from a couple thousand customers" @35:55.
- Title then: SVP of Sales at Owner; joined via Scott Barker's intro during GTM Fund's investment; said he wouldn't do Series A again @04:09–05:11.
- Shopify: three years; senior revenue roles had base + stock only; boss Ian Black @22:38.
- E-com: penetration ~25–30% of retail (was ~15% "a few years ago"); growth ~30% YoY → ~10% post-COVID; trendline ~15%; Knix sold for "320 million bucks" @07:14–08:16.
- Prior international expansions: Vision Critical (North America → Europe), Shopify (North America → Europe), League (Canada → US); Shopify sent three people to seed Europe POS @38:02–42:07.

## Frameworks & named ideas

- **Sober second thought** on hiring-plan pressure @20:34.
- **100-year company** incentives; anti-50/50 leader comp @21:37–26:44.
- **Learning to disagree well**: socialize 1:1 → seek advice → common objectives → ration urgency @29:51–33:54.
- **New-market pod** (proven rep + local hire; isolate variables) @40:06–42:07.
