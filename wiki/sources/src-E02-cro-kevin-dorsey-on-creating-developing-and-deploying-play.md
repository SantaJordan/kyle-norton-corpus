# E2: CRO Kevin Dorsey on creating, developing, and deploying playbooks
**Show:** The Revenue Leadership Podcast · **Guest:** Kevin "KD" Dorsey, CRO @ Finally (ex-Bench, PatientPop, ServiceTitan; founder, Sales Leadership Accelerator) · **Published:** 2024-10-02 (RSS) · **Kyle's role:** host · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/own-show/E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play/transcript.md`

## TL;DR
- A playbook is "a documented set of best practices" covering five Ps: people, prospect, problem, process, product. It should make reps good; going beyond it makes them great.
- Build it with the team. In a weekly one-hour "weekly wiggle", managers and reps build rather than discuss. Base plays on observed top performers, judged by efficiency metrics rather than raw revenue.
- Deploy through practice ("meant to be practiced, not read"), by sending people to the playbook, by using it in coaching, and by updating it constantly. Measure with the metric plus an issue-diagnosis checklist.

## Guest's core ideas
- The five Ps, in order. People: virtues, which reps sign, plus expectations, quotas and promotion paths. Prospect: the buyer's matrix (Jill Konrath). Problem: PIC chart (problem, implication, cause). Process: stages, scripts, call scorecards, issue-diagnosis checklists. Product last, connected back to the problems. @10:29–16:56 ([[playbooks-and-documentation]])
- If your top three reps don't follow the playbook, "the playbook's the problem". "Imitate first, innovate second." KD personally runs a 3-hour first onboarding session every month. @20:01–23:12 ([[sales-coaching-and-enablement]])
- Build with the team in the weekly wiggle (one hour, build not discuss) and "Never Alone" Slack channels. Pick the rep to model on efficiency: a 35% close rate beats a 14% rep with higher revenue. Observe, don't ask (BIPSY). @26:23–41:17 ([[playbooks-and-documentation]])
- Prioritize "volume or volume": a high-frequency problem beats a loud one. Tie every problem to a metric. "Controlled burns." @36:04–37:09 ([[prioritization-5p]], [[metrics-and-forecasting]])
- Don't put reps on the phones in four days. New reps practice 3–4 hours a day with AI role play. "if you can't do it in practice, I know you can't do it in the game." @42:17–46:25 ([[sales-coaching-and-enablement]])
- Deploy by practicing, referencing, using in coaching, and updating. The quarterly "studying greatness" exercise assigns each manager a metric. A playbook's version history shows when anyone last looked at it. @47:27–51:42 ([[operating-rhythm]])
- Measure adherence through 1:1 docs (number-one metric, issue diagnosis, coaching plan) and issue-diagnosis checklists per metric. "Dark week": a week off work to work on work. "If you can't explain it, you can't claim it." @55:50–63:04 ([[playbooks-and-documentation]], [[metrics-and-forecasting]])
