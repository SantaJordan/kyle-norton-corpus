## TL;DR
- Pre-Owner Kyle (2020, Shopify Retail): joined to scratch-build a ~7-person sales team; chose it on people, product, problems. COVID response was Tobi's mandate: "rip up every plan" and "start from the merchant".
- Startup sales advice: narrow focus on the customer, sell to change agents who share your worldview (not ROI-spreadsheet buyers), founders stay in sales; early reps are generalist "trailblazers", not coin-operated.
- Leadership: servant leadership first; "you can't lead people until you can lead yourself" (exec coach, therapy); traditional frat-house sales culture should be discarded; "my fault first".
