# E70: Manufacture Motivation, Sell More
**Show:** The Revenue Leadership Podcast · **Guest:** Kevin Bailey, CEO @ Dreamfuel · **Published:** 2026-05-27 · **Kyle's role:** host · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/own-show/E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more/transcript.md`

Part two of Kevin Bailey's first appearance (recorded "about two months ago"), which Kyle calls "the single most popular episode I've ever recorded by a pretty good shot." This one covers how to cascade mental-performance practice through a revenue team. Dreamfuel has coached executives at Owner.com (per the cold open @00:00).

## TL;DR
- Mental performance can't be deployed on a team as a program. The leader has to live it first. Then give people "breakthrough moments" (group breathwork, visualization, sauna/cold plunge), introduce one habit at a time, and gamify it. A players adopt first; C players push back.
- Dopamine is the motivation molecule and leaders can "regulate" a team's dopamine, mainly by recognizing effort and personal bests rather than only outcomes. That inoculates the team against slumps. Kyle says this is how Owner got through its hardest quarter (Q1) without missing.
- Tactical tools: a ~5-minute pre-call warm-up (30 rapid breaths, breath-hold, pump-up music, journal the call as if it's already over, i.e. "mental contrasting"). A "serotonin day" every two weeks. Sleep hygiene (Matt Walker's 3-2-1). Opt-in HRV trackers with recovery contests.

## Guest's core ideas
- The leader must embody the practices. They "cascade down to the team naturally when the leader embodies the practices." @05:04 ([[executive-performance-and-mindset]])
- Adoption curve: A players chase an extra 5–10%, young B players can gain 10–20%, and C players "punching the clock" are the skeptics. @06:08–07:12 ([[talent-density]])
- Create breakthrough moments first. Greenlight Guru case: breathwork at the start of a sales meeting put the team in flow; co-founder John Speer went from skeptic to investor. @08:15–11:17
- HRV is the best measurable correlate of flow state. Raise HRV ~20% and you materially raise time in flow. Dreamfuel's app texts reps short questions on resilience, confidence and discipline; leaders see only aggregates. @17:29–20:33
- Start with frontline managers (the highest-stress role). Run quarterly or monthly team visualizations. One habit at a time, because habits take 30–120 days. @20:33–24:41 ([[sales-coaching-and-enablement]])
- Pre-call warm-up: about 30 rapid breaths, hold, pump-up music, then journal dated after the call as if it went well. Bailey says his close rate "about doubled" doing this for a year of enterprise selling. @26:44–32:53
- Woo-woo vs neuroscience manifestation: don't just visualize the win; use that energy to reinforce hard behaviors and overcome "limbic friction." @34:57–36:01
- Why people quit: when noradrenaline is spent without dopamine reward, the brain quits. Leaders must learn to control team dopamine (triggers: progress, recognition, achievement, surprise/novelty). Recognizing effort "pulse[s] the team dopamine." @36:01–40:07 ([[culture-and-high-standards]])
- Serotonin day every two weeks: a full day without goals or time pressure. It produces "bottom-up" creative thinking. "In the age of AI, it's all the quality of our thinking, not so much about the grind." @46:22–52:32 ([[executive-performance-and-mindset]])
- Shared language comes from neuroscience. Start with the four performance zones (activation × emotionality: fight, flight, focus, freeze). @65:54–67:57
