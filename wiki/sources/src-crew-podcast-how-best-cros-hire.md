# The Crew Podcast (aired as the Vibe Scaling Podcast): How The Best CROs Actually Hire Salespeople

**Show / host:** Metadata says "The Crew Podcast"; in the audio the host introduces it as the *Vibe Scaling Podcast*, host Chris Balestris (partner, Vibe Scaling) · **Published:** 2026-03-24 · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xLSkTfmB-e8

## TL;DR
- Hiring: question every inherited sales-hiring habit. Owner's own data showed mock-call case-study scores barely predicted in-role performance. Discrete scorecarding beats the charisma "halo effect," and being organized turned out to be the most common trait of top reps.
- Infrastructure: bring in RevOps and enablement as two separate functions, and do it early. RevOps (tools reps use) and GTM engineering / applied AI (building intelligence) are distinct. AI is built centrally and pushed into the rep's existing tools.
- Career: companies and employees are "barbelling." Take a smaller title at a tier-one hyper-grower rather than VP at a good-not-great company, and work hard, because the ZIRP era of easy outcomes was "make-believe."

## What Kyle argued
- He moved on from "almost half" of the inherited sales team when he joined Owner. The rest are still there almost four years later @08:14–09:16. [[talent-density]]
- Hyper-transparency in hiring. For director-plus hires he does **mutual references**: a Superhuman snippet listing every direct report from the past 10+ years and his peers. He flagged this as possibly never discussed on a podcast before @10:18. [[sales-hiring]]
- "you have to question everything." Mock-call case-study scores were not well correlated with in-role performance. He also prefers algorithmic stage-weighted forecasting (lead volume, opps booked, carryover pipeline, historical percentages) to bottoms-up commits, at least in SMB @12:26. [[hiring-scorecards]] [[metrics-and-forecasting]]
- Language is "very underrated." Owner built its own sales methodology ("Plusprofit"; the leading "t" stands for table setting) that blends ACE/SPICE (Winning by Design), gap selling, Challenger, and *Made to Stick*. New leaders must "create within the system," not import BANT, Miller Heiman, or Command of the Message @13:28–16:43. [[playbooks-and-documentation]] [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]
- RevOps and enablement are two separate functions, and both belong early. Steve (VP RevOps, ~10 years across three companies) and Rob (enablement) started as consultants and went full-time. This applies only with strong PMF and funding in SMB/mid-market @17:43–19:45. [[revops]] [[org-design]]
- Going from 10 to 40 reps in 18 months requires infrastructure. Owner "grew faster in 2025 than we grew in 2024" because of early GTM investments @19:45. [[capacity-planning]]
- RevOps 80/20 is "boring stuff": lead quality, a mapped and enriched market, scored accounts, contacts, and transcripts auto-filling Salesforce. DataLane (Owner was the first customer) made the BDR function "three times more effective" @20:46–21:46. [[revops]] [[outbound-at-scale]]
- RevOps and GTME are "quite distinct functions." RevOps owns rep tools. An applied AI team builds intelligence. RevOps and enablement handle the last-mile adoption that GTMEs usually miss. He uses a basketball-team analogy @22:47–24:57. [[gtm-engineering]] [[org-design]]
- Scorecard to beat the halo effect (Kahneman). Hire for DNA → craft → knowledge. DNA covers resilience, history of excellence, coachability, curiosity, preparedness, and organization. The best reps are "sicko workers and they're operators" @29:05–31:10. Ambiverts beat extroverts @30:05. [[hiring-scorecards]] [[sales-hiring]]
- The case study isn't "broken," it "only tells you one thing." Owner now has candidates redo it after feedback, which turns it into a coachability signal @32:13–33:14. [[hiring-scorecards]]
- Logo halo: Shopify's inbound-heavy, too-good product didn't produce great sales talent ("five pound weights") @34:18. [[sales-hiring]] [[shopify]]
- Company selection: a "flight to quality" means there is more risk than ever in good-not-great companies, point solutions, and A-quality (not A+) teams. Take the first- or second-line seat at a tier-one hyper-grower. Diligence by talking to customers, board members, and especially former employees @36:26–43:52. [[career-and-leadership]]
- Owner itself is "more triple, triple, double, double plus than... 10X," but has durability plus an AI story @37:33. [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- Point solutions are now easy to build internally. Non-technical people at Owner built "multiple companies" worth of internal apps in Claude Code in 60 days @38:37. [[build-vs-buy-software]]
- No "$250,000 BDR" A BDR who can generate that much pipeline becomes a GTME or growth marketer. Comp take-off goes to high-leverage roles and AI-native VPs/CROs @54:25–56:38. [[comp-plans]] [[ai-in-gtm]]
- Centralized versus decentralized AI: "We are a very centralized model. Our ICs don't do much in the tools themselves" @55:32. [[ai-in-gtm]]
- Stack: Momentum (acquired by Salesforce), Avara (AI sales simulator, 50+ tuned sims), Trigg (post-sales journey automation), and OneMind (avatars), which Owner is repurposing to sell a full deal to a new customer base @57:43–60:48. [[ai-sales-stack]] [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]]
- Personal AI: Claude Code plus OpenClaw chief of staff "Kai" over Telegram. It keeps dossiers on every leader and drafts leaders' weekly updates. He built a Claude Code performance-review skill: "maybe 30 minutes each" versus "hours per person" @60:48–66:05. [[ai-in-gtm]] [[executive-performance-and-mindset]]
- Work ethic: "you got to be strapped the hell in right now". The pre-2022 era was "make-believe." AI-capable high-agency employees are "worth like 4x" @51:17–52:18. [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[career-and-leadership]]

## New or changed vs. earlier positions
- **ARR jump:** "almost at 100" (Mar 2026). The host notes his notes said "two to 50" only six months earlier @05:08. That is consistent with the ~$50M mid-2025 figure.
- **Case study:** consistent with the earlier finding that case-study scores didn't predict performance. New here are the **redo-after-feedback** twist (now a coachability signal) and **organization/conscientiousness** as the most common trait of top reps.
- **Mutual references** are new: "I don't know if I've ever talked about this on a podcast" @10:18.
- **AI selling full deals (OneMind):** contrasts with the Nov 2024 line *we don't do any AI outbound* The OneMind use is AI selling an entire deal to a new customer segment the reps can't serve economically. It is not described as outbound @60:48.
- **Centralized AI** is stated as a firm model ("Our ICs don't do much in the tools themselves").
- **Salesforce/Momentum:** Momentum was "bought by Salesforce" @57:43.
