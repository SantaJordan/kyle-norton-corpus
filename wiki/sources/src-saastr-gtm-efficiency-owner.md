# SaaStr: How to Build Go-to-Market Efficiency in SMB Sales with Owner.com CRO Kyle Norton

**Show / host:** SaaStr Podcast (SaaStr AI channel; Q&A with Jason Lemkin) · **Published:** 2024-04-30 (YouTube upload date) · **Kyle's role:** guest (presenter) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/saastr-gtm-efficiency-owner/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KAz03HJ5nHg

**Duplicate talk.** This is an edited (~41 min) re-upload of the live SaaStr workshop documented in full at [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] (uploaded 2024-04-18). It drops the warm-up interview and the audience Q&A after Lemkin's questions; the presentation content is the same, but timestamps here are for this upload. **For concept pages, prefer citing [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]]** (the superset); use this page only when linking to this specific video. Do not count the two as independent evidence.

## TL;DR
- Same three pillars: say no (pause growth, cut ~40% of prospects, ML "expected GMV" score to fix early churn), scale smart (prospect data first, 2 BDRs per AE, fewer better-paid reps, single-threaded ownership, early RevOps/enablement), operate with rigor (async MBR, stack-rank performance management, documentation, P&L fluency).
- Growth context: ~$3M ARR at June 2022 join → $21M ARR in 22 months at a sub-1 burn multiple.
- Lemkin Q&A: comp right-sized as playbooks matured; 2024 is about "making the reps beg for mercy"; boiler-room roots explain his outbound skill.

## What Kyle argued
- Fix customer quality before quantity; pausing the growth plan was deliberate (@06:10–@07:14). See [[icp-and-saying-no]], [[churn-and-retention]].
- Invest early in prospect data; BDRs should do no lead research (@11:19–@14:23). See [[gtm-engineering]], [[outbound-at-scale]].
- Reps × quota models are wrong without pipeline; 80% of companies have a pipeline problem (@16:25–@17:25). See [[capacity-planning]].
- Hire fewer, better, higher-paid reps (@17:25–@18:26). See [[talent-density]].
- Single-threaded ownership of post-sales by the sales leader (@18:26–@20:35). See [[org-design]].
- Bring in RevOps and enablement early, even fractionally (@20:35–@22:41). See [[revops]].
- MBR + performance management + documentation are the Pareto operating tools (@22:41–@30:00). See [[metrics-and-forecasting]], [[culture-and-high-standards]], [[playbooks-and-documentation]].
- Revenue leaders must become P&L fluent (@31:03). See [[cro-role-and-board]].
- Comp ratcheted back as the role got more structured; exploring tying comp to customer outcome (@32:07–@33:09). See [[comp-plans]].
- Golden-script discipline; inbound-bred VPs rarely learn outbound (@38:16–@39:19).

## Numbers & facts he cited
Same figures as [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]]; timestamps for this upload:
- Joined ~$3M ARR; previously ran a quarter-billion-dollar GTM unit at Shopify (@01:01).
- $3M→$6M (H2 2022), $6M→$16M (2023), $21M ARR in 22 months; sub-1 burn multiple (@03:04).
- Inherited 1 manager + 4 reps; exited 2 of 4 AEs in ~45 days; AEs 6→10 in 2023 (@04:08). BDRs 1–2 → 10–12 → 20+; 2.5x growth at ~4.5:1 LTV:CAC (@05:08).
- Said no to ~40% of prospects (@08:14).
- Data accuracy 40%→85%; >$60K closed-won ARR per BDR per month (@13:22). Connect rate 3% → 14–16% (@14:23–@15:24).
- First startup 25% layoff (@16:25). Paid ~$30K above market, top-5% President's Club hires (@18:26).
- Single-threaded ownership from October 2023 (@19:31).
- MBR within four days of close (@23:41).
- BDRs generate 6–12 closed-won deals cold per month; per-month bookings more than doubled with AE headcount roughly constant (@35:13–@36:14).
- ~5x per-BDR output at League (@37:16).
