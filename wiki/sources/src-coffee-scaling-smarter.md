# Coffee: Scaling Smarter: Building High-Velocity Sales Teams for Long-Term Growth

**Show / host:** Revenue Renegades (YouTube channel "Coffee") / Doug Campbell · **Published:** 2025-05-05 · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/coffee-scaling-smarter/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=acDTXCZmoGg

Note: recorded around March 2025 ("running this March Madness spiff right now" @21:20) while Kyle was preparing to move to the Bay Area @00:00, @36:24. Whisper spellings kept inside quotes ("Avara", "Data Lane", "LWAM" = last week of month).

## TL;DR
- Owner grew from $2.5M ARR / 5-person team to "well over $30 million" / 100+ because Kyle invested early in RevOps and enablement — but only advise that if you know you're "playing for a big outcome."
- High-velocity SMB operating choices: AEs paid on customer *launch*, no pipeline reviews (input metrics forecast within ~5%), and a set of end-of-month levers (spiffs, LTOs, AQL rerouting, managers on the front line).
- Founder advice: know the game you're playing, narrow ICP, point-of-view positioning ("choice, not comparison"), month-to-month contracts and long-term decisions; AI is to augment reps on top of great internal data, not AI-SDR spam.
