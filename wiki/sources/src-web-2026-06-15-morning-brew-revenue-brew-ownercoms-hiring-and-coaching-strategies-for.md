# Morning Brew / Revenue Brew: Owner.com's hiring and coaching strategies for driving sales growth

**Outlet:** Morning Brew (Revenue Brew), by Tricia Crimmins · **Published:** 2026-06-15 · **Type:** written interview ("edited for length and clarity") · **URL:** https://www.revenuebrew.com/stories/owner-com-kyle-norton-hiring-coaching-strategies · **Raw:** `raw/web/2026-06-15-morning-brew-revenue-brew-ownercoms-hiring-and-coaching-strategies-for.md`

*Truncated: the raw text is capped at 6,000 characters and stops mid-answer on cutting underperformers. The subtitle's "marshmallow test" methodology section is not captured. Editor glosses in brackets are the outlet's, and one is wrong: "XDR [extended detection and response]" — at Owner, XDR means SDR/BDR. "making tiles" is likely *making dials*.*

## TL;DR
- Owner as "a vertical AI company"; the opinionated product lets it run experiments across ~10,000 websites and roll winners out to every customer.
- Headline efficiency claim: **comp-to-close 20x — a $150,000 rep brings $3M ARR per year**.
- In person: decided midway through 2024; from the start of 2025 all net-new hires are five days in office; "Basically every single metric got better".
- Since the start of 2025, ~**70% of new hires are brand-new university grads**, made possible by a heavy enablement investment (a PhD learning-experience designer).

## What Kyle said
- Owner gives restaurants website, online ordering, email/SMS marketing, branded app, review management, listings, "AI phone ordering", and point of sale in one solution; less choice and flexibility than competitors is the point. See [[point-of-view-positioning]].
- Owner is "most famous" for AI in GTM: ML lead scoring and customer-success prediction "in mid-2022", then all-in after ChatGPT; a biz-ops/data leader, the RevOps leader, and Kyle drove adoption; there is a whole applied AI team. See [[ai-in-gtm]].
- Reps don't do list research or list building; CRM enrichment, notes, and deal handoff to onboarding are automated, so XDRs make dials and build pipeline and AEs run demos and close. See [[ai-in-gtm]].
- Fully remote when he started in 2022; decided midway through 2024 to return to in-person teams; no forced return-to-office, but every net-new hire from early 2025 is five days in person. Ramp, productivity, effort, output, culture, engagement, and retention all improved. The cost: experienced AEs became harder to hire, so Owner mostly promotes from within. See [[office-vs-remote]].
- ~70% of hires since the start of 2025 are new grads. See [[sales-hiring]].
- Onboarding is "one of the best in the world", built on neuroscience, elite-performance principles, and adult-learning psychology; hands-on, not lecture; a PhD on the enablement team does only learning experience design. See [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]].
- On joining: four reps, he "parted ways with half of them"; many of the first 20–50 hires came from his network (truncated here). See [[talent-density]], [[culture-and-high-standards]].

- Experiments across "like 10,000 websites".
- Company size: "from five employees to over 250" (interviewer's question, not Kyle).

- Join: "four reps", parted with "half"; "first 20 to 50 hires" largely from his network.
