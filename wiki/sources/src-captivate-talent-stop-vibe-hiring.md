# Captivate Talent (Human First): How to Stop Vibe Hiring and Build a Sales Team That Actually Scales

**Show / host:** Human First, the go-to-market hiring show (Captivate Talent) / host Danielle Parker, head of marketing · **Published:** 2026-04-14 · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CIu_Cjql9Do

## TL;DR
- AI use is table stakes. Kyle lays out an **AI sophistication ladder**: chat → custom GPTs/gems → Clay/Make/n8n workflows → Claude Code/Cowork → vibe-coded apps. His view on how much AI fluency different levels need has changed. Seniors and leverage roles (RevOps) must be "AI-pilled." Frontline managers need it less because Owner builds AI centrally.
- Owner's rep hiring flipped from **experienced President's Club reps hired remote** to **~80% straight-from-college hires, in person in Toronto**, trained through a heavy enablement program. Internal promotion is now a bit over 50/50.
- The fix for "vibe hiring" is structure: the same questions in the same order, discrete scorecard criteria to box in charisma, divided-and-conquered interviewers, and retros that tie scores to in-role results.

## New or changed vs. earlier positions
- **Explicit change on AI fluency requirements:** "my views on this have changed"... "has evolved" @08:06. AI fluency is now mandatory for senior and leverage roles but not for frontline managers. This follows from centralization.
- **Rep-hiring profile reversal:** from "hire president's club performers from similar types of companies" paid "top of market" (remote) to ~80% new grads, in person in Toronto. Consistent with 2025 remarks that new grads ramp faster, but now quantified and structural.
- **Mock call deprioritized** in the loop @28:30. This continues the finding that case-study scores didn't predict performance.
- **Marketing no longer reports to Kyle:** he once owned growth, and the CMO now reports to the CEO @33:35.
