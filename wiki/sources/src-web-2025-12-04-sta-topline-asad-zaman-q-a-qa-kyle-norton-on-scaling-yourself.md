# STA / Topline Q&A: Kyle Norton on Scaling Yourself

**Outlet:** STA (Sales Talent Agency) "Topline" Q&A, by [[person-asad-zaman]] · **Published:** 2025-12-04 · **Type:** written interview (edited Q&A) · **URL:** https://www.stagtm.com/topline/q-a-kyle-norton-on-scaling-yourself · **Raw:** `raw/web/2025-12-04-sta-topline-asad-zaman-q-a-qa-kyle-norton-on-scaling-yourself.md`

*Truncated: the raw text is capped at 6,000 characters of a "15 min read" and stops mid-sentence ("a number that next year is like 7x or"). Only the first two sections are captured.*

## TL;DR
- How the job changed from SVP at $2.5M to CRO: from frontline manager (coaching calls, scripts, forecast) to director to VP/CRO, as people replace parts of the job.
- VP vs CRO: **time horizon and level of abstraction** rise; VP = deliver the number every month/quarter; CRO = do things today that hit the number one to three years out.
- Planning model: **three north stars + two growth horizons**. 2026 goals: core business **$65M → $135M ARR**, and a point-of-sale business with a **$5M** financial goal that he thinks could be $10M.
- "Fear is a planning problem": leaders are scared because they have no path, not because numbers are big.

## What Kyle said
- Inherited four reps and a manager; converted the manager to an IC rep ("still like one of my best reps"); was effectively a frontline manager at first. See [[career-and-leadership]].
- "I've spent a lot of 2025 thinking about 2026 and I'm already thinking about 2027 now." See [[cro-role-and-board]].
- Three north stars and two growth horizons each year; launching point of sale was a 2025 growth horizon and is a core 2026 north star. See [[operating-rhythm]], [[prioritization-5p]].
- Fear of big targets comes from having no plan; his own path is hiring reps, a strong CMO scaling channels, "more redundancy in outbound", and stretching to mid-market customers. See [[capacity-planning]], [[executive-performance-and-mindset]].
- Prior experience helps, but at League he "only went $0 to $25M". See [[career-and-leadership]].
- Interviewer framing (not Kyle): "Kyle joined at $2.5M ARR; they're about to cross $100M now" and "next year you’ll be past $100M".

## Key quotes
- "The way that I've explained it in the past is your time horizon and level of abstraction go up as you as you get more and more senior and your team gets bigger."
- "For a VP its deliver the number no matter what happens."
- "For a CRO it’s ensuring we are doing the things today that will enable us to hit the number a year, two or three down the line."
- "So we want to go from $65M to $135M and that's just the core business."
- "The financial goal is only $5M but I think I could build a $10M point of sale business next year."
- "If people have a path, I don't think they're scared."

## Numbers & facts
- 2026 core-business goal: "$65M to $135M" ARR ("double ARR in the core business").
- Point of sale: 2026 financial goal "$5M"; Kyle thinks "$10M" possible; "could be like a $50M point of sale business in 2027"; exit 2026 with "hundreds of really happy point of sale customers".
- Inherited team: "four reps and a manager".
- Interviewer: joined at "$2.5M ARR"; "about to cross $100M" (Dec 2025). This sits awkwardly with Kyle's own "$65M" base in the same piece; possibly run-rate vs. core ARR, unreconciled.
- League: "$0 to $25M".
