# TestBox (New Go-To-Market Playbook): Lessons in Scaling with Kyle Norton

**Show / host:** TestBox interview series "the new Go-To-Market Playbook", host James Kekus (CRO/Chief Revenue & Experience Officer, TestBox) · **Published:** 2024-12-03 (YouTube upload date; recorded at SaaStr — Kyle says he is "speaking at Sasser tomorrow" @26:13) · **Kyle's role:** guest · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/testbox-lessons-in-scaling/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89OyHKirrQg

## TL;DR
- Kyle (≈2 years into Owner.com) says revenue grew ~10x in two years, with a GTM org scaled from 5 inherited people to ~100.
- His operating thesis: invest early in the "foundations" (RevOps, enablement) instead of spreadsheet-scaling headcount; replace "best practices" with context-dependent "high probability practices."
- Contrarian on AI and comp: the rep's *role* won't change much (AI strips non-revenue work); revenue leaders shouldn't be on 50/50 plans — a less-levered plan let him cut ~40% of deal flow for churn quality.

## What Kyle argued
- "Best practices" should be "high probability practices": what works in SMB (e.g., not running bottoms-up weighted commit forecasts) differs from enterprise; apply ideas in context @08:14–09:18. [[metrics-and-forecasting]] [[smb-sales-motion]] [[career-and-leadership]]
- Being a generalist (cites *Range*) is an advantage — he studies product management and ran a small MMA academy where he built his own website and CRM @08:14. [[career-and-leadership]]
- Domain/product experience in a VP Sales is great "all things being equal," but he isn't hung up on it for a non-complex product like Owner's; curious learners can pick it up @10:22–12:30. [[sales-hiring]]
- Growth is modeled wrong: not reps × quota but "pipeline generated times a conversion rate"; companies should invest in RevOps and enablement much earlier @14:36–15:41. [[capacity-planning]] [[revops]]
- Hire RevOps early — "it makes everything downstream better"; late enablement leaves you "nine months behind" at ~15 AEs @15:41–16:46. [[revops]] [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]
- Enablement and RevOps fail when bureaucratic/top-down (required fields, certifications); hire enablement leaders who carried a bag and serve the rep @17:46–19:54. [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]] [[revops]]
- Hiring: 50–65% of the interview scorecard is hard-to-teach traits (drive, curiosity, learning); identical questions in identical order; a mock call with feedback then a re-run tests coachability — applied even to VP Sales candidates @20:59–23:05. [[hiring-scorecards]] [[sales-hiring]]
- Framework originated from Mark Roberge's *Sales Acceleration Formula* (2015) plus Kahneman on scorecards and halo/horns effects; interviews are recorded and reviewed by VPs, and he is exploring AI to check question adherence @24:06–26:13. [[hiring-scorecards]]
- AI: Owner uses it predominantly internally (data capture, lead scoring, onboarding copy, support) rather than to "carpet bomb our industry with like crap messaging"; data quality determines AI success @28:24–29:28. [[ai-in-gtm]] [[ai-sales-stack]]
- The rep's role won't change much; AI and tooling change the *experience* by removing non-revenue work — BDRs do no lead research; an internal tool auto-loads leads to hit 80–100 calls/day @31:34–33:40. [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]] [[outbound-at-scale]]
- 50/50 plans for revenue executives are "pretty silly"; a less-levered plan let him propose cutting almost 40% of closed deals that wouldn't produce world-class churn @35:40–36:41. [[comp-plans]] [[icp-and-saying-no]] [[churn-and-retention]]
- Revenue leaders should own onboarding; SEs should stay through implementation (done at Shopify) — accountability improves alignment @37:46–38:50. [[org-design]] [[cro-role-and-board]]

## Frameworks & named ideas
- **High probability practices** (vs. "best practices").
- **Growth = pipeline × conversion rate** (vs. reps × quota "spreadsheet mode").
- **Foundations-first scaling**: RevOps and enablement before the 15-AE wall.
- **Servant-leader enablement/RevOps**: the bar is reps Slacking excitement after training.
- **Trait-weighted interview scorecard** + feedback-and-rerun mock call to test coachability.
- **Rep role vs. rep experience** (jobs-to-be-done lens on AI).
- **Centralized lead research + auto-routing** ("I route") for BDRs.
- **Less-levered revenue-leader comp** + customer-outcome-tied rep comp.
- **Revenue leader owns onboarding; SEs through implementation.**
