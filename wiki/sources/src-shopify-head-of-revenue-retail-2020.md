# DMZ Chats: Kyle Norton, Head of Revenue, Retail at Shopify (2020)

**Show / host:** DMZ Chats (Toronto DMZ incubator) / moderator "Sar" (founder, Sea Tribe Festival; VP Canadian Operations, Autonomic) · **Published:** 2020-08-17 (YouTube) · **Kyle's role:** guest (Head of Revenue, Shopify Retail — sales, enablement, RevOps, solutions engineering, implementations) · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/shopify-head-of-revenue-retail-2020/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TE4rfMz48Zw

**Canonical page for this DMZ Chat.** The 2023 YouTube re-upload is [[src-dmz-chat-shopify-2020]] (Whisper-corrupted first ~7 minutes, timestamps ~1 min later); cite this page instead.

## TL;DR
- Pre-Owner Kyle (Aug 2020, Shopify Retail): picked the job on people, product, problems — a scratch build from ~7 reps; COVID response was Tobi's mandate to "rip up every plan, start from scratch… start from the merchant".
- Startup sales: stay "tied at the hip" with customers and over-invest in service; ROI spreadsheets won't be believed from a startup — "You need to find change agents" who share your worldview; founders should stay in sales "for longer than they would probably like".
- Leadership: servant leadership is "probably the most important principle I have"; "you can't lead people until you can lead yourself"; traditional frat-house sales culture should be discarded; "my fault first"; reps should know their market value.

## What Kyle argued (2020)
- Job choice on three things: people, product (after five boiler-room years he can be "picky"), problems; Shopify Retail was ~seven sellers to scale across geographies; came via former boss (Shopify Plus sales head) and hired by Ian @04:04–06:12. [[career-and-leadership]] [[shopify]]
- Broke into tech by cold-calling the sales head (Mark) who was then at Vision Critical, asking only for advice; tech is "snooty" about non-tech sales résumés @06:12–09:23. [[career-and-leadership]]
- COVID: nobody knew whether it would crush Shopify; Tobi said rip up every plan and "start from the merchant"; sales led with empathy, rewrote cadences and tone, coached constantly, and took care of the team @09:23–13:35. [[shopify]] [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]]
- Crisis management: calm the amygdala hijack first, gather data (daily dashboards), lean on network/community, read a lot @14:40–17:52. [[executive-performance-and-mindset]] [[metrics-and-forecasting]]
- Shopify's emergency response was "how do we help our merchants?" not cost-cutting; Go Digital Canada built in record time; curbside pickup and local delivery shipped @18:54–22:05. [[shopify]]
- Early-stage: narrow focus on who sees your value; over-invest in service/CS — satisfied customers are the snowball @23:06–24:11. [[churn-and-retention]] [[icp-and-saying-no]]
- Buyers won't believe a startup's ROI; sell to change agents and contrarians who share your "earned secret" worldview, be transparent about gaps, turn them into an advisory board and evangelists @24:11–27:20. [[point-of-view-positioning]] [[icp-and-saying-no]]
- Founders: stay heavily involved in sales longer than comfortable — no one is more credible than the founder; skip transactional work, show up for big meetings @27:20–28:21. [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- Sales is uncomfortable for most (esp. Canadians) — practice it; cites Daniel Pink's *To Sell Is Human* @29:27–30:33. [[person-daniel-pink]]
- "winter is coming": fundamentals matter more (Challenger's teach, tailor, take control); protect the base; cut aspirational segments/geographies (SMB + enterprise split, premature new markets) before you're forced to @30:33–34:45. [[sales-methodology]] [[icp-and-saying-no]] [[churn-and-retention]]
- Servant leadership is the core principle; coin-operated, disposable-rep sales management is "a legacy of a past time"; engagement drivers = trust boss, believe mission, learning, career path, values @34:45–37:54. [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[recognition-and-motivation]]
- "you can't lead people until you can lead yourself" — mindfulness, gratitude, a long-term executive coach ("a huge unlock"), therapy @37:54–39:57. [[executive-performance-and-mindset]]
- Early-stage reps: generalist "trailblazers", not purely money-motivated; at League he tried to scare candidates off at the end of every interview; those who opt out "were gonna crumble anyways" @44:12–48:28. [[sales-hiring]]
- Later-stage scorecard: good listener, coachable, history of success in any domain (tuba, soccer captain, debate), and growth mindset that "never changes"; recommends *Antifragile* @48:28–50:33. [[hiring-scorecards]]
- Traditional sales culture "is not productive" and not inclusive; walk the walk on transparency; "my fault first"; train people so well they outgrow you; take the recruiter meeting and know your market value — "I want to win you over every month" @50:33–55:45. [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[recognition-and-motivation]]
