# Topline (Pavilion): E99: How GTM Revenue Leaders Are Operationalizing AI for Growth

**Show / host:** Topline (Pavilion) / Sam Jacobs, Asad Zaman, AJ Bruno · **Published:** 2025-03-09 · **Kyle's role:** guest panelist (about 80 min total; Kyle leaves around @69:20; much of the airtime is the hosts). Transcript has no speaker labels, so lines are attributed to Kyle only when context makes it clear (first-person Owner/Shopify, or addressed as "Kyle"/"Giles"/"Karl"). · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/guest-appearances/topline-e99-operationalizing-ai/transcript.md` · **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSEW5vyyQYA

## TL;DR
- AI value accrues two ways. Central application (RevOps/data building tools such as Momentum and Avara sims) lifts bottom and middle performers. Individual application rewards curious people. Owner does both: tops-down tooling plus bottoms-up pushing (Karpathy videos, a Jordan Crawford workshop).
- His tip of the week is meta-prompting: dictate (Super Whisper) to o1 Pro, have AI write the prompt, test it, and iterate in the same thread. Impact is hard to isolate, but he guesses "50% better". Hard numbers: AI mobile enrichment took BDR dial-to-DM connect rate from 3% to 13%.
- Values only work when woven into everything (monthly report card, promotion and hiring scorecards, all-hands spotlights). "Execution is the only moat". He wants an "AI Iron Man suit" that checks managers run his seven-pillar operating system, and plans to nuke recurring meetings in March.

## What Kyle argued
- He calls the top-decile-vs-middle debate a false binary. Central AI (RevOps/data instrumenting tools) benefits rote roles and bottom/middle performers. Individual AI use favors "inquisitive, innovative, curious" people @12:28–13:33. [[ai-operating-model]]
- Owner runs both. Centrally: Momentum for CRM enrichment, data flow and audit triggers, plus the Avara AI simulator. Bottom-up: "cramming" Karpathy's videos "down people's throats" and bringing in Jordan Crawford to write prompts against real problems @13:33–15:38. [[ai-sales-stack]] [[ai-operating-model]] [[person-jordan-crawford]]
- Personally, asked what he did before AI: "Honestly, I just was way worse at my job". AI got him back to pre-kids productivity @15:38. [[personal-ai-stack]]
- Not encouraging teams to explore AI tools on their own is "definitely holding them back" @17:45. [[ai-operating-model]]
- Meta-prompting (credits Jordan Crawford): any reusable prompt is written by AI. He dictates via Super Whisper into o1 Pro, answers its clarifying questions, tests the prompt in a new window, then returns to the meta thread to fix it (e.g., "way too cringy" LinkedIn output). Some prompts end up thousands of words @18:49–20:53. [[personal-ai-stack]] [[person-jordan-crawford]]
- Measuring AI impact: too many simultaneous changes. Signal: in three newly staffed segments, the best starters were the reps with the most Avara sim time (he flags selection bias). Managers with 7–8 reps can't give four hours of practice a week @22:00–24:01. [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]] [[metrics-and-forecasting]]
- Mobile-phone enrichment (AI-powered) roughly 4x'd BDR output via connect rate @24:01–25:07. [[outbound-at-scale]]
- Shopify context on usage pricing: about 70% of revenue was non-subscription (payments take rate, variable on Plus GMV). Usage pricing is long established, so the real question is whether the market rewards growth or profitability @39:43–40:45 (the @40:45 continuation is likely Kyle). [[fundraising-and-investability]]
- Communicating strategy at scale is a learnable skill, not a natural one, even for extroverts. Use "three points of contact" (three times, three channels) and repetition. Fight the bias that what's clear to you is clear to your team @49:00–50:00. [[culture-and-high-standards]]
- Values must be "weaved through everything" (all-hands, awards, decisions), not "a plaque on the wall". Shopify did it best with "100-year company" decisions. At Owner, values appear in the monthly report card (MRC), the promotion scorecard (1–5), and his final-stage hiring scorecard, and one value is spotlighted at every all-hands @51:02–54:05. [[culture-and-high-standards]] [[hiring-scorecards]] [[shopify]]

He wants a vendor to build an "AI Iron Man suit" that grades managers against his seven-pillar framework (e.g., coaching that is question-based, single-focus, with follow-up work, plus talk–listen ratio), and offers to be its first customer @56:06–59:09.
