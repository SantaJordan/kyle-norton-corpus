# SaaStr "My App Stack": Kyle Norton, CRO at Owner

**Outlet:** SaaStr ("My App Stack" series) · **Published:** 2023-12-17 · **Type:** written interview · **URL:** https://www.saastr.com/my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro-at-owner/ · **Raw:** `raw/web/2023-12-17-saastr-my-app-stack-my-app-stack-kyle-norton-cro.md`

*The answer to question #1 ("core stack of apps") is missing from the captured text (probably an image). The bio paragraph is third-person.*

## TL;DR
- Top new app of 2023: **Replayz.io** AI call auto-scoring, credited with win rate **38% → 53% in 10 weeks in Q2 2023**.
- Can't-lose app: **Salesforce**, though he flags the data warehouse (Snowflake) plus AI as something that may change his view.
- Undiscovered gem: **[[momentum]]** ("Troops with AI on steroids") for Salesforce–Gong–Slack automations.

## What Kyle said
- Replayz.io scores calls against Owner's own call frameworks, giving reps instant feedback and frontline managers "coaching scale". See [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]], [[ai-in-gtm]].
- Salesforce stays because everything is built on it and it is the only CRM he has used; the shift to a warehouse as source of truth "and AI" might change that. See [[ai-sales-stack]].
- Momentum delivers reminders, call summaries, suggested next steps, and custom prompts to market-facing teams; chosen because the vendor ships fast. See [[momentum]].
- Bio (third-person): 14+ years in B2B SaaS; LP/advisor/investor in SaaS funds and startups; founding member of [[pavilion]]'s Toronto chapter.

## Key quotes
- "It helped us improve win rates from 38% to 53% in a 10 week period in Q2 of this year."
- "Maybe the shift to the data warehouse as the source of truth (Snowflake for us) and AI change my opinion here though."
- "It’s like Troops with AI on steroids."
