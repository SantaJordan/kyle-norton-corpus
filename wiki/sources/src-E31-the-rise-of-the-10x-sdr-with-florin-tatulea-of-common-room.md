# E31: The Rise of the 10X SDR with Florin Tatulea of Common Room
**Show:** The Revenue Leadership Podcast · **Guest:** Florin Tatulea (transcript: "Florence Atulia"), Head of Sales Development @ Common Room · **Published:** 2025-04-30 · **Kyle's role:** host · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/own-show/E31-the-rise-of-the-10x-sdr-with-florin-tatulea-of-common-room/transcript.md` (the transcript is punctuated but ends mid-sentence at about @59:16, so the rest of the episode is missing)

## TL;DR
- Outbound has moved from 1.0 (sequencers blasting the TAM) to 2.0 (account-level intent) to 3.0, where AI resolves the person behind the signal so you can message only those in market.
- Because AI does the list-building, Tatulea argues the SDR seat can go senior: ex-practitioners from the ICP with a network, paid more, who add off-line "internal signals" (SKO decks, politics) that AI can't see.
- Ownership of a signal motion should sit with RevOps or GTM Ops (with a cross-functional task force), or marketing/growth if there's no RevOps.

## Guest's core ideas
- Intent (on-site or in-product, the warmest) is different from signals (public events like funding or hiring). Market split: 3% actively evaluating (inbound), 7% problem-aware (the SDR sweet spot), 30% mildly aware, 60% unaware (marketing and creators). @03:04–08:12 ([[outbound-at-scale]])
- Outbound 0.0 (Yellow Pages), then 1.0 (Outreach/Salesloft sequencing, 2013–15), then 2.0 (6sense/Demandbase account intent), then 3.0 (AI identity resolution, "the right account with the right person, the right message at the right time"). @09:15–13:27
- The 10x SDR: hire senior, ICP-native SDRs and pay them more. AI removes the roughly one-third of BDR time once spent on list building. @13:27–17:39 ([[sales-hiring]], [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]])
- Skills for 3.0 reps: AI proficiency, a platform-mastery certification alongside cold call, email and research certs, and building prompts and agents for an "insight layer". @22:45–23:45
- Ownership: the most sophisticated orgs put RevOps or GTM Ops in charge, with a task force (SDR, demand gen, GTM strategy, VP Sales). Agencies ("clagencies") are a risk if you have no internal expertise. @25:47–37:16 ([[revops]], [[gtm-engineering]])
- Selling lesson: RevOps holds the budget but "is never going to be your champion". Build champions among the SDRs and AEs who use the tool. @32:00–33:02
- AI SDRs built only on commoditized data (funding, jobs, 10-Ks) produce generic messages. Add first-party data (product usage, Gong summaries) and keep manual human steps for off-line intel. @41:26–43:31 ([[ai-sdrs-and-agents]])
- What can't be automated wins: in-person events and offsites with a point of view (no demo), humor and GIFs on LinkedIn (hard to automate at scale), referrals, and code-named initiatives from SKO slides. @47:45–49:48, @55:07–59:16 ([[point-of-view-positioning]])
