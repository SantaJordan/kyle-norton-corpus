# E39: CRO James Roth on Resilience, Reinvention, and AI at ZoomInfo
**Show:** The Revenue Leadership Podcast · **Guest:** James Roth, CRO @ ZoomInfo (ticker now GTM); ex-SVP Enterprise & Field Sales @ Vonage · **Published:** 2025-07-09 · **Kyle's role:** host · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/own-show/E39-cro-james-roth-on-resilience-reinvention-and-ai-at-zoominf/transcript.md`

## TL;DR
- Leading through the post-ZIRP collapse: set context (people who joined in 2019–21 only saw up and to the right), lead from the front "in the proverbial valley Forge" with customers, treat every brutal quarter as "I get to" learn, and be radically transparent when results are hit by things outside the team's control (the Q2 2024 SMB write-offs).
- Rebirth: refocus on data quality. Copilot pushes signals instead of waiting for users to pull them. GTM Studio unifies first/second/third-party data. ZoomInfo now partners with Gong, Salesloft, Outreach and Clari instead of fighting them.
- GTM rebuild: kill product overlays (150 → 8), one account manager per account, cut account loads sharply (strategic 2–6), add Salesforce-style ECS pods, segment new business into a career "ladder", and fix SMB with pay-upfront e-commerce.

## Guest's core ideas
- Grit: door-to-door sales ("50 doors a day") builds resilience. Hire for proxies (hard backgrounds, athletics). The reps who left to "find Zoom Info 5 years ago" often boomeranged. @02:01–08:08 ([[sales-hiring]])
- Personal resilience: "I have to verse I get to". Treat the public-company CRO grind (analysts, board) as learning. John Wooden's test: did I do everything I could? @11:12–13:15, @29:34–31:36 ([[executive-performance-and-mindset]], [[cro-role-and-board]])
- Lead from the front in bad times. You can't build a strategy if you aren't hearing the churn reasons from customers yourself. @15:16–18:19 ([[churn-and-retention]])
- ZIRP SMB was "this ticking time bomb". There was no upfront payment, so write-offs in Q2 2024 turned a bookings beat into a guidance miss. The fix: e-commerce, PLG, pay-upfront risk models, and a pivot to 70%+ upmarket. @17:17, @26:31–35:40 ([[smb-sales-motion]])
- Product: the AI era made CRM data quality valuable again, so reinvest R&D in data. Copilot pushes signals to the 30–40% of users who never log in. GTM Studio adds an ecosystem partner strategy. @38:42–48:53
- "Good co bad co" portfolio review. Overlays meant "two dumb salespeople in the same call". Replace them with SEs and one account manager per account (talent sells separately). @49:54–58:04 ([[org-design]])
- Lower account loads, piloted cost-neutral at strategic enterprise (25 accounts → 10 → 5) with quarter-by-quarter milestones promised upfront (pipeline Q2, deals Q3, blowout Q4). Then roll out to other segments with ECS pods and renewal specialists. @59:05–68:13 ([[capacity-planning]])
- Founder-led (Henry Schuck): you "can't get away with just like a BS nothing burger answer", which forces preparation. @68:13–72:18
- Advice: "80 to 90%" of getting promoted is hitting your number consistently. There's "no wormhole". Take the role that fills your gap (his "Schwarzman interview" test). @82:28–93:38 ([[career-and-leadership]])
