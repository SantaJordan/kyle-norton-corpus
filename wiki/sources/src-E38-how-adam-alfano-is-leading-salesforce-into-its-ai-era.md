# E38: How Adam Alfano Is Leading Salesforce Into Its AI Era
**Show:** The Revenue Leadership Podcast · **Guest:** Adam Alfano, EVP Global SMB & Emerging Products @ Salesforce (transcript: "Alano") · **Published:** 2025-06-27 · **Kyle's role:** host (recorded live at Salesforce World Tour Toronto, which he calls his "first live pod") · **Raw:** `raw/podcasts/own-show/E38-how-adam-alfano-is-leading-salesforce-into-its-ai-era/transcript.md`

## TL;DR
- 17 years at Salesforce: longevity comes from mental fitness (Michael Gervais / Finding Mastery), continuous reinvention, listening to customers, two-sided thinking, and loosening tight process so newer SMB sellers can experiment and make mistakes.
- Culture at scale: ownership, career pathing, the office as SMB's "heartbeat", and leaders as the "four T's" (teacher, tone setter, transformer, technologist) plus talent.
- AI at Salesforce SMB: every process is reviewed for Agentforce use (a point-of-view agent, sales coach, an SDR agent across 500 SDRs, quoting, renewal agents). Sellers who guide customers through change carry a rising premium.

## Guest's core ideas
- Win "in the periphery" (music, fiction, other industries), not only the linear, hyper-competed track. @05:06–07:08
- Mental fitness as push-ups for the mind (Michael Gervais). Scale it through your directs and their plus-ones, not "frivolous" all-hands. SMB is "a competitive stamina game". @09:13–18:20 ([[executive-performance-and-mindset]], [[smb-sales-motion]])
- Stay in the field (listening tours, ad-hoc kitchen questions). Where a big job "goes to die" is losing touch. He's wary of making the team "about me". @19:20–25:27
- Ownership: clear admin away ("look at their calendar or their bank account"), connect people to mission and career pathing. The office is SMB's heartbeat, and remote hurt energy and onboarding. @26:27–32:35 ([[office-vs-remote]], [[recognition-and-motivation]])
- The four T's: teacher, tone setter, transformer, technologist, plus "talent magnet": "the talent around you is going to make or break you". @36:40–39:42 ([[talent-density]])
- Reinvention: comfort with ambiguity, two-sided (Dalio) thinking, holding convictions loosely, experimentation. He leads with letting people use the product rather than gating it behind hours of discovery. He now tolerates mistakes over tight process. @40:42–49:52 ([[sales-methodology]])
- Agentforce rollout: review every process. Point-of-view agent, sales coach, SDR agent, quoting agent, Tableau NL analytics, renewal agents across "hundreds of thousands of accounts". A cross-functional group drives it (SE leader, RevOps, a "VP of productivity", product). AI lets Salesforce sell more horizontally. @56:57–68:09 ([[ai-sdrs-and-agents]], [[ai-in-gtm]])
- The premium on human sellers who guide change "is going up". Redeploy people, for example service staff into selling. @69:10–71:11
- Great CROs: an "unshakable prioritization of talent", being "hyper evolutionary", and "a real nose to market". "Every set of conditions is an opportunity to go take market share." @74:15–77:18 ([[cro-role-and-board]])
