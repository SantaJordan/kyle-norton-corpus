## TL;DR
- Same episode as [[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]]: senior hires are "feel" + pattern recognition; rep hiring is programmatic.
- Structured, champion-based (not consensus) hiring, a quarterly hiring retro, and case-study sales craft "not that indicative" of success.
- References matter most for leaders: mutual references, self-sourced back-channels, percentile ranking, multiple downward references.
