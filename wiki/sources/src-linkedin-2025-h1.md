### 2025-01-07 — I'm excited to kick off January with a big announcement… Owner is opening an office here in Toronto
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kylecnorton_im-excited-to-kick-off-january-witha-big-activity-7282380475371794432-xbmX
- Owner opens a Toronto office later in January 2025 as the sales hub; all new SDRs and BDRs hired in Toronto with an in-office expectation; reps "graduate into hybrid and remote" over time; existing remote hires may stay remote. See [[office-vs-remote]].
- Still "a champion for remote work" for makers, but had "a nagging feeling" it wasn't ideal for early-career sales teams; spent months reading research and talking to leaders.
- Five reasons: faster ramp for the least experienced, productivity, better early-career experience (Community and Mastery), attracting/retaining XDRs, and lower headcount cost via earlier-career hires, "tripling down on enablement and promoting from within". See [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]], [[sales-hiring]].
- Quotes: "I've long been (and still am) a champion for remote work." · "It’s hard to learn sales and it’s even harder when you’re alone"
- Numbers (secondhand from other leaders): "between 30-50% reductions in ramp time and 20-50% improvements in productivity". Office at Queens Quay and Bay, "less than a 7-minute walk from Union Station".

### 2025-03-26 — This week, I shared my complete guide to high efficiency outbound on my Substack
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kylecnorton_this-week-i-shared-my-complete-guide-activity-7310702822033899520-Qzam
- Growth models assume linear BDR ramp and ignore turnover, bad data, poor enablement; up to 50% of theoretical capacity is lost over 18 months. See [[capacity-planning]], [[outbound-at-scale]].
- Typical BDR lifecycle: 4 months to productive, 7 months producing, gone by month 11.
- "10 secrets": start with the data, keep it clean, personalization at scale, efficient process, tight scripts, coach relentlessly, performance-manage, dissect the funnel, attack show rates, retain your best people.
- Quotes: "up to 50% of your theoretical capacity is lost to extended ramp time, low productivity, and turnover." · "Most BDRs take 4 months to get productive, start delivering results for 7 months, then leave by month 11"
- Numbers: "15 years of expertise"; "18-month period"; 4 / 7 / 11 months; "easily my most popular Substack article ever".
