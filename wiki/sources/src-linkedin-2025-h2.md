### 2025-08-21 — We discovered recently that a lot of our sales hiring process was built on BS
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kylecnorton_we-discovered-recently-that-a-lot-of-our-activity-7364311061539278851-phWC
- With Natalie Sturino and frontline managers, ran a "hiring autopsy": mapped every hire's interview scores against actual performance. See [[hiring-scorecards]].
- Finding: mock demos and case studies have **zero** predictive value. He now hires for hard work, learning speed, and organization. See [[sales-hiring]].
- Every 90 days the team reviews misses, including which manager has a 40% vs 80% hit rate. Promotes the "How I Hire" podcast with Andy Mowat ([[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]]).
- Quotes: "Mock demos and case studies have zero predictive value for sales success." · "I hire insanely hard workers, with the desire and ability to learn quickly and are highly organized."
- Numbers: "Every 90 days"; "40% hit rate vs 80%".

### 2025-12-18 — Want to know the #1 mistake revenue leaders make with AI agents?
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kylecnorton_want-to-know-the-1-mistake-revenue-leaders-activity-7407471559084470272-Qw4I
- Promotes his episode with [[person-jason-lemkin]]: leaders "delegate too early"; the CRO, CMO, or VP Sales must personally deploy the first agent. See [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]].
- Kyle's own takeaway: "Deployment failures are training failures, not product failures"; post-Claude 4, a non-performing tool is "probably you".
- Lemkin's numbers (relayed, not Owner's): 70,000 AI emails vs 7,000 human; 15% of SaaStr London revenue from agents; 20 agents; $0 on agencies; 20–30 hours over 30 days.
- Quotes: "Here's the insight that changed my thinking: Deployment failures are training failures, not product failures." · "Post-Claude 4, if your AI tool isn't performing, the problem is probably you."
