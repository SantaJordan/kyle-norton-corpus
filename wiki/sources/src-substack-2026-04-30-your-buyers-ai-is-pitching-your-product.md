# Your Buyer’s AI Is Pitching YOUR Product (Badly) | Adrian Rosenkranz, CRO @ Webflow

**Type:** episode companion (guest: Adrian Rosenkranz, CRO, Webflow; ex-Tableau global sales) · **Published:** 2026-04-30 · **Paywalled:** no · **URL:** https://www.therevenueleadershippodcast.com/p/your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product · **Raw:** `raw/substack/2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product.md`

**Attribution note.** Webflow numbers, the efficiency-trap story, narrative funnel, self-updating ICP file and build-vs-buy heuristic are Adrian's. Kyle's own contributions: endorsing centralized AI (and referencing his own earlier post), the Gardener at Owner, cutting Gong for Momentum, and the "track customer minutes" stance.

## TL;DR
- The "efficiency trap": reps and managers building their own AI tools pulls them away from customers; measure AI by whether reps spend more time with humans.
- AI agents are now a material share of web traffic; AEO competes for LLM citations, and CROs must "shape, or be shaped."
- Living, self-updating context files replace static knowledge bases; walled-garden vendors that hold context hostage will lose.

## Kyle's argument
- Efficiency trap is "a leadership failure": decentralized AI turns highest-paid customer-facing staff into "part-time rev ops engineers" (guest framing: Adrian Rosenkranz; Kyle endorses). See [[ai-in-gtm]], [[org-design]].
- Adrian collapsed marketing ops, sales ops, post-sales ops and rev ops into one GTM engineering team; reps aren't on it (guest: Adrian Rosenkranz). See [[gtm-engineering]], [[revops]].
- Kyle: an earlier Substack of his "arguing for centralized AI deployment in GTM" was his most popular post "by 3X." See [[gtm-engineering]].
- Kyle's metric: "Track customer minutes. Everything else is theatre." Every AI investment judged by whether reps spend more time with humans.
- Narrative funnel beats feature enablement; sort features into "reason-to-call" vs. "ambient" (guest: Adrian Rosenkranz). See [[sales-coaching-and-enablement]], [[marketing-alignment]].
- AEO: ~20% of Webflow customer-base traffic is AI bots, 43% of that live agents (guest: Adrian Rosenkranz). Kyle's CRO takeaway: dashboard LLM citation share, assign someone to AEO, design the site for two audiences. See [[buyer-ai-aeo-geo]].
- "Shape, or be shaped": buyers query LLMs mid-meeting about pricing and alternatives (guest: Adrian Rosenkranz); Kyle frames AEO as content strategy and competitive intelligence, not just schema. See [[buyer-ai-aeo-geo]].
- Knowledge bases become living context: Adrian's cron-updated ICP markdown file (guest); Owner's VP of rev ops built "the Gardener," a master business-context file scraped from Slack, Notion and call recordings that every agent treats as ground truth (Kyle). See [[playbooks-and-documentation]], [[ai-sales-stack]], [[icp-and-saying-no]].
- Kyle "cut Gong off our stack two years ago" for its walled-garden call data and moved to Momentum to push call context anywhere and run his own prompts. See [[ai-sales-stack]].
- Build vs. buy: buy systems you can build on with AI; don't rebuild governance/permissions or anything with a 1,000-day shelf life; build throwaway quarter-long tools (guest: Adrian Rosenkranz). Kyle: vendors that can't explain agent read/write are "a short-term dependency." See [[build-vs-buy-software]].
- Winning 2026 CROs are "modeling the way themselves" and "getting their hands dirty" (Kyle). See [[cro-role-and-board]].

## Numbers & facts
- (guest: Adrian Rosenkranz) Webflow: 300,000 customers; AI-bot share of traffic <1% sixteen months ago → nearly 20% now; 43% of bot traffic is live agents fetching answers for a human; ChatGPT citation format change (last August) → 30–40% drop in referrals overnight; 6x conversion lift on referred vs. typed-in traffic; Adrian 16 months into the CRO seat.
- Efficiency-trap example tool "saves four hours a week."
- Kyle: centralized-AI Substack was his most popular post by 3X; cut Gong "two years ago" (i.e., ~2024) in favor of Momentum.

## Frameworks & named ideas
- **Efficiency trap** (Adrian) / **"hyper-realistic work-like activity"** (phrase Kyle borrows from Slack founder "Stuart [sic] Butterfield"); Goodhart's Law applied to AI adoption.
- **Customer minutes** as the AI success metric (Kyle).
- **Narrative funnel**; **reason-to-call vs. ambient features** (Adrian).
- **AEO = agent experience optimization**; "shape, or be shaped" (Adrian).
- **Living context files**: Adrian's self-updating ICP file; Owner's **Gardener** (Kyle).
- **Context is the new moat; walled gardens are dead** (Adrian, Kyle concurs).
- **Buy what you can build on; build short-lived tooling; 1,000-day shelf-life test** (Adrian).
