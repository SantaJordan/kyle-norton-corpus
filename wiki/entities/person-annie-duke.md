# Annie Duke

Author of *Thinking in Bets*. Kyle's go-to prioritization framework is adapted from her, and he has cited it in at least seven sources over 2025–2026.

## What Kyle has said about her

- Decisions are bets, and even 80% confidence is good enough (*Thinking in Bets*) ([[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @19:33).
- **4 Ps** (potentials/possibilities, payoff, probability, perspiration), "modified from Annie Duke", used to stack-rank bets and fit revenue strategy to company strategy. "The commonality in all our jobs is the effective allocation of capital" ([[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] @28:00–30:04; [[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @21:04–22:04; [[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @29:21–30:21).
- By 2026 he calls it **5P**: priorities/possibilities, payoff, probability (payoff × probability = expected value), then perspiration, including *whose* perspiration ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @32:02–34:05; [[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @33:40–34:45; [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @22:21–24:23).
- Worked example: $7M × 50% = $3.5M EV vs. $5M × 90% = $4.5M EV ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @33:05).
