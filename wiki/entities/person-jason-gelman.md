# Jason Gelman

Operating Partner @ Primary Ventures (co-founder, Jump). Guest on [[revenue-leadership-podcast]]: E21 ([[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]], 2025-02-19).

## What Kyle said/asked in the episode

Bullets are copied from the source page's "What Kyle said" section; quotes are verbatim from the transcript.

### E21 (2025-02-19) · [[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]]

- **No best practices:** advising GTM Fund and Stage 2 portcos suffers from "such a lack of context". "there's no such thing as a best practice. There's high probability practices", so he became "much less certain of the advice that I wanted to give" (Dunning-Kruger). [[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]] @32:16 ([[career-and-leadership]])
- **CRO job diligence:** "primary might make 12 bets a year and you make 0.3 bets a year as an operator." "If you're interviewing for a CRO role, ask for the last board deck. When I was talking to owner about this role, the founder flipped me through a board deck and then I asked for time with the COO who ran finance". They spent an hour on a screen share going through the growth model segment by segment (growth, retention, unit economics, cash burn). "And if it's a no, in my opinion, it's a pretty big red flag." [[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]] @45:58–48:04 ([[owner-com]], [[cro-role-and-board]], [[career-and-leadership]])
- **Scaling without PMF:** "Super talented team, but we were brute forcing growth... We never had customers that were willing to do customer reference calls for us, but we still jammed that business to 25 million ARR." [[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]] @37:34 ([[fundraising-and-investability]], [[churn-and-retention]])
