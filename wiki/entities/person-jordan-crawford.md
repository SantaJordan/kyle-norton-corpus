# Jordan Crawford (Blueprint)

Founder of Blueprint (Blueprint GTM). Kyle calls him a friend ("we're buddies") and a teacher. Kyle credits several ideas to him: "the list is the message", meta-prompting, "eat your vegetables", job unbundling, and the Clay-table workflows behind his podcast prep. He has been a guest twice (E20, E60). Everything below is only what Kyle has said on the record.

## What Kyle has said about him

- **"the list is the message" (Dec 2024).** The earliest mention. Kyle cites Jordan as "one of the smartest GTM AI thinkers" and repeats his line "the list is the message" ([[src-E11-mastering-strategic-planning-with-eric-gilpin-cro-of-g2]] @19:21).
- **Waterfall AI campaigns (Jan 2025).** Kyle cites Jordan's "micro-targeted campaigns with multiple agents in a waterfall" as the early-mover case for AI SDRs, then adds: "I generically am not a big believer in AI SDR writ large" ([[src-E15-the-blueprint-for-sales-and-marketing-alignment-with-kyle-la]] @41:09–46:13).
- **E20, his first guest episode (2025-02-12).** Kyle introduces him as "more colloquially known as my smartest AI friend. This is the guy that I go to when I've got AI questions" ([[src-E20-building-data-driven-sales-processes-in-the-ai-era-with-jord]] @00:00–01:00). In the episode Kyle:
  - says Owner already works Jordan's way: "this is largely the approach that we've taken as well. I think we just happened to get there before you and I started spending so much time talking about this stuff... It's all about the data." (@41:22–42:26). Owner narrowed "500,000 independent restaurants" to "our top 30,000" (@54:03).
  - pushes back on Jordan's claim that signal platforms are a trap: "this is a really interesting opinion. I'm trying to think through of like how that would not be true." (@43:28)
  - credits Jordan's methods: he has "built clay workflows with your help" (@62:24–64:38); "Your meta prompt process was super valuable." (@69:57–75:10); and on naming the experts in a prompt, "I stole that from you, obviously." (@76:11–78:16)
  - pitches him: "I want to pitch you on making a CRO AI course" (@80:24), and closes with "I can vouch. These are very valuable prompts and, and videos that I get back from Jordan." (@91:56)
- **Meta-prompting and a workshop (Mar 2025).** On Topline, Kyle credits Jordan for meta-prompting: any reusable prompt is written by AI, which he dictates via Super Whisper into o1 Pro, tests in a fresh window, and fixes in the meta thread ([[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] @18:49–20:53). Owner's bottom-up AI push included bringing Jordan in to write prompts against real problems, alongside Karpathy's videos ([[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] @13:33–15:38).
- **Personalization at scale done right (Jan 2025).** Most AI-SDR use is a carpet bomb, "But then there are people that are doing it incredibly well. I look at Jordan Crawford, and Florin Tutulia [Tatulea]... who have really nailed how to do high-intent, very specific, tailored, high-quality personalization at scale" ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @02:07).
- **The 86-prompt chain.** "you need a thinker like Jordan to be able to do it. His prompt chain is 86 prompts to go from account, finding individuals, learning about them, creating messaging to email sent. He did one for owner because we're buddies." Kyle's verdict: "yeah, I would totally send that." Still, "that's the exception, not the norm right now" ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @05:21).
- **"go hire Jordan".** Asked how outbound leaders close a gap between budget and target, he lists the options: "Maybe I'm going to carpet bomb my, my market with like crappy messaging through an AI STR or go hire Jordan and just have him do it for you." ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @24:10)
- **Cold-email project (Jun 2025).** "we're actually doing a project right now with Jordan Crawford to eventually, yeah, to do cold email at scale. But even that is going to come from the inboxes of the sales rep." ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @07:20)
- **"Eat their damn vegetables" (Sep 2025).** "this is a Jordan Crawford thing. He's like, people need to eat their damn vegetables." That means doing the hard work on data foundations, ICP, key segments, the "digital footprint of those specific high-fit segments", and positioning before feeding models ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @36:00; same audio in [[src-kyle-nortons-bets-for-gtm-domination]] @36:39). By Apr 2026 he uses the phrase without attribution: "we've done all the eat your vegetables stuff early" ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @49:44).
- **Learning resource (Mar 2026).** His Clay-table podcast-prep flows were built "with the help of Jordan Crawford, who is an awesome learning resource that everybody should go follow" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @64:02–65:03).
- **Job unbundling (May 2026).** "when I recorded the podcast with Jordan Crawford, who I learned a lot of this stuff from, he had this great framework. He was like, job's just a big bundle of tasks. And in this AI world, we need to unbundle all those tasks". He applies it to the BDR job, where a lot of hours go to finding the right people to call ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @29:29). The same idea underlies his unbundle the JD → more specialization stance in [[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @13:45–14:50 (no attribution there).
- **E60, his second guest episode (2026-01-22).** The first episode was one "that I got a lot of messages about and people want more from". Kyle: "I learn uh from Jordan all the time with his content and through my incessant uh text messages", and "he helps me a ton at owner.com as well" ([[src-the-claude-code-era-mastering-autonomous-gtm-agents-jordan-crawford]] @01:01–02:03). He spent the holidays "cooking some stuff in cloud code that uh I'll eventually need your help fixing" (@02:03–03:06). On Jordan's business: "you're a oneman show. It's just it's just like you and Claude." (@03:06–04:06). When Jordan set the bar at "an 80% improvement in a quarter", Kyle replied "Two weeks." and cited Owner's BDR pilot: "this pilot group made 85% more calls and produced 85% more opportunities than than their baseline" (@40:54–42:57, @49:05–50:05). On Jordan's output: "the like artifact of your work is just like a monster spreadsheet"; on "deploy not destroy": "That's smart." (@74:38)
- **Speaker lineup.** Kyle's Revstar Toronto plug (June 2, 2026) lists Jordan as another speaker ([[src-substack-2026-05-17-stop-paying-for-saas-the-ai-playbook]]).
- **AI Fluent Revenue Leader event (July 22, 2026, SF).** Kyle's post lists Jordan (Blueprint GTM) as a fellow speaker. Kyle's own session covered build vs buy and admitted that "some of what worked isn't working anymore" ([[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-07-16)).
