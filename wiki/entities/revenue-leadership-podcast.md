# The Revenue Leadership Podcast

[[kyle-norton]]'s own weekly interview show for revenue leaders, with a Substack companion. All 74 numbered episodes (E1, 2024-09-25, to E74, 2026-09-02) now have `sources/` pages. Kyle is the host on every episode except two where he is the one interviewed: the GTMnow re-air (E47) and E65.

## Show history

- **Launch and the Topline era (2024-09 → late 2025).** E1 opens: "Hello and welcome to the Revenue Leadership Podcast by Topline. I'm your host, Kyle Norton, CRO at Owner.com." ([[src-E01-saastr-founder-jason-lemkin-on-why-revenue-leaders-must-run]]). Topline is [[pavilion]]'s media brand (the [[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]] and [[src-topline-e99-operationalizing-ai]] pages list it as "Topline (Pavilion)"). E1 also carries a Pavilion ad: "You can get it at joinpavilion.com slash GTM 2024." Mid-roll ads in E36–E46 tell listeners to "use code topline for 10% off your GA ticket" ([[src-E46-the-revops-mind-behind-ownercoms-billion-dollar-valuation]]). The Sep 2025 re-air was uploaded to the Pavilion YouTube channel as The Revenue Leadership Podcast, "by Topline/Pavilion" ([[src-kyle-nortons-bets-for-gtm-domination]]).
- **The "by Topline" intro fades out (late 2025).** The spoken "by Topline" intro is still used in E46, E47, E49 and E52 (2025-11-05) ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]]). It alternates with the old intro until E52 and then replaces it. The wiki has no source that says when or why the Topline/Pavilion distribution ended. Do not infer a date.

The channel name is Kyle Norton, the Revenue Leadership Podcast." ## Prep and production process

- **Intake → Clay table → docket.** A Superhuman invite template leads to a Tally intake form. That feeds a [[clay]] table where each column is a research prompt: everything the guest has said online, then themes and what is unique about them. Kyle hand-picks three topics, and the last column writes the docket. He spends 10–15 minutes editing the questions ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @19:03–21:10). "The prep is super simple because I have a clay table… it webhooks into my clay table" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @64:02). He built these flows with help from [[person-jordan-crawford]] ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @64:02–65:03). The workflow took ~6–10 hours to build and "saves me like hours a week" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @41:41).
- **No outlines to guests.** He used to send a docket ahead, but guests pre-wrote answers "and those episodes were the worst". Now he gives 10 minutes of framing and starts: "I do this job every day. So I don't need to do a bunch of research to know what interesting questions to ask." ([[src-E71-the-most-efficient-marketing-org-in-the-world-35-people-350m]] @71:14–72:18)
- **Time cost:** "I record for 90 minutes" plus 30–45 minutes a week ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @23:14; [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @64:02).
- **Post-production:** the transcript goes to a newsletter-writer skill. It offers 5 hook options and 10 stack-ranked takeaways for him to choose from, then writes a LinkedIn post ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @21:10–22:12). His OpenClaw agent "Kai" will write five LinkedIn posts per guest ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @47:23). Adrian Rosenkranz is the "second guest" to get the voice-post package ([[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @39:48).
