## Personal practices

- **Training:** reached his 1,000th consecutive day of working out ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @06:06). He calls himself "a corporate athlete" and gets 8 hours of sleep ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @06:06–07:09).
- **Cold plunge:** "I have a cold plunge at my house." He started at about 45 s at 45 degrees. His heart rate spiked to ~100 on the first plunges, and he now brings it "into the like low 60s" ([[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @27:38–28:40, @37:50–38:50; [[src-substack-2026-04-03-how-to-optimize-exec-performance]]).
- **Starter list:** a 4-question morning gratitude practice, meditation, and something physical ([[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @86:50). He started gratitude and meditation "almost 10 years into" his career and cold plunging "only in the last four or five years" ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @05:04).
- **Sleep stack, added one item at a time:** mouth tape, blue-light glasses, eye mask, glycine, Eight Sleep ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @62:45–64:52). His HRV was 51 after 3–4 drinks versus 88 on the day of recording ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @57:37). He now drinks rarely ([[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @33:27–36:30).
- **Learning:** "54,000 minutes of podcasts last year" ([[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @31:55; [[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @38:01–39:01). He learns after the kids are asleep, "with an AirPod in" ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @23:14).
- **Writing and brand:** has been writing on LinkedIn for 5–6 years ([[src-frontlines-how-i-hire]] @43:47). He has "just under 40,000" followers ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @34:55) and has hit the 30,000-connection limit ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @50:48). He says the brand's purpose "always" started as recruiting: "Kyle writes a LinkedIn post and then 1,600 people apply" ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @15:54–18:02).
- **Personal AI stack:** writing "skills" built from a 150-page doc of his posts ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @18:02–19:03). A Claude Code performance-review skill pointed at his leaders' call transcripts ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @64:02–65:03).
