# Momentum

A conversation-intelligence and CRM-automation vendor that runs Owner's first-party data layer. It is also a sponsor of [[revenue-leadership-podcast]]: Kyle reads its ads himself.

## What Kyle has said about it

- **What it does at Owner:** runs custom prompts over call transcripts and writes structured answers into Salesforce fields ([[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @18:51–20:52). It also alerts managers and auto-scores SPIFF behaviors ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @07:28–08:30). It is "still more accurate than a rep" at CRM fill ([[src-outbound-kitchen-scale-outbound-sales]] @33:22–37:32). Prompts can back-test old calls, e.g. to check whether new pricing is working ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @39:00).
- **Why Momentum over Gong:** it first ran on top of Gong, then replaced it ([[src-kyle-nortons-bets-for-gtm-domination]] @33:31). "This was one of the original reasons I turned off gong": Gong's "walled garden" kept him from piping data his way, while Momentum lets him "write my own prompts" ([[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @63:38). He cut Gong "two years ago" ([[src-substack-2026-04-30-your-buyers-ai-is-pitching-your-product]]). He chose it because he could engineer the prompt per field ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @39:11–43:22).
- Owner was the first customer on its CI platform ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @46:14–50:21). "We're a momentum shop" ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @30:05).
- Ad read: "At Owner, we ripped out Gong and went all-in on Momentum." ([[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @01:01; [[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @33:55)
- Momentum was "bought by Salesforce" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @57:43). That acquisition is part of why he went "from being a bear on Salesforce 24 months ago to being like very bullish" ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @40:03).
