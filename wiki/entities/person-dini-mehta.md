### E3 (2024-10-09) · [[src-E03-dini-mehta-on-mastering-multi-product-strategy-building-hi]]

- Jack Altman, Lattice's founder CEO, is "on our board at Owner". Altman's [[saastr]] talk said multi-product brought "higher win rates, bigger ACVs at the top of the funnel", not only expansion. [[src-E03-dini-mehta-on-mastering-multi-product-strategy-building-hi]] @06:15 ([[owner-com]])
- Shopify POS story. He joined Shopify "to greatly expand the sales-led motion for our point of sale product". It was a separate team that didn't roll into the same CRO, because the core team's number was "like a billion dollars in net new revenue and my target was like 130". The team became "too successful for our own good". When it was folded back in, POS "didn't grow anywhere as fast as it should have" that year. Shopify had three non-overlapping sales teams (financial products, POS, Plus) that are now combined. [[src-E03-dini-mehta-on-mastering-multi-product-strategy-building-hi]] @23:01–25:04 ([[shopify]], [[org-design]])
- His conclusion: "the default answer should be give it to the existing reps unless it is so complex that they can't do it." He filed away Mehta's lesson to add specialist SEs earlier: "I'm going to put in the old memory bank". [[src-E03-dini-mehta-on-mastering-multi-product-strategy-building-hi]] @25:04–27:08 ([[org-design]])
