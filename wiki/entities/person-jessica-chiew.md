### E41 (2025-08-01) · [[src-E41-jessica-chiew-of-canva-on-building-ai-native-gtm-org]]

- Owner uses the same title, "GTM AI lead". Its job is workflow problem-solving: vibe-code an internal app, build "an extravagant clay table", teach people MCP, or ship "a prompt template library". The data layer (enrichment, waterfalls) sits instead with a strong VP of BizOps and data and their data scientists and engineers. [[src-E41-jessica-chiew-of-canva-on-building-ai-native-gtm-org]] @12:11–14:13 ([[gtm-engineering]], [[ai-operating-model]], [[revops]])
- His evolving opinion is to start with the data: solve third-party data first ("mobile phone numbers in particular"), then first-party (product data, CRM hygiene), because "AI is a pattern recognition and a replication engine." "this is becoming more of an opinion and so I keep stress testing that". [[src-E41-jessica-chiew-of-canva-on-building-ai-native-gtm-org]] @22:27–23:28 ([[ai-in-gtm]])
- Surfaces over solutions: "I'm okay if it's a point solution in the background". What he avoids is "new surfaces for the reps to live", since reps already juggle inbox, Slack, Salesforce and Salesloft. Owner runs an AI support bot, Guru, and is "piloting an AI SDR now". [[src-E41-jessica-chiew-of-canva-on-building-ai-native-gtm-org]] @33:35–34:36 ([[ai-sales-stack]], [[ai-sdrs-and-agents]])
