### E40 (2025-07-23) · [[src-E40-partnership-lessons-from-coelevate-with-author-richard-ezeki]]

- On timing, he is skeptical of programs that start too early: "you've got 70 customers and 4 million in ARR and nobody wants to partner with you". [[src-E40-partnership-lessons-from-coelevate-with-author-richard-ezeki]] @19:22 ([[icp-and-saying-no]])
- Owner's referral program with a big partner organization took "multiple quarters to start getting real momentum". It's "going really well now", but "patience was certainly required": the partner doesn't "move that fast" and its frontline sellers needed educating. [[src-E40-partnership-lessons-from-coelevate-with-author-richard-ezeki]] @63:21 ([[owner-com]])
- Partnerships need "a level of business acumen that's unique", and they become relevant for CROs at about "20 to 50 million AR". [[src-E40-partnership-lessons-from-coelevate-with-author-richard-ezeki]] @72:33 ([[cro-role-and-board]])
