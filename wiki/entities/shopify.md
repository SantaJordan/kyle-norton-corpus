# Shopify

Kyle's employer from 2019 to 2022, before [[owner-com]] ([[src-testbox-lessons-in-scaling]] @03:06).

## What Kyle has said about it

- He took a "senior lead" title running "an 80 person org" over a CRO offer, choosing company quality over title ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @48:21–49:23).
- He ran POS GTM (a ~$70M revenue business when he joined; [[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @11:14), growing the team "from like five people to 70 and change in 18 months" ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @12:17). He then ran Canada, "a quarter-billion-dollar business" ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @04:06).
- During COVID, Shopify paused variable comp and guaranteed full OTE ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @24:42–25:43).
- The late-2020 regional reorg was "basically a train wreck". He wrote an eight-page doc against it ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @18:28–20:33).
- Practices he borrowed: the **Chaos Monkey** calendar reset ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @38:18–39:19), pair programming turned into "pair prospecting" ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @06:09–11:23), and "not shipping the org chart" ([[src-E72-i-collapsed-my-gtm-org-chart-this-is-why]] @09:17–10:17).
- He says Shopify's product was so good, and so inbound-heavy, that it didn't produce great sales talent ("five pound weights") ([[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @34:18). Field sales there would have needed an 80% win rate to work ([[src-outbound-kitchen-ai-sdrs-dead-wrong]] @43:02).
- He "emphatically did not want to leave Shopify" ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @69:39).
