# Jason Lemkin (SaaStr)

Founder of [[saastr]] and an Owner investor and board member. Kyle says "Jason Lemkin's on our board" ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @05:12), and Lemkin discloses that he led Owner's seed via SaaStr Fund and is a board member ([[src-web-2026-saastr-lemkin-owner-ai-rebuild-past-100m-arr]]). He was in the room at Kyle's first Owner board meeting and is an early influence on how Kyle thinks about the executive role. He was Kyle's first podcast guest (E1) and came back for E58.

## On Kyle's show

- **E1 (2024-09-25), "Why Revenue Leaders Must Run Towards Bad News".** SaaStr was "one of the most important knowledge bases for me when I was first breaking into tech"; Kyle found Lemkin on Quora and kept "a book full of notes" on *Impossible to Inevitable* ([[src-E01-saastr-founder-jason-lemkin-on-why-revenue-leaders-must-run]] @03:07). Kyle tells the board story in full: his first full month was worse than the month he started, he "had to let two of the four reps go", and when he showed leading indicators Lemkin said they were interesting "but it's not revenue"; Kyle "sulked away from that meeting feeling terrified" (@43:27–44:29). Lemkin's own advice: unexpected bad news is "10 times more a confidence destroyer than anything else" (@14:41–16:44), and before joining, ask for the last board pack (@55:49–64:11). In a closed session as Owner neared about $10M, Lemkin said "Kyle's really coming into his own" (@22:49, @39:23).
- **Reaction to E1 (Oct 2024).** "When Lemkin was talking about this whole thing around fear, fear is sort of the end of you as a revenue leader." ([[src-topline-e78-executive-fear-boards]] @05:07). The podcast had just launched with Lemkin as first guest ([[src-chief-revenue-podcast-063]] @41:04).
- **E58 (2025-12-17), "Replacing Your Sales Team with AI Agents".** Kyle: "Jason's been incredibly helpful for me personally", and Lemkin led Owner's seed ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @01:01–03:04). Kyle shares Owner detail: "like nine high impact production use cases of AI", bookings per dollar per AE "3x any team I've ever managed before", yet "I'm hiring like 25 people in sales over like a 75day period" (@25:22–26:25); Lemkin pushes back that at a true 3x the team could be smaller. Lemkin's own view is that mid-pack AEs and SDRs are in "terminal decline" (@24:22–33:30). Kyle on Salesforce: Owner is "leaning into Salesforce more than ever which I am personally surprised by" (@63:00).

## SaaStr stages together

- **2024-04-17/18:** SaaStr Workshop Wednesday on GTM efficiency ([[src-linkedin-2022-2024]] (post 2024-04-17); [[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]]).

## What Kyle has said about him

- Kyle started reading Lemkin's content in about 2014 ([[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @17:28). He went "so crazy deep" on SaaStr and Lemkin while learning to think like an executive ([[src-bridge-the-gap-scaling-startups]] @14:22–16:25).
- **First board meeting:** Lemkin said "sounds great, none of it's showing up in your revenue number" ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @13:16). The next month the numbers turned and Lemkin gave him credit ([[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @16:28–18:30). They now laugh about it ([[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @13:32).
- Lessons Kyle credits to him: "run towards bad news" with your board, and "clarity is kindness" ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @51:24–52:28; [[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @25:42–26:42). Also leaders must "lead from the front" and build their own tools ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @42:42).
- On AI BDRs: "If Jason freaking Lemkin can be tuning AI BDRs... you... could be doing that as well" ([[src-union-square-cro-stories-60m-arr]] @44:33–45:35).
- "Jason Lemkin's on our board" (now confirmed by Lemkin's own disclosure, above). He recalls Lemkin saying at their SaaStr talk, "if your CRO is not AI-pilled by then, they gotta go" ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @05:12).
- He points to his hour-long SaaStr keynote with Lemkin on AI ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @55:27). E72 guest Ian Tickle references Kyle's conversation "with Jason" about governing AI agents ([[src-E72-i-collapsed-my-gtm-org-chart-this-is-why]] @45:02).
