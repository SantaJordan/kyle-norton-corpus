CRO @ Motus (ex-Achievers). Guest on [[revenue-leadership-podcast]]: E24 ([[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]], 2025-03-12).

## What Kyle said/asked in the episode

Bullets are copied from the source page's "What Kyle said" section; quotes are verbatim from the transcript.

### E24 (2025-03-12) · [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]]

- **"High probability practice":** "I'm on a mission to get people to stop saying best practice and replace it with high probability practice". He likewise tells reps "don't set a next step, give... the customer an offer of value". "I think language has such... power." What worked "in another vertical SaaS SMP context" doesn't transfer automatically: "I sold to restaurants, you sold to mechanics." [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @21:48–22:53 ([[playbooks-and-documentation]], [[sales-methodology]])
- **Two altitudes:** "I talk about this a lot. The best revenue leaders can live at two simultaneous different levels of abstraction". Get in the weeds on call, connect and conversion data, "and then also think about where we go in 24 months." "The world is skewing towards the operator more and more" (P&L and system design). [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @45:37–46:43 ([[cro-role-and-board]], [[executive-performance-and-mindset]])
- **Owner pays on launch:** "my VP of sales and director of sales, like the AE director, are both paid not on close one business, but on launched MRR." "I think paying them on retention is probably a step too far". "Our launch cycle's only two to three weeks, so it's workable, but the AEs are also paid not on close one, only on launch." On clawbacks: "The AE has pre-spent the money half the time." [[src-E24-what-pe-backed-cros-do-differently-with-vanessa-brangwyn-cr]] @64:40–66:49 ([[comp-plans]], [[churn-and-retention]], [[owner-com]])
