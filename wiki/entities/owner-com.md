# Owner.com

Kyle's company. It is vertical software (now pitched as "vertical AI") for independent restaurants, described as "HubSpot plus Shopify for little tiny takeout places" ([[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @01:00). Kyle has been SVP Sales and then CRO since June 2022 ([[src-saastr-gtm-efficiency-smb]] @04:03).

## Company profile

- **Customers:** US restaurants that rely heavily on takeout and delivery ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @01:02). These are mom-and-pop places with "one, two, three locations, owner operated" ([[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @04:08), mostly one to five locations ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @04:12).
- **Contracts:** month-to-month. An earlier money-back guarantee was dropped ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-skeptical-icp]] @00:00–01:02). "We're in a month to month business. So we like to say it's like we get 12 Super Bowls a year." ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @11:17)
- **Deal shape:** ACV around $10K. The host asked "like 10?" and Kyle said "Yeah, right around there" ([[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @47:13). Elsewhere he gives "10 to 12 K high velocity" ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @08:27), "8 to 12K" and a "six-day sales cycle" ([[src-firmable-best-practice-killing-sales]] @06:15–07:21), and "usually like a one or two call close" ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @12:36).
- **Board and investors:** Jack Altman is "on our board at Owner" ([[src-E03-dini-mehta-on-mastering-multi-product-strategy-building-hi]] @06:15; [[src-E25-transitioning-from-product-led-to-sales-led-growth-with-andr]] @42:31). Alex Bard of Redpoint is a board member ([[src-E27-how-matt-braley-built-a-multi-million-dollar-alliance-ecosys]] @76:02). Kyle lists Jason Lemkin, Redpoint and Jack Altman as board members or investors ([[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @25:21). The Series B was raised from insiders: Redpoint (since seed), SaaStr Fund and Altman's fund ([[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @13:23–14:25). Kyle helped get GTM Fund into a round ([[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @18:00).
- **Founders:** Adam Guild and Dean. Kyle met them through GTM Fund's investment ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @02:08–03:15). Adam was "like 23" when Kyle started ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @61:13).
- **History:** an earlier product passed $1M ARR in 2018–2020 and went to zero with COVID. Owner rebuilt from March 2020 and was back over $1M ARR within a year ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @01:01–02:03).
- **Positioning:** [[point-of-view-positioning]]. "you're going to have less control, but that means that we will control it for you" ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @46:36–47:41).
- **Ambition:** "tens of billions of dollars in market cap," "far beyond IPO" ([[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @57:45).
