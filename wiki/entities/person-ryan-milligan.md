# Ryan Milligan

VP of Sales, Marketing & RevOps @ QuotaPath. Guest on [[revenue-leadership-podcast]]: E29 ([[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]], 2025-04-16).

Bullets are copied from the source page's "What Kyle said" section; quotes are verbatim from the transcript.

### E29 (2025-04-16) · [[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]]

- Owner moved comp from closed-won to *launched* revenue in July. In a high-velocity SMB business the pay point "only gets moved out by between four and 14 days". Deal quality and early churn "Everything got better". [[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @13:33–14:38 ([[comp-plans]], [[churn-and-retention]])
- Why he did it: Owner had a clawback model, and "clawbacks suck". They breed blame because "humans have negativity bias". Pay-on-launch puts everyone on one team. Reps now pressure the launch org, which he calls "positive friction" (citing his G2 interview with Eric Gilpin). [[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @63:47–64:47 ([[comp-plans]], [[culture-and-high-standards]])
- Spiffs must "drive some warp" (= WAR, wins over replacement), meaning truly incremental behavior, not something reps already do. This is also his challenge to CSM variable pay. [[src-E29-mastering-sales-commission-plans-with-quotapaths-ryan-milli]] @21:47–22:50 ([[comp-plans]], [[recognition-and-motivation]])
