# Kevin Bailey (CEO, Dreamfuel)

Co-founder and CEO of Dreamfuel, a mental-performance coaching company. He was a two-part [[revenue-leadership-podcast]] guest (E66, E70). Per the E66 intro, he is a three-time Inc. 500 founder whose first company hit $12M ARR with 100 employees by age 27, and he has coached "over 1,000 leaders" ([[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @01:01–02:01).

## What Kyle has said about him

- **Before the episodes (May 2025):** Owner had already brought in DreamFuel for somatic breathwork plus a 20-minute visualization of the month's goals, with the team lying on the office floor ([[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @36:49).
- Part 1 is "the single most popular episode I've ever recorded by a pretty good shot" ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @02:00).
- Dreamfuel has coached executives at Owner.com ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @00:00).
- In his 1:1 Dreamfuel session, the breathwork was "the craziest physical sensation I've had like ever", and his hands were "tingling and vibrating for like, honestly, hours" ([[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @50:02–51:02).
- After it, Kyle ran an org-wide "Kyle number" visualization and got about 10 fired-up messages from reps ([[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @54:07–55:08). A group Dreamfuel visualization for the team was coming up "next week" ([[src-E70-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]] @11:17–12:21).
- Kyle adds Bailey's "drop one dopamine waster" tip to his own starter list ([[src-E66-win-more-by-losing-less]] @89:54).
- He pushes back on Bailey's serotonin day: "that sounds like a nightmare to me", though recovery is "part of the operating model" ([[src-substack-2026-05-29-manufacture-motivation-sell-more]]).
- **LinkedIn promos.** Part 1 promo ([[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-04-07)). Part 2 promo, calling episode 1 "one of my most popular episodes of all time" and drawing the takeaway "mental performance is sales infrastructure" ([[src-linkedin-2026]] (post 2026-05-29)).
- Bailey's frameworks (performance chain, four states, warm up / hold form / cool down, ACT) are attributed to Bailey, not Kyle ([[src-substack-2026-04-03-how-to-optimize-exec-performance]]).
