He's "obsessing" over how to scale this across his team without jamming "it down somebody's throat". [[src-E38-how-adam-alfano-is-leading-salesforce-into-its-ai-era]] @14:16–15:17, @17:19 ([[executive-performance-and-mindset]])

New hires write their own. He is a brainstormer who is "now I'm better at writing out my ideas". We don't do like value engineering", so build the AI-delivered version of the enterprise motion for SMB.
