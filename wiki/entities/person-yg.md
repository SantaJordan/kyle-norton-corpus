# YG (Owner applied-AI lead for sales)

Owner's applied-AI lead for sales. The sources use only the initials "YG"; no full name appears in any of them.

## What Kyle has said about YG

- YG was the first headcount Kyle added after the Series C. The headcount was Kyle's, but YG reports to the VP of Data in BizOps, does sales-only work for the first 12 months, and is "massively AI-pilled" ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @07:18–09:28). Kyle repeats the post-Series C point in [[src-E74-your-agent-isn-t-broken-its-foundation-is]] @30:29 and [[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @13:12–14:13.
- YG is based in New York, while the sales team is in Toronto. YG flies in for 3–4 days to build next to reps ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @07:18–08:20; [[src-saastr-2m-arr-per-rep-with-ai]] @25:25, @27:27).
- **Experiment pods:** 2–4 respected top reps, ~4 days, 3–5 vibe-coded iterations a day, "pair prospecting". This produced AI pre-call research (86% more DM connects by day two) ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @06:09–13:25).
- YG's output is "10, 20 times better" than a savvy AE's ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @12:40). A manager's side-of-desk tool "still sort of sucks", while YG's work is "10 X better and, and scalable" ([[src-E67-your-buyer-s-ai-is-pitching-your-product-badly]] @27:13–28:16).
- "80% of what our AI lead does is pipe gen focused" ([[src-topline-world-class-ai-in-gtm]] @36:57). The work is framed as growth, "not really a cost reduction play" ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @31:47–32:48).

## The role before and around the hire (2025)

Most 2025 sources describe Owner's "GTM AI lead" or "head of applied AI" without a name. Where a source page links the role to YG it is noted; the rest describe the same role.

- **Apr 2025:** Kyle was "just about to post a role for... a Head of Applied AI" (the title borrowed from Ramp) to custom-build internal tools beyond what RevOps runs ([[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]] @09:09–10:11).
- **May 2025:** "I wish I had been more insistent about hiring a head of applied AI." ([[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @59:57)
- **Aug 2025:** Owner uses the title "GTM AI lead". The job is workflow problem-solving: vibe-code an internal app, build "an extravagant clay table", teach people MCP, or ship "a prompt template library". The data layer sits with the VP of BizOps and data ([[src-E41-jessica-chiew-of-canva-on-building-ai-native-gtm-org]] @12:11).
- **Oct 2025:** "we hired a head of applied AI for um go to market to try to just like solve it in this part of the business." ([[src-work-sprawl-and-the-future-of-marketing-with-global-vp-marketing-cli]] @23:24–24:24). Owner "hired a GTMAI lead" who is building "our cold outbound email infrastructure and internal automations" ([[src-all-about-ai-and-gtm-at-clay-round-two-with-everett-berry]] @24:28–25:30).
- **Dec 2025:** the GTM AI lead came from Atomico's venture studio and had started companies. He built the cold outbound email infrastructure "in like three weeks", and Owner was then "sending 1,800 cold outbound emails every single day" ([[src-replacing-your-sales-team-with-ai-agents-jason-lemkin-founder-ceo]] @31:30–32:30).
- **Jan–Feb 2026:** the four-XDR pilot, run with Owner's "super sick GTMAI lead and a really good bisops team", "made 85% more calls and produced 85% more opportunities than than their baseline" in "two weeks of work" ([[src-the-claude-code-era-mastering-autonomous-gtm-agents-jordan-crawford]] @40:54–42:57). The same pilot, with YG named by the source page: four BDRs, the GTM AI lead and a bizops person in Toronto ran hourly iteration loops, and "within 2 days" output was up ([[src-a-product-informed-approach-for-gtm-usha-iyer-chief-customer-growt]] @51:56–53:59). On The Signal, Kyle said he hired a GTM AI lead "a couple months ago", then a director for applied AI focused on growth ([[src-signal-learn-build-scale]] @13:17).
- **May 2026:** "Our applied AI lead" watched BDRs work and collapsed everything between calls into one box, AI PCR ([[src-web-2026-05-01-attio-gtm-atlas-start-with-the-data-gtm-atlas]]).
