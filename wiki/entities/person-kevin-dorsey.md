# Kevin "KD" Dorsey

CRO @ Finally (ex-Bench, PatientPop, ServiceTitan); a two-time guest and a peer Kyle trades AI notes with. Guest on [[revenue-leadership-podcast]] E2 ([[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]], 2024-10-02) and E30 ([[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]], 2025-04-23). Kyle also cites him in E23 ([[src-E23-a-guide-to-high-stakes-decisions-with-ashley-grech-cro-of-x]]) and E33 ([[src-E33-what-it-takes-to-be-a-revenue-leader-in-the-ai-era-with-mike]]).

## What Kyle said/asked in the episodes

Bullets are copied from the source page's "What Kyle said" section; quotes are verbatim from the transcript.

### E2 (2024-10-02) · [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]]

- Why he does the show: "Most of the reason I chose to do this podcast is now I get to drill people I respect with questions for an hour every single week." [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]] @22:11 ([[revenue-leadership-podcast]])
- Bloom's taxonomy: design skill-building at the top of the pyramid (analyze, create). He has reps write their own call script from his cold-calling principles (pattern interrupt, credibility, relevance, intrigue, offer of value), because "the process of creation bakes in". He keeps a "management development book". [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]] @32:51–35:01 ([[sales-coaching-and-enablement]], [[sales-methodology]])
- "We're in the resource allocation business." [[src-E02-cro-kevin-dorsey-on-creating-developing-and-deploying-play]] @35:01 ([[prioritization-5p]])

### E30 (2025-04-23) · [[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]]

- He was "just about to post a role for... a Head of Applied AI" (the name borrowed from Ramp) to custom-build internal tools beyond what RevOps runs. [[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]] @09:09–10:11 ([[gtm-engineering]], [[ai-in-gtm]])
- Owner works with a vendor ("Avara" in transcript) on AI sales sims trained on Owner's methodology and call records, and is pointing the same tooling at real calls. His goal is a "management intelligence layer": know whether each rep is "on or off wiggle" on every taught behavior, bucketed into a taxonomy and weighted by correlation to win rate. [[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]] @18:19–20:23 ([[sales-coaching-and-enablement]], [[ai-sales-stack]])
- Uses Super Whisper or ChatGPT voice mode to draft docs: "it's about ship rate. Like, just ship it." AI lets you "dress up" a rough checklist. [[src-E30-building-ai-powered-sales-teams-with-finallys-cro-kevin-kd]] @26:27–29:32 ([[playbooks-and-documentation]], [[personal-ai-stack]])
