# Adam Guild (Co-founder & CEO, Owner)

Co-founder and CEO of [[owner-com]], with co-founder Dean. Kyle met both through GTM Fund's investment in Owner and joined in June 2022 ([[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @02:08–03:15). Everything here is what Kyle (or, where marked, Jason Lemkin) has said about him.

## Origin story (as Kyle tells it)

- Adam ran the second-biggest Minecraft server at about 14, then saved his mom's dog-grooming business, which led to restaurant digital marketing; he chose restaurants as the biggest market ([[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @18:54–19:55; [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @03:04–07:10; [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @30:20).
- Adam got the earlier business over $1M ARR (2018–2020). COVID took it to zero; he rebuilt online ordering from March 2020 and was back over $1M ARR within a year ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @01:01–02:03; [[src-startwell-cro-tells-founders]] @03:04–07:10).

## What Kyle has said about him

- **Why Kyle joined.** He met Adam through GTM Fund's investment, held weekly calls and advised, then joined out of FOMO: "I can fast-forward this movie three or five years" ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @27:47–28:50). Adam is "a force of nature" ([[src-the-simmer-owner-cro]] @27:58–30:00). Adam "put the tractor beam on me" ([[src-E52-winning-in-crowded-markets-the-cro-playbook-jonathan-leaf-cro-ba]] @69:39); Scott Barker told Kyle the diligence call was really a sales call ([[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @19:55–20:58).
- **Age gap.** Adam "was like 23 when I started" ([[src-kyle-nortons-bets-for-gtm-domination]] @62:07; [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @61:13). Kyle "was like 36 at the time" they met ([[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @22:00).
- **Learning speed.** Adam read *Made to Stick* within about 18 hours of Kyle mentioning it and came back with expert-level questions ([[src-grow-and-tell-selling-to-smbs]] @30:55; [[src-venture-podcast-cro-owner]] @29:18–30:20; [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @62:17–63:21). Adam out-working Kyle was a self-esteem shock; a Thursday idea came back Sunday night as a 17-page document ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @08:10–10:15).
- **Focus.** The CEO spotted a lower-churn, higher-performing segment, and narrowing TAM about 90% sharpened messaging ([[src-gtmnow-5-slow-is-smooth]] @33:54–37:00). Advisory work gave Kyle alignment with Adam that pre-live churn was too high, so fixing it came first ([[src-pavilion-decoding-churn-retention]] @12:32; [[src-pocus-scaling-3m-to-21m]] @12:36–14:45). Kyle gave Adam a heads-up before the short-term dip ([[src-andys-pod-saying-no-record-growth]] @15:28–16:28).
- **Brand bet.** Organic/YouTube was Adam's contrarian bet, and the VP marketing reports to Adam ([[src-20vc-20sales-2m-to-40m-arr]] @32:09–34:13). Owner spent "probably like a million dollars on organic brand when we were like a 10 million ARR company"; Adam spent about 10 hours every Saturday recording content ([[src-finn-thormeier-sales-life-career]] @12:19–13:22).
- **Customer closeness.** Adam gives customers his cell number; both co-founders stay close to customers ([[src-E18-confessions-from-a-first-time-cro-with-catie-ivey-of-walnut]] @42:04–43:07). Owner's founder sends the investor update on the first of the month and has "Never, ever missed that deadline" ([[src-E21-from-building-startups-to-betting-on-them-with-jason-gelman]] @55:22).
- **Transparency.** Adam shared his own critical feedback at an all-hands ([[src-pillar-talk-navigating-sales-leadership]] @25:51–27:56).
- **Working together.** With Adam, Kyle practices something closer to trusting conviction than disagree-and-commit, given Adam's customer intuition ([[src-verticals-revenue-machine]] @15:39–16:44). Adam is the model behind Kyle's founder framework (force of nature, learning machine or "supercomputer", founder-market fit, humility) ([[src-bravado-scaling-smb-30m]] @16:55–20:07; [[src-gtmnow-163-2m-to-50m-arr]] @61:13–66:35). The founders hiring someone at Kyle's level meant they were "all in" ([[src-substack-2026-04-09-sales-hiring-has-a-charisma-problem]], preview only).
