### E37 (2025-06-18) · [[src-E37-jason-wolf-on-uncovering-hidden-growth-opportunities-in-go-t]]

- Companies pick growth vectors badly "because of reasoning by analogy": they mimic competitors going upmarket or adding capabilities instead of reasoning from first principles. [[src-E37-jason-wolf-on-uncovering-hidden-growth-opportunities-in-go-t]] @09:12 ([[icp-and-saying-no]])
- GTM's role in product: with too much GTM influence, "your road map just ends up being whatever the biggest most recent customer" wants. With too little, product disconnects from revenue. Not every GTM leader should weigh in on the roadmap, because it "takes a lot of humility" to admit you're not "a full stack thinker". [[src-E37-jason-wolf-on-uncovering-hidden-growth-opportunities-in-go-t]] @35:41, @56:05–57:07 ([[marketing-alignment]])
- AI-informed buyers: he ran ChatGPT deep research to get a full market map (competitors, pricing) before a purchase. "I've never been the easiest guy to sell to", and a well-armed buyer is "a nightmare situation" for an unprepared AE. [[src-E37-jason-wolf-on-uncovering-hidden-growth-opportunities-in-go-t]] @49:58–50:58 ([[buyer-ai-aeo-geo]], [[ai-in-gtm]])
