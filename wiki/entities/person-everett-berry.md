# Everett Berry (Clay)

Clay's head of GTM engineering. The E65 transcript spells his name "Everett Barry". He interviewed Kyle at the closed-door Clay CRO Summit for E65 and introduced it as "part three of Kyle and I's podcast series on GTM engineering" ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @01:00).

## The three-part series

- **Part 1, E45 (2025-09-03), "The Inside Scoop on GTM Engineering".** Berry explains Clay's forward-deployed GTM-engineer sales model. Kyle introduces "lossiness" from his "half-written Substack post": chained prompts with no human check add up to "slop" ([[src-E45-the-inside-scoop-on-gtm-engineering-with-clays-everett-berr]] @16:17–18:20). Kyle's GTM25 thesis: first-party data via [[momentum]] and third-party data via [[clay]] are "like 80% of your AI roadmap"; Owner "built a bunch of this in-house because it was before Clay like exploded" and is talking about "converging on Clay" (@37:44–38:47). On Owner: "our system of record is Snowflake not Salesforce" (@43:52–44:54). The advice he gives "the most most commonly" is to fix data quality first, and to "spend four to six months" on it (@34:42–35:42). He prefers Anthropic models for writing (@25:29).
- **Part 2, E49 (2025-10-15), "All about AI and GTM at Clay - Round Two".** A listener Q&A. Kyle: "I'm becoming a bit of a onetrick pony these days cuz all anybody wants to talk about is AI in go to market" ([[src-all-about-ai-and-gtm-at-clay-round-two-with-everett-berry]] @02:01). His rule: "no new surfaces. Don't give people a new surface" (@21:25). Owner "hired a GTMAI lead" ([[person-yg]]) and Kyle has "earmarked... potentially custom building our tech stack" because sales-engagement tools are email-centric and Owner sells by phone (@24:28–25:30). A parallel dialer had reps "making three times as many dials, booking less ops" (@25:30–26:32). He expects "a bifurcation" of reps, with the best doing "three, four, $5 million in AR every year" (@35:40–36:42), and calls fundraising "a flight to quality" (@45:48).
- **Part 3, E65 (2026-03-25).** Berry interviews Kyle at the Clay CRO Summit (below).

## What Kyle has said about him

- Kyle pushed back on Everett's suggestion that GTM collapses into two roles, GTM engineers and reps, calling it "a little reductive". Owner calls the role "applied AI" ("Some people say GTME, like tomato, tomato") ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @13:45).
- Kyle argues the opposite: more specialization on the front line, with convergence only at the leadership level ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @13:45–14:50).
