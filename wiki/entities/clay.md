# Clay

A GTM data enrichment and workflow platform. Its head of GTM engineering, [[person-everett-berry]], interviewed Kyle at the closed-door Clay CRO Summit for E65 ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]]).

## What Kyle has said about it

- **He'd buy it today.** Owner built waterfall enrichment in late 2022 "before clay was a thing", and only two people can operate it. "I would just use clay" ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @26:26).
- **Podcast prep runs on a Clay table.** An intake form webhooks into the table, and prompt columns research the guest and draft the docket ([[src-E65-inside-our-ai-sales-stack-kyle-norton]] @19:03–21:10; [[src-crew-podcast-how-best-cros-hire]] @64:02).
- **Email at scale:** a planned "monster clay table" plus an MCP server connected to Snowflake, to warm up accounts before BDRs call ([[src-collin-cadmus-growing-owner-1b]] @09:23).
- **Third-party data option:** "Clay + a Clay agency" as an alternative to building it yourself ([[src-kyle-nortons-bets-for-gtm-domination]] @31:30–35:36). Clay and FullEnrich for a market map ([[src-gtmshift-your-gtm-worked-live]] @02:05–03:06).
- **Sophistication ladder:** Clay/Make/n8n workflows are rung 3 ([[src-captivate-talent-stop-vibe-hiring]] @02:02–03:02). See [[ai-sophistication-ladder]].
- (Guest, not Kyle) On E68, Cassie Young cites "Becca at Clay's" maturity curve (individual → centralized → GTM alpha) ([[src-E68-are-you-uninvestable-the-new-gtm-reality]] @34:48–41:04).
