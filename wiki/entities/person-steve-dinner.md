# Steve Dinner (VP RevOps, Owner)

Owner's VP of RevOps and Kyle's longest-running collaborator. Kyle introduces him on his own episode as "my VB of RevOps here at owner" ([[src-E46-the-revops-mind-behind-ownercoms-billion-dollar-valuation]]). They worked together at League, then Shopify, then Owner, where Steve was RevOps hire #1 ([[src-E46-the-revops-mind-behind-ownercoms-billion-dollar-valuation]] @01:00–02:00, @74:27).

## His own episode: E46 (2025-09-10)

- **Origin.** Kyle and Steve "have worked together for almost a decade". Kyle hired him out of a rep role to be a BDR manager at League "because I interviewed him and he just thought in systems and programmatically". Steve became BDR director and took over RevOps, with "stops at Shopify", and had been at Owner "for over two years" ([[src-E46-the-revops-mind-behind-ownercoms-billion-dollar-valuation]] @01:00–02:00).
- **Design philosophy.** Kyle: "how do we make our golden path the lowest friction path and how do we add friction to the places where you don't want people to go" (@28:28). Treating people as untrustworthy with validation fields backfires: "people will adopt that mindset if that's the way you treat them" (@22:23).
- **Invest early.** Owner hired Steve full-time at about $5M ARR, began staffing at about $7–8M, and had seven people at $50M ARR, adding a head of architecture. Kyle: "It's seven people and we're 50 million AR." and "what's expensive is having a hundred frontline go to market reps that are 2% 5% 20% less efficient than they should be." (@68:20–69:22)
- **How Steve runs it.** He spends 30–40% of his week in functional rituals and does monthly ride-alongs (@04:01–06:02, @27:28). The team runs two-week agile sprints with four product owners (marketing, sales, launch, CS), a PM and contractors (@40:42–45:48). His motto for SOPs: "The whole process is turning variables into constants." (@48:51–50:54)
- Steve recalls Kyle telling him at their first meeting, "We see the world the same way." (@55:00)
