### E34 (2025-05-28) · [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]]

- "great enablement looks way more like sports training than it looks like uh HR learning and development". Enablement "is criminally underinvested in", often "an afterthought". [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @05:07 ([[sales-coaching-and-enablement]])
- He interviews every sales candidate "all the way to SDRs". He tells them what's *not* great about Owner: "We're not a big work life culture company. Like we work really hard... It's a performance culture." Even top performers get coached monthly on a "secret sauce" with their manager. A rep who wants to be left alone while crushing "won't like owner". [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @24:34–26:36 ([[sales-hiring]], [[culture-and-high-standards]])
- "Flexibility within a framework": overly rigid scripts take away autonomy and agency, which has "very like negative motivational consequences". Shared deal language (e.g., Challenger Customer's skeptic/blocker) "accelerates things so much" and is "the oil that really like lubricates an organization". [[src-E34-why-enablement-is-the-most-overlooked-growth-lever-with-luke]] @57:13–59:15 ([[sales-methodology]], [[playbooks-and-documentation]])
