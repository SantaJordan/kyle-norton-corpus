- "one of my most formative experiences as revenue leader was learning how Shopify built product". [[src-a-product-informed-approach-for-gtm-usha-iyer-chief-customer-growt]] @02:02 ([[shopify]])
- Instrumentation at Owner: to be "a hypothesisdriven experiment experimentationdriven revenue org", mandate data capture. "We use momentum as our call recorder and sort of like CRM uh AI CRM updater like you have to use it on every call." Emails go through Salesloft, "not just randomly from your inbox", while removing as much friction as possible. [[src-a-product-informed-approach-for-gtm-usha-iyer-chief-customer-growt]] @39:39–40:40 ([[revops]], [[ai-sales-stack]])
- Pilot-then-scale with AI: "if you push out two or three tools you're like this is gamechanging... and then it flops... you very quickly lose credibility with your team". Owner example: "we're able to improve the per rep output by 85% of our BDR team". A group of four BDRs, the GTM AI lead ([[person-yg]]) and a bizops person went to Toronto and ran hourly iteration loops. "within 2 days" output was cranked. Other BDRs saw the results, and it rolled out to outbound BDRs, then other BDR/SDR teams. Doing it org-wide first would mean troubleshooting "across like 50 reps instead of four".
