# Asad Zaman (CEO, STA)

CEO of STA (Sales Talent Agency), an executive and sales recruiting firm, and a co-host of Pavilion's Topline podcast. He is a two-time guest on Kyle's show (E6, E57), has hosted Kyle on Topline three times (E78, E92, E99), and published a written Q&A with Kyle. Kyle calls him "one of the most thoughtful people in the space that I know. Somebody that I lean on uh for insight when it comes to hiring" ([[src-the-new-rules-of-executive-recruiting-in-the-ai-era-asad-zaman-ceo]] @01:02–02:03).

## On Kyle's show

- **E6 (2024-10-30), "Untold Strategies of High-Impact Sales Recruitment".** Kyle says "this is the first recording of the podcast" and that hiring is what "I get asked about the most of anything" ([[src-E06-untold-strategies-of-high-impact-sales-recruitment-with-asad]] @01:05–02:05). Asad's system: launch, search and last mile; talent = Drive, Nature, Acumen; and the hiring memo. His lines: "nobody is better than somebody bad." (@07:11) and "give me another one is the most underappreciated interview tactic ever" (@55:41), which Kyle called "a light bulb for me". Kyle shares Owner's process: a 20–25 minute role video, job posts listing what's good and not good, a case study, mutual references, and LinkedIn posting, which saved "hundreds of thousands of dollars" in fees (@26:55–37:09). On planning: "you can torture a financial model and make it tell you anything you want" (@10:17).
- **E57 (2025-12-10), "The New Rules of Executive Recruiting in the AI Era".** Kyle: "I think the era of the pure people manager is is ending." ([[src-the-new-rules-of-executive-recruiting-in-the-ai-era-asad-zaman-ceo]] @08:11). He describes his senior-hire case study, "come in and coach me as if I'm the rep", with the feedback "Just coach one thing, but let's go deeper on that thing." (@53:10–54:12). He calls himself "a real snob about CRO" (@38:48). Asad says founders and CROs look at Owner and say "I want what Kyle has" (@23:36), and that STA moved from take-home case studies to live whiteboarding because AI made homework low-signal (@05:08–06:09).

## On Topline (Asad as co-host)

- **E78 (2024-10-06).** Kyle, two to three weeks after launching his show, sides with Lemkin: "fear is sort of the end of you as a revenue leader" ([[src-topline-e78-executive-fear-boards]] @05:07). Asad says Kyle out-listened his ~50,000 minutes of podcasts the prior year (@02:02, host claim). Kyle lists Asad among his first guests (@62:22–64:29).
- **E92 (2025-01-19).** Asad and AJ Bruno host Kyle on how great CROs drive enterprise value ([[src-topline-e92-great-cros-enterprise-value]]).
