### E26 (2025-03-26) · [[src-E26-signals-that-separate-great-companies-from-risky-bets-with-n]]

- **Leading AI from the front:** "unfortunately, I am probably the most deeply entrenched in AI right now of anybody in my go-to-market team... I would prefer that not to be the case". "I just have such a strong conviction that this will touch every single part of our business." [[src-E26-signals-that-separate-great-companies-from-risky-bets-with-n]] @31:59 ([[ai-operating-model]], [[ai-in-gtm]])

Even at two failures and three successes, the cost of not pursuing them "is much too great in my opinion". Waiters end up "12, 18, 24 months behind", and "you just better hope that your direct competitor is not farther along". "for us, the advantage in rep productivity and like revenue growth and the LTV CAC profile is like pretty profound. Like it is a very durable business advantage for us."
