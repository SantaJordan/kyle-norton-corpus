Apply it to org design: pick what serves the customer best, because otherwise "you disappear into a spreadsheet." "We close like hundreds of deals a month, like 400 or 500 deals a month." The gap was "we weren't sharing the rationale behind the decision making publicly enough". Owner now publishes a synthesized version of each manager-channel debate, including one where a manager admitted the mistake and gave up credit.
