- **Owner 2026 vendor cuts:** Owner is "going through like line by line, vendor by vendor, agency by by agency, asking ourselves like is this absolutely necessary? Could we do with 50% less?" Why: "there's lots of mechanisms to add things to companies that there's no cadence or rhythm or ritual to remove things other than maybe annual planning if you have a CFO that has good stewardship." - **Owner applied AI:** jamming context across Claude MCPs and OpenAI tools leaves you "in this wonky spot". "we hired a head of applied AI for um go to market to try to just like solve it in this part of the business." Slop comes from chaining "multiple layers of generative outputs ... you're stacking lossiness, you're stacking hallucination".
