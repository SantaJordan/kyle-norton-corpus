### E32 (2025-05-08) · [[src-E32-layering-slg-without-breaking-the-product-with-john-eitel]]

- Owner uses a product-intelligence tool ("Pavlov" in transcript; the guest calls it "Palpat") to find the key adoption metric and run campaigns. He calls the hybrid "semi-selfserve": reactive (a self-serve user raises a hand) plus proactive (deploy people when users stall). "that line is going to get blurred". [[src-E32-layering-slg-without-breaking-the-product-with-john-eitel]] @57:58–59:00 ([[smb-sales-motion]], [[ai-in-gtm]])
- Talent market "flight to quality", mirroring venture. Good revenue leaders "are swimming in opportunity", but for a "middle of the pack revenue leader, it's actually a very unkind market". His best former reps are "swimming in interesting opportunities". [[src-E32-layering-slg-without-breaking-the-product-with-john-eitel]] @69:08–71:10 ([[talent-density]], [[career-and-leadership]])
- His career advice: "optimizing way more for company quality and way less on title and role and scope". "you're much better off being a firstline manager versus a second line like quoteunquote VP of sales at a mediocre company." [[src-E32-layering-slg-without-breaking-the-product-with-john-eitel]] @79:17 ([[career-and-leadership]])
